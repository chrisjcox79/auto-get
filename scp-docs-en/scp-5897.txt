                        

Keter

Containment area must be 10 meters by 10 meters. 05 permission to enter containment area/ testing. One person in the containment area at a time.

SCP-5897 is a humanoid looking child that doesn’t seem to age and seems to be able to bend reality. Can be harmed by almost nothing and seems to be completely heat resistant. Has the ability to cause a nuclear level of explosion that doesn’t harm them. The skin of the entity temperature 1,300 Fahrenheit but can’t actually melt anything. the entity does need food and water.
