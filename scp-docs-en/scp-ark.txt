SCP-ARK

20 Dec 2020 05:39

Seeking Greenlights:  Yes

Page Type:  SCP Article

Genre (Optional):  (Other: slightly creepy)

Page Layout (Optional):  Containment, Description, and then hints at what the greater picture is via an incident

Elevator Pitch:  An SCP that bring life via anomalous means, and has ties to the biblical SCP's such as Dr. Clef's Proposal, and 4840

Central Narrative:  In simple term's: its Noah's ark, with that SCP twist to it. The ark is buried underneath a mountain, now making it a 'man made cavern'. This cavern is constantly dripping water via anomalous means, and once a day, a drop of water turns to blood, and replicates until it turns into two full specimens of a normal animal species: one male and one female. Originally the foundation was fine with discretely relocating the animals to a natural habitat, until during a surgical inspection one started speaking, talking of biblical anomalies and telling the foundation they should just let the cavern make life. It tells them that after a catastrophe this was chosen as “life’s safe haven”, and itd be better to stay that way in the event another cataclysm occurs. After this, the foundation started killing all the new specimens, in fear all of them are unnoticeably anomalous.

Hook/Attention-Grabber:  It's short, sweet, and too the point. It tells the story and leaves the mystery. Think of it like a different take on SCP-2000: an anomaly that (for the most part) creates normal life, but the foundation decides to contain it as they understand it even less.
