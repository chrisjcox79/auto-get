SCP IDEA: 

20 Nov 2020 23:03

Seeking Greenlights: Yes

Page Type: SCP Article

Elevator Pitch: A sentient Siberian town wants all who come across it to never leave.

Central Narrative: The SCP is a small village in Siberia. The village is populated by human-like creatures. The longer you stay in the village the more it starts to figure out your personality. It will slowly form itself to fit your ideal town. As time goes on the village is almost like a paradise for you. If you try to leave the people in the village will take you to a room and turn you into one of them. If you don't try to leave you slowly transform and become one of the people there. Basically, the village doesn't want you to leave. It learns about you to keep you from leaving.

Hook/Attention-Grabber: The town consumes all who come across it and turns them into the people who inhabit the town.
