F.A.Q.

5159

Level1

Containment Class:
safe

Secondary Class:
none

Disruption Class:
vlam

Risk Class:
notice

link to memo

~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Special Containment Procedures:  SCP-5159 is to be kept in a standard humanoid domicile at Site-19, and is free to roam around the site at will. No additional monitoring or containment procedures are necessary with the exception of one security camera inside his domicile.

Description:  SCP-5159 is an elderly male of American descent, measuring 1.6 meters in height, and 72 kilograms in weight. SCP-5159 speaks with a southern American accent, has blue eyes, and gray hair, though he is practically bald. SCP-5159 is roughly 80-90 years of age and has not aged since its discovery by the foundation. A tattoo reading “Mr. Emotions, from Little Misters ® by Dr. Wondertainment appears on the back of SCP-5159’s neck.

SCP-5159 appears to exhibit a glowing outline around its body, with a constant luminance of approximately 10 lumens for the majority of the time. The outline changes hues depending on his mood and/or emotions. The outline is blue when sad or distressed, purple when nervous or anxious, green when lying, white when surprised, red when angered, pink when confused, yellow when serious, orange when happy, and black when tired or asleep. SCP-5159 seems to be aware of this outline, and said that it has affected several aspects of his life in ways that “he would rather not talk about”. SCP-5159 claims that should he die, he will still possess his outline, this time in a permanent pitch-black hue, that instead of displaying light, absorbs exceeded amounts of it.

SCP-5159’s outline has been observed to glow brighter depending on the intensity of the emotion, with the maximum recorded luminance displayed by 5159 being 80 lumens, and the maximum recorded hue being yellow for 25 hours and 14 minutes before changing to black. When SCP-5159 was instructed to act sad, the outline turned the hue of the related emotion but was very dimly lit, at approximately 3 lumens of light, which is the lowest recorded luminance displayed by 5159.

SCP-5159 eats and drinks on a regular basis and to date has shown no other anomalous properties besides the outline surrounding its body. SCP-5159 has presented a strong interest in chameleons with extensive knowledge about them and attempts to engage in conversation when they are presented, yet despite his old age claims to have never owned one as a pet. SCP-5159 also has a basic understanding of light and waves.

Discovery:  SCP-5159 was discovered in South Texas by undercover Foundation agents in 2018, at which point he was transferred to Site-19 for further study and questioning. When found, he was wearing a white tank top, brown pants, and black shoes.

Addendum 5159-A1: 

To date, SCP-5159 has requested:

A Pet Chameleon (Awaiting Approval)
18 Chameleon Posters (Approved)
Meeting SCP-905 (Denied, In Reconsideration)
6 Decorative Plants (Approved)
A 32x16 Painting Of A Chameleon (Approved)
Expensive Soaps and Body Washes (Approved)
A Canvas and Painting Pallet (Approved)
An 88” TV (Denied)

Addendum 5159-B2: 

Interviewer: Dr. Gerald Tralmaxoth

Interviwee: SCP-5159

[BEGIN LOG]

[SCP-5159 enters the room, his outline purple, Dr.Tralmaxoth waiting for him in a chair, with Agent Darren listening through the cameras and intercom.]

[SCP-5159 sits down.]

Dr.Tralmaxoth:  So, shall we begin?

SCP-5159:  [His outline slowly fading to yellow] Yes, sir.

Dr.Tralmaxoth:  To start, are you aware of your “outline”?

SCP-5159:  Yes, however, I do not recall where or how I got it. I’ve just had it for as long as I can remember.

Dr.Tralmaxoth:  I see, do you have any other relatives with the same outline you possess?

SCP-5159:  No, sir. I believe I have been like this since birth and I haven’t even had relatives.

Dr.Tralmaxoth:  Very well. Now that we're on the topic of birth, where and when were you born?

SCP-5159:  [His outline immediately turning pink.] I cannot recall exactly where I was born, but I can guarantee you it was somewhere in America, as I’m sure you can tell by my accent. And just like my birthplace, as for my birthdate, I cannot tell you exactly, but doing the math it must have been in the 1920s-1930s. [SCP-5159’s outline slowly fades to yellow]

Dr.Tralmaxoth:  Alright then, about all the chameleons, why? What is so interesting about them? Why do you have so many posters all over the walls of your domicile?

Agent Darren:  Sir, that's not-

Dr.Tralmaxoth:  I know.

SCP-5159:  [Outline quickly fades to white] Well sir, [Outline fading to orange] I’m certain you know this but chameleons change color based on their moods, like me, not based on their surroundings! I’ve always wanted one my entire life, to finally, at last, have someone with the same- what do you call it?.. anomalous properties as me!

Dr.Tralmaxoth:  I see.

[SCP-5159's orange outline becomes brighter as he smiles]

Dr.Tralmaxoth:  Moving on, what do you know about SCP-905, Mr.Chameleon?

SCP-5159:  I don’t know much, but from what I’ve heard he also produces light but in a different way. Even then, I would still love to meet him!

Dr.Tralmaxoth:  I'm afraid that’s not possible, but I’ll talk to my superiors. Anyways, has your outline affected your life in any way? Both positive and/or negative?

SCP-5159:  [Outline turning blue] I- I would rather not talk about it. May we please move on to the next question?

Dr.Tralmaxoth:  5159, plea-

SCP-5159:  [Outline turns bright red] I said next question!

Dr.Tralmaxoth:  Alright, alright. What knowledge do you have regarding Dr.Wondertainment?

SCP-5159:  [Outline quickly turning pink] I don’t know who that is.

Dr.Tralmaxoth:  Alright, one last question. Hypothetically, If you were to die, what color would your outline be?

SCP-5159:  [Outline turning green] I believe it would be black, just like how when I’m asleep, no emotions, my brain is completely shut off. Instead of showing light, it would absorb it. A lot.

[END LOG]
