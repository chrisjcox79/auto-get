SCP idea: greg the normal guy

30 Dec 2020 06:08

Greg is a 23 year old white man who is described by those who interacted with him as unremarkable and boring in every way. He is noted as having an aura of normalcy that temporarily makes people around him as boring as he is, as well as seemingly nullifying any anomolies until they have left an area of 10 feet around him

On humans, the effects can last anywhere from 2 hours after they've left or even a full week.

I'd love some feedback and ideas for this article
