                        

SCP-5162

Class: Keter

Codenames: The Decaying Savanna, The Death Fields, and The Monochrome Decay

SCP-5162 is a mostly monochrome field in the savanna of the [REDACTED] country, [REDACTED]. The grass of SCP-5162 seems to be a mixture of several shades of blue and gray, blueish gray trees with orange leaves are very prominent in SCP-5612. The inhabitants consist of bovine creatures, that seem to decay the longer you look at them, these bovine creatures are 5 meters tall and 3 meters wide, with horns that approximate to be 12 meters long and a foot wide, these bovine creatures are referred to as SCP-5162-1. Any other organisms that enter SCP-5612 become a blueish gray tint, remaining in SCP-5162-1 for a long periods of time infect the organism with a fungus refered to as SCP-5162-2, SCP-5162-2 infects and decays any organism(s) in scp-5152. If metal is put inside of SCP, it quickly rusts.

It is unknown if SCP-5162-2 speeds up the organism(s) infected, or decays the organism(s).

If footage is taken of SCP-5162, it changes its color to match a normal savanna. But reviewing the footage of SCP-5162 it is back to its original state, but a organism refered to as SCP-5162-3 walks from behind a tree, presenting itself. SCP-5162-3 is a humanoid creature, that seems to be made out of SCP-5162-1’s skin, but twisted to make a humanoid shape, it has one glowing eye where it’s face should be, SCP-5162-3’s presence disorientates the audio and camera footage. SCP-5162-3 is usually docile, but if looking through a camera while taking footage of SCP-5162, it is hostile and attacks the viewer, infecting them with SCP-5162-2.

It is theorized that SCP-5162 may be able to exterminate SCP-682, the theory is unlikely but there is still a chance it may be able to exterminate 682. Testing this theory is currently waiting for approval.

SCP-5162 was brought to the foundation’s attention by [REDACTED] a residence of [REDACTED]

The best containment method known to the SCP-foundation for SCP-5162 is creating a concrete barrier with the diameter 20 by 8 by 13 meters, and 24/7 surveillance. Only accessible by personnel approved by the 05-council.

No further information is known about SCP-5162
