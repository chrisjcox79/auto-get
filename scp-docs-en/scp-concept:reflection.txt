SCP Concept: Reflection

21 Nov 2020 19:50

Seeking Greenlights:  Yes

Page Type:  SCP Article

Elevator Pitch:  This SCP is an anomalous event in a gas station, where if an individual looks at the bathroom's mirror for enough time they will be controlled and later killed by their reflection.

Central Narrative:  An SCP Foundation Researcher is driving in his car to his house, he suddenly sees a gas station and stops there to wash his face. He enters the bathroom and begins realizing this gas station was never here before, hence why he didn't recognize it. As he was washing his face, the Researcher began looking intently at the mirror and started scratching his arm uncontrollably, grabbed his hair and breathed heavily, showing signs of paranoia, then suddenly began acting against his own will and followed his reflection's movement. After a bit, the reflection smiled and grabbed a hammer, so did the Researcher, they both swung the hammer towards the mirror, shattering it, and the Staff member dropped dead in the bathroom with multiple cuts around his body. The Foundation later found the body and started studying the SCP, theorizing it's origin and it's containment.

Hook/Attention-Grabber:  The gas station is often moved between places and awaits for it's next victim, and what's interesting to note is that the individual starts becoming their own reflection and begins following it's movement. It swaps the real person with the reflection, which can't escape the mirror and can only kill others.

Additional Notes:  The SCP manipulates and kills people in the same way every time, however the anomaly doesn't work if multiple people are witnessing, which is why it mostly targets one or two people. After the mirror is shattered and the individual is dead, the gas station will move locations which leaves the body on the ground in the location of the murder and rebuilds the mirror instantly.

DISCLAIMER: I am a newbie and I can't give green-lights.

I'm leaving this here temporarily, I shall add critique below this as soon as I am able to.

Ello there! So there's quite a bit I really enjoy about this, and then there is some stuff I'd like to give advice on.

I'll start off with what I thought was really good about this concept.

1. I really love how your SCP is setup, it's simple and to the point. It makes it really easy to come across and understand.

2. I also really like the concept, it's one that can lead to a lot when it comes to storytelling of the Foundation staff or people around the SCP. The fact that the SCP moves between different places with not many restrictions means that your story is not constrained from one part physical world. This means you can add lore and interviews revolving around the SCP from multiple different places. Heck, I'd say go one step further and add some wackiness in there. If the SCP isn't restricted to being in a place like a city, have one of the reported locations that it was found in be something crazy like in the middle of a uninhabited desert.

+block

–block

Now I do have some things I'd like to address that I'm quite confused on.

1. Try being more specific about how the SCP operates. I especially think you need to give some more detail into the  part of the SCP itself.

What kind of gas station is it? Is it of a specific custom brand or does it change randomly between other gas station brands to help its manipulation?

Does the gas station change its products, shape, and pricing based on where it's located or is it the same no matter where it is?

Does the SCP have any restrictions to where it changes its location? Can it appear in any part of the world or does it only spawn in areas like cities and common gas-station locations? I get that you described in the findings that the SCP was .

2. How does the Foundation know about these findings? According to your story, the body of the staff member was found, but that doesn't describe to the Foundation what exactly happened.

If the Foundation can't find the gas station (since it moves after the mirror is shattered) how does the Foundation know that the staff member was even killed by an anomalous entity?

I recommend giving more explanation into this. Here's some ideas I can give:

Have the Foundation ask other staff in the local area of any sudden changes.
Have the Foundation spot a correlation between multiple discoveries of the same kind of events.

Re: SCP Concept: Reflection

Disclaimer: I am a newbie and cannot grant you greenlight. 

Hey there, Cesss. The idea itself is pretty unique, if nothing else. I've been following the SCP Foundation for a couple of years and I don't think I ever read about a Gas station being an SCP before. I could also see a couple possible options for a good interview being made out of the narrative you have provided, which is always nice to see.

Now, I would have a question, if I may: does this SCP seek out Foundation personnel to make into it's victims on it's own? Because while I know that Foundation personnel are the most likely to die at the hands of SCPs, I find it peculiar in your article.

You said the Researcher was driving home from work, meaning he was leaving a Site or Facility of the Foundation. I would be of the opinion that the Foundation keeps a good patrol around their Facilities, taking in consideration what they work with.

Next, you said that this SCP swaps the real person with the reflection. I take it then, that the Researcher was trapped in a pocket dimension within the mirror, looking at the reflection which was now controlling his body? Then, if they both swung a hammer at each other, which can only inflict bludgeoning wounds, why did the Research die of multiple lacerations?

Another question I would have: how would this SCP be contained?

Lastly, there are a couple of SCPs with reflection as their main selling point on the side. Currently I can think of SCP-125, and SCP-1620. As the author of your SCP, you know the exact details to your article, so I would recommend you looking up any other SCPs like 125 and 1620 and check for similarities, in order to avoid your article being too similar to already existing ones.

With that said, I kind of really like your article here. With a bit of fine-tuning and a bit more highlight into the swapping process, I could see this getting somewhere.

Again, please remember that I am a newbie. Take everything I said with a grain of salt.

Hey there Cesss, I'll be critting your idea today!

Note that I am not a particularly experienced nor official reviewer (like the others here), so take what I say with a grain of salt. With that being said, let's begin the crit!

++

--

Elevator Pitch

I'm going to be honest, I don't really like this idea. All it is is a random gas station that, for some reason, has mirrors that cause your reflection to take control of you and then kill you. Why? Are the reflections like some kind of demon or something? This just seems like a less interesting/creative version of SCP-919, which presents a moral/ethical dilemma over whether the reflections are animate or not, rather than a reflection that just kills you.

Central Narrative

This narrative is adequate, but I still don't like the concept behind it. It just isn't interesting enough for me to have a random gas station mirror with murderous, possessive reflections. There is also a logistical problem behind your narrative: how did the Foundation find this footage? Unless there was a camera on the Researcher's body (extremely unlikely), there is no way they could have information this detailed on the Researcher's death.

Hook/Attention Grabber

So the gas station moves and people start swapping consciousnesses their own reflections. That's it. The swapping part could be interesting if explored more, but the gas station (and the fact that it moves) seems disjointed. Why are the reflections part of a gas station? Why does the gas station move?
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

++

--

At the moment, your idea would likely be downvoted by members of this Site (myself included) if it became an article, mostly due to a lack of substance. This lack of substance takes the form of:

- A boring/uncreative anomaly

- Little story

- The slightly disjointed/random nature of the separate parts of the anomaly

- The fact it is a 'thing that kills you' SCP (a common trope on this Site, whereby the anomaly is simply something that kills you without much backstory or substance)

Simply put, this idea isn't interesting enough. All it is a killer reflection in a random, moving gas station. Now, if this were reflections trying to take over their 'real' counterparts (i.e switch bodies with them) and having identity crises, I'd be interested. Hearing the tale of a scientist who lost their body and gained it back after hunting their eisoptrophobic (afraid of mirrors) reflection and cornering them in a room with a mirror (idk, that's just a story I came up with. You can use it, if you want) is interesting to me. As it stands, this is just effectively an anomalous murderer. The end.

I'd recommend two things:

1. Check out the Guide Hub for some great ideation/narrative creation advice!

2. Contact reviewers from the Butterfly Squad to get some experienced alternative opinions!

And finally, to sum up your idea in one sentence1:

Moving gas station has mirrors which cause your reflection to possess you and make you kill yourself.

Just some things for you to reflect on.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Anyway, that's about it for my crit today. Thanks for reading, and hopefully this helps!

Footnotes
1. This is to assist you in getting a quick, outside view of your idea, which may help you change something you might have missed/you don't like about your own idea.
