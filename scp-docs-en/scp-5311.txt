                        

Description : SCP-5311 Class: Euclid is a 9 year old girl who loves to make paintings. She is 4'3 in height and weighs 153lb. The girl has short black hair and a white dress down to her knees. She has 2 beaded bracelets on her right arm that are also white. Not to mention the fact that she has incredibly bad hearing and will have to resort to reading things off of a piece of paper wen communicating with others. She is very sensitive to high pitched sounds. She has no known name and researchers has not found any info about her past yet.

Every 3 days personnel are to give the little girl a temporary new set of clothes while they wash her dress. She carries around a thick piece of paper and a paintbrush in order to make said art. he only paint in the color red, or sometimes she will paint with the blood of living things. Everyday they ar ordered to bring 1 Gallon of red paint served in a tub. Personnel tried to give her different colored paint but she will not accept it and often throw to to the side.

When SCP-5311 is friendly and will not attack anyone, but you try to harm her in any way she will drain all of your blood almost immediately. Every painting she makes that is finished will automatically duplicate itself. She has to power to heal herself by painting directly on the wound. Personnel are to carry out 1 Gallon of blood to the little girl each day to paint. The little girl loves making painting for staff but all contact with her must be limited at all costs.

Containment Procedures : SCP-5311 is contained in a 3 by 3 by 3 metal box with a Plastic table and a chair. She will also need 3 meals a day that needs to contain some kind of protein. She will be rewarded sweets she behaves well. Any videos or pictures are strictly prohibited and not allowed in any circumstances. That goes for any type of harm to her too. Anyone who tries to harm SCP-5311 will be shot immediately.

Testings : In May 4th, there was a test to put SCP-053 and SCP-5311 in the same room together for at least 5 hours. No video recordings were allowed so personnel had to watch, thy would rotate Every 9 minutes and report to researchers what they has seen. SCP-053 was too afraid to engage with SCP-5311 so she hid away from her. It been more than 10 minutes and SCP-5311 has not shown any signs of aggression to the young girl. SCP-053 then came out of hiding and crawled next to the little artist. She watched her paint and paint and both of them communicated for 3 hours.They seemed to be having a great time together. After that both of the girls fell asleep and SCP-5311 had to be escorted back into containment. The test has been a success and researchers are considering doing this again.
