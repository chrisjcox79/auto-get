(I wrote out this comment just now but wikidot lost it, so apologies if this is a double post)

I gotta be honest, this one doesn't quite work for me.

I can't deny that it's very well done, and it's a neat idea, but

For starters, the format screw isn't justified. Why is this its documentation?

And put completely bluntly, I feel like I've been scammed out of actual content. All these ideas are presented to me, but they're poor substitutes for actual content. It feels like these are the dregs of your other ideas, the things you've scrapped, and now you're hand-delivering them to me on a silver platter in place of a real SCP.

But what frustrates me most of all is that all of this is completely intentional. The fact that it has no justification to defend itself from my complaints is literally the whole point of the SCP. The skip scams people out of content, and the article does the same. It's genius, it really is. But it's the fact that I can't level a criticism at it without it laughing in my face that makes me feel like it's forcing my hand into an upvote when fundamentally it just doesn't work for me.

Ultimately this feels like a cop-out for actual content - a well-designed and incredibly executed cop-out, but a cop-out nonetheless. That makes me want to downvote.

If this were a -J or a tale, you'd get my vote no questions asked. But then it wouldn't quite have the same effect that it does now.

As it is, the SCP is actively fighting against my downvote. It's a stalemate - no vote from me, for now.

EDIT: It won.
