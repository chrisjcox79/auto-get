                        

Item #:  SCP-5129

Object Class:  Euclid

Special Containment Procedures:  When any personal member enters the room to clean it they must give it a gift or it will turn into its second form and escape

Description:  SCP-5129 is a creature with noted form-changing properties. The entity can take two forms, that of an unknown female cervid with white fur and that of a Canis Lupus with thick black fur. SCP-5129 is sapient and is capable of speech in Early Modern English. Whenever a human is seen by SCP-5129 the first thing it will do is ask for a gift by saying “Wh’re is mine own gift.”, it only accepts fruits and vegetables as gifts it will not accept anything else and after eating them it will stay in its first form. If a gift is not given within six seconds, if the gift is wrong, or if it is insulted or assaulted SCP-5129 will change into its second form and kill the victim, after eating said victim or victims SCP-5129 will change back into a white deer and walk away as if nothing happened.

When SCP-5129 is in its first form it is completely harmless, is capable of speaking, and perfectly willing to answer questions. When SCP-5129 turns into its second form it becomes life-threatening, unlike its first form it is completely incapable of speech only growling, it is highly resistant to gunfire, and it can move at speeds up to 96km. When this form is taken any personnel who is its target is to be abandoned and will not be aided in any way. When SCP-5129 is finished killing its targets it is usually covered in blood but when it changes back into its first form with any blood being absorbed by its fur.

Studies have shown that SCP-5129 is incredibly sensitive, with any insult or attempt at assault no matter how small causing it to change into its second form, It is also shown to be quite greedy always asking for a gift when it sees a member of personal even if it is a returning personal member who it has met before. When speaking to SCP-5129 it prefers to be talked to in a way a servant would talk to a member of royalty if attempting to talk to it casually it will begin to get more agitated until it changes into its second form. If SCP-5129 begins to become increasingly agitated with the questions being asked the interviewer must leave its room immediately.

Addendum 

Discovery: SCP-5129 was discovered in 20██ in ████████ following a string of hikers being murdered In a way that a carnivorous animal would eat its food, when field agents began searching the area of the deaths to apprehend SCP-5129 the containment process resulted in the death of most of the team except for one Agent known as Agent [REDACTED] who lived but is now missing most of his lower leg. The following is a description of that encounter from Agent [REDACTED] to Dr.████.

(Begin Log)

Dr.████: Please describe your Encounter with SCP-5129

Agent [Redacted]: Alright my team and I were investigating the forest where all of those hikers were murdered and we were keeping close together as we trained and then we hear some rustling in the bushes and outcomes this white deer.

Dr.████: which I’m going to assume is SCP-5129.

Agent [Redacted] Yeah, and at that point, we didn’t really care, and then we heard a female voice say where is mine gift or something like that I can’t really remember. So Capitan [REDACTED] says he’s not going to give it anything and orders it to lie down but instead the SCP turns into a wolf and it kills the Capitan really fast. Then it turns to us and it asks using this weird way of speaking if none of us have gifts I’m not really sure it talks like a character in a Shakespeare play. And were all just in shock of what we just saw happen so none of us say anything so It changes into a wolf and begins attacking us.

Dr.████: Was it at this point that you ran from it.

Agent [REDACTED]: Hey I ran to save my life this thing was killing my team like they were ants and guns weren't working on it!

Dr.████: I’m not trying to insult you I’m just trying to get details of the encounter.

Agent [Redacted] Fine, fine! So when I was running the SCP came behind me and then it bit my fucking leg off and I was lying on the ground waiting for it to finish me off and then It noticed a blueberry bush and I guess it thought this was it’s gift so I just agreed to it.

Dr.████: that’s when you called in backup.

Agent [Redacted]: yeah and thank god they listened to me about bringing in vegetables and fruits who knows how many more it would’ve killed.

Dr.████: Alright Agent thank you for your time.

(End Log)
