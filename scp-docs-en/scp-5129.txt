                        

Item #:  SCP-5129

Object Class:  Euclid

Special Containment Procedures:  SCP-5129 is to be held in a standard secure containment chamber on top of a 1 x 1 x 1.5 table against the back wall of the chamber to avoid contact in Site-98, and is to be monitored by no less than 2 guards. Due to its nature, in the event that SCP-5129 needs to be transported elsewhere, the plastic and lenses must be disassembled and shipped to their destination in a small box labeled as fragile accompanied with a pair of gloves. Only Class-D personnel with low IQ scores are allowed to wear SCP-5129 for testing and it must be approved by over ½ of the O5-Council.

Any civilians that use, come into contact with, and/or even see SCP-5129 must immediately be given amnestics by any means necessary. Anyone caught wearing SCP-5129 becomes an instance of SCP-5129-2 and is to be regarded as a Keter-Level threat. Any improper use of SCP-5129 will result in immediate neutralization of SCP-5129-2. The glasses are not to be damaged during neutralization. Should SCP-5129-2 remain unneutralized after 30 days, SCP-5129-2 is to be simply given the Apollyon object class.

Description:  SCP-5129 is a pair of sunglasses consisting of yellow plastic and green glass lenses. SCP-5129 is rather squared, as if it was what could only be described as “8-bit”. Anyone who comes into any type of physical contact with the glasses will feel a strong urge to put them on. Even a mere rub with SCP-5129 can trigger its luring effects. The luring effects of SCP-5129 are only triggered if they have made contact with human skin, but this does not stop non-human creatures from wearing it.

Upon wearing SCP-5129, it will adjust to the measurements of its wearer’s head, and the wearer’s perception of reality will immediately affect everyone else’s. Whatever the wearer believes becomes real, and that's anything. Even accidentally. For example, if the wearer dislikes someone, everyone else will dislike that particular person. Or if the wearer views a subject from a distance, it would be seemingly small, causing it to actually shrink in size. (And vice versa.) All whilst SCP-5129 remains unaffected by its own powers. In short, SCP-5129 makes everyone perceive reality the way the wearer does, turning the wearer's thoughts real in a literal sense.

It is not known what the original measurements of SCP-5129 are due to the fact that it was constantly changing wearers prior to containment. However, at the time of writing it is 4 x 4.5 x 1.2 inches, and 33 grams in weight. Whoever or whatever has been affected by the reality-bending effects of SCP-5129 will remain oblivious to the changes caused to themselves, however, they will not remain oblivious to the changes around them.

Upon examining the lenses, they showed no unusual physical properties, but when looking through them, even without actually wearing them, the reality-bending effects of SCP-5129 were triggered. Likewise, the plastic seemed to be regular plastic, but when touched by a D-Class he felt an urge to put it on. The wearer of SCP-5129 shows resistance to take the glasses off due to constantly being in contact with his or her skin. Should anyone try to remove SCP-5129 from another subject, the luring effects of SCP-5129 will also be triggered.

Upon removal, any changes caused by SCP-5129, including deaths, will be restored within a few minutes. Anyone to wear SCP-5129 automatically becomes an instance of SCP-5129-2, and renounces this designation upon removal of the glasses. Any attempt to destroy SCP-5129 thus far have been unsuccessful or disapproved, because after significant damage SCP-5129 seems to teleport to the last place where it was completely intact, and these places can in fact include previous wearer’s heads.

Discovery:  Little is known about where SCP-5129 originated, but it was located in the back dumpster of The Crypt Night Club, and caught the attention of the foundation when there were several reports of people of abnormal sizes in the area. It also has the initials “E.S” written on the plastic in between the lenses in what was subsequently identified as black marker ink. Any DNA such as fingerprints are yet to be identified.

SCP-5129 in containment at Site 98.
