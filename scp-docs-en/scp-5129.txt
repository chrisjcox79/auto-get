                        

Item #:  SCP-5129

Object Class:  Keter

Special Containment Procedures:  SCP-5129 is to be kept in a 15m x 15m Titanium room designed to look like a grazing field. The room is big enough to suit SCP-5129 running needs. Steel shackles are to be locked to SCP-5129 hind legs to at least slow it down. Reinforced sliding steel doors are locked in place when SCP-5129 goes on its rampage. Two foundation agents who have taken courses on exorcism and have been dispatched by their teacher are to be kept at SCP-5129's containment cell at all times.

Description:  SCP-5129 is a male horse who has the ability to open a pocket dimension inside its underbelly. SCP-5129 is normally friendly and isn't easily agitated. due to the side affects of the pocket dimension, It will go on a rampage once a week. This rampage usually lasts 1-2 hours. During the rampage, The pocket dimension opens and disembodied arms and hands burst from SCP-5129. Before recovery, these arms pillaged villages in Greece while SCP-5129 is on its rampage. The foundation members managing SCP-5129's containment cell have given it the nickname, Trojan horse.

SCP-5129 was discovered in [REDACTED], Greece on a farm where it was in is docile state. There had be a number of reports that week of a number of raids and pillages at neighboring villages. Later that week while field agents where following SCP-5129, They encountered it having its rampage. The pillage resulted in 10 civilians casualties and 2 agent casualties. The first recorded rampage the foundation had of SCP-5129 lasted 1 hour. SCP-5129 was recovered /9/10/2016/. Amnestics were given to all the affiliated villages.
