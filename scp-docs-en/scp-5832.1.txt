SCP-5832

05 Dec 2020 17:37

Seeking Greenlights:  Yes

Page Type:  SCP Article

Genre (Optional):  History, Action

Page Layout (Optional):  Documentation and Experiment Logs

Elevator Pitch: 

*Miniature Medieval Europe made out of paper.

*Inhabited by mini satient paper beings

*There are wars and conquests.

*Can communicate with humans.

Central Narrative:  Field agents found a miniature castle made entirely out of white paper in the basement of an old building. They bring it back as a present for a foundation scientist. The scientist discovered one day that the castle was filled with little talking paper people. They interview the supposed  of this land. The castle slowly expands larger and larger, with more and more people. This scientist then proposes to make this an SCP which is granted. The tiny people refer to the scientist as their god. When a researcher accidentally brought in a red paper for unknown reasons, the red paper duplicated and became a separate empire that was enemies with the white paper empire. They are now stored separately.

Hook/Attention-Grabber:  I don't think something like this have ever been done before. It is an very interesting topic to see paper people going about their day.

Additional Notes:  This is classified as Safe.

Re: SCP-5832

06 Dec 2020 02:18

1) You don't need to make it an SCP; once it demonstrates anomalous attributes it's a given. Also, it feels that this has not been greenlighted but it already has a number.

2) The Red Paper Castle: It may be better to phrase it such that the red paper was transformed into a duplicate of the original castle. I know it's semantics, but it just feels better. You may want to go through and look for similar instances; there are a number of similarly bulky phrasing.

3) This is a good start, but it needs to be expanded upon. Do they have a history? Why did they automatically become enemies, with no apparent attempt at diplomacy? Where are they getting more paper to build the castle? If I put a pack of white paper near the castle, what would happen? Did they have a religion prior to the researcher? What form does that worship take?

Heck, what's the psychology of the castle dwellers? Is it based off real medieval people or an idealized form, such as the SCA? For that matter, why was it built in the first place, was it for a warg game or just to see what could happen?

Again, really cool start. Now flesh it out.
