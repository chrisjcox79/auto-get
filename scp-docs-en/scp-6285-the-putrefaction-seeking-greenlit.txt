SCP-6285 - The Putrefaction

21 Nov 2020 08:28

Seeking Greenlights:  Yes

Page Type:  SCP Article

Genre (Optional):  Horror, Action, Drama

Elevator Pitch: 

+) It is a contagious fungus that is capable of raising the dead, then after a particular time, the now living dead will grow extra sharp bones out of their body, particularly the limbs. The reason being that having sharp  is the most useful way for SCP-6285-1 (The corpses) to kill other living humans which spreads the infection even further. The fungus shoots out spores and coming into contact with human corpses will begin to reanimate them. If there is a living human around there, it will attach to them as transportation without them knowing.

+) The unpredictable neverending evolution and mutation of the fungus is what makes it so deadly. This means SCP-6285-1 can have limitless potential and growth to serve the fungus's purpose.

+) SCP-6285-1 also has an invisible gooey substance which helps to patch its wounds if necessary. Using extreme heat is the best way to neutralize it.

Central Narrative:  It should be from going normal, peaceful to a hell hole. At first, the SCP seems harmless and all, as time goes by, the horror and dreadfulness of it will soon come cause of how infectious it can spread and fast reanimation

Hook/Attention-Grabber:  The Putrefaction is unique in terms of reanimating the dead if this is a thing. To be honest, I was inspired by the Necromorphs in Dead Space to that the host growing extra limbs because it is the most helpful tactic to rank up more kills for the fungus to raise an army. The terrifying existence of the fungus can cause human extinction.
