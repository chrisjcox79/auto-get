SCP idea (draft available)

08 Dec 2020 02:43

Seeking greenlight status in order to then (as a draft has already been written) seek review and such for a new SCP idea

I moved and trimmed your thread summary, since it was taking up extra space in the main forum screen. Try to keep thread descriptions (the text in the little box under the thread title) and titles short so you don't crowd out other authors in the forum listing. ~Zyn

Seeking Greenlights:  Yes

Page Type:  SCP Article

Genre (Optional):  Drama/Emotional

Page Layout (Optional):  Procedures, Description, and an Interview. Open to adding more, such as testing logs, or diary entries.

Elevator Pitch:  A child discovers he can make living shadow puppets on walls with a light source and his hands. Unfortunately, he doesn't know how to completely control them. Ends with implications that the Foundation may use him as a weapon.

+block

–block

Central Narrative:  A child is playing under a blanket fort with his parents. After his father shows him how to make shadow puppets with a flashlight and his hands, the child follows suit. The shadows he makes are living, and proceed to  his parents. After not seeing the family leave their house for a while, police are called to the scene. After they enter, gunshots are heard, and then shadows dart around the house. A junior researcher who lives nearby calls in the situation and Foundation agents contain the child. The junior researcher is assigned to the SCP and tries to calm him down, though he knows what the Foundation might do to the child.

Hook/Attention-Grabber:  The story of an innocent child doing normal things that becomes under the control of the cold and calculating Foundation.

Additional Notes:  As mentioned, I already have a sandbox page with the first draft written. I had written the majority of this idea in a notebook, and then transferred the idea to my sandbox before I had found out about greenlighting. I apologize for going out of order.

As a courtesy to our readers on mobile devices, please collapse long posts. ~Zyn
