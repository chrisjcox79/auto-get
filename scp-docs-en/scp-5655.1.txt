This one has a lot of issues, but lets start with the positive. I like the idea of a decomposed justice-loving Santa. But your execution leaves a lot to be desired.

Firstly, there are a couple of plot holes. There are probably millions of crimes committed in the month of December in just America alone. If this SCP showed up for every single one, the foundation would have a serious problem preventing the public from finding out about it. To fix this, perhaps you can change the article so that this scp only appears for extremely heinous crimes.

The same goes for instances where the scp shows up to give people presents. The scp showing up to give people presents could result in the public finding out about anomalies. The foundation needs a way to prevent the public from finding out, so either remove this bit or add a way for the scp foundation to track the scp so they can amnesticize individuals who were exposed.

The concepts also leaves a lot of unanswered questions. For instance, can the scp be in two places at once? If a bunch of people commit a crime at the same time but in different places, will the scp appear for only one person, or will a manifestation of the scp appear for all of the people.

+block

–block

Another question is if people have to  for the scp to leave them alone, what counts as fixing an unlawful act. If you murdered someone, would you need to find a way to bring the person you murdered back to life. You need to create a specific set of rules that can be applied universally.

Additionally, you never specify what powers the scp has. Can it teleport? What if you try to fight it while the scp is trying to disembowel you. Does it have a way to immobilize its targets through combat or an afflicted paralysis? That needs to be specified.

Finally, why do people exposed to the scp need to be killed if any unlawful act can summon the scp? That seems excessive and inefficient, which is why the foundation wouldn't do it. It seems more likely that the foundation would investigate what those individuals did and determine and appropriate response. Additionally, why does the tree as well as the dishes/containers for the milk and cookies need to be incinerated. You said that was to prevent contamination, but I thought the scp only affected individuals who did unlawful acts. Is the scp diseased or something?

Overall, there are lots of unanswered questions and glaring plot holes.
