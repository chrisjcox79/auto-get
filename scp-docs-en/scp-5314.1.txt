The idea of the chickens effectively re-enacting the story of jesus isn't like, necessarily a bad idea here; but the execution feelsreally lacking? The break-neck pace of the story telling and the complete lack of commentary on the story combine to mean this has no impact on me as a reader. Like, if this was making some kind of point about Christianity or the forming of religions as a whole, then maybe I could vibe with it. Alternatively, having some kinda unique spin on the story and really giving us a sense of charm towards the characters in the style of e.g. SCP-4693 could help kick this over the line.
