Real quick, so if I take a photo from google images it probably is copyrighted?
附加记录：    

Probably, yes. Unless you use the filters or search specifically for free images (and even then you're not guaranteed a creative commons compliant image).

I looked for an abandoned barn in this wikimedia but unfortunately I didn’t found any.
附加记录：    

Well, because these are unidentified pictures. There are plenty of pictures of abandoned barns on wikimedia itself, because people know what an abandoned barn is.

How do I get that “permission” you guys talked about in the guidelines of writing an SCP?
附加记录：    

Typically you would need to contact the owner of the image and ask for their permission to use the image for a creative commons licensed work.
