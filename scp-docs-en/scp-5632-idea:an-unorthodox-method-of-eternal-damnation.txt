SCP-5632 Idea: An Unorthodox Method Of Eternal Damnation

27 Nov 2020 17:13

Hi. So, I have an idea for an SCP, obviously. That idea is as follows:

Temporal  anomaly located in Germany, concerning an American strike team attacking a German outpost (5632-3). SCP-5632-1 is a member of the strike team (5632-2) and is the only member of 5632 still self-aware. The loop always ends with 5632-1's termination, at which point all entities involved in the loop slow to a stop, then reverse. SCP-5632-1 can approach Foundation Personnel, at which point entities in the loop will behave in a confused and agitated manner. Consequently, interviews can be performed with the subject, however it will always be terminated at the end of the usual duration of the loop. SCP-5632-1 is under severe mental distress. Also, 5632-1 cannot communicate verbally (excluding simple sounds), due to the lack of a tongue. It instead communicates via ASL or Morse code.

I'd like feedback on this please. I have a draft in progress, just in case.
