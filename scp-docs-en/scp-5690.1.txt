Son of a- I fell asleep after work yesterday. I had my crit all typed up this morning and WHAMMO. ITS ON THE FRONT PAGE!

Oh well, here it is.

Mycrit.

Solate.

Maybe I'm missing it, but aren't Incubi also classified under Ardor-Class? So, why  ?

The second addendum might have more punch if the line was logged as an after-action report, creating the required unknown gap in time for them to, well,  it's a brothel.

Besides that, it does lack a certain level of escalation. The dip from the second addendum works fine, but I feel like there should be an incident, or some instigating event that requires the third interview beyond just, well, an excuse for more exposition. There doesn't seem to be a reason for the third addendum at all - the foundation is already aware of the brothel, what it does, etc. Even some minor incident as an excuse to bring the  in for questioning would provide the right amount of escalation.

Beyond that, the twist ending is super cute, but a bit out of left field. It opens up a lot of possibilities really quickly (i.e. Big J is up and about and getting his freak on) but shuts them just as soon. It's a good twist, just jarring.

+1, but the narrative pacing is a bit of a drunk walk.

P.S. Per the whole Illegal Prostitution argument - this isn't Vegas, it's Sin City, Undervegas, Hell Vegas, whatever. The establishing universe set out by 4661 means that I can absolutely believe this new Vegas quickly turned around on the brothel argument.

0 from me.

I really enjoyed myself reading it, and it was undeniably funny. However, the camp factor is keeping me from the upvote. To my mind, the name of the game is verisimilitude when it comes to SCP articles in particular. And what keeps me from getting there isn't the fact that this is a brothel or the fact that a Foundation agent went to one, or even that a demonic madame would charge for her time.

What keeps me from getting there is the consistent use of comedic writing tropes in the transcripts. Funny is one thing, but in this case the piece is too self-aware of its comedy to read as a natural event. That's what I'm looking for when I crack open a skip. So I'm sorry, but I don't feel right upvoting it in its current form.

I reckon you could get it there with a bit of work on those transcripts, though.
