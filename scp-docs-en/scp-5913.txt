                        

Item #:  SCP-5913

Object Class:  Euclid

Special Containment Procedures:  Containment of SCP-5913 is a 16 x 40 ft (about 3.7 x 5.5m) room constructed out of tungsten, subject is to keep under the observation of the foundation surveillances at all times. All personnel are to test bi-annually for cognitohazardous corruption before they entered its containment cell. I advised all personnel of the foundation to not make any kinds of physical interactions with the subject.

Description : SCP-5913 is a humanoid entity dressed up in a bartender suit of the 1920s, the subject is measuring approximately 6 to 8 feet tall has a general appearance as a tall black figure that has no human facial features; the subject shows very little muscle mass, arms are grossly out of proportion with the rest of the body, the subject has shown to have no signs of hair, eyes, nose, its mouth can open up to two (2) times the norm of a normal human, and has normal-looking bare hands, albeit with fingers longer and bonier than a typical human approximately 1.5 meters each. SCP-5913 is capable of speech and understands normal human languages. Though its speech is hard to be understand by normal peoples, the subject seems to have different respiratory sounds which cause it to have issues in speaking normally.

Attempting to kill the subject often has some major problems because of its significant physical and anomalous abilities. I have always observed the subject to have chief strength, speed, and reflexes. Documented speed has varied from three-thousand and eighty-seven kilometers per hour (3087km/h), however, the subject prefers to teleport rather than running because of being exhausted, other than that the subject is not invulnerable it can still be injure easily, although the subject has shown to have remarkable ability to ignore pain, shock, and have a strong regenerative healing factor that could help it recovered from injured wounds on it’s the body.1

The tendrils that SCP-5913 sprouts out from its body can grab, and attack, other than that it can also use the tendrils as a shield to protect the entity away from harm2

Documents of SCP-5913 anomalous abilities have also shown that certain contemporary views usually see the subject movement as extremely unlimited as if it can move its body properly. It’s rarely seen to actually and goes from standing still to rapidly teleporting within the blink of an eye. There is no apparent coherency to its movements or position, and it can teleport several dozen times to various locations in a few seconds. It does not flee and can be just as dangerous standing still as when teleporting. It is capable of–and often will – stay within several dozen feet of its target, no matter how they try to escape. It seems to be part of its psychological attack to stay just within visual range without attacking—the fact that it is there, no matter what, it described as worse than being attacked, the psychological effects often deteriorating the mind of the person.

It appears to follow all targets regardless of where they go, or where and how they hide. It isn’t certain how it does this, but the subject seems to be able to keep a perfect trace on its targets, often speculated as a part of its supposed existence as a potentially omnipresent being.

The skin of the subject can inflict diseases upon those who touch it, any humans who come in contact with the subject’s skin would cause them to suffer some common diseases.3

Addendum 5913.1: Discovery 

The files of the foundation have written that on 6/9/1955 at the South East of America, following witness reports of something that is lurking inside a large abandoned row home that is located somewhere deep in the forest. These reports resulted in the dispatching of Mobile Task Force Omega-7, who arrived on site with 15 armed personnel. During the time of capturing SCP-5913 it resulted in 7 personnel being lost in action, however, a while later one agent had weakened it. After that, SCP-5913 got transported back to Site-19.

Addendum 5913-1 Interview Log: 

After that, SCP-5913 got transported back to Site-19 where one of the foundation researchers, Dr.█████ interviewed a session with it to see whether if he can find anything to learn about SCP-5913.

[BEGIN LOG] 

(SCP-5913 growls in an aggressive tone)

SCP-5913:  Where am I!?

Dr.█████:  Calm down. We don’t mean any harm to you at all.

(Silence)

SCP-5913:  are you? And what do you want with me?

Dr.█████  Yes, let me introduce myself, my name is Dr.█████, and what I want is for you to answer some questions I’m about to ask.

SCP-5913:  What questions?

Dr.█████:  It’s nothing serious at all, I just need to ask you a few simple questions, and that’s all.

SCP-5913:  (Silence) Okay, I’m listening.

Dr.█████:  So, first, may I know your name?

SCP-5913:  Name? I don’t think I have a name.

Dr.█████:  Is that so? In that case, would you mind if I refer to you as SCP-5913?

SCP-5913:  Wellalright.

Dr.█████:  Okay, so, what date were you born?

SCP-5913:  I also have no clue of what day, month, and the year I was born in.

Dr.█████:  I see, however, I’m curious about one thing, earlier we’ve sent a group of agents to come at your location, and you’ve killed 7 of our agents before being weakened by the rest, if you were capable of speech then why didn’t you tried to talk to field agents?

SCP-5913:  I thought they won’t listen and would only kill me, so I have to fight back. I swear I don’t want to kill or hurt anyone at all; it was just self-defense (sighs heavily) I should’ve just teleported away.

Dr.█████:  Wait, did you say you can teleport?

SCP-5913:  Yes, but I thought that if I let them live, they would lead back more, and more “agents” to where I’m from, and like I said from earlier it was just self-defense, I just didn’t want to die that’s all.

Dr.█████:  I see, so how do you feel now?

SCP-5913:  Excuse me?

Dr.█████:  How do you feel, what is your impression of your time in the foundation so far?

SCP-5913:  (Silence)

Dr.█████:  It’s okay to be honest, we don’t take any offense at these things.

SCP-5913:  Well, this placebut also I’m also curious of one thing, what is going to happen to me now?

Dr.█████:  Depends on the decisions of the foundation, they will decide what to do with you.

SCP-5913:  (Silence) Okay, I get itthanks for telling me this.

Dr.█████:  No problem, and also, thank you for cooperating with the foundation so far.

SCP-5913:  Yes, no problem.

[END LOG] 

Footnotes
1. Prior encounters have shown that SCP-5913 is capable of:

Rip through a wall made of steel with the course of using only two of its hands.

Dodging various bullets without showing out signs of being tired.

Teleporting to various locations.

A single swipe of its hand destroys an assault rifle.

Changing its height and body shape.

Sprouting out tendrils from all over its entire body.

2. So far subject’s tendrils shown to have:

Blocking gunshots and melee weapons.

Move with the speed faster than the blink of an eye.

Able to stretch for an extensive range of distance.

Able to withstand the powerful explosions from grenades, and sometimes it can even be sustainable against an explosion coming from a missile, although it depends on how much strength does the subject still has left inside of it.

3. The diseases that the skin of SCP-5913 has applied to those who touch it:

Ischemic heart disease.

Lower respiratory infections.

Chronic obstructive pulmonary disease.

Stroke diseases.

Cerebrovascular diseases.

Alzheimer’s disease and other dementias.

Diabetes mellitus.

Trachea, bronchus, and lung cancers.

Tuberculosis
