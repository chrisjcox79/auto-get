6/5913
CLASSIFIED

Item #: SCP-5913
Object Class: Euclid

~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Special Containment Procedures:  Containment of SCP-5913 is a 16 x 60 ft (about 3.7 x 5.5m) room constructed out of tungsten, subject is to keep under the observer of the foundation surveillances at all times. All personnel is to test for cognitohazardous corruption before they entered its containment cell. I advised all personnel of the foundation to not make any kinds of physical interactions with the subject.

Description : SCP-5913 is a humanoid entity dressed up in a bartender suit of the 1920s2; it shows very little muscle mass, arms are out of proportion with the rest of the body, the subject has shown to have no signs of hair, eyes, nose, its mouth can open up to two (2) times the norm of a normal human, and has normal-looking bare hands, albeit with fingers longer and bonier than a typical human approximately 1.5 meters each. However, it is possible that this is not SCP-5913’s proper form, and that it is an eldritch being I cannot comprehend whose correct form.

SCP-5913 is capable of speech and understands normal human languages, however, its speech is hard to be understood by normal peoples, the subject seems to have different respiratory sounds which cause it to have issues in speaking.

Attempting to kill SCP-5913 has some major problems because of its significant physical and anomalous abilities. It has always been observed to have chief strength, speed, and reflexes. Documented speed has varied from three-thousand and eighty-seven kilometers per hour (3087km/h), however, the subject prefers to teleport rather than running because of exhausted, other than that the subject is not invulnerable it can still injured, although the subject has shown to have remarkable ability to ignore pain, shock, and have a strong regenerative healing factor that could help it recovered from injured wounds on it’s the body.3

The tendrils that sprout from SCP-5913’s body can grab and attack, it can also use the tendrils as a shield to protect the entity away from harm4

Its presence causes several negative effects on the minds of its targets including inhibiting memory loss while also adding memory, altering sense and perception of a person’s environment, influencing a person’s personality very negatively such as causing a great deal of paranoia, nightmares, and hallucinations, etc. it is believed that SCP-5913 directly causes negative effects on the minds of whom it manifests to. there is evidence that supports this belief. People who encounter SCP-5913 constantly become extremely paranoid of its next return, easily irritated or agitated, severely depressed, and sometimes even suicidal this method of negatively changing a person’s personality could most likely result from its mind control.

Documents of SCP-5913 anomalous abilities have also shown that certain contemporary views see its movement as unlimited as if it can move its body. They rarely see it move and goes from standing still to teleporting within the blink of an eye. There is no apparent coherency to SCP-5913’s movements or position, and it can teleport several dozen times to various locations in a few seconds. SCP-5913 does not flee and can be just as dangerous standing still as when teleporting. SCP-5913 capable of – and often will – stay within several dozen feet of its target, no matter how they try to escape. This seems to be part of SCP-5913 psychological attack to stay just within visual range without attacking — the fact that it is there, no matter what, it described as worse than being attacked, the psychological effects often deteriorating the mind of the person.

SCP-5913 appears to follow all targets regardless of where they go, or where and how they hide. It isn’t certain how it does this, but it seems to be able to keep a perfect trace on its targets. These accounts vary from person to person, but being a target of SCP-5913 is almost contagious or memetic. One can become its victim by encountering someone it is already targeting. Basic research and even collecting information doesn’t appear to incur its notice. A theory sprouts from this, discussing how it could be able to track several people at once, due to the apparent number of people invested. This theory states he is an omnipresent being.

The skin of the subject can inflict diseases upon those who touch it, any humans who come in contact with the subject’s skin would cause them to suffer some common diseases.5
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Addendum 5913.1: Discovery 

The files of the foundation have written that on 6/9/1955 at the South East of America, following witness reports of something lurking inside a large abandoned row home that is located somewhere deep in the forest. These reports resulted in the dispatching of Mobile Task Force Omega-7, who arrived on site with 15 armed personnel. During the time of capturing SCP-5913 it resulted in 7 personnel being lost in action, however, a while later one agent had weakened it. After that, SCP-5913 got transported back to Site-19 were one of the foundation researchers, Dr.█████ interviewed with it to see whether if he can find anything to learn about SCP-5913.

Addendum 5913-1 Interview Log: 

[BEGIN LOG] 

(SCP-5913 growls in an aggressive tone)

SCP-5913:  Where am I!?

Dr.█████:  Calm down. We don’t mean any harm to you at all.

(Silence)

SCP-5913:  are you? And what do you want with me?

Dr.█████  Yes, let me introduce myself, my name is Dr.█████, and what I want is for you to answer some questions I’m about to ask.

SCP-5913:  What questions?

Dr.█████:  It’s nothing serious at all, I just need to ask you a few simple questions, and that’s all.

SCP-5913:  (Silence) Okay, I’m listening.

Dr.█████:  So, first, may I know your name?

SCP-5913:  Name? I don’t think I have a name.

Dr.█████:  Is that so? In that case, would you mind if I refer to you as SCP-5913?

SCP-5913:  Wellalright.

Dr.█████:  Okay, so, what date were you born?

SCP-5913:  I also have no clue of what day, month, and year I was born in.

Dr.█████:  Okay, but I’m still confused, however, earlier we’ve sent a group of agents to come to your location, and you’ve killed 7 of our agents before being weakened by the rest, if you can talk, then why didn’t you tried to talk to field agents?

SCP-5913:  I thought they won’t listen and would only kill me, so I have to fight back. I swear I don’t want to kill or hurt anyone at all; it was just self-defense (sighs) I should’ve just teleported away.

Dr.█████:  Wait, did you say you can teleport?

SCP-5913:  Yes, but I thought that if I let them live, they would lead back more, and more “agents” to where I’m from, and like I said from earlier it was just self-defense, I just didn’t want to die that’s all.

Dr.█████:  Alright, then how long have you been inside of that abandoned row home?

SCP-5913:  To be honest, I can’t even possibly remember how long has I been in there, probably years perhaps

Dr.█████:  I see, however, if you can’t remember, or maybe don’t have a name, and the date that you were born in even how long have you been in that abandoned row home, then were you human once?

SCP-5913:  (Silence)

Dr.█████:  Uhis everything alright?

SCP-5913:  Well, I don’t recall any memories I have, but I do still keep a few memories of my past life before I become like this.

Dr.█████:  So you were once human, huh?

SCP-5913:  (Nods) Yesexcept for a little that can I still remember.

Dr.█████:  Is that so?

SCP-5913:  YeahI-I can remember working as a bartender during the 1925s.

Dr.█████:  Other than that, do you recall any other things else than your former job?

SCP-5913:  No, I think that’s all I could remember…

Dr.█████:  I see, so how do you feel now?

SCP-5913:  Excuse me? I didn’t quite get that. Can you be more specific about what you said just now?

Dr.█████:  What is your impression of your time in the foundation so far?

SCP-5913:  (Silence)

Dr.█████:  It’s okay, to be honest, we don’t take any offense at these things.

SCP-5913:  Well, this placebut also I’m also curious about one thing, what is going to happen to me now?

Dr.█████:  Depends on the decisions of the foundation, they will decide what to do with you.

SCP-5913:  (Silence) Okay, I get itthanks for telling me this.

Dr.█████:  No problem, thank you for answering my questions also, SCP-5913.

SCP-5913:  Yeahno problem.

[END LOG]

Addendum.5913.1:  Relevant Experiment Logs and Attempted Containment Breach.

Experiment 5913-e-1: 

Name:  Dr. Alex

Date:  08/16/2007

Procedure:  A D-Class subject armed with an MP5 is to introduce inside the observation chamber with SCP-5913. SCP-5913 is to be asked to show its anomalous powers by Dr. Alex.

Results:  SCP-5913 dodges the bullets without getting hit by any of them. SCP-5913 soon teleported in a blink of an eye and swiped its hand to destroy the MP5 that subject D-234532 subject holding.

Afterword:  SCP-5913 was escorted back to its containment unit and asked how long it would need to test its anomalous abilities. They informed site staff they ought to avoid causing out disturbances upon SCP-5913 while it is resting inside of its containment cell, for the continuation of SCP-5913’s compliance.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Note:  Seems like it has some unchecked limitations. More testing to determine the boundaries of its anomaly will follow.

Experiment 5913-e-2: 

Name:  Dr. Alex

Date:  08/17/2007

Procedure: Another D-class subject is to be brought into the observation chamber with SCP-5913. SCP-5913 will be instructed to make direct eye contact with the D-class subject.

Results: SCP-5913 stares at the D-class subject, Subject D-234536, but did not vocalize any concerns. Dr. Alex instructed SCP-5913 to comply with the request, and subject D-234536 was told to make direct eye contact with it to see whether if it’s safe. Subject D-234536 made multiple explicit comments and was reminded of the consequence of unruly behavior. After 3 seconds later, Subject D-234536 reported that their eyesight is seeing some static, and just fainted. SCP-5913 walks forward and checks on the condition of the subject D-234536 as it became shocked, and shouts out that the D-class subject is dead.

Afterword: As SCP-5913 was being escorted back to its containment unit, it asked what will happen to it. The escort team ignored all questions.

Note:  It appears like SCP-5913 can cause out an instant death by just staring at the subject for not even longer than three (3) seconds.
