                        

Item #:  SCP-5913

Object Class:  Euclid

Special Containment Procedures:  SCP-5913 is to be contained in a room that is 16 x 40 ft (about 3.7 x 5.5m) constructed out of tungsten, subject is to be kept under observation of the foundation surveillances at all time, it's containment cell is furnished with furniture and items, which proper beddings, bathrooms are to be maintained inside of its containment cell at any time. Additionally, SCP-5913 is only allowed to have a computer for information researching, some books, and a newspaper per day. All personnel is to be tested bi-annually for cognitohazardous corruption before they entered its containment cell. All personnel of the foundation was advised to not make any kinds of physical interactions with the subject.

Description : SCP-5913 is a humanoid entity that is dressed up in a bartender suit of the 1920s, the subject is measuring approximately 6 to 8 feet tall has a general appearance as a tall black figure that has no human facial features, the subject shows very little muscle mass, arms are grossly out of proportion with the rest of the body, the subject has shown to have no signs hair, eyes, nose, the subject's mouth can open up to can open to two (2) times the norm of a normal human, and generally has normal-looking bare hands, albeit with fingers longer and bonier than a typical human approximately 1.5 meters in length each. SCP-5913 is capable of speech and understands normal human languages, though its speech is hard to be understood by normal peoples, SCP-5913 seems to have different respiratory sounds which cause it to have issues in speaking normally.

Attempting to kill SCP-5913 often has some major problems due to its significant physical, and anomalous abilities. The subject has superhuman strength, and a unique speed varied from three-thousand and eighty-seven kilometers per hour (3087km/h), however, the subject only uses its speed to react and dodged anything that attacks it, SCP-5913 prefers to teleport rather than using its speed to run due to becoming extremely tired after running several of miles, and it is also not invulnerable, SCP-5913 can still be injured easily, although the subject has shown to have remarkable ability to ignore pain, shock, and have a strong regenerative healing factor that could help it recovered from injured wounds on it's the body.1

The tendrils that SCP-5913 sprouts out from its body can be used to grab, and attack, other than that the tendrils can also be used as a shield to protect the entity away from harm2

Documents of SCP-5913 anomalous abilities has also shown that certain contemporary views usually sees the subject movement as extremely unlimited as if the subject is capable of moving its body very properly in dodging. It is rarely seen to actually uses its speed to run and goes from walking, dodging to rapidly teleporting within the blink of an eye. There is no apparent coherency to its movement or position, and it is capable of teleporting several dozen times to various locations in a few seconds. SCP-5913 is capable of and often will stay within several dozen feet of its target without using its speed, no matter how they try to escape. It seems to be part of its psychological ways of attacking, it is to stay just within visual range without actually attacking, the fact that it is there, no matter what, is often described as worse than actually being attacked, the psychological effects often deteriorating the mind of the victims as a result, other than that SCP-5913 appears to be able to follow any and all targets regardless of where they go, or where and how they hide. It isn't certain how it does this, but it seems to be able to keep a perfect trace on its targets.

The skin of the subject can inflict diseases upon those who touch it, any humans who come in contact with the subject's skin would cause them to suffer some common diseases.3

Addendum 5913.1: Discovery 

The files of the foundation have written that SCP-5913 was found on the date of █/█/20██ in the South East of America, following witness reports of something that is lurking inside a large abandoned row home that is located somewhere deep in the forest. These reports resulted in the dispatching of Mobile Task Force Omega-7, who arrived on site with a total of 15 armed personnel. During the process of capturing SCP-5913 it resulted in 7 personnel was lost in action, however, later on, the personnel were able to contain the entity when it is weakened by the personnel. After that, SCP-5913 was been transported back to Site-19 were one of the foundation researchers, Dr.█████ conducted an interview session with it to see whether if he can find anything to learn about SCP-5913.

[BEGIN LOG] 

SCP-5913:  wHeRE aM I!?

Dr.█████:  Calm down, calm down. We don't mean any harm to you at all.

SCP-5913:  wHOaRe yOu?

Dr.█████  Yes, let me introduce myself, my name is Dr.█████, and I am here today to ask you a few questions.

SCP-5913:  WhaT KiND oF qUeSTioNs?

Dr.█████:  It's nothing serious at all, I just need to ask you a few questions, and that's all I want.

SCP-5913:  i'M lIsTEning.

Dr.█████:  Okay so, first, may I know your name?

SCP-5913:  nAMe? I dOn'T tHiNk I hAvE A nAMe. I kNOW tHAt eVeRYoNe was BorN wiTh naMEs, bUt I dOn'T haVe OnE.

Dr.█████:  Is that so? In that case, would you mind if I refer to you as SCP-5913?

SCP-5913:  wELliF tHat'S wHaT yOU wAnT to caLL mE ThEn, aLrIghT.

Dr.█████:  Okay, so, what date were you born?

SCP-5913:  I AlSo hAVe nO cLUe oF wHaT dAy, MOnTh, aNd yEaR I wAs borN.

Dr.█████:  I see, however, I'm curious of one thing, if you were capable of speech then why didn't you tried to talk out with the field agents that were sent earlier?

SCP-5913:  I THoUGhteD tHAt tHEy wOn'T lIsTen aNd wOuLD oNlY kILl mE, sO I hAVe nO cHOice bUt tO fiGHt bACk. I sWeAR tHAt i dOn'T waNt tO k-kIlL, iT wAs JUsT seLf-dEfEnse, (sighs heavily) i sHouLD'Ve jUst tELePorted aWAY.

Dr.█████:  Wait, did you say that you can teleport?

SCP-5913:  yES, hOwEVER, I tHouGhted iF i leT thEm lIvE tHeY wOUld leD bACk mORe aRmEd meNS tO wHeRE I wAS cUrReNTly hIdiNG, sO I hAd tO mAde sUrE tHaT aLl oF tHEm wErE deAd, bUt tHaT dOesN't mEaN i'M bAd, iT wAS jUsT sElF-DEfEnSE.

Dr.█████:  I see, according to reports received back from the agents of Mobile Task Force Omega-7, it was said that you have other abilities other than teleportation, which the reports say that you've dodged the gunshots of the agents from Mobile Task Force Omega-7, you also have destroyed one of the agent's weapons when their firing at you, so if I'm not wrong you have fast reaction speed, am I correct?

SCP-5913  yEs, hOwEvER i dOn'T aCTualLY usED iT iN runNinG, bEcAuSe lAsT tIMe whEN i'VE rAn sEVeral of milES I'vE bEcaMe eXtRemEly tIreD.

Dr.█████:  Okay, so, is there anything else that you're capable of, that I must know?

SCP-5913:  weLL, mY bODy, oR pErHaPS sKiN sEemS tO bE aBle tO sprOut ouT teNDrils, uSuaLly I uSeD tHEm tO CoVEr mYsElF fRoM geTTing weT dUrinG raINy dAys, aND sOmETiMEs iT iS uSeD in deFenDing.

Dr.█████:  Okay, that's all I need to know, sir, thank you for being cooperative with the foundation.

SCP-5913  nO prOBlEm, bUt wHat'S gOnNa haPpEned tO mE nOw?

Dr.█████:  Well, what we need you to do now is to remain patient until yourroom cell.

SCP-5913:  (Silence)

Dr.█████:  I guess you still haven't gotten the hang of the situation yet, but this foundation is a place where we keep anomalies and entities as you contained, I hope you could understand.

SCP-5913  oKay, bUt wHaT dO yOU mEaN bY rOOm cEll?

Dr.█████:  When I say room cell, I meant containment chamber, or maybe containment cell if that's what you want to call it.

SCP-5913  iS tHiS lIKe PriSOn oR sOMethINg?

Dr.█████:  Nowell, maybe, but don't be panic SCP-5913, for the foundation would be providing for you all the foods and clothing that're in need.

SCP-5913  OkaY, OKaY, I sEE, tHaNK yOu fOr LeTtInG mE kNoW aBOuT tHis mIstEr █████.

Dr.█████:  Please, sir, call me Dr.█████ that's all, nothing else to say.

SCP-5913:  yES, dR.█████.

Dr.█████:  No problemthank you for answering my questions.

[END LOG] 

Footnotes
1. Prior encounters have shown that SCP-5913 is capable of:

Rip through a wall made out of steel with the course of using only two of its hands.

Dodging various bullets without showing out signs of being tired.

Teleporting to various locations.

A single swipe of its hand destroys an assault rifle.

Changing its height and body shape.

Sprouting out tendrils from all over its entire body.

2. So far subject's tendrils are capable of:

Blocking gunshots and melee weapons.

Move with the speed faster than the blink of an eye.

Able to stretch for a long distance.

Able to withstand the strong explosions from grenades, and sometimes it can even be sustainable against a missile, although it depends on how much strength, and energy does the subject still has left inside of it).

3. The diseases that the skin of SCP-5913 has applied to those who touch it was discovered to be:

Ischemic heart disease.

Lower respiratory infections.

Chronic obstructive pulmonary disease.

Stroke diseases.

Cerebrovascular diseases.

Alzheimer’s disease and other dementias.

Diabetes mellitus.

Trachea, bronchus, and lung cancers.

Tuberculosis
