This is well written and there’s not anything about it that I think is bad but the overall article is unsatisfying. It’s got a decent length but I think there’s a lot of bloat that could be cut, because at the end I didn’t feel that reading through it had a good pay off.

Ultimately this is a no vote from me, again I don’t think it’s bad but it doesn’t have an emotional through line.
