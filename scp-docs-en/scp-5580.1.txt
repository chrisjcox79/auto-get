I love it. Very well written, as expected, but the articles really comes into its own with its amalgamation of themes. Fifthism, IJAMEA, and Lyle Burnley seem very obscure and incompatible (probably making it the hardest request of the Art Exchange) yet you succeed with flying colors in combining these entities' essences. I would never think to relate Fifthism's reality-bending madness to colonialism's truth-garbling horrors.
