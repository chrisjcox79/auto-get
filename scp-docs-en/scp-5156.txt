                        

Item #:  SCP-5156

Object Class:  Euclid

Special Containment Procedures:  SCP-5156 is to be contained in a Suppression-Exclusion cell, with dimensions of 10m x 10m x 10m. The interior and exterior is to be lined with 1inch-thick tungsten plates, each separated by 24 inches of concrete in solid form. The cell is to be only accessible by a series of three tungsten airlock mechanisms, each 3m x 3m x 3m. The ceiling of the cell is to be equipped with 430,000 watt Ultraviolet lights, one in each corner of the aforementioned cell. Only personnel with level 2 clearance are to be allowed to enter SCP-5156's containment cell.

SCP-5156-01 is to be kept in a Storage locker approximately 17,000 meters from SCP-5156, while a Level 3 security officer is to be stationed outside the locker area at all times. Only personnel with level 3 clearance are to access the locker, with anyone attempting to damage or steal SCP-5156-01 are to be terminated immediately.

Description:  SCP-5156 is a sexless,hairless,bipedal humanoid entity measuring 4 meters tall while standing erect. It has very little body mass and very little coloration in its skin, similar to SCP-096. The arms and legs of SCP-5156 is covered of abnormally large cuts and scratches, (of which would be fatal to a human) exposing the entity’s muscular system. The entity has no visible hands or fingers, but it is suspected to have perfectly invisible claws.(See SCP-5156 Testing Logs for more Details.) . SCP-5156’s feet are replaced by stumps, which after close investigation it has been concluded the entity once had feet but have since been amputated by an unknown source (See SCP-5156 exploration log 2 for more details.) . On the back of the entity’s neck there is a symbol burned into its flesh, which has been identified as the aztec miquiztli, which translates to death; it is unknown how or why SCP-5156 received this mark. The creature's face is relatively human in appearance, though its eyes are sunken 2 inches into the entity's skull. SCP-5156-01 has 2 rows of human teeth, with abnormally large incisors.

SCP-5156-01 is a large glass helmet measuring 3 meters in diameter.

Addendum:

Both SCP-5156-01 and 02 were first discovered in __ Mexico on July 4th, 2019 after a series of unknown disappearances caught the attention of Maria Lopez, a level 3 Scientist for the foundation who was on vacation at the time. The following is a transcript of the audio logs taken during the investigation

4:30 am

Dr. Lopez: July 4th 2019. This is Dr. Maria Lopez, along with a native I will be investigating a possible anomaly located in __ Mexico.

5:27 am

Dr. Lopez: We have been walking for around an hour, in front of me is a strange pile of red ooze, I assume it is blood of some kind. In the distance I can see more of the piles.

5:46 am

Dr. Lopez: I have started seeing things running through the forest, they are much too fast for me to fully see.

Native whistles*

Dr. Lopez: My associate has given me ear protection, may I ask why are these necessary?

Native: No good English, protect from screams.

Dr. Lopez: Screams? What screams?

screeching from behind *
A loud thump followed by what sounds to be a struggle between Dr. Lopez and an unknown entity *

*Gunshot*

*Dr.Lopez breathing heavily*

Dr. Lopez: Thank you Amigo. I was just attacked by some kind of primate. Hold on, it may be the heat getting to me but at first glance the creature appears to be a Homo Habilis. Interesting.

6:19 am

*Loud screams muffle Dr. Lopez’s words*

Audio Logs end

Dr. Lopez later describes a strange Aztec temple, inside she found SCP-5156-01 chained to a wall, SCP-5156-02 sat on a pedestal in the center of the room.

SCP-5156 displays two anomalous qualities. At all times the entity emits a deafening scream, those unfortunate enough to hear it begin an anomalous transformation, which will be referred to as SCP-5156-02. SCP-5156-02 has been proven to not affect any other species. Exposure to the scream causes a human to rapidly devolve, this devolution stops at Homo Habilis. After 3 days any instance of SCP-5156-02 will explode due to unknown means, all attempts to prevent this outcome have failed. The only known way to silence SCP-5156’s scream is to place SCP-5156-01 on its head. Although SCP-5156-01 has no visible claws it has been seen making scratching motion over its skin and at the door, After examination it appears SCP-5156 is equipped with perfectly transparent claws capable of cutting through steel.

Three tests have been performed using SCP-5156-01. In each test SCP-5156 was subdued and locked to a large tungsten chair, SCP-5156-01 placed over it’s head. The tests prove that the entity is sentient, when shown various images the entity displayed several emotions, these include smiling, crying, and scowling. When shown a picture of the mark on its neck it displayed great amounts of shame, hanging its head and weeping. Dr. Lopez Requested a sketch of SCP-096 to be shown to SCP-5156, she was, “Curious if it knew who SCP-096 was, given the two’s similar appearance.”. Upon seeing the sketch SCP-5156 displayed extreme distress. The Entity shook violently and screamed louder than ever previously recorded, Noise sensors recorded over 200 decibels. The screams caused a large crack in SCP-5156-01. SCP-5156 shook its head violently, flinging SCP-5156-01 off of its head. All personnel present died later that week due to cases of SCP-5156-02. After the incident no further tests have been performed.
