                        

5156

Level2

Containment Class:
euclid

Secondary Class:
none

Disruption Class:
vlam

Risk Class:
warning

link to memo

SCP-5156 inside a subjects body.

Special Containment Procedures:  SCP-5156 is to be kept in a 1767.84 centimeters wide cell with special NORA security cameras and iron doors due to SCP-5156's exceptional strength.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Description:  SCP-5156 is a six foot tall humanoid that looks roughly like a king with white eyes.

SCP-5156 is able to merge with anything granting it the body and/or powers due to it's virus.1

SCP-5156 can speak english, spanish, and french.

SCP-5156 is very intelligent and is able to learn very quickly.

SCP-5156 can't attack the person they morph into due to being in the body and if the body SCP-5156 is in gets destroyed so does SCP-5156.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Discovery:  SCP-5156 was first discovered on 11/██/███ in a factory at ██████ and was found by J████ B████ during a security search at the █████ factory. SCP-5156 was trying to attack J████ B████ due to unknows reasons. SCP-5156 was in a 6 foot box when found for unknown reasons.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Addendum.6467.1:  Due to SCP-5156's ability to speak our researchers were able to get a interview with SCP-5156

+log

-interview

Interviewed:  SCP-5156

Interviewer:  ██████

Foreword:  The point of the interview is to find out why SCP-5156 was in a box.

 

██████:  Hello SCP-5156 today you are here so we can find out why you were in a box got it?

SCP-5156:  Got it.

██████:  So first question why were you in a factory?

SCP-5156:  Well it's because of my owner.

██████:  Who is this ?

SCP-5156:  Their name was █████████ ██████████ I think.

██████:  Ok and now why were you in a box?

SCP-5156:  Well once I got in the factory it was like she wanted to send me somewhere.

██████:  So what happened after that?

[SCP-5156 starts to look around the room]

SCP-5156:  Well it was like I was in her body and after I was in I started to cause chaos since I felt betrayed.

██████:  Yes so how did you get into the box?

SCP-5156:  well I fell out of her body and into the box everybody said I was too dangerous and just kept me in there with the lid closed.

██████:  Thank you for your time SCP-5156 you are free to go back to your room now.

 

Closing Statement:  After the interview SCP-5156 was taken back to it's cell and the research team started to see if they could get more information about █████████ ██████████ no progress has been achieved.

~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Addendum.6467.2:  To research SCP-5156's ability to morph into anything a test was preformed on D-34734.

+log

-log

Test Number: 1/1

Subject: D-34734

Time: June 27th 4:43 PM EST

Procedure:  D-34734 was placed in SCP-5156's room with two guards near the door.

Result:  SCP-5156 went into D-34734's body as planned and started causing chaos. While the chaos happened a guard removed SCP-5156 from D-34734's body

Analysis:  SCP-5156 can cause chaos inside a body but it it very easy to stop.
