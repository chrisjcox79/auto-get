Scp of Future Past, and Everything Else...

26 Dec 2020 13:34

Seeking Greenlights:  Yes

Page Type:  SCP Article

Elevator Pitch: 

Scp XXXX is a Reality Bender

Scp XXXX had been discovered in a cave near Quebec, Canada

Scp XXXX is spanned through time and space.

Central Narrative: 

Scp XXXX was discovered by an Amateur Cave Explorer in 1973, in a cave near Quebec, Canada. The Amateur Cave Explorer was taken into Foundation custody and amnestisized, alongside all other civilians involved, after reporting to a local hospital, stating he heard a voice in his head talk about freeing himself from the wizards bonds. The Foundation learns Scp XXXX voice spreads into Humans Consciousness when any contact is made beit physical or verbal. The cave of origin is blocked and all Personnel that had been infected had been anesthetized. Scp XXXX is reclassified from euclid to safe. 14 years later, Dr. Mason Cooper reports hearing voices inside his head, though he had only been working for the Foundation for 11 years. Staff had concluded that Personnel already infected by Scp XXXX previously had seemed to get reinfected. Though some personnel had either left the Foundation and been Anesthetized or were somewhere else in the world. Scp XXXX is reclassified as keter as it now spreads into the entire world. As more and more instances of Scp XXXX begin to be heard in peoples head the more mist and fog begins to surround Scp XXXX’s point of origin. Until at a Estimated 1000 cases where the fog slowly turned into a human like form and moved into Montreal, Canada. The Foundation theorizes that Scp XXXX was sent across space and time by the Wizards in 1200, so any Instance of Scp XXXX would imbalance its containment as it would be more in one time then the others, meaning it would start to remeterialize. The Foundation enlists Mobile Task Force Simga 32 ( Tbh I Dont Any Good Mtf Who Travel Through Time ) To travel to Canada and release a amnestic and start a PowerGrid Failure to all of Quebec to stop the spread of Media related to Scp XXXX, Finally Sigma 32 puts down a temporal rift near the anomaly, sending all living creatures in the radius to various points in the future in order to contain it. Scp XXXX is currently pending classification of either safe or neutralized.

Hook/Attention-Grabber:  The archived containment class would go from euclid to safe to keter to pending, meaning there is lots of story.

Additional Notes:  If you could help find a name for the Scp Article and a Mtf that specializes in Time Travel I would be even more grateful, but if you just want to critique I'm still very thankful.
