SCP Draft: The Place I Belong

03 Dec 2020 07:20

I want this draft to read a bit mysterious, but ultimately melancholic.

Its an SCP about a town abandoned for 70ish years and incredibly lonely. The dialogue needs to read well, and I want it to be put together how the solution is reached re: containment but I have not directly spelled it out.

Thanks in advance! (About 3000 words)

Re: SCP Draft: The Place I Belong

04 Dec 2020 19:17

Hey, Grig! I'll take a look for ya!

Just know that my clinical tone is meh, but I'm a lot better with dialogue.

Alrighty.

+Let'sit!

-Let'sit!

Disinformation campaigns are to be spearheaded to label SCP-XXXX-A as a UNESCO World Friendship and Peace Village to draw in more tourism.
附加记录：    

The fact that a ConProc demands more attention is already weird enough to draw me in.

I dig it.

SCP-XXXX is a series of anomalous dreams that can occur in a certain segment of the population of Europe and Asia: most often creatives and individuals who attest to feeling at odds with society.
附加记录：    

I feel personally called out here and I'm not sure how to mentally process this.

I just finished the Description  and I don't really have any qualms with it. The dream sequences reminds me heavily of the River Styx and Charon, something I'm not sure if you did on purpose or not.

Either way, no clinical issues so far.

Aside from this:

genius loci
附加记录：    

Did you mean 'genus'?

A little confused on that bit. I had to Google 'loci' and it gave me stuff about genetics and mathematics, neither of which this piece seems to be going for. Iactually no clue what this bit is supposed to show.

I'm normally not a fan of free verse (that is what that poetry style is, right?), but the poem written by Tefvik is wonderfully done and the allusions to the hooded figure in the SCP-XXXX dreams is also nicely done.

Especially 'blessed be the stones of your dreaming streets.'

prototypical oneirosensory visualization recording device.
附加记录：    

Ironicwe're both currently drafting something that headcanons pioneering dream-recording Foundation tech.

Idon't know how to process this either.

I do think this is great headcanon, and I think your method of wording this is a lot better and a lot more developed than mine.

Dr. Rossi has confirmed that she is experiencing SCP-XXXX events and has been periodically for the previous three weeks.
附加记录：    

We can clean this up and shorten it into, 'Dr. Rossi has confirmed that she has been periodically experiencing SCP-XXXX events for the previous three weeks.'

[ Initial view is of a mist shrouded body of water, which appears to be a river. The night is dark, with only a crescent moon serving as illumination.]
附加记录：    

Unnecessary extra space before 'Initial' and after the bracket.

[The boat has stopped directly in front of subject in a manner inconsistent with the flowing water of the river, as if being held in place. The figure extends his arm out with an open palm.]
附加记录：    

I love dream logic. This is a lovely detail to show how unearthly this place is. Other than, you know, the weird hooded figure, the mist and—

You know what I mean.

Sixteen minutes of footage are lost due to technical failure.
附加记录：    

Both of our headcanoned dream-recording technology also involves significant lapses in dream recording to simulate the feeling of actual dream timeline-logic.

That'sfrighteningly coincidental.

I love it. Love it, love it, love it 3

[Subject is within the village, walking down its streets. The stone buildings resemble architecture from the 18the century
附加记录：    

There's an extra 'e' in '18th'

Rossi: The air is chilly, like an early spring night but not so cold as to be uncomfortable. Significant moisture in the air, consistent with misty conditions. The river in front of me is jet black, I cannot tell how deep it is by eye. I –”
附加记录：    

This is very prose-y instead of diaogue-y (if that makes sense), but this is a dream and we could say she is trying to ground herself in a world where normal logic doesn't function.

[Sounds of a scuffle and Rossi cursing loudly cursing.]
附加记录：    

Two 'cursing's here.

Removing either would fix it, funny enough.

Many instances8 of SCP-XXXX-1 are seen lining the square and staring at subject.]
附加记录：    

There has been no mention of SCP-XXXX-1 up until this point, so I don't know what they're supposed to be.

Subject stands in a town square, the cobble stones shifting beneath her feet.
附加记录：    

I absolutely adore the usage of dream logic and dream visuals here.

Answers don’t come in dreams at least not for you.
附加记录：    

Comma after 'dreams'. Unless you're going for a sort of mysterious thing where the run on sentences show how unearthly this being is.

Also, odd how its dialogue switches to a different, bolded and italcized style  here wheras earlier, it spoke like this.

Also, earlier, it was addressed as Unknown , whereas here, there's no addressing as such.

Maybe I'm just a stickler for consistency, I don't know, but I found this a tad jarring.

Wrong 
附加记录：    

No period.

Rossi: What are you talking about?
附加记录：    

I personally imagine this character responding with 'What you do mean 'wrong?'' as a way to sass it. Can't explain why.

I just finished the first Addendum.

Hmmmso far, I find the dialogue pretty decent. There's a good sense of mystery and a good sense of buildup, but I don't feel attached to Rossi. She feels like she's just there.

Also, the hooded figure feels a little too 'generic mysterious dream person' to me.

Like, I really dig it all, but it's missing a little bit of that pizzazz which makes me go from nodding my head and approving to making the following face:

:o

And losing all breath at the insane BS occuring before me.

Descriptions are lovely, though and the use of dream logic is very good. A little too downplayed, though, in my opinion; it's a dream and yet, despite lapses in timeline and everything being misty and hard to make out with shadowy figures, it doesn't get weird enough in my opinion. The weirdest we see is the dream figure levitating and twirling in the air. It's a dream and I think the surreal nature can be cranked up a notch or 12.

Then again, I can see that that isn't what you're really going for, so this part can be taken however you wish.

Personally, I just love seeing dream logic become as surreal and weird as possible. Probably just me.

11. There is no body of water within twenty kilometers of Kayaköy.
附加记录：    

I like this touch here to show the difference between the 'real' version and the dream version.

Waltham: I can get to the edge of town, it’s not infinite… but then I’ll blank and it will fold in on itself; fall apart until it reforms and I’m sent right back to the fucking church..
附加记录：    

Lovely dream logic. Sorry I'm being a broken record, I just really love weird things.

Waltham: I’m not alone here. I thought I was, but I’m not. There’s something here. I haven’t seen anything but… I’m being watched.
附加记录：    

This feels a tad too rehearsed.

Waltham: I'll be the judge of that. I said, come out where I can see you.
附加记录：    

You are looking right at me. Do you like it?

Needs a space between the two.

The street and buildings are also exhibiting non-Euclidean geometrics as they fold in upon themselves, shifting the camera between different elevations and rapidly restructuring from one layout to another.
附加记录：    

This. I personally would like more stuff like this. That said, I'm having a difficult time imagining all of this. Then again, describing non-euclidean geometry is very difficult, so I'm not really sure what else to offer here.

Personally, this makes me think of the video game Control when I imagine the walls moving. Same with the streets.

Do you like it this way? 

[Waltham does not respond. She is believed to be unconscious.]

Oh. 
附加记录：    

So the thing has a clumsy side. Idon't really like this scene. To me, this moment feels almost Tom and Jerry-ish in a piece that's meant to feel melancholy and strange.

And there’s no one else… You have to stay! 
附加记录：    

This was the twist that gave me chills.

After her debriefing, the current Special Containment Procedures were put in place. To date, no further SCP-XXXX events have occurred.
附加记录：    

Well, shit. The hooded figure is just lonely and wants company. Well, that's a twist I wasn't expecting.

Other than that last bit, a good bit of the scenes just felt really rehearsed. The sequence of events were intriguing enough to keep me going and it was built up well and the dialogue was serviceable, but I think I can just summarize it as that:serviceable.

The twist? Fantastic.

I can understand the clumsy moment earlier now. The child-like clinginess and that clumsy moment from earlier, the way it begs for people to stay. It's like a lonely little kid that just wants friends.

That's a great twist.

I just think the journey along the way needs to be patched up.

Rossi and Waltham just feel like vehicles to show some weird stuff happenng.

I get the feeling that Rossi is meant to be sassy and strongwilled while Waltham is supposed to be serious and soldier-tough with a soft side, but if that's what you were going for, it doesn't come through enough.

Anywho, I hope I helped and I bid you a lovely day, Grig!

3

If you got any questions or I was clumsy and was unclear on a few things, feel free to PM or .tell me!
