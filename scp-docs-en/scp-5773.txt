Special Containment Procedures:  Instances of SCP-5773-1 are to be contained at Biological Containment Site-11A. Site-11A's atmosphere is to be routinely sterilized once per thirty minute cycle. Foundation personnel assigned to Site-11A are to wear Class-V extreme biological hazard suits at all times.

Interaction with instances of SCP-5773-1 is strictly prohibited for personnel above D-Class. Expired instances of SCP-5773-1 must be disposed of by incineration.

Description:  SCP-5773 is a spontaneous, cognitohazardous phenomenon that affects Homo sapiens, resulting in subjects1 compulsively following one another in a continuous circle. SCP-5773 is caused by the anomalous production of the trail pheromone, Y-802, which is not naturally produced by humans.

SCP-5773 events typically include between two and twenty individuals. However, due to the inherently social nature of humans, it is speculated that unmitigated SCP-5773 events could involve millions of SCP-5773-1 instances.

It is possible to influence SCP-5773 events by impeding the movement of the lead instance of SCP-5773-1. Implementation of this strategy has not only reduced the size of SCP-5773 events but has also allowed for the containment of affected instances.

Instances of SCP-5773-1 display no evidence of self-preservation instincts and will inevitably expire from exhaustion2. As of 21/10/2020, only one instance, identified as SCP-5773-1-A251, has remained conscious during an SCP-5773 event. On 03/03/2017, successful attempts were made by the Foundation to establish communication with the affected instance.

Addendum 5773.1 : Interview Log

~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Date : March 3rd, 2017

Interviewer : Dr. Jeong Sol-ji, Research Head, Department of Psychology

Interviewee : SCP-5773-1-A2513
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Foreword : The following interview took place after Foundation staff identified SCP-5773-1-A251 as displaying signs of consciousness and lucidity. Attempts at communication were made throughout the interview.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

[Begin Log] 

Dr. Sol-ji:  

All instances continue to rotate around in a circle within the containment chamber. Each instances' vision is fixated directly ahead of them.

Dr. Sol-ji:  

SCP-5773-1-A251's pupils dilate in response.

Dr. Sol-ji:  

SCP-5773-1-A251 blinks once.

Dr. Sol-ji:  

SCP-5773-1-A251 blinks once.

Dr. Sol-ji:  

SCP-5773-1-A251 blinks once.

Dr. Sol-ji:  

SCP-5773-1-A251 blinks once. Shortly after, it blinks twice more.

Dr. Sol-ji:  

SCP-5773-1-A251 begins blinking erratically and starts to visibly sweat.

Dr. Sol-ji:  

SCP-5773-1-A251's jaw and neck muscles spasm uncontrollably.

Dr. Sol-ji:  

SCP-5773-1-A251 turns towards the intercom with a panicked expression and stammers inaudibly.

Dr. Sol-ji:  

SCP-5773-1-A251:  

[End Log] 
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Closing statement:  SCP-5773-1-A251 failed to respond to any further attempts at questioning and resumed typical SCP-5773-1 behaviour. The subject expired two hours after the interview's conclusion.
