SCP-XXXX A blessed exchange (Seeking greenlight)

26 Dec 2020 11:45

Seeking Greenlights:  Yes

Page Type:  SCP Article

Genre:  Horror/Mystery

Page Layout:  Object description and containment are minimal, and main focus will be on testing logs. There will be 10-15 of them, starting with mundane, and increasing in strangeness.

Elevator Pitch:  The object swaps two aspects (body parts/memories/reality of their life) of a person with a new one, seemingly from its  of obtained aspects. Since an unknown number of exchanges have occurred, over an unknown time/places, it has an unknowable number of aspects in its collection.

Central Narrative:  Minimal. The testing logs will add mystery around the objects age/origins and purpose, suggesting alien/extra-dimensional life. Since the object can alter reality, detailed records and logs need to be kept in order to keep track of reality alterations.

Hook/Attention-Grabber:  The object removes aspects of a person and gives a brand new one. The removal of aspects could kill or dramatically change a person, even removing people/companies/ideas related to them. The new aspects can be memories, new organs, or reality altering itself as well. The testing logs will start with mundane things, eg gaining memories of another person, growing a new limb etc, and will progress to stranger exchanges where friends/family cease to exist, or they gain non-human knowledge. Adding to the intrigue is that every aspect  is assumed to have been taken at some stage, suggesting that the object has altered reality many times in the past in unknown ways.
