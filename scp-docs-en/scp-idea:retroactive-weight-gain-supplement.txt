SCP idea:  Retroactive weight gain supplement

06 Dec 2020 01:24

A scam supplement that is advertised as causing weight loss but it actually rewrites your memories to make you only think you have lost weight. Use it too much and your memories start getting weird.

I moved and trimmed your thread summary, since it was taking up a lot of space in the main forum screen. Try to keep thread descriptions (the text in the little box under the thread title) short so you don't crowd out other authors in the forum listing. ~Zyn

Edit: Added the required template.

Seeking Greenlights:  Yes

Page Type:  SCP Article

Genre (Optional):  Horror

Page Layout (Optional):  Page starts with basic containment procedures and a description. The main part of the narrative will be in the experiment log, which will reveal more about the effects of the SCP and may include interview logs with the test subjects.

Elevator Pitch:  A scam supplement that is advertised as causing weight loss but it actually rewrites your memories to make you only think you have lost weight. Use it too much and your memories start getting weird.

+block

–block

Central Narrative:  The SCP is a weight loss pill that you can order a subscription to though an anomalous phone number. Each pill rewrites your memories a little bit to make you believe you lost weight after taking the pill. Since it does not actually cause weight loss in the present, you probably will actually gain weight without knowing it. The more pills that are taken the fatter you will believe you used to be and the further back in time it changes your memories. Eventually, you will be convinced that you have been morbidly obese your entire life before you started taking the pills. If you keep taking the pills past this point (I will call this the critical point for now), things start to get strange. The boxes that the pills come in have two languages on them. One is English (or whatever language you speak) and is all lies, and the other is an unknown language. After the critical point, you gain the ability to read the unknown language, which tells the truth about what the pills do, and you start to gain memories from before you were born of being something not human.

Hook/Attention-Grabber:  It is a weight loss pill that retroactively turns users into gluttony monsters in human form, or at least makes them think they are.

Additional Notes:  I haven't decided yet how much it affects other people's memories or physical records like pictures. I haven't decided yet what happens if you stop taking the pills, though it probably depends on if you have passed the critical point or not. I may end up leaving it ambiguous if it is actually changing the past or just your memories.

Re: SCP idea:  Retroactive weight gain supplement

08 Dec 2020 02:06

Let's see what we have here.

[+]crit

[-]crit

you start to gain memories from before you were born of being something not human.
附加记录：    

This part seems to appear out of the blue. Like, you have all these properties about weight loss and then you mention this. What does this have to do with the rest of the concept? I mean, you can add a depth meaning to the pills thing, but you need to have some kind of build-up, not just making it to appear spontaneously.

That said, I think some kind of twist like that would be interesting. The anomaly itself is pretty basic, and I think something like this would help a lot to improve it. What are these  about? What's the meaning of these memories? Maybe some kind of being wanted us to remember them? Maybe that being created the pills in order to attract us by using a common human desire? If you focus on this part, I think your article would be very interesting.

I haven't decided yet how much it affects other people's memories
附加记录：    

I think other people's memories would be unaffected. Like, maybe they just don't want to hurt the subject's feelings? Maybe the subject's in denial about what others say? This is a common effect of real eating disorders, so it would make this concept to feel somewhat realistic.

or physical records like pictures.
附加记录：    

Like I said, maybe they're just in denial of these records? Or maybe they see the records in a different manner?

I haven't decided yet what happens if you stop taking the pills, though it probably depends on if you have passed the critical point or not.
附加记录：    

Maybe if they stop eating the pills, the memories slowly start to get back to normal? Maybe they forget about eating the pills in the first place? Maybe they start to have some kind of side effects? Perhaps the pills are like a drug?

Overall, I think that you should add some kind of backstory to the existence of the pills.

Feel free to contact a member of the Butterfly Squad to get more crit, or PM me to take another look.
