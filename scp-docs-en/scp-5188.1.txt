Re: Please read. (FROM AUTHOR)

07 Dec 2020 16:38

First off, I am new to this site, but I still want to give you critique.

+cave

-back!

Object Class:  KETER OH GOSH RUN
附加记录：    

Keter means hard to contain, not dangerous. Also, you should not be putting this type of emotion in your writing. Have you ever written an informative essay? Would you put 'THE CHEETAH CAN RUN SUPER FAST DANGER!' in your writing? The answer is no, and it's even more emphasized on this site.

SCP-5188's cell door must be chained by 10 heavy padlocks, have a 5 digit access code, a level 4 card reader, and voice and oral recognition. NOBODY is allowed into SCP-5188's cage UNDER ANY CIRCUMSTANCES (See incident 5188-A-1).
附加记录：    

This contradicts itself. You say nobody is allowed in, but it has a level 4 card reader, and an access code. If the former is the current containment procedure, you should strike-through the other part to show it's not valid anymore.

It looks like a mother████ing cardboard spider.
附加记录：    

You're writing a research paper, and you decided to put a swear word in your formal report. You need to use plain and professional tone; this is supposed to be a research document on a dangerous object, and you need to write the part.

SCP-5188 is a standard amazon box, with no anomalous effects. SCP-5188's anomalous affects only trigger when touched. When touched, SCP-5188 will suddenly sprout human arms and legs, through the bottom.
附加记录：    

There's no narrative to this, and it seems like 'a thing that does a dangerous thing when x happens' cliché. Is there any research into why it does this or how it magically conjures up legs and arms?

SCP-5188 will then undergo a rage state, where it will kill everything in its path by [DATA EXPUNGED] them.
附加记录：    

But.. why? I read this and came out feeling bored, because there's no plot or anything; just a dangerous thing which does not have any form of development.

Exactly 69 seconds (1 minute and 9 seconds) after a human is killed, more  drag them away.
附加记录：    

Wait, how are more cardboard spiders coming out? I thought there was one box which sprouted legs and arms. Also, 69? Really?

therefore they are dubbed SCP-5188-1, and are to be treated as such.
附加记录：    

The word choice here is suboptimal. You should say something such as, '(these entities are hereinafter referred to as SCP-5188-1.)'

INCIDENT-5188-A-1:
附加记录：    

First off, before the incident, there was a Level 4 padlock. Level 4 clearance is rarely given out to administrative personnel, and even so, why would a Level 4 administrative personnel be in close proximity to a dangerous anomaly?

Dr. ███: THE ████? Is this normal? Ill write this down.
附加记录：    

This is not normal human dialogue. A professional researcher wouldn't say this out loud.

Dr. ███: OH ████ ITS WEARING HIS FACE AND ITS ████ING SMILING AT ME! ITS ████ING SMILING! WHAT THE F-
附加记录：    

I thought that he would've been prepared for the inevitable, since this was already written in the document before the test (I presume.)

SCP-5188 jumps through the glass and tackles Dr. ███. It then proceeds to

[DATA EXPUNGED]

[REDACTED]

[REDACTED]
附加记录：    

Huh? Why are there 3 redactions on 3 different lines? This makes no sense. Redactions =/= mystery value; only if they're done right. There's a good guide to redactions here.

Overall, this is a messy and rushed article, and I'm pretty sure this is a cold post as well. -1.
