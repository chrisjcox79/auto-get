SCP-XXXX (Veracity)

26 Dec 2020 05:04

Seeking Greenlights:  Yes

Page Type:  SCP Article

Page Layout (Optional):  Technical SCP including developments associated with the testing of this SCP. Focus on addendums and Incident Reports to describe the Foundation’s efforts to contain and understand this object.

+Pitch:

–block

Elevator Pitch: 

Foundation Discovery of GRU-P Experiments at New Research Area Kantubek located on the Vozdrozhdeniya Isthmus (Formerly island) in the now diminishing Aral Sea region near the Kazakhstan/Uzbekistan border. SCP-XXXX is a Polygraph machine with cognitohazardous properties which affect those exposed to the results produced by the machine making them believe with certainty and without question the accuracy and truth of statements analyzed by the machine, even when presented with factual evidence to the contrary (cognitive dissonance). Further experimentation reveals additional congnitohazards associated with the machine as well as evidence of suspected retrocausal effects generated by the machine. Much of the story development in the article will occur via incident reports filed by Foundation personnel.

+Narrative:

–block

Central Narrative: 

SCP-XXXX is one of many SCPs the Foundation currently contains (or is attempting to contain) at Research Area Kantubek, formerly a GRU-P research and development facility. Central tension focuses around GRU-P mismanagement of anomalous items and persons and the implications of containment breaches. I would like to expand the story of SCP’s at Research Area Kantubek as well as create a number of tales which describe the lives and personal experiences of Foundation Personnel working within this former GRU-P compound. While this article is just to begin seeding plot ideas and characters regarding this small-ish setting, I would like to develop it into a larger series spanning around 20-25 years of Foundation efforts occurring at this area as well as their interactions with other GOI’s (and the associated physical, emotional and political challenges Foundation Personnel face here).

+Hook/Attention-Grabber:

–block

Hook/Attention-Grabber: 

Focus on a relatively mundane object which is discovered to have exceptional properties beyond original Foundation expectations and the Foundations scramble to expand Special Containment Procedures.

+Notes:

–block

Additional Notes: 

I’d like this article to be both enjoyable as a stand-alone read as well as effectively seed interest in the wider story regarding SCPs and Foundation Personnel at RA-Kantubek. I don’t want the first piece to be of too grand a scale as to overshadow future pieces, but it also needs to be involved enough as to hint at other developments and stories in RA-Kantubek. I’m also concerned about writing something too “big”, but I’m not sure how much content should be in a single SCP article to retain interest but also to decently seed plot points for future articles. Any input and feedback is welcome and appreciated!

As a courtesy to our readers on mobile devices, please collapse long posts. ~Zyn
