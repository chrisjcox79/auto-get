I wanted to love this, so, so badly. In truth, I really like this concept, and the disjointed aspect of the white boxes sort of peering into inner life in this town of Astra was fun, albeit a bit off-toned.

However, unfortunately, there's just a massive level of  dream sequence, which may be intentional, but for myself personally it's hard to associate.

I really want to love this, and I see some potential. I'd love to see this rewritten and reworked, since it's just conceptually pleasing for me personally.

EDIT:  Should also mention that I like the fourth-wall break (I'm always a sucker for them), but after re-reading, this time it feels entirely off-base from everything else, not to mention gimmicky at best.
