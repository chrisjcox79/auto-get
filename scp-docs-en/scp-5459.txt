Item #:  SCP-5459

Object Class:  Keter

Special Containment Procedures:  SCP-5459 is to be kept in a 5x5 meter box at all times. No one is to enter the box unless a controlled testing is taking place. 2 armed guards are to be stationed around the box and containment area, rotating every 20 hours. Anyone who is tested, or has been within 20m of SCP-5459 has to undergo a psych brief and debrief, and a day quarantine. SCP-5459 is able to enter others' minds and cause intense emotional distress if in an enraged state. SCP-5459 is agitated and enraged if more than 3 people are in his box. He will stay in normal form if calm, and if enraged may attempt to telepathically confuse others to get himself out or to harm others. Anyone who's mind has been entered by SCP-5459 are to be quarantined for seven (7) days.1 In the event of a containment breach, all personnel are advised to not  approach within 20m of SCP-5459 to avoid getting controlled or enraging SCP-5459. If SCP-5459 is needed to be approached, a *maximum* of 2 personnel may approach. Any Class-D attempting to approach SCP-5459 in a containment breach must be terminated.
