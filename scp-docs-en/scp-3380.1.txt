02 Nov 2017 23:57

LSF/9V collab yay

After a few months of writer's block/motivational/business hell this SCP is finally up! It's probably the weirdest thing I've been involved in the creation of, and I should probably take a shower because of how dirty I feel.

Massive credit to LordStonefish! This article wouldn't be anywhere near as good without their writing and ideas. 
Thanks to taylor_itkin, and some other people I've definitely forgotten for critting this! (if I didn't list you let me know!)

**ITEM #:** SCP-

EDIT:  Apparently some people have been concerned about this being an anti-atheism and anti-STEM piece. Here's a comment I posted on reddit (and in this discussion thread) that I'll just put here as well.

Just a disclaimer from one of the writers of this SCP. This SCP isn't meant to be an attack on atheism or STEM (I'm an atheist and I'll likely end up in a STEM career). This is about how atheism and a love for STEM can be taken to the extremes, in this case turning into a fanatic cult that thinks STEM is superior to all else and that anyone that isn't an atheist is lesser than them. That and an admittedly blatant parody of militant atheists and STEMlords.
