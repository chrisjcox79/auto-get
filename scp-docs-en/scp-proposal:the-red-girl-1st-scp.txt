SCP Proposal: The Red Girl (1st SCP)

20 Nov 2020 09:01

Seeking Greenlights:  Yes

Page Type:  SCP Article

Elevator Pitch:  SCP-XXXX, AKA the Red Girl, is a humanoid anomaly which takes the appearance of a 15-16 year old girl with with long hair and irises in a unnatural shade of bright red. The Red Girl shows usually sweet, affective behavior, but will get triggered if she gets visually or audibly exposed to rage or hatred, or senses rage in a nearby person. SCP-XXXX will undergo a rapid transformation, in which she grows red scales on 60% of her body, will grow claws and fangs and the hair will turn into spikes. SCP-XXXX will then attempt to viciously attack the person that it sensed the anger from, often resulting in a chain reaction if multiple people are nearby, although it will never attack more than 8 people in a row. After the attack, SCP-XXXX will show shock and remorse over its own deeds, and slowly regain her human appearance. Attempts learn SCP-XXXX to control this states have proven ineffective, as the Red Girl seems to lose all memories of the incident within the next 8 hours.

Central Narrative:  Although the origins of the Red Girl are unknown, SCP-researchers have constructed several theories of why its attacks are aimed at people who carry negative feelings towards others: what if SCP-XXXX was created with the purpose of simply erasing all negative feelings in this world? Did the creator attempt to slowly rebuild a more positive and tolerant society? Or did they simply want to create a environment of fear and sadness for those who have committed the sin of wrath? The answer is still unknown, as SCP-XXXX doesn't seem to know herself about her creation and her memories of incidents continuously get erased.

Hook/Attention-Grabber:  I think that the mystery of her creation might become an interesting topic. Furthermore, I think the Red Girl fits the category of humanoid SCP's who have limited power over their anomalous abilities and actions, such as SCP-053, SCP-073 or SCP-2662, without being too similar to either of them. I also feel an SCP with something that resembles a dual personality disorder is a rather unused concept.
