SCP Proposal: The Red Girl (1st SCP)

20 Nov 2020 09:01

A new humanoid, Euclid-class SCP, which undergoes some dangerous changes when exposed to rage, and might have a connection towards the SCPs Cain and Abel.

I moved and trimmed your thread summary, since it was taking up extra space in the main forum screen. Try to keep thread descriptions (the text in the little box under the thread title) and titles short so you don't crowd out other authors in the forum listing. ~Zyn

Seeking Greenlights:  Yes

Page Type:  SCP Article

Elevator Pitch:  SCP-XXXX, AKA the Red Girl, is a humanoid anomaly which takes the appearance of a 15-16 year old girl with with long hair and irises in a unnatural shade of bright red. The Red Girl shows usually sweet, affective behavior, but will get triggered if she gets visually or audibly exposed to rage or hatred, or senses rage in a nearby person. SCP-XXXX will undergo a rapid transformation, in which she grows red scales on 60% of her body, will grow claws and fangs and the hair will turn into spikes. SCP-XXXX will then attempt to viciously attack the person that it sensed the anger from, often resulting in a chain reaction if multiple people are nearby, although it will never attack more than 8 people in a row. After the attack, SCP-XXXX will show shock and remorse over its own deeds, and slowly regain her human appearance. Attempts learn SCP-XXXX to control this states have proven ineffective, as the Red Girl seems to lose all memories of the incident within the next 8 hours.

Central Narrative:  Although the origins of SCP-XXXX are unbeknownst even to her, Foundation researchers have found one potential clue: on the left shoulder blade, SCP-XXXX bears a tattoo of Sumerian origin, meaning 3 in the original language. This might be a link to SCP-073, who carries a similar mark on his body. Some researchers even started to reference to SCP-XXXX as 'the third sibling', although further research will be necessary before a link can be confirmed.

Hook/Attention-Grabber:  I want to expand the concepts of SCP-073 and SCP-076. Wouldn't it be interesting to see what would happen if it gets confirmed that Cain and Abel in the SCP universe have a younger sister, who possesses yet another set of anomalous abilities, alongside resemblance in personalities from both brothers?
