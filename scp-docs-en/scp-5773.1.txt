This started out as me wanting to explore how modern people are like ants and from there it turned into a full blown meta-commentary on society and the Foundation.

TL;DR: We live in a society.

+spoilers

-Spoiled

The anomaly isn't just the pheromone. It's everything related to the Spiral. The essence of the spiral is cognitohazardous and spontaneous. There's no unknowable eldritch horror, no malicious Foundation secret, nothing that goes bump in the night and hides under your bed.

It just exists because it does and those are the most horrifying things to me.

The Foundation's pursuit of knowledge and answers, their insatiable desire to know everything is absolutely futile here. In fact, it is straight up detrimental to them because everything becomes a spiral, sooner or later.

I couldn't draw any meaningful commentary from this piece at all. Virtually none of the subtext you want to convey is present in the piece because you aren't actually exploring the Foundation's response to this problem. You'll need to actually depict the progression of the Foundation's research into this anomaly, its continual defiance of their attempts to explain it, and how the Foundation personnel trying to solve this problem are actually affected by and pulled into the spiral. In its current state, this article says less about society than an actual Joker meme.
