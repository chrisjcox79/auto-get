17 May 2017 16:25

My only issue with is it that I don't think this counts as an infohazard.
附加记录：    

How not? Its activity is triggered either at random or by people talking about it in text (which is an infohazard trait that's in other articles in the infohazard tag, admittedly, like 2521 for example):

+excerpt

-collapse

SCP-3101:  just type something and I'll come for you

SCP-3101:  in multiple ways ;)

Agent Shaw:  Interesting. And I am on MTF Lambda-12, yes. How did you learn that?

SCP-3101:  i read a lot and i talk to people

Agent Shaw:  Hmm. What was that about getting your attention? What do we say if we want you to show up?

SCP-3101:  well uhhhhh you guys haven't given me a name yet but once you do I'll answer to it ;;

Agent Shaw:  Let me ask.

Agent Shaw:  It looks like the available slot was SCP-3101.

SCP-3101:  Oh ok 3

SCP-3101:  if you say that I'll come to the document I see it at and I'll talk
附加记录：    

It functions as a textual infohazard. It just happens to also be sapient and claim that it won't creep on and/or edit any documents that get its attention, but obviously there's no way to guarantee it'll keep its word. If it weren't capable of conversation, it would look like a text-editing and text-searching anomaly that needs to get wiped out before it messes something up  which is arguably all it actually is, but the Ethics Committee didn't like that idea because it's intelligent.
