SCP Idea - Algernon-13

13 Dec 2020 18:49

Seeking Greenlights:  Yes

Page Type:  SCP Article

Genre (Optional):  Sci-fi, Light horror

Elevator Pitch:  A dim, distant star is discovered by a group of astronomers who discover that, when extensively gazed upon, said star can temporarily impart seemingly supernatural enhancements to the viewer. Said enhancements come at a price, however.

Central Narrative:  A small research team in Argentina discovers a hitherto unknown star. The initial astronomer who extensively gazes at the star notes a small mote of light in the corner of his eye, though the light dissipates after several hours, since he chooses to ignore it. His partner, however, continues to be bothered by the light in the corner of her eye and keeps staring at it. Once she stares at it for an arbitrary amount of time, she suddenly seizes a marker and begins scribbling strange symbols on a whiteboard. This fit lasts for approximately thirteen hours before she collapses from exhaustion. When she reawakens, she realizes that her knowledge of mathematics and science has been dramatically reduced to roughly that of a first-grade level.

SCP is made aware of this development and has its own team investigate the star, which has been designated Algernon-13 (after the novel ). Numerous personnel undergo tests, including a former soldier (who experiences increased aggression and muscle growth), a linguist (who briefly becomes an onmiglot), and an artist (who paints gorgeous pictures unlike any she'd ever made before, and which offers some insight into the nature of the star), all of whom suffer severe degradation of their enhanced abilities (i.e. the soldier experiencing muscle atrophy) after thirteen hours have passed. It also becomes apparent that the star is attempting to communicate in some way, and the SCP would detail their efforts to figure out what the star wants.

Hook/Attention-Grabber:  The hook involves the mystery behind why the star induces  on its viewers. The mystery reaches its zenith with the pictures painted by the artist, as they give a nebulous picture of what's going on around the star, leaving one with as many new questions as answers.

Additional Notes:  Not sure what to rate this anomaly - Euclid or Keter class. Suggestions would be helpful.

Re: SCP Idea - Algernon-13

14 Dec 2020 00:22

I like the idea of there being a mysterious star exerting influence over humans, and then gradually learning that the star is intelligent and acting deliberately to some terrible end. On the other hand, the  effect you described doesn't really interest me at all. I think part of the reason is that the Algernonification is tied to the victims' respective professions in such a straightforward way. There's no intrigue when the outcome of an experiment is so predictable, and there's no impact in inflicting misery on a series of characters without having some degree of emotional buildup and payoff.

No greenlight from me I'm afraid, but I think the idea is worth revisiting and revising.
