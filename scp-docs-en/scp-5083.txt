                        

Item #:  SCP-5083

Object Class:  Euclid

Special Containment Procedures:  SCP-5083 is kept in a brown briefcase behind two locked doors in the secured general holding area at Site-15. Staff lower than level two is not permitted access to SCP-5083.

Description:  SCP-5083 is a silver USB drive. It contains launch codes of Soviet missiles hidden on U.S. soil. The object was discovered in 1992 in an abandoned Soviet research facility located in the ████ mountain range after the cold war.

Due to its recent discovery and authorization checks, there is a lack of information regarding the contents of SCP-5083.

Addendum:  It has been suggested by [DATA EXPUNGED] that we allow SCP-079 access to SCP-5083 in order to learn about the content within.

Currently pending clearance from higher authority in order to proceed.
