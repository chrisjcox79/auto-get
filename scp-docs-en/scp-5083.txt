                        

Item #:  SCP-5083

Object Class:  Euclid

Special Containment Procedures:  SCP-5083 is to be stored inside of an airtight containment chamber with no exits or entrances.

Description:  SCP-5083 is a body of clear gas originally found inside of a jar located inside of a house in ██████████, Wyoming. SCP-5083 when inhaled gives off a taste of pine sap inside of the subject's mouth and a heavy feeling in the lungs.

It is observed that when SCP-5083 is inside of the lungs, it multiplies and the old body of gas is absorbed by the lung tissue. Within the first 30 minutes of inhalation, no effects are able to be observed, however, approximately 45 minutes after inhalation, the subject will contract a sudden desire to be relocated to a heavily forested area. If they are not transported to the desired location, they will begin to experience negative emotions and become fatigued.

After approximately an hour and thirty minutes, if the subject is not in a comfortable location such as a forest, SCP-5083's old body will become active and the subject will begin to experience excruciating pain and manifesting suicidal thoughts. If the subject is kept from killing themselves, the transformation to wood spreads to other internal organs. The subject is killed approximately a day after inhaling SCP-5083, however the infection continues to spread until the entirety of the subjects body has been transformed into wood. A test conducted on said wood revealed that it is pine wood, and as is true with trees, the inside of the wood contains rings that tell of the subjects lifetime and injuries.

Research conducted on subjects who were allowed transportation to the desired location seem to get a burst of energy upon arrival, and begin to frolic amongst the trees and plants. Subjects conflicted that were transported do not show any further negative symptoms of the infection. However, after approximately a day has passed and they are kept happy, subjects begin to mutate and generate a natural aura around them. Subjects with these symptoms are to be classified as SCP-5083-2.

Instances of SCP-5083-2 are classified as Safe and are to be contained within a secluded forest in ███████, North Carolina. The appearance of SCP-5083-2 instances resemble a human with unkept hair, pointy ears, and fingers with green tips. Instances of SCP-5083-2 appear to have supernatural abilities, allowing them the revival of withered plants, speeding up the growth of plants and trees, and the ability to telepathically communicate with humans and wildlife. These instances have been shown to build societies and towns when exposed to each other. These instances have also created their own language, consisting of clicks and grunts, as well as a language that has little info and is under extensive research. Effects of being within 30 feet of an instance consist of a desire to garden, a boost in serotonin and dopamine, and the feeling of comfort and safety.
