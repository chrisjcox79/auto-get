Special Containment Procedures:  SCP-5083 is located at what was previously known as █████ Inc. which is under a facility wide lock down. No personnel below level three clearance may enter the facility without appropriate level three supervision. A tether attached to the elevator and under lock and key must be secured to all D-Class used in testing to avoid escape attempts.

Incident-5083-A Report:  Dr. Spencer was never found, and therefore the task of locating Dr. Spencer has been given the utmost priority. Should Dr. Spencer be found, he is to be contained in a standard human containment cell and administered a Class A Amnestic.

On file image of Dr. R. Spencer, if a man fitting this description is seen report findings to O-5 Command immediately.

Procedures Following Incident 5083-A:  Following the unauthorized use of SCP-5083, no personnel below level four clearance are allowed in the same building as SCP-5083. All requests to experiment with SCP-5083 are to be denied. Anyone who uses the elevator without level five clearance is to be suspended of duty.

Description:  SCP-5083 is a 1.6 x 2.1 meter elevator and 2 x 30 meter elevator shaft. The elevator is comprised of steel and has seven buttons on the inside of it. The shaft however, is comprised of steel and an unknown substance. SCP-5083 functions as a normal elevator but when button seven is pressed the person(s) inside the elevator will be taken to the place they subconsciously most desire. This function is automatic and does not require user input. Places recorded have been The Fiji Islands, low Earth orbit, and England. In all instances, the elevator returns within 20 minutes after leaving the facility area.

Test 1 

Test Subject: D-6496 and one camera

Foreword: Has a slowly deteriorating mental state. Was a farmer before he was sent to prison. Would often refer to a location he called .

Location: The elevator arrived in Illinois, in an area commonly used for corn fields.

Dr. Spencer: Hey the camera feed is pretty bad, what's going on?

D-6496: I-I'm home! HAHA, I'M HOME! Thank you so much I haven't seen sunlight in months!

S: You're welcome 6496 but you aren't there to relax and have fun. Tell me what your surroundings look like.

6496: I'm in a field, it looks like a farm, I can see some animals in the distance.

S: Okay that would make sense, the GPS I attached to the elevator says you are somewhere in Illinois and there is lots of farmland located there.

S: It's going to be about time to come back soon, why don't you relax and get some fresh air. Your cooperation at the foundation earned it.

[D-6496 spent the next 15 minutes reading in the sunlight]

Results: D-6496 seemed to be in a lot better state than he was when he left. He showed much gratitude to personnel for the inclusion of him in the testing of SCP-5083.
