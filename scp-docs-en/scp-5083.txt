                        

SCP-5083 “An Everyday, Normal Door.”

Object Class: Keter. Head Researcher: Mr. Clockwork. Site Locations: [DATA EXPUNGED]

SCP-5083 is a set of 2 objects in total, a key, and a standard door with normal dimensions. Both parts of SCP-5083-1 and SCP-5083-2 must be separated by 100 kilometers of distance, unless stated otherwise by someone of 05-clearance. If SCP-5083-1 and SCP-5083-2 meet, and are unlocked by any human. It will cause an SK-Class world ending scenario, where the old world will be replaced with an exact replica, 1 year in the past. It will also force them inside, to prevent them from changing their mind. Once they are inside the replica world, they will be exactly where they were 1 year in the past, and SCP-5083-2 will vanish after it has been used. A year later, after they have used SCP-5083, they will die, by any means necessary. When they die, their body will be replaced with an instance of SCP-5083-2. Which will get closer, and closer to SCP-5083-1. It’s current movement speed, is 1 meter every 24 hours, which is enough to place SCP-5083 back in it’s glass cage, every day. If by any means, SCP-5083-2 comes within 50 meters of SCP-5083-1, SCP-5083 will summon 2 objects within it’s range. A standard welcome mat, and a gold engraving on the door, random in size, stating: “An Everyday, Normal Door.” If this is seen under any means, it must be documented and all researchers, and guards on duty, must be given a Class-B amnestics. Recently discovered by Agent [REDACTED] any instance of SCP-5083-2 will minimize movement under surveillance.

Containment Procedures:  SCP-5083-1 must always have at least 2 on duty guards, at all times. It must be contained in 12m by 12m chamber made of 5 layers of concrete. Any human interaction with SCP-5083-1 is not recommended, and must be supervised by a person of 04 clearance. SCP-5083-2 must be guarded by 3 on duty M.T.F units, and must be under surveillance at all times. Any human interaction with SCP-5083-2 is forbidden, and is punishable by immediate termination.

Discovery Locations: SCP-5083-1 was discovered in a small farm house, run by a family of 3. They were confused about the sudden door in the middle of the house, so they called a few friends. At [DATA EXPUNGED] these calls were intercepted and all 3 family members were given Class-C amnestics. SCP-5083-2 was discovered at [DATA EXPUNGED] by Researcher [REDACTED], in a small cabin, receiving weird radiation signals, that seemed paranormal. 3 hours later after his discovery, Researcher [REDACTED] had died from cardiac arrest.

Update Log 1:  A VHS tape had been discovered in front of SCP-5083-1, the video consisted of a standard Class-D opening SCP-5083-1 with SCP-005, The Skeleton Key, after he did this, the door opened, and a huge entity left SCP-5083-1, and terminated the shown Class-D in a 2 second window. Any testing requiring any other key than SCP-5083-1, is now forbidden.

Update Log 2:  Any testing is now forbidden, due to Experiment-B57.

Experiment-B57 LOGS:

Researcher 1: D-78134, your objective is too attempt to open SCP-5083-1 with brute force.

D-78134: Let me guess, it’s gonna turn me into a fucking frog, or some useless shit like that?

M.T.F 1: D-78134, cooperate or suffer immediate termination.

D-78134: Alright, Alright. Jesus. So, how am I gonna open this piece of shit anyways?

Researcher 1: Through any means necessary, D-78134.

D-78134: I have a damn name, you know! But fine, I’ll play your little game.

D-78134 then proceeds to attempt to open SCP-5083-1.//

D-78134: Ugh! Why won’t this shit open!

Researcher 1: D-78134, please calm down!

D-78134: Shut the hell up! I’m gonna open this fucking door!

D-78134, then starts to show aggressive tendencies, and starts becoming set on breaking open SCP-5083-1.

D-78134, then stops suddenly, after a prolonged period of time.

10 minutes later, it was discovered everyone within a 100 meter radius of SCP-5083-1 had died from cardiac arrest, and total organ failure in a 2 minute window.

END LOG

UPDATE LOG 3:  SCP-5083-1, and SCP-5083-2, are now both considered cognitive hazards, and sitting near either SCP-5083-1, or SCP-5083-2, is forbidden. If anyone sits within either instance of SCP-5083, they will attempt to open it with no regards for their safety or of others safety. Anybody not regarding this rule, will suffer termination, and anyone people being affected by the cognitive hazard, are to also, be terminated.

THIS SECTION OF SCP-5083 IS RESTRICTED TO 05 CLEARANCE ONLY, DISREGARDING THIS RULE WILL BE PUNISHABLE BY DEATH CONTINUE? 

Identity confirmed Access granted..

SCP-5083-2 has started moving at a higher rate, it is now moving 2 meters every 12 hours, closer to SCP-5083-1. Extra Containment Procedures Granted

UPDATE LOG 1:  The cognitive hazard has become stronger, even being near either SCP-5083-1, or SCP-5083-2, for 12 hours, will cause the same effect. Further Duty Shifts Granted

UPDATE LOG 2:  Hydrochloric acid, has started to leak from the cracks of SCP-5083-1, at an alarming rate. Containment Relocation Request Granted

UPDATE LOG 3:  SCP-5083-2 has not only sped up to 5 meters every 1 hour, has heated itself up to 73 Celsius, by unknown means. Containment Cameras, and Further Guarding, Granted

UPDATE LOG 4:  The hydrochloric acid leaking from SCP-5083-1, has become stronger, and has become a black color. It’s rate of coming out, has also increased.

UPDATE LOG 5:  Knocks can be heard coming from the other side of SCP-5083-1, along with inaudible vocal noises. Further Guarding Granted

UPDATE LOG 6: At [DATA EXPUNGED] SCP-5083-2 teleported exactly 55 miles southeast, it was discovered, after an entire town was found dead. Along with SCP-5083-2 in the middle of said town. 

UPDATE LOG 7: SCP-5083-1 had opened for a short time, and released SCP-5083-3, a 7 foot entity, that was leaking the acid, that had been coming from the creaks of SCP-5083-1. M.T.F Unit Guarding Granted 

UPDATE LOG 8: SCP-5083-3 has started coming out of SCP-5083-1, daily, Relocation to Site-[DATA EXPUNGED] Granted. 

UPDATE LOG 9:  SCP- 5083-2 has started moving too fast to contain it, and SCP-5083-1 has started releasing too many instances of SCP-5083-3. An SK-CLASS world ending scenario, is inevitable. Neutralization Request Pending

Estimated Time Until Event: [DATA EXPUNGED]
