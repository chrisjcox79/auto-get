22 Dec 2020 15:53

A bloodied, nude woman (don't worry, the blood covers anything inappropriate) that appears in the nightmares of her victims.

I moved and trimmed your thread summary, since it was taking up extra space in the main forum screen. Try to keep thread descriptions (the text in the little box under the thread title) and titles short so you don't crowd out other authors in the forum listing. ~Zyn

Seeking Greenlights:  Yes

Page Type:  SCP Article

Genre (Optional):  Horror

Elevator Pitch: 

+block

–block

SCP-XXXX appears to be a bloody, nude female entity (blood covers any extremities) with multiple stab wounds that appears in the nightmares of distinct individuals. Nearly all subjects reported seeing copious amounts of blood pouring out of said wounds.

All relevant data has been collected by interviewing test subjects (D-Class) directly exposed to SCP-XXXX-1, a handcrafted knife from the 14th century, after multiple sleep studies.

Central Narrative 

+block

–block

90% of encounters follow this path; The subject walks into a dark room with bloody tools and knives lining the walls. In the center of the room is a table on which SCP-XXXX lies. Upon approaching the table, 100% of subjects reported that the figure began to rise from of the table and approach them.

SCP-XXXX-1 seems to have a strong connection to SCP-XXXX, possibly the weapon that killed it in the first place. The weapon had traces of blood in which the DNA of a young woman named  was extracted.

Hook/Attention Grabber: 

+block

–block

Contact with SCP-XXXX has been reported to cause several health issues, such as heart palpitations, unprovoked panic attacks and restricted airways, even in young, healthy individuals with no pre-existing conditions. In extreme cases it causes cerebral hemorrhaging and unexplainable chest wounds.

Re: SCP-XXXX 

22 Dec 2020 17:11

What you have here is still the basic of the basic and I doubt you'll get much attention to your article as it is now. This is basically a cursed object story and those have been made dozens of times at the SCP wiki and media in general. So far this proposal lacks a narrative arc that will get readers invested. Who is this Vivian Fay? Who killed her and why? Why has she become an anomaly when thousands of people are killed daily and most of them don't become anything? How did the Foundation got the knife?

Also, the whole naked woman covered in blood is kinda old and tacky? Is there a reason for her to be naked and specifically have blood covering her intimate parts? If there isn't any reason for this I suggest scrapping this part and giving her a time appropriate outfit or just skip this description at all.
