                        

3/5531
CLASSIFIED

Item #: SCP-5531
Object Class: Pending

~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Special Containment Procedures:  SCP-5531 is contained in a standard humanoid chamber in Site-346. Embedded assets in emergency response teams are to track any missing person cases in Detroit for future reference. MTF Gamma-5 () is to identify and track the IP addresses of online users deemed as eyewitnesses to an SCP-5531 sighting for administration of amnestics by deployed agents.

Clandestine investigations of corporate practices in the United States, and possibly in foreign countries, are currently underway by their respective Foundation Sites.

Description:  SCP-5531 is an emaciated, naked humanoid entity measuring 1.83 meters and 58 kilograms. Its physique vaguely resembles that of a non-anomalous member of the Homo sapien with significant biological and anatomical discrepancies:

Sexual reproductive organs are absent.
Skin across SCP-5531's body possesses a charred pigmentation, and exhibits greater elasticity than the regular human epidermis, producing a  texture upon physical contact. Applying pressure causes an exudation of a dark, viscous fluid with a high concentration of cortisol hormones.
No fingerprints1 can be extracted.
Despite the presence of vocal cords, SCP-5531 does not vocalize.
Facial features are limited to three orifices, sparsely interspersed from one another.

Two circular, hollow holes are situated on the upper half of its face, while a third, ellipsoidal opening is located near its chin.

The third orifice remains open at all times, revealing a full set of adult teeth and gum. Whether SCP-5531 chooses to maintain this expression or if this is a result of forced adjustment is unknown.

In its dormant state, SCP-5531 exhibits extreme lethargy in reacting towards external stimuli, such as following orders by staff members following containment.

However, in the occasion where at least one human being is within close proximity to SCP-5531, it enters its active state. SCP-5531 will approach, restrain and attempt to subdue them, often through violent means should the victim resist. During this event, SCP-5531 demonstrates an innate ability to grab nearby mundane objects or tools and strike them upon the victim's body region nearest to SCP-5531. In contrast, SCP-5531 does not exhibit aggression if the victim complies.

Due to stringent safety protocols regarding poorly-understood humanoid entities, no casualties were reported. It is unclear what would occur should SCP-5531 succeed in fulfilling the aforementioned criteria.

On 10/23/2020, SCP-5531 was first discovered by local law enforcement in Detroit, Michigan, southeastern United States after receiving a phone call from Lucy Trott (hereby designated as PoI-1445), a 49-year-old retiree; she was forced to retire due to her condition of high blood pressure after spending 15 years as a sales manager in ███████████ Pte. Ltd. According to reports, SCP-5531 was seen staggering on a pavement in a residential neighborhood with a bloodied hammer in its hand, leaving behind a trail of viscous black liquid.

SCP-5531 was apprehended by embedded agents and transferred to Site-346 under the cover story of a disillusioned, intoxicated coal miner. The following was recorded by Dr. Richard Charleston during containment of SCP-5531.

OBSERVATION REPORT 
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

10/23/2020: securement of SCP-5531 is successful. Had a three-minute standoff in the middle of a road intersection, yelling at it to drop the hammer (luckily, it was at a less inhabited section of the city, so unnecessary attention was at a minimum). It stared at us for two whole minutes, seemingly unfazed by the situation; or rather, I don't know  it didn't have a human face for us to even decipher its reaction.

After a while, it appeared to understand the context, and slowly dropped down to its knees as it casually threw the hammer away. It drew its arms behind its head as Agent Krowlski approached it, sidearm at the ready. That was when things started to become messy.

In one swift motion, it slapped the pistol out of Krowlski's hand, slid its arms from under his arms while he was stunned and threw him down onto the asphalt, before it locked Krowlski in an armbar. Fortunately, the other guys were quick in freeing Krowlski and sedated the anomaly.

Krowlski seemed a little shaken, but otherwise okay, apart from the little red mark on his throat. I sent him to the infirmary to be checked up to see if he had any other issues from the confrontation earlier. Medical said he was fine too, so that was that.

However, later that day, he came to my office and shared with me an oddity he observed during SCP-5531's capture. He said the armbar did catch him off-guard, and it did hurt. But he claimed that during that short time span, SCP-5531 was using its other free hand to crack Krowlski's knuckles and did some quick snaps to his neck.

He said none of them were life-threatening; if at all, he admitted that it felt good, as perverted as it may have sounded. Said he felt his headaches go away like magic; wasn't a rare sight to see him taking pills to get it off his mind before missions back then. I thanked him for the info, and dismissed him afterward.

OBSERVATION REPORT 
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

10/26/2020: Another incident in SCP-5531's containment chamber today, logged at 9:12 PM. Hours earlier, I tried to interview it or get even the smallest of a response through the microphone, didn't work. It just sat there, cross-legged while staring at the window. Sometimes it would stand motionlessly for hours, sometimes it would lie down in a fetal position.

Even if this anomaly wasn't the average human you would see everyday, we could not risk starving it, especially if it needed sustenance to survive. To do that, we sent in D-Class into the chamber with a food tray thrice daily. We tried diversifying the type of food on the tray  but it didn't seem to react to any of those.

Though, it did seem to take notice of the D-Class approaching it. It would snap its head towards their direction and stare at them until they were completely out of sight before returning to its usual routine. From the first day of confinement, SCP-5531 kept scratching the west-facing wall, playing around with a loose piece of paint flake.

One day, per usual, a D-Class (D-2234) was sent in  security footage was too grainy to see what it was doing, and D-2234 got too close.

SCP-5531 spun around and threw the paint flakes towards him. Caught off-guard, D-2234 struggled with the sudden confetti, while SCP-5531 held his leg closest to it and pulled, knocking him onto the floor. It started to climb all over him, removing his left shoe and clasping his foot. One of the guards quickly took it down and sedated it, while D-2234 was sent to medical.

Strangely, similar to Agent Krowlski, the medical staff tending to D-2234 informed me that he felt slightly more rejuvenated and relaxed after the incident, saying that SCP-5531 was his foot.

Well, now that we know it doesn't have an established diet, I have recalled all D-Class formerly assigned to SCP-5531 and transferred them to other, more useful projects. I would need more time to know the reason for its behavior.
