SCP-IDEA (MITTERNACHTSONNE) 2nd ATTEMPT

05 Dec 2020 17:39

Seeking Greenlights:  Yes

Page Type:  SCP Article

Genre (Optional):  Horror

Page Layout (Optional):  Standard SCP Article

Elevator Pitch:  SCP-XXXX commonly called (Midnight sun) is a:

⦁ Large 45m dark quadrupedal aquatic life form

⦁ Similar in appearance to the prehistoric lizard known as mosasaurs

⦁ Has a Tentacle mane around the back of cranium

⦁ Travels at speeds equal or in access of 400km/h

⦁ Strength to body to ratio is 20:1

⦁ Covered in Robust Black scale armour exterior

⦁ It Can only viewed at night

⦁ Avoid direct eye contact, avoid viewing for more than 1 minute continuously

⦁ It's origins remain unknown

⦁ Was on the move until it was contained at the THE RING 

⦁ Can increase area of influence

Central Narrative: 

SCP was first alerted to the foundation through an agent located in NOAA(National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration), NOAA noticed a drastic change in salinity in area 1km2 somewhere in the Atlantic. Initial expedition failed resulting in the loss of all life. Data emitted during time of expedition discovered: salinity in the water had changed and that the underwater local flora/fauna had mutated and become highly aggressive, SCP causing this was found to be also extremely aggressive and intelligent. SCP was contained at the site known as the THE RING  after 3 failed expedition and considerable loss of life. Containment was achieved with the aide of new countermeasure technology such as: PUVDS ( perimeter ultra violet defence system- a defence systems which emits a strong ultra violet light deep into the water)- pinning SCP into a confined area, SPS( Sea perimeter system- a system designed to prevent the influence of SCP-XXXX from exceeding a 20km2 zone and for the salinity of water to not change outside of the zone. Loss of life was deemed acceptable to contain SCP-XXXX given its aggressiveness and intelligence and usual parameters of containment failing. SCP-XXXX full name is Leviathan der Mitternachtsonne (Leviathan of the Midnight sun) and was named by a formerly deceased Kreigsmarine U-boat/K version captain.

Hook/Attention-Grabber:  SCP most dangerous ability is that it is capable of salinity conversion, which if left uncheck can cause cataclysmic change in weather, flora/fauna, disrupt oxygen levels. SCP-XXXX is also capable Phycological Physical Mutation (Able to transform a victim using their brain to mutate their body, without the need of physical contact provided certain conditions are met) creating 5 sub entities of various forms like the Gespenst(wraith). It uses this ability to great effect in order to escape its confinement.

Additional Notes:  Constructive criticism is greatly appreciated.👍
