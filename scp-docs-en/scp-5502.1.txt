In part, I agree with what cybersqyd mentions here, but I'd also like to voice some other gripes that caught me while reading. For starters, there is a goofy setup, and you don't really play around with this. This, in turn, follows into a problem I have with the content: there's just not enough of it. The table brings forth information that the reader sort of already knows, save for the SCP-5502-1 instances being contained — so it feels pretty redundant, and when we're given that feeling we're followed up with a note that doesn't really have a buildup to it because there isn't enough meat in the middle.

somecrit

closecrit

I think, personally, you could do a lot more with this note other than having it just wrap it up with . And when the Foundation begins to intervene, that's when these occurrences start to become less frequent? Because this person finally has somebody who's played with him for all these years, but he thinks it's time to go and move on. This is where you hit the reader with the ending note, where it ends on a more bittersweet tone. I think, personally, this could be much more effective than what you have here.

That aside, there are a few tonal inconsistencies and/or improvements you could make I found while reading, which I will point out now:

SCP-5502 is an event that occurs at a random time and location. It starts with the manifestation of an approximately 15-25 centimeters tall humanoid entity whose body is composed of solid gold, hereafter referred to as SCP-5502-1.
附加记录：    

You can combine these two sentences, it helps it stop from becoming stoppy-starty.

SCP-5502-1 typically manifests in the vicinity of large groups then begins to run at 40km/h speed in the opposite direction of the group.
附加记录：    

I feel this could be worded better: [SCP-5502-1 typically manifests in the vicinity of large groups, in which it will begin to run at 50km/h, in the opposite direction the group faces.]

The aforementioned SCP-5502-2 member will then receive a variable amount of money in their bank account, sometime in the next 10-12 hours, equal to the value of the gold in the SCP-5502-1 instance's body.
附加记录：    

This could also be worded better, I feel: [The aforementioned SCP-5502-2 instance will then receive an amount of money equal to that of the gold in an SCP-5502-1's body within the next 10-12 hours.]

Only humans who were in the vicinity of SCP-5502-1's initial manifestation can become a member of SCP-5502-2.
附加记录：    

This is a personal nitpick, but I always prefer using [individual] over [human].

If SCP-5502-1 encounters any other humans following initial manifestation, they usually won't make any attempt to chase or eat SCP-5502-1.
附加记录：    

[Usually] is a bit subclinical here.

After SCP-5502-1 escapes or is eaten, all members of SCP-5502-2 forget the events since SCP-5502-1's manifestation.
附加记录：    

This can be moved to the first paragraph, for better flow.

The only known evidence of SCP-5502's existence is the video recordings made by surveillance cameras and the eyewitnesses of the event.
附加记录：    

I suggest: [The only known evidence of SCP-5502's existence are video recordings by surveillance cameras, as well as eyewitnesses of the event.]

The contained entities don't try to communicate with Foundation personnel or to breach containment, instead, they run around their containment chambers in the opposite direction of the nearest human.
附加记录：    

This sentence reads a bit subclinically to me.

With all the mentions above, unfortunately, I'm leaving this with a downvote.

As mentioned above, I very much do like this article on the whole, but there are some things I still feel could be improved.

SCP-5502 is an event that occurs at a random time and location which starts with the manifestation of an approximately 15-25 centimeters tall humanoid entity
附加记录：    

I feel like a comma between :

SCP-5502 is an event which occurs at a random time and location, and starts with the manifestation of an approximately 15-25 centimeters tall humanoid entity
附加记录：    

although I'm not a native speaker, so don't take my word as gospel when it comes to SPaG.

The contained entities don't try to communicate with Foundation personnel or to breach containment, instead, they run around their containment chambers in the opposite direction of the nearest human.
附加记录：    

I feel like breaking up this sentence into two could help it flow better, and perhaps replacing the :

The contained entities don't try to communicate with Foundation personnel or to breach containment. Instead, these run around their containment chambers in the opposite direction of the nearest human.
附加记录：    

When it comes to the table, I feel like it's common practice to not include most punctuation in your headings, so I would replace

SCP-5502-1
Place of manifestation.
Status.

附加记录：    

with

SCP-5502-1
Place of manifestation
Status

附加记录：    

In general, I'm not sure how necessary the table is, but it fleshes out the anomaly a bit and was somewhat entertaining once I figured out the correlations in the place and appearance, so that's up to you.

To wrap this up, I have to disagree with some of the criticism that has been mentioned here, mostly, it appears, on the grounds of personal taste. I enjoyed this article because it's short and goofy; if it were longer, I fear the goofiness would get boring [unless you found really creative ways to employ it]. Changing the tone or adding unnecessary descriptions where you can count on the reader's imaginations to do it for you would, for me at least, defeat the spirit of the article.

All in all, I definitely enjoyed this article, and will be looking forward to more stuff from you!
