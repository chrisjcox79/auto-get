SCP - The Infinite Quarter

18 Dec 2020 21:59

Seeking Greenlight:  Yes

Page Type:  SCP Article

Elevator Pitch:  SCP-#### “The Infinite Quarter” is a 2019 San Antonio Mission Texas Quarter with the head side scratched out with a unknown instrument. The quarter seems like an average coin but when left unobserved the coin will start to rapidly multiply until observed or all open space is occupied. All other instances of the scp do not share the same anomalous duplication ability as the main coin and will only share the ability of ceasing duplication when the coins are observed though if one were to observe a duplicated instance of the coin and not the original coin it will not stop the original from duplicating.

Central Narrative:  The coin would of first been discovered when foundation agents caught wind of a man who seemed to of out of the blue came into a fortune oddly enough in quarters. Upon investigating and obvious administration of amnestic, they discovered a shoe box completely full of quarters. It wasn’t until the agent investigating the box looked away and then back to discover that the box had begun to overflow with quarters before stopping. After finding the anomalous coin they brought it to the foundation and contain it in a specially made plastic case big enough to fit a single quarter.

** Hook/Attention-Grabber:** All duplicated instances of the coin will be in perfect condition and not sharing any of the scratch marks on the head side of the coin like the original does. I had also thought to make it so that the actual original coin is always the easiest to spot as to make it more easier to be observed to warrant its duplication stopping when viewed.

** Additional notes:** possible idea is the scp foundation will sometimes use this scp for possible funding for resources and the like.

Re: SCP - The Infinite Quarter

20 Dec 2020 09:55

Building off what AnActualCrow said, my concern with your central narrative section is that it doesn't reallydo anything? There's this kinda, little mystery but there's not really a lot of time spent developing it and also bear in mind: this is a SCP article. Your addenda/discovery section exploring the mystery is going to come after the Description of what the SCP is, and so the reader will already know that the answer to the question 'where did the man get all the coins from' is 'infinite quarter'.

The idea of the Foundation using this for funding is interesting and building off that so it goes somewhere could be really fun: it might be worth considering like, how this could break something or draw unwanted attention that causes conflict.
