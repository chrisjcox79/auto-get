Re: SCP idea

01 Jan 2021 19:02

Just as a note before I get into this: you can like, reply to people in the forum without using the format btw. Like, you can just answer their questions using prose or w/e; you're only limited to using the format in the main post. Also I would highly recommend editing the main post to update your idea too: there's an edit button under the blue 'Options' button in the bottom right corner of the post.

I have assumed that the most recent response containing the format is the one you want me to look at most and have not read the rest.

[+]crit

–block

An scp that resembles old writing machine, but is able to speak via writing on paper itself.
附加记录：    

when you say 'old writing machine', do you mean a typewriter? Or like, a printing press or something?

Inside scp is trapped a museom director.
附加记录：    

I'm assuming in like, a 'the director is a ghost inhabiting the typewriter and in control of it to allow it to type' way?
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Moving past these relatively shallow concerns, my core issue is that the narrative just, isn't very interesting. 'things what tell the foundation about scps' are super commonly proposed and they're generally all kinda bland because it's really hard to make that a compelling narrative: there's not a tonne of stakes to it, or real reason for a reader to get invested; and often feels like it's just an excuse for you to throw like 40 weird things you thought of at the reader without having to do anything interesting with them.

Beyond this, the generic 'it tells a bunch of people and then the o5's limit it's use' angle feelslike it doesn't really give you any space to do something interesting with it?

My thoughts here are that it being a museum director inside it is actually the most interesting angle: it feels like it opens you up to like, really comparing and contrasting the Foundation and a museum and like, having the director be all  and like really question how that works.

Iunno, you don't have to do this but my advice here would be figure out an interesting story you can tell about this without dragging a bunch of other SCPs in: this should stand on it's own, rather than by reminding us of other works that we've read.
