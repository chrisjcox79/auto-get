SCP Idea - Livin on a Prayer

18 Dec 2020 19:18

Seeking Greenlights:  Yes

Page Type:  SCP Article

Genre:  Horror, Emotional

Page Layout:  Containment » Description (Place of discovery, appearance, known history) » anomalous effects » explorations

Elevator Pitch:  SCP is supposed to be about human's pilgrimage to become better person hidden under intend to gain human's deepest desires.

Central Narrative:  SCP is mummified fragmented corpse of man with engravings on his whole body. When human comes in contact with part of corpse, part of corpse individual got in contact merges with their own body part or replaces body part if it's missing, having appearance of host's own body part with engravings from corpse. Once it happens part that merged with corpse is now between 2 dimensions. It can influence our dimension and even dimension which corpse connects to. Once the corpse is fully gathered and merged with host, individual mirrors actions (harm included) done in SCP dimension except movement, yet the person is unable to directly communicate with our dimension. In order to separate host from corpse, host's body has to be cooled down to 32°F) which will cause corpse to separate and could be pulled off from host with no harm.

Then pilgrimage will start.

Pilgrimage could be described as 7 tests on human mind which are connected to 7 deadly sins. Through the pilgrimage tested person is influenced by shape shifting entities taking form of things important to tested individual and questioned by snake like entity. It points on flaws in human personality showing that nobody is perfect. It's supposed to be dark as it seems like way to become better person, but it only leads to certain death.

Hook/Attention-Grabber:  I don't know. I've seen many awesome SCP against which my idea couldn't stand a chance, but I took this as opportunity to show my ideas and who knowssomeone might like it and find it unique. This SCP was made from my daily thoughts so I don't find it too interesting.

Additional Notes: 

In this SCP appears my personal scientist Doctor Alistair Lovecraft.
