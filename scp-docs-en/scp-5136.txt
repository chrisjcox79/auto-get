                        

Item#: Scp 5136

Object class : euclid

Special containment procedures: Scp 5136 could not be contained in anyway. Any symptoms of Scp 5136 should be reported immediately and be given class A amnestics .

Description : Scp 5136 is a dream that will go from victim to victim. Although the dream can take many forms, the most common form taken is a child like face with hollow eyes spiked hair and a dirty face. Scp 5136 'S hunting pattern is unknown, and can take place anywhere on earth so long the person is alive.

Scp 5136 will chose a random person and haunt them in their dreams, Scp 5136 will first let the person dream of a loved one who they lost such as a friend or family member that passed away recently in their lives, Scp 5136 will then tell them a date that is seemingly random. For a few days the subject will be fine until it reaches the date that Scp 5136 said. During the night the subject will dream of the face mentioned earlier and flash multiple times from a mid range then ending it with a scare in the front with a scream,the subject will then immediately wake up and regardless of them being scared of the face or not ,will immediately wake up in a panicked state, if the subject tries to go back to sleep., it will repeat the process.

After the day the subject will have the face of Scp 5136 appear in random places, sometimes on the face of their families or friends, or it will continually flash the face at the subjects eye. After six hours of continuous faces scaring the subject, the amount of faces will start to increase,and flash more frequently. After two days of scaring, the subject will then be unable to sleep, have high levels of paranoia,be constantly in a panicked state, difficulty breathing and have trust issues.

On the third day, the subject will have a normal day without the faces, but when subject goes to bed, the faces will all appear all at once, scaring the subject to high levels of stress and blood pressure which leads to death.

Scp 5136's symptomes can be treated with high levels of therapy or class A amnestics. However this must happen before the third night or else it cannot be cured.

The first case of Scp 5136 is dated back in 1957 November 26th in a small village , the victims body was found without any scars or bruises, but with the victims veins have become more darkened and more visible, with his eye pupils minimized and eye width enlarged. After the case, Scp members and scientist were dispatched to recover the body for investigation.
