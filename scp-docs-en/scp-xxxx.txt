SCP-XXXX

07 Dec 2020 21:24

Seeking Greenlights: Yes

Page Type: SCP Article

Genre : Supernatural

Page Layout : A classical format, with the item's name, the object class, the containment procedures, the description, and then the different experiences and incidents that have happened with this SCP.

Elevator Pitch: It's a bedroom. Once one walk in it, they enter a state of euphoria and start gathering as many pillows as they can find. Then, they will look for another human being and throw a pillow at them. If the target catches the pillow, they will enter the same state of euphoria and start a pillow fight with them. Once done, the two infected will look for others to contaminate through a pillow fight.

Central Narrative : SCP-XXXX is actually fillled with the remains of the souls of 7 young girls who died mysteriously in that very same room during a slumber party. After many experiences with the room, they ended up accidentally creating a second SCP-XXXX in a testing room of the SCP Foundation.

Hook/Attention-Grabber : SCP-XXXX could potentially cause the end of the world. Pillow fighting ending the world would be quite unconventional, wouldn't it ?

Additional Notes : I would like to know if anything like that is on the site already, since I haven't found anything like that myself.

Re: SCP-XXXX

17 Dec 2020 18:12

Well basically, by investigating that room and the house that it is in, they discover that years ago, 7 young girls who had a slumber party there were found dead in the room. The police never found out what happened, but the Foundation suspect another potential SCP to have been responsible. When they died, the room was impregnated with the remnants of the girls' souls with the feeling they had when they died. That's how SCP-XXXX was created. Now, whenever someone enters the room, they are  by the remnants, and act as previously described. The remnents then behave like some kind of virus, spreading from one person to another through a pillow fight. That's why it could potentially cause the end of the world. If everyone starts running around giggling and pillowfighting forever, human civilization would collapse.

As to how to bring it up, I was thinking of having the Foundation investigate and learn about the 7 girls (as previously stated), then do experiences and talking with one of the possessed one. Then they'll understand that this person is possessed with the remnants of the girls' souls though dialogue.

I know it sounds jarring, but that's why I like it. I love SCPs that are seemingly funny or ridiculous, just to be revealed to actually be dangerous and creepy.
