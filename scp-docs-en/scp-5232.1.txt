Aside from the unprofessional writing style, it doesn't make sense that knowing about the dice will get you terminated. The dice itself isn't an infohazard which warrants a termination upon discovery. Even if it needed to be hidden from level 1 below, amnestics should suffice. Also, researchers don't leave out untested variables unless if it's too dangerous/unethical. Since they were about to test if the dice will roll even with a barrier between the subject, it should've been included in the description. Other tests that should've been included was how it would react if a group were to enter its (Please remove that. It's unnecessary and not what a researcher would normally do.) Make sure you cover all your bases.

Try reading How to Write an SCP and go ask in forums to improve the style and idea of what you're writing.
