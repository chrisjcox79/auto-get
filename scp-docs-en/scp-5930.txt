                        

Item# : SCP-5930

Object Class : Euclid

Level access:  Clearance level 3

Special Containment Procedures: 

SCP-5930 is to be kept in a standard humanoid containment cell, with a glass panel for observation and interviews. The cell must contain a bed, 2 tables, a bookshelf and a bathroom. Its containment cell must be cleaned at the end of the day for the mess caused by 5930 while utilizing paint, water, paper and sewing materials. Any finished creations from it are to be removed. The containment cell is to be guarded by 2 armed guards at the entrance and be observed through security cameras hidden in its cell. The entity is to be sedated in case it becomes aggressive and attempts to breach containment or harm personnel.

The subject is allowed to make request for good behavior as long as it does not conflict with protocol, any requests made are to be approved by personnel of level 4 clearance.

Since its containment it has requested the following:

-Various books (Some have been accepted, most have been denied)
-Various colors of yarn (Accepted)
-Paint pallets (Accepted)
-Canvas (Accepted)
-Clay (Accepted, No longer Permitted)
-A block of Marble (Denied)
-Play-dough (Accepted)

As off incident-2, it is to be closely monitored by the guards, so it does not attempt to create any hostile instances of 5930-C while it’s in a trance, if it does attempt to do so it’s to be reported immediately and 5930-C is to be removed and terminated through incineration.

Note:  In light of incident-5930-2, the subject is no longer permitted to utilize sculpting materials until further notice.

During testing two guards and Dr. Schmidt are to be present alongside the subject in case any sudden changes in behavior or attempted destruction of 5930-B or 5930-C occur. In light of this occurrence, 5930 will grow increasingly hostile and attempt to attack the subject of its aggression.

With the occurrence of Incident-2 it’s been noted, that it will attempt to break containment to hunt down the subject. Attacks caused by 5930 resemble the harm inflicted to 5930-C, though the attacks tend to lead to minor injuries, there has been one recorded casualty after the destruction of 5930-C-5, see Incident-2. In case containment is breached the subject is to be located and sedated, and returned to its cell, afterwards 5930 is to be monitored very closely in case any signs of the trance or agression remain. Any personnel causing maltreatment towards 5930-B or 5930-C are reprimanded and must be relocated away from SCP-5930 so it does not attempt to continue its hunt. 5930 is aware of any maltreatment that occurs with 5930-B and 5930-C.

In light of 5930 aggression when the removal of 5930-B or any injury towards 5930-C occur and to maintain his collaboration, he is to be aware of 5930-B location and any experiments conducted on them.

Description: 

SCP-5930 is a Caucasian male of seemingly British origins, it appears to be in his mid-twenties, standing around 175cm in height, with black hair, blue eyes and freckles. The subjects also calls itself Cian, though it refuses to give a last name. The subject does need to be fed on a regular basis. If on good behavior SCP-5930 may request a fruit or sweet for a snack.

It has been noted that it seems to produce a black ink substance at times, mainly when its anomalous qualities are being displayed or during moments of distress or when entering the trance. The substance seems to come out of the pours and other orifices.

5930 has demonstrated to be of a very cheerful and caring nature and enjoys talking with personnel especially those with an understanding on art, despite it's friendly and polite yet very stubborn nature, personnel must note that it suffers from rapid mood swings. It has shown a vast understanding and knowledge on art and history, it demonstrates as well to have a variety of abilities involving art.

For reasons that are currently unknown 5930 will enter a trance like state when harm comes upon 5930-C, causing it to attack the subject that inflicted the harm. 5930 will return to a normal state after ether attacking the subject or if successfully restrained before breaching will return to a normal state after 30 minutes.

SCP-5930 was seen having a brownish-red shoulder bag, referred to as SCP-5930-A that has a message stitched onto the front ''with love, from Dad''. The subject seemingly pulls out various materials needed for any art-based maters, some of these have include a pencil, paint pallets, sewing materials and many more. When personnel researched the contents of the bag it was seemingly empty, the place of origin of the objects are currently unknown. 5930-A is currently stored in a normal locker alongside other safe class items.

SCP-5930 is also seen with a Blue book, named 5930-B, when it was confiscated the subject became very upset and attempted to take 5930-B by force, succeeding, after the retrieval, it calmed down. During an interview, it confessed that the objects is of great value to it and said that the contents of the book were about all the creations and projects that he made. During the viewing of 5930-B, many different drawings and concepts where documented, when questioned on the location of the creations, 5930 refused to answer. The subject is currently allowed to keep 5930-B.

SCP-5930 anomalous properties take form during any art-based maters. It will create paintings, toys or sculptures out of various materials and then attempt to give life to the creation by extracting the pigment from surrounding objects and living organisms, leaving the area with grey monochromatic colors, during research it was observed that despite of the removal of colors from living beings they continue showing signs of being alive. The entity has state that after a while the pigment will return to its natural state, this occurrence has yet to be documented. After the extraction it will then pass the colors to the creation, referred to as 5930-C.

Instances of 5930-C replicate real organisms in behavior and appearance and depending on 5930 emotional state the creation will harbor similar feelings, making them hostile or docile. Though it’s been noted that Sculptures tend to be much more hostile than the sewn creations. 5930 mentioned that its creations are made of its feelings, that since it can't control its emotions, he puts a part of it into 5930-C.

The sculptures and statues are very resilient and hard to destroy compared to other creations. Some 5930-C have demonstrated the ability to reconstruct themselves and have also displayed anomalous effects on people, some of the effects being mild euphoria or extreme levels of Paranoia. 5930 has the ability to command instances of 5930-C and they also have shown to be willing to put themselves into harm’s way to protect their creator, though 5930 does not enjoy that.

Addendum: Discovery 

+block

–block

SCP-5930 was discovered in ████, England in an abandoned art studio, after several reports of local residents about the wild life and plants in the area seemingly having lost their colors. A few agents where sent to investigate the situation, upon entering the studio it was seemingly very dark, yet was reported to be full of mannequins and sculptures they seemingly attacked the agents. The instances of 5930-C, where destroyed and SCP-5930 was located in a room in the studio awaiting the Agents. The agents stated that it greeted them and stated that he was wondering when they would find him, SCP-5930 was willingly apprehended and located to the nearest site until further notice.

Interview with SCP-5930 

+block

–block

Interviewed:  SCP-5930

Interviewer:  Dr. ███ Schmidt

 

Dr. Schmidt:  Good Day SCP-5930.

SCP-5930:  Hello Doctor, what brings you to my room today? You certainly enjoy visiting me~

Dr. Schmidt:  Don't start that againYou know exactly what where doing here.

Dr. Schmidt:  Well, SCP-5930 (SCP-5930 proceeds to interrupt Dr. Schmidt)

SCP-5930:  Its Cian. Calling someone by numbers is a bit rude.

Dr. Schmidt:  I cannot do that. Well lets starts the questions.

Dr. Schmidt:  Why don't you tell me about yourself, where did you come from?

SCP-5930:  I'm from England, i think. Well I did grow up there, though I don't recall of when I was born. What year was it, again? I believe it was a good time before what you people call the Renaissance period, I was already traveling around at that time, learning and exploring. But the area I can't recall I passed a good time stuck inside that room she used to put me in

Dr. Schmidt:  She? Could you elaborate?

SCP-5930:  Oh that's my Mother. I had a family while growing up, two parents and a brother

Dr. Schmidt:  Could you specify about that room?

SCP-5930:  (No response)

Dr. Schmidt:  SCP-5930?

(SCP-5930 stays quiet for a while)

SCP-5930:  Well, she would put me and my brother into that very dark room, all we could do was draw and create. Sometimes she would come back and make us drink a weird black substanceand other things too.

(SCP-5930 looks to the side avoiding eye contact)

Dr. Schmidt:  Well let's change the subject, shall we?

SCP-5930:  Alright

Dr. Schmidt:  Could demonstrate how you're powers work?

(The subject immediately appears to change in mood)

SCP-5930:  Sure I can do that! All i need are art materials!

(A guard proceeds to give SCP-5930 a paper and a pen, the subjects proceed to draw a very detailed butterfly)

Dr. Schmidt:  And now you're going to bring it to life?

SCP-5930:  Correct!

(SCP-5930 absorbs the color of the table and pen and lays both hands on the drawing, producing an Ink like substance in the process. After a while a blue Butterfly appears out of the drawing and proceeds to fly around the room)

Dr. Schmidt:  That is impressive

Dr. Schmidt:  If you don't mind me asking but how did you're powers come to be?

SCP-5930:  I rather not answer (The subject moves its gaze to the table, avoiding eye contact)

Dr. Schmidt:  Well I believe it’s time to conclude this interview, SCP-5930 thank you for your collaboration.

SCP-5930:  (Incoherent mumbling) It was her

 

Closing Statement:  SCP-5930 was returned to its cell and the butterfly seemingly disappeared.

Interviewer note: Any future interviews conducted with SCP-5930, should attempt to gain more information about his ''parents'' and past

Incident-1 

+block

–block

During the afternoon of the 12/05/19██, SCP-5930 was reported missing by the guards, stating ''That it had vanished from thin air in its containment unit''. There was no attempted breach through the containment unit’s door nor glass panel, an investigation was started to attempt to locate SCP-5930 in Site-19, where its currently housed. 1 hour after its disappearance he was found back in its containment cell. When questioned on its wear-about the subject stated that:

''I was lacking the necessary materials for a certain art piece that I was planning so I went out to get some, turns out the store was missing what I needed, so I had to go to a different store. Sorry for taking so long :)''

After incident-1, security measures where increased and SCP-5930 was relocated to its current containment unit.

It’s being theorized that SCP-5930 might be able to create portals while drawing on the containment units’ walls, which he was allowed to do prior to the incident, though this event has to yet be documented.

Incident 2 

+block

–block

During testing with SCP-5930, Dr.████, was presented with 5930-C-5, which was confiscated by the guards relocated to a containment unit where other instances of 5930-C are kept. In the following days Dr.████ experimented with 5930-C-5 but during testing Dr.████ failed to take the necessary precautions and led to the termination of SCP-5930-C-5.

In the following days SCP-5930 was seen making an instance of 5930-C labelled 5930-C-6, who were constructed using clay. When interacting with the subject, he seemed to be in a trance like state. After the completion of the sculptures, they were commanded to attack personnel, during the commotion caused, SCP-5930 was observed, through security cameras, to wander around until locating Dr.████ and attacking him. Mobile Task Forces located SCP-5930 and successfully tranquilized it returning it to its cell, the instance of 5930-C was contained. Dr.████ didn't survive the attack. When Dr. Schmidt went to interview SCP-5930on what had occurred, the subject was found sitting on its bed weeping and mumbling incoherently in different languages, a few words where deciphered, these are the following:

''I'm sorry'' - In English

''Why did I do it?'' - In Italian

''Why did she make me like this?'' - In German

''I didn't mean to hurt you Doctor'' - In German

After the incident it had to be revised as to what SCP-5930 is allowed to create, the subject is no longer allowed the utilize clay or other similar materials to create Statues or Sculptures.
