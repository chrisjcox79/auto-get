SCP Concept: Guitar Hero

29 Dec 2020 04:44

Seeking Greenlights:  Yes

Page Type:  SCP Article

Genre (Optional):  Horror

Page Layout (Optional):  Standard layout

Elevator Pitch:  SCP-XXXX consists of two components, SCP-XXXX-1 which is a seemingly normal electric guitar and SCP-XXXX-2 which is an also seemingly normal amplifier. However, SCP-XXXX-2 has a unique connector only compatible with SCP-XXXX-1 that is unable to be unplugged on SCP-XXXX-2’s end. There is also no external power in the form of an electrical cable or battery compartment, but the power level seems to be associated with a colored light next to the connector port.

Central Narrative:  SCP-XXXX-1 first appears to its victim at random. It appears to be an ordinary guitar, but when played it gives its victim talent far beyond their own, allowing them to play any song, chord, or note by simply thinking of it. In addition, SCP-XXXX-1’s strings release an endorphin-like substance referred to as SCP-XXXX-ɑ that, when absorbed through the victim’s fingers, releases a feeling of euphoria and an increased desire to use SCP-XXXX-1. If the person tries it, after several days SCP-XXXX-2 will appear in the same manner as SCP-XXXX-1.

+block

–block

If SCP-XXXX-1 is ignored, rather than SCP-XXXX-2 appearing, SCP-XXXX-1 will disappear before revealing itself to a new victim. If SCP-XXXX-1 is plugged into SCP-XXXX-2, SCP-XXXX-2 seems to enter an active state. After a seemingly random number of uses, SCP-XXXX-2 will suddenly attack the victim, striking them in the throat with its connector. It lodges into the throat below the vocal cords. SCP-XXXX-2 will then broadcast the inner thoughts of the victim. It will attempt to draw out thoughts that are embarrassing or hurtful, and will then feed off the negative emotions of the host. The light on SCP-XXXX-2 will gradually change from its current state to a bright green as it absorbs emotions, and when it has fully fed off the victim and is fully charged, the adapter will be removed and the victim left to bleed out as both objects teleport away to repeat the cycle. It is currently unknown where or how SCP-XXXX-2 uses stored energy and needs to recharge between victims. In extreme cases, the light flashes a deep red and SCP-XXXX-2 has been known to attack seemingly without activation by SCP-XXXX-1 in a desperate attempt to sustain itself. It is currently unknown what will happen if SCP-XXXX-2 is completely drained of power, and until containment procedures are considered adequate any experimentation on that subject has been banned by the Foundation for fear of any of SCP-XXXX's unknown anomalous effects.

Hook/Attention-Grabber:  The SCP chooses victims based on interaction, and simply ignoring it is enough to spare oneself from falling victim to it. The Safe-class component of the object is used to attract and serve as bait for the Euclid-class portion of the SCP. It is capable of teleportation, and so containment proves difficult as someone, usually D-class personnel, must routinely interact with SCP-XXXX-1 which will inherently draw SCP-XXXX-2 to it, but the two must never be allowed to be used together. When SCP-XXXX-2 appears in SCP-XXXX-1's containment chamber, any personnel in the room are to be immediately evacuated and anyone who had contact with SCP-XXXX-1 is to be put into quarantine while a containment team returns SCP-XXXX-2 to its original containment.
