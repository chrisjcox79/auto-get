So, I'm seeing the initial downvotes happening, and I can honestly see why. But then I looked in here and saw a lack of anyone commenting alongside, and I wish they would. That's why I'm going to comment myself, even though I normally just passively read on this site.

Through the tonal issues and various other problems I can't quite nail down in words myself, I can see a story here that I like. A desire to return to simpler times, put into practice with unforeseen consequences. An anomaly which was, in a sense, its own first victim, and is gradually becoming aware of this. An artist constrained by the rules of their own creation even as they start to see the flaws - but they're committed now, they can't unmake what was made. A living story written in permanent ink, mistakes and all.

I really wouldn't want to see this concept pass quietly into deletion just because it doesn't  the modern Foundation. I really hope some more experienced critics than me decide to weigh in on this.

As a general note, if a piece is dipping into negative ratings, reviewers may prefer to provide feedback elsewhere so said reviews aren't lost if the author decides to delete the page themselves. Staff running deletions save the feedback given in the announcement thread, but it's often more efficient to have a dedicated thread for discussing how to edit a draft, when there isn't the added pressure of the rating module or deletion timer.

Reviewers who provide feedback on mainsite posts in the negatives are much appreciated, but they're doing a favor more than fulfilling an obligation. Author, you're definitely free to contact reviewers to ask for feedback on this post, but you'll likely get more interest in the chat and forums.
