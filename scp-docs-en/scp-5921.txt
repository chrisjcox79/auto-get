                        

Item #:  SCP-5921

Object Class:  Keter

Special Containment Procedures:  Scp 5921 should be contained in a 13x13x13 foot containment cell lined with Cognito-hazard blocking materiels, with a pentagram painted with the blood of humans below it. No personnel are allowed to enter the chamber unless under the effect of Cognito-hazard blocking materials or Scps. should any Sapient Scps breach containment, Guards are to establish a perimeter around Scp 5921 and not allow any personnel within the perimeter until the breach has been contained. Should Scp 5921 breach containment, liquid Gallium should be applied to the armor until the wearer is visible. Once the wearer is visible, the wearer should be terminated, and the armor should be moved back to containment

Description:  is a set of blood-red armor with gold-colored embellishments, and ram horns protruding from the helmet. When fully upright, Scp 5921 is 8.7 feet tall, and carries a broadsword 7 feet in length. The armor and the sword are made of an unknown metallic substance that has a tensile strength of 150 Gigapascals, and a melting point of 6200 degrees Fahrenheit. The armor suffers an effect similar to Aluminum when exposed to Gallium, resulting in a drastic loss in tensile strength, to the point where standard Foundation firearms can pierce the armor. The entity’s effect is dulled by pentagrams painted with human blood. The armor can regenerate from any portion of its armor. Scp 5921 exerts a form of mind control on all sapient humanoids within a 30-Foot radius that draws them to enter the chamber and put on the armor, which will change it’s interior to fit the wearer. Scp 5921 will then completely take over the brain and attempt to breach containment and go on a killing spree. Scp 5921 will be capable of talking and will speak in a deep male voice, regardless of the subject’s gender, previous voice pitch, or ability to speak. Scp 5921 is fully sapient and will converse with foundation personnel once it realizes it is trapped, attempting to persuade them to free it. If a subject is removed from Scp 5921 before 30 minutes, they will be able to revert to their previous personality, though as removing the subject from Scp 5921 will usually result in significant loss of Foundation security personnel, the wearer is usually terminated. Scp 5921 was discovered in a crypt, buried with a warrior named William The Brutal, after reports of a walking suit of armor murdering anyone it saw surfaced in Fordwich, England, in 1988. MTF Epsilon 6 “Village Idiots” was assigned to recon and capture of the entity. They encountered Scp 5921 sitting in the crypt, polishing its sword, when it became aware of the team’s presence, and proceed to attack. As Standard weapons proved ineffective, an Anti-Tank missile was discharged, subduing the entity and allowing the Foundation to secure and transfer the Scp to site ███.
