SPECIAL CONTAINMENT MEASURES 

SCP-5267 is to be marked on all maps as a military base, and all satellite images taken of the area are to be altered according to Procedure Rohndrey-761

photograph of SCP-5267-Prime

Any civilians approaching SCP-5267 are to be brought into Foundation custody and dosed with a Class-A amnestic. After dosage, civilians are to be returned to the nearest city to SCP-5267, █████.

Due to its nature, SCP-5267-Prime cannot be transferred to a site location.

Prime’s tunnel entrance and central entrance is enclosed with a ten-inch-plated steel door, 8 sections of the tunnel are guarded by 3 MTF personnel, each section is armed with firepower, etc.

The landmass above is barred by security measures, and are monitored by personnel on duty, a coverup story has been placed to adhere to normalcy.

Inside Prime multiple foundation facilities are constructed to assess groundwork which is easily secured by electrified palisade fences outside a two-kilometer-radius, these fences are installed to simply ward off entities from foundation operation.

Outside the 2-kilometer-radius Prime would be checked for hostiles daily, Mobile Task Force-Kilo Tango-22 (Dire_Wolves) has been formed for this duty.

MTF-Kilo Tango-22 (Dire_wolves)

uniform-logo

SCP-5267-A assaults are to be treated by on-site medical personnel using medication prescribed in Medical Chart 5267-A-1. In extreme cases in which an infection is at risk, amputation of affected limbs is authorized.

In the event of specimens of SCP-5267-A becoming hostile, security personnel is to respond immediately and subdue/electrocute specimens in a non-fatal manner, or simply neutralized depending on severity, Any SCP-5267-A creatures that are captured, alive or dead, are to be loaded into class 3 BCU storage containers to await transport to either site-36 or site-91, where appropriate staff will take over the operation.

Prime’s walls are covered with a set of cameras 3 meters apart and, another line, 3 meters above, after the breach2 to secure monitorization all cameras had been installed with firearms and software-███-56, all individual organisms are to be tagged by a collar complete with a tracker and a self destruct sequence.

If any civilian is caught near SCP-5267-Prime, they are to be brought in for questioning and are to be administered Class-B or Class-C amnestics at the discretion of the on-site supervisor. The civilian in question will then be transported off-site to their most recent dwelling.

No research personnel is to enter SCP-5267-Prime without entering Maintenance-Tunnel-a63 leading to the designated facility with an escort of two (2) members of security. Six (20) maintenance personnel are to be on duty on Foundation facilities. SCP-5267-Prime is to be checked for breaches daily. In the event of a breach, SCP-5267-Prime all personnel would enter the foundation facilities to gain weaponry leverage.

inside SCP-5267-Prime, multiple foundation facilities were established to accommodate the population SCP-5267-A.

SCP-5267-ECHO will be monitored by, and camped by Kilo-tango-22, all reports about ECHO will be submitted to senior staff, appropriate measures are still under-developed.

SCP-5267-A-1, is contained in a 5 by 3 by 5-meter containment chamber, a water bowl and a food bowl are fixed to the floor, above is a faucet, this pours a mixture of compound B315-L and the other essentials, such as dog food and cat food, with no handle, opened and closed only by personnel manually from the observer’s room.

Cleaning would be done five times a week, a high potency tranquilizer dart will be shot from the observer’s room when SCP-5267-A-1 falls, a minimum of 3 D-class personnel will enter and immediately bind the SCP-5267-A-1’s limbs and mouth, one D-class would watch SCP-5267-A-1 for movement, if SCP-5267-A-1 does appear conscious the D-class would electrocute SCP-5267-A-1, until unconscious, the other D-class would sanitize the area from feces urine among other things, once done the D-class would leave the chamber, and SCP-5267-A-1 left still in bounds.

Discipline attempts are made by kilotango-22 representatives. Discipline procedures by kilo tango-22 are conducted every 48 hours, SCP-5267-A-1 was found by a group of researchers in SCP-5267-Prime, upon discovery SCP-5267-A-1 was found to had killed a non-Foundation-researcher and leaving another severely wounded, the surviving researchers were hired after interviews by Dr. Mercer, captivation of SCP-5267-A-1 was fairly easy said by the MTF captain.

Using meat on disciplinary attempts are authorized.

Specimens of SCP-5267-B are stored in a standard storage room.

All specimens of SCP-5267-C are to be sealed in an airlock case to prevent its influence on typical plant life until reaching a biohazardous facility, all are done with care by foundation staff with /3/clearance and above.

DESCRIPTION 

SCP-(redacted)-Prime is a subterranean cavity of a Holocene ecosystem with a land area of

59.1B.C.E. on carbon dating, its main biome is a Temperate Broadleaf Mixed Forest.

cave entrance/photograph was taken prior discovery of SCP-5267-Prime

Prime contains instances of neutral to hostile creatures, crystals, and plants,

all plants and animals of which are dependent on the compound B315-L,

for no sustainable source of water (H2O) was discovered

instead, several craters filled with the compound B315-L

the plant of which shows no anomalous behavior other than producing compound B315-L through their roots regularly, these compounds are used to aid bodies in the way water is to normal anatomies, and to alter normal cells into highly instructive B315-L-induced-cells in a span of 7 days. These altered cells have unique abilities to self-renew and to recreate functional tissues. B315-L-induced-cells can self-renew stem cells indefinitely, which are mainly stored in bone marrow, a known concentration of induced cells reside in the mandible cavity which was enlarged 150% as the requirement for B315-L-induced-cells to develop

when induced by the compound-B315-L the effect is irreversible.

photograph of SCP-5267-B

SCP(redacted)-B are crystals that live on the inorganic hides of dead creatures susceptible to the compound, they illuminate up to 3000lux, in a range of colors, but the color most commonly seen is a light-yellowish tint.

The crystals glow light of 2000 lux on an average day, which is 15 hours, the next 15 hours of the light's absence is the second cycle in which light has rarely been shown by the crystals, giving Prime a night and day cycle of approximately 30 hours.

The cavity houses a self-sustaining ecosystem with a population of reptiles, insects, amphibians, but thrives the most are the mammals.

The majority of affected entities are considered docile, as the animals are rather predictable, other than that, active creatures who roam around the vicinity of Prime are fairly easy to monitor.

Insects are the only creatures without an effect from the compound but they thrive nonetheless.

photograph of a B315-L river within SCP-XXXX-ECHO

SCP-5267-ECHO is a cavity found beside Prime, ECHO contains a Late-Mesozoic ecosystem, and has an approximate space of 695,662km². It has been structured with an unidentified type of lining that gave ECHO formidable structural integrity in which also made foundation equipment no ability to detect it, the light inside ECHO Was estimated from 50,000 lux-120,000 lux at an average estimate on ECHO's day.

ECHO's crystals were known to emanate an orange hue which made the temperature in ECHO higher than Prime to sustain the reptilian population.

ECHO has been identified to support a population of previously extinct species of reptile, as indicated on a recorded account of multiple  that were recovered at the recorded incident4 which were known to be dependent on the compound.

ECHO's biome has been suggested as tropical rainforest and desert, with multiple states of grassland inhabited mainly by reptilians, The estimated size of ECHO is 877.987 km2.

SCP-5267-A are individuals/creatures that are subsequently mutated by the compound, Normally SCP-5267-A uses their highly-induced-cells,5 they produce from their bone marrow to regenerate themselves of mutate according to their will and supply, a concentration of these cells are located inside their mandibles, which had been enlarged for this purpose.

No adverse effects were shown through an overdose of compound B315-L.

Instances of SCP-XXXX-A do not age normally and, if kept with sustenance and a productive psychological condition, they will live on indefinitely.

photograph of SCP-5267-C

SCP-5267-A-16, is a dark coated, 1.94-meter Lynx-rufus (bobcat), as induced by compound B315-L, SCP-5267-A-1 possesses the distinct lower jaw approximately the size of its skull with protruding canines from the mandible, with the mandible itself is roughly 250% the original size, SCP-5267-A-1 possesses four ears, two sets of the skull and two on the mandible, the specimen is highly adaptable, as it has been observed to mutate at will, yet it is limited by the amount of sustenance given, but the ability for it to do so has been inconsistent.

SCP-5267-C are compound-B315-L altered plants that create compound B315-L and spreads from organic hides to normal soil. They have also been known to influence other species of flora through spore contamination in an effort that other plants may gain this ability, they use a variety of inconsistent external molecules to create the compound B315-L, yet no matter how trivial the external molecules they incorporate, the byproduct7 will always be manufactured with indisputable quality
