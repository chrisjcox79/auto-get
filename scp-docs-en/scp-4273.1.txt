Junior Staff - Site Crit

26 Jan 2020 00:37

Hello! I'm MalyceGraves and I'll be performing a site crit review of this piece today.

+Crit

-Crit

Overall

I think that some of the problems that you're running into here is that it's simply not that interesting. The bulk of the writing isn't bad, just it's a story that doesn't really go anywhere. The narrative arc of a group of trees that become more aggressive is meh.

I think that you have ground here to build a more complete narrative, but as it stands this is simply a thing what does a thing with a few more steps.

SCP-4273 entities demonstrate a competent level of mimicry when observing human behaviour.
附加记录：    

I saw this and I was expecting more of this. I was disappointed when I didn't really see anything that really speaks to this. Maybe a bit when the neutralized instance shrank in fear, but that was about it.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
SPaG

It's clear that you've gone over this a bunch and fixed any gross errors. That's a good thing.

in the presence of Dr.Allegre since the second week of January.
附加记录：    

Missing a space after Dr.

These changes are often accompanied by the restructuring of the trunk, repositioning of branches and growth of additional roots.
附加记录：    

Oxford comma. Also, re-positioning.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
Consistency/Flow

They will not engage in activities that they perceive to be harmful to their well being or other plant life.
附加记录：    

Consider: They will not engage in activities that they perceive to be harmful to themselves, or to the well-being of other forms of plant life. This indicates an awareness of themselves as part of a greater ecosystem, and may connote the existence of feelings of camaraderie.

I suggest the addition as a way to greater showcase their sentience/potential sapience. Self awareness plays a role in that, and I think drawing attention to it adds a bit more to our willingness to  for the plight of what is otherwise a decorative plant.

Pejorative language directed toward the entities will also elicit a negative response.
附加记录：    

Is this a tone thing, or actual understanding of the language being spoken? Many domesticated animals have shown that they are more aware of the tone than of the actual words.

I assume that the instances were forcibly separated between the first video log and the second. There is no link between the two, so it appears as if they're all present, then suddenly there's only one. I think this could be fixed by adding something in the  section.

1437: SCP-4273-1 grows several additional branches and attaches them to Dr. Allegre's arm.
附加记录：    

How do they 

Dr. Allegre switches on the television present within the cell and tunes into [REDACTED],
附加记录：    

Why did you redact that? You could say that it is a live feed, pre-recorded news footage, etc.

Also, modern televisions don't have switches anymore, so I think the more accurate phrasing here would be  seems incredibly archaic in this context.

that could prove sapience if we test further. I'll ask the site manager for a pair of shears.

~Melissa
附加记录：    

I assume this is Dr. Allegre. I also recognize that this is her personal note, but in this context, I believe that signing it Dr. Allegre is more appropriate.

Entity reconstructs itself into [REDACTED] times its original height.
附加记录：    

This seems pointlessly redacted.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
Closing Remarks

I think that one of the things here that I should point out is that  is not referencing a specific type of plant, but rather the practice of producing an end result. I think that that is grounds for very fertile storytelling, and should be utilized.

Example, do the different trees behave differently depending on their original species? Maybe the reason they're so unfriendly towards being sheared is due to some past trauma? Do they have any similarities to the large trees they are supposed to represent? Is there any additional iconography within their tray/pot/whatever?

Furthermore, I think you could explore more about where they came from, how the Foundation got a hold of them. Did they just appear in containment one day? Did someone drop them off? Were they acquired in an estate sale?

Like I said before. The skip isn't poorly written from a technical standpoint, but it leaves a lot out that I don't think that it being streamlined and short is best served by.

I hope that this helps you, not only with this piece, but your future pieces as well.

Ehh..

28 Mar 2020 07:25

I have two main problems with this skip,

The Writing
The Appeal

First, the writing. It feels very stilted and rather rough. None of the sentences flow together well to create a good reading experience. Writing out the skip with sentence structure in mind would have helped here. Near the end of the skip you write about one of the SCP-4273 instances being contained in a pot in the very last recording.

Dr. Allegre manages to knock SCP-4273-1's pot onto the ground, shattering it.
附加记录：    

This is not mentioned in any other part of the text. This sounds like something that would be important to include in the Special Containment Procedures. Speaking of Containment Procedures, I know that short Special Containment Procedures are normally a nitpick, but this is a bit too little.

All SCP-4273 entities are to be contained within a standard botanical containment cell. Each entity is to remain isolated outside of testing purposes.
附加记录：    

There should be more than two short sentences that barely tell us anything. Don't ignore them, they're there for a reason. There's also other instances of you including things that should be in the containment procedures in other parts of the article.

+block

–block

Dr. Allegre enters SCP-4273-2's containment cell and proceeds to water the entity with the provided pail.
附加记录：    

Do the SCP-4273 instances require water? Do they require sunlight? All these questions were most likely not thought of in the rush to get the  details into the story. Think through the basics of your skip before you go for the interesting stuff. There also seems to be a moderate amount of grammatical errors throughout your story, this can be solved with a peer review (preferably 2).

The other SCP-4273 entities grow several dozen branches as their leaves change into a brilliant shade of red.
附加记录：    

Skips aren't Novels  nobody wishing to know about your scp cares about how much imagery is included. Ditch the fancy stuff and get straight to the point if you can.

Secondly, the appeal. What I mean by this is what makes your skip actually interesting. Personally, I find this work to be rather dull. There's nothing that really make's it interesting. There's nothing interesting in the recordings (Why should I care if they're sapient or not). Your special containment procedures are at best poorly thought out and at worst lazy. The overall plot of these plant's being sentient doesn't interest me at all because I've seen this a thousand times over better executed. Overall, your skip is poorly constructed and poorly thought out.

This skip mainly needs more planning out and peer editing. It always helps to have a second set of eyes to look over your work.

Those are my thoughts. Thanks for reading.
