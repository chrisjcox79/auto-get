SCP-XXXX Where the Heart is

22 Dec 2020 21:27

Shared pain and experiences can bring people closer together, who doesn't want that!? Something else wants to attain its former glory - there seem to be some  situations here.

I moved and trimmed your thread summary, since it was taking up extra space in the main forum screen. Try to keep thread descriptions (the text in the little box under the thread title) and titles short so you don't crowd out other authors in the forum listing. ~Zyn

Seeking Greenlights:  Yes

Page Type:  SCP Article

Genre:  Horror?

Elevator Pitch: 

A creature that haunts Christmas as well as Halloween
Object Class: Keter For its innumerable ways of evading capture, enclosures, and personnel
There are three types of encounters (with the main entity specifically), they are:

Black Events: Where the ritualistic consumption of a child is done by the child's family, along with the entity during Halloween
White Events: Causes a family to become closer to each other as serial murders after SCP-XXXX is taken in (while posing as a homeless person)
Red Events: Are when it's outside of a Black or White Event and gouges out the heart of a person(s)

+block

–block

Central Narrative:  The creature was once one of the  - becoming a being that encompasses both holidays. Though this caused it to become even more unstable, now consuming the hearts of 'innocents' to regain its purity in its madness.

Hook/Attention-Grabber:  While the creature seems very violent, it sometimes leaves people better than before: having a better outlook on life and being an overall better person.

Don't let that small chance of salvation fool you though - while its intentions may not be evil, what it does is extremely traumatizing.

Additional Notes:  I did NOT include all of the context/info with these events and capabilities to make it easily digestible in this format.

This is my first time making one of these or if there are any questions :)

As a courtesy to our readers on mobile devices, please collapse long posts and properly space your paragraphs. ~Zyn
