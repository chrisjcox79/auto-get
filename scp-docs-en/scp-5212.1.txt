I think the biggest problem wit this article is its language. It is good that you are going in depth with you idea, but you need to be more informative about your descriptions.

SCP-5212, very little is known about this being, but any attempts to contain or view it are unsuccessful, as its strength is far too great to overpower and open the door.
附加记录：    

The language you used in this sentence does not fit with the informative language that is required for most articles. How does the Foundation know that SCP-5212-2 even exist if they have never seen him. If the door does not open, and attacks only seem to be characterized by tapping, then why would the fondation not just think its the building going that. When writing you need to think that while you know all the info on the creature; the Foundation (who you are writing in the perspective of) does not have that information. Also when you post a article with multiple parts don't make multiple posts just use collapsible text, or any other html code. Overall I think the idea is good but I think you need to make a few more drafts to help Improve your language, and your perspective.
