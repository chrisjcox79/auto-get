Item #:  SCP-5234

Object Class:  Euclid

Special Containment Procedures:  SCP-5234 is to be contained within a small 30x30 centimeter box of volcanic rock. All personnel who transport or interact with SCP-5234 should wear a hazmat suit lined with volcanic rock. The container should not be opened unless the object is needed for testing, requires transport to a new container, or needs to be shortened. There should be no metallic substances located within a ten-meter radius, and in the event of transport between facilities, the object should be carried in a half-meter thick layer of obsidian, with the carriers wearing the aforementioned hazmat suits.

In the event that SCP-5234 grows to a larger size, it is recommended to allow it out of the box into a wooden cell, though this will cause it to grow at an even quicker pace. To avoid this occurrence, it is recommended that the object be checked every week and if longer than 20cm, it should be cut to the nozzle. Do not allow SCP-5234 near any form of plant life, and in the event of such occurrence it is recommended to attempt to relocate it to a cell similar to that of SCP-106, however, this will often result in the cell rapidly rusting and will need repairs or possibly replacement.

In the event of a containment breach, it is recommended that all surrounding plant life be killed. Though manually removing all living plant material still works as a prevention method, the most efficient way discovered is to have SCP-073 anticipate the travel of SCP-5234 and move ahead of it, killing all plant life. Once SCP-5234 has been reduced to a length of at most 10 centimeters, it is recommended that a crew member in a volcanic rock lined hazmat suit transport SCP-5234 back into its case. This length can be reduced by having SCP-5234 within a 5-meter radius of any metal, though this metal will often rust beyond repair. In case of emergency, SCP-5234 can be cut with an obsidian-bladed knife, and the segment separate from the nozzle can be burnt.

Description:  SCP-5234 resembles the nozzle of a common garden hose at its shortest length, but can expand and will most often be seen around 10 centimeters long. The  is near plant life, SCP-5234 will rapidly elongate, and the longest recorded length has been one kilometer . SCP-5234 has been recorded to grow quicker around materials made of formerly organic material, such as wood, but it grows at its fastest rate when near live plants. SCP-5234 grows at its slowest when contained in a container of volcanic rock, with the most efficient type being obsidian.

SCP-5234 has a unique property of rusting any metallic substance within an eight-meter radius. The metal affected by SCP-5234 will rust beyond repair, and SCP-5234 will also shrink at a rate of 5 centimeters per hour in the presence of metal. This effect also applies to explode

When SCP-5234 leaves only the , it will still rust any metallic substances within a 5-meter radius.

SCP-5234 cannot move around when it is less than a meter long, but once SCP-5234 reaches at least a meter long, it will start to slither around like a snake. During movement, SCP-5234 will expand as it slithers, and in a location with an abundance of plant life, it may appear that SCP-5234 is not moving due to the rapid rate of lengthening. SCP-5234 will avoid metal at all costs, and in the event of reaching metal, it will leave a trail of rust around it.

SCP-5234 is not hostile to humans, though it is very dangerous to be around. Due to the effect that SCP-5234 has on metals, SCP-5234 will cause the iron within an organism's bloodstream to deteriorate almost instantly, and they will die in a very short amount of time if they do not receive proper medical attention. It is recommended that all personnel stay as far away from SCP-5234 as possible.
