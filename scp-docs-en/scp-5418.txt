Special Containment Procedures:  SCP-5418 is to be held in the standard humanoid containment chamber. Scranton Reality Anchor device is to be placed within the cell away from the subject’s reach, and three armed guards are to be established outside of the entry point at all time. Only personnel with proper level 4 credential are prohibited from entering the subject’s containment chamber.

UpdatedProcedure

UpdatedProcedure

SCP-5418 is to remain in its designated location. The area is an underground chamber, underneath Altai Tavan Bogd Mountain, the activity towards the Mountains is to be restricted under the guise of Military operation by the Mongolian government. No personnel without a proper security clearance are prohibited from entering the confined area. The underground chamber is to be strictly monitored 24/7 for SCP-5418's activity. The area's perimeter is surrounded by an SRA device to prevent Reality distortion from leaking through the mass populace. Motion censoring device is placed within The underground chamber to locate the subject’s movement if something happens to the anomaly.

No more than five personnel wearing hazmat suits are to enter the fixed location simultaneously to verify the subject's state. Individuals who entered the area interact and talk to the subject if it ever resurfaced and should not stay longer than three days, at the assigned location.

If the subject is seen leaving its location, the entity should be immediately apprehended and questioned. If SCP-5418 refused to cooperate, they must return the entity to its previous containment chamber and wait for further instruction.

Description:  SCP-5418 is a seventeen-year-old male humanoid of Mongolian descent. SCP-5418 introduces itself as Oulen. The entity posses low telekinesis ability and is Type green reality-bender.

SCP-5418 can control a large source of carbon-based compounds found within a natural, terrestrial and aquatic environments. It favours mainly natural segments to distort, restructure and assemble a new reality for its benefit. The entity detests implementing its ability on innocent civilians; however, the subject will go into a hostile state when provoked. Regardless, SCP-5418 is known to be subtle and easily disturbed. Due to the subject's unpredictable nature, it poses an improbable danger to the mass population.

The entity can tear through the shreds of spatial reality to produce an entrance to an unknown dimension. But as of this date, SCP-5418 had only used its aforementioned ability once. More information on the occurrence of this reality-bending ability is yet to be determined as testing is currently not feasible.

Discovery:  SCP-5418 was found within its apartment, when MTF agents raided the establishment after an extensive investigation of reality distortion reports across Khan Uul district, Mongolia. The subject fled the premises by distorting the dimension of the apartment. Based upon MTF agents' information, the entity changed the residents into the hallway, and the subject ran through it. The agents reported, “feeling of not advancing as if walking on a treadmill” while trying to capture the entity.

Due to the distortion of reality, agents had to use an SRA device to block its anomalous ability; upon activation, the apartment reverted to its previous appearance. The subject presumably knocked unconscious during its attempt to flee the vicinity and was taken into custody without any further incident.

Since its containment, SCP-5418's attempt at gaining control over its power had been thwarted by Dr Fox, and special training has been approved to subdue it.

Addendum 5418.1:  Upon being brought to the site ██, SCP-5418 caused an accidental reality distortion when entering the interview room1. SCP-5418 had been brought back to its containment to be questioned.

INTERVIEW LOG 
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Interviewed:  SCP-5418

Interviewer:  Head Researcher and Director of Site ██ Dr Natalia Fox
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

SCP-5418 is seen sitting across the table from Dr Fox with a handcuff.

Dr. Fox:  Hello. I’m Head Researcher Dr Natalia Fox, and I’ll be talking to you today.

SCP-5418:  (Stammers in heavy accent) H-Hello. M-My name is Oulen.

Dr. Fox:  Oh, you seem to know English a lot.

SCP-5418:  I-I uh, I was learning English. Um, I hope you can understand.

Dr. Fox:  You're doing fine. Now please tell me when you learned about your ability.

SCP-5418:  Um, it's been a couple of months now, I first learned about it when I was at school, trying to concentrate but it wasn’t working so I just started daydreaming I realized there was snow falling inside the class. My teacher wasn’t present, so I guessed it was her doing it as a special effect for the upcoming Christmas celebration. Later I was walking with my friends when I saw a dead bird. I picked it up and tried to bring it back, but it was already dead, so I placed it back on the ground when the bird started to turn. It came back. I brought it back. That’s when I learned about this ability.

Dr. Fox:  I see, So your friends had seen the instance of your ability. Thanks for telling me that.

SCP-5418:  Is that a bad thing?

Dr. Fox:  Yes. The knowledge of your anomalous ability would be too much for your friends.

SCP-5418:  Why would that be? I know them, they won't tell anyone about me.

Dr. Fox:  That is a chance we can't take XXXX.

SCP-5418:  Can I at least leave?

Dr. Fox:  No, 5418, you will not be able to leave. You’re dangerous to outside people we are protecting. We even safeguarding you, from them as well.

SCP-5418:  Should I be thankful for that?

Dr. Fox:  You can think whatever you want as long as your not hurting anyone.

SCP-5418:  What will happen to me? Will, I able to do whatever I want inside the cell? Can I roam around the facility? I'm somewhat claustrophobic.

Dr. Fox:  We'll see what we can do with the claustrophobia, so you don't have to worry about that. The second, you wouldn't roam around the facility as it is dangerous to you and others. We need you to stay inside your containment chamber.

SCP-5418 nods.

-Close

Before its containment, SCP-5418 studied at █████ High School, ███████████. Due to the classmates and civilian around SCP-5418 that witnessed its reality-bending ability, they had been tracked and administered Class-A amnestic. Any information involving SCP-5418 has been confiscated and erased from every database.

INTERVIEW LOG 
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Interviewed:  SCP-5418

Interviewer:  Head Researcher and Director of Site ██ Dr Natalia Fox
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Dr. Fox:  How have you been 5418?

SCP-5418:  Good. Umm, they gave me a book. To be honest, I'm not much of a reader. It's kind of lonely inside the cell.

Dr. Fox:  Your English have improved. I guess the book has been good help?

SCP-5418:  Sure, its the only form of entertainment inside these four walls. Also, the guards outside are scared of me, won't even talk to me.

Dr. Fox:  Of course, you are dangerous.

SCP-5418:  Why? What is it about me that is dangerous? Is it because of my power?

Dr. Fox:  (nodding) Yes. Your ability is potent; its enough to rewrite the whole concept of reality.

SCP-5418:  Whoa, that's something. Wait, you won’t kill me, right?

Dr. Fox:  We will see, it depends. I want to ask you a couple of questions. How was your home life before containment? In your private file said your parent died of a car crash.

SCP-5418:  Yeah they died when I was nine or ten. I was in the car at that time when the accident happened. I don’t remember much, really.

Dr. Fox:  Who did you live with after their death?

SCP-5418:  Lived with my uncle. He was a drunken man, and well, he hated me. He used to leave alone basically for days, leaving a small amount of food to um, feed me.

Dr. Fox:  I understand, based on the report, he died three days before your containment. What were you doing at that time?

SCP-5418:  Studying, trying to live without a guardian. My mom and dad both had left me money and adding on with my uncle's income. I was pretty rich for my age.

SCP-5418 giggles and Dr Fox clears her throat.

SCP-5418:  Sorry about that.

Dr. Fox:  Tell me about your parent, were they nice?

SCP-5418:  Well, they were alright, a bit energetic.

Dr. Fox:  I see. I have been observing you for a while now, and it seems you tend to create a carbon-based life from nothing, why is that?

SCP-5418:  I love nature, the fresh air and the wide land's silence are very calming and refreshing for me. When I get out, I'll immediately travel to Mount Bodg and climb the top and scream from the top of my lung. It is a promise.

Dr. Fox:  Right. It sounds like you want to leave. That is not possible, and you will stay here until there's nothing to learn from your ability, then we’ll see whatever you can leave.

SCP-5418:  I doubt that will ever happen to be honest.

Dr. Fox:  (Sighs) Alright, I think this is enough for today.

SCP-5418:  Goodbye.

Dr. Fox leaves the room.

-Close

Before its containment, SCP-5418 had been living in the negligent household. Due to this, the causable explanation on the subject ability is currently being investigated.

SCP-5418 and the containment cell returned to its location about an hour after it disappeared, but without Dr Fox. The subject proceeded to manifest a bird features resembling an eagle approximately 8,5m in size and flew to Mountain Altai Tavan Bogd in the straight sector.

Between 8 am to 8pm, the ground shook in minor 10 decibels until a cave's entry point had formed on the mountain's skirt.

Subsequently, MTF-Omega-12 entered the cave and explored the unknown sinkhole. They found entirely independent ecosystems made from reality restoration with a high amount of Hume2. About three hours into the exploration, they found SCP-5418 waiting for them outside of what it seems to be a suburban cabin.

Closing statement:  The MTF-Omega-12 returned to the base and expressed uproar towards the agreement, as said before SCP-5418 will keep it's a promise as long as we keep ours. Anyone opposed to this notion will be reassigned to a different project.

Dr Fox returns to our reality within 5 hours, with minor psychological damage and bodily wounds. She had been sent to the Foundation rehabilitation centre to recover. SCP-5418 agreed to remain in the underground chamber it created; however, it did not want to be visited and ignored the researchers when establishing communication.

Subsequently, on 3/02/2021 SCP-5418 went permanently silent and ceased to be visible inside of the cave. No thermal imagery shows its current location; thus, no communication devices had worked since the cave's inception. Regardless SCP-5418 still resides inside the cave as motion sensor revealed the subject roaming the location in pattern. Albeit the tracking device placed within SCP-5418’s hand had worked significantly less than as expected, the only way to track the subject is when it's inactive during the nights.
