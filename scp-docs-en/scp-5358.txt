                        

Item Number:  SCP-5358

Object Class:  Euclid

Anomaly Type:  Humanoid, Cooperative

Containment Procedures:  SCP-5358 is to be contained in a standard HO4 Personnel room (with office attached), Given Level-2 Clearance and allowed to roam freely at on-site as long as it does not attempt to leave the facility, or access any devices that have access to the internet or foundation interweb. (unless explicit permission is given) SCP-5358 may be provided with a closed network “practice” computer for it to practice its abilities on.

Description:  SCP-5358 is a human male about twenty-four (24) years in age, and standing about 1.8 meters in height. Medical checkups so far have found no physical anomalies on the internal and external body of SCP-5358. IQ tests have given it a slightly above-average intelligence level of 116.

SCP-5358 Has the anomalous ability to hack into almost any computer system, the only known way to stop SCP-5358 is [DATA EXPUNGED, SECURITY THREAT]. SCP-5358 has been utilized as a foundation asset and given Level 2 clearance.

Addendum 5358-A:  Recovery

SCP-5358 was assigned an investigation team after several high-profile information leaks and attempted white-hat hacking. At [DATE REDACTED] the FBI UIU reported a virtual breach of security. After that point foundation agents tracked down and located SCP-5358, which at the time was in a farmhouse in Wilmington, Delaware. After containment and investigation of its abilities SCP-5358 showed high cooperation [See addendum 5358-B]

Addendum 5358-B:  SCP-5358 has been accepted into MTF Rho-9 “technical support” And given Level-2 Clearance because of its extreme technical abilities and continued observed good behavior.
