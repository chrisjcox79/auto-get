                        

Item #:  SCP 5192

Object Class:  Safe

Special Containment Procedures: 

SCP 5192-1 are to be monitored 24 hours a day to prevent the release of SCP 5192-A1. If personal notice releasing of SCP 5192-A1 they are immediate to incinerate SCP 5192-A.

If personal fail to incinerate SCP 5192-A within 5 minutes 3 Class-D personals are to be brought to the containment chamber of SCP 5192 as a sacrifice.

Description: 

SCP 5192 consists of SCP 5192-1 and SCP 5192-2. SCP 5192-1

appear to be multiple cows with carnivorous nutrition. Their appearance consists of a thick layer of crimson fur and long pairs of horns. The subjects appear to be extremely hostile to every form of life. Their height is approximately 1.92m while their width is approximately 1.5 meters. Among instances of SCP 5192-1 stands out SCP 5192-A which is known as . The mother's appearances consist of a crimson corrosive substance and a pair of violet glowing horns. The mother is approximately 2.5 meters tall and 3 meters wide. She seems to be resistant to the explosives, incineration, and regular gunfire. The mother is as well incapable of moving due to its missing limbs. The mother will attempt to lure its victims in by releasing a substance known as SCP 5192-A1. The gas will then proceed to spread around the area within a period of 1 hour. When its victims inhale the substance they will begin unconsciously walking towards SCP 5192-2. SCP 5192-2 is a cube-like shaped pit containing instances of SCP 5192-1. The area doesn't allow instances of SCP 5192-1 or any living creature which enters its range to leave. Once the victim is in SCP 5192-2 the instances will then proceed to bring them to the mother. The mother will then proceed to pour an unknown substance onto the victim. Once the mother is finished pouring the substance the victim will begin melting into a fleshy cube. The instances will then proceed to feast on it. One piece of the food is left to the mother to consume. After consuming the remainings piece of flesh will then fall off the mother and begin developing into another instance of SCP 5192-1.

Notes: 

It has been discovered that the effectiveness of the substance gets better with the capacity of SCP 5192-1 increasing. To decrease the effectiveness of SCP 5192-A1 the personal are to terminate every new instance of SCP 5192-1. It has been proven as well that incinerating SCP 5192-A stops the spreading of substance.
