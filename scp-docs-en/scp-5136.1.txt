It seems like this article isn't going to survive very long, but I'm going to try and point out a thing that, for me, could have been a very easy improvement in the writing style.

show

hide

One of the clearest markers of poor writing in this article, for me at least, was the insufficient use of full stops. Every single paragraph in this article is a single sentence. This means there are a lot of extremely long sentences here, which readers can't keep themselves focused on. Beyond that, it also means that in places where paragraphs are fairly uniform in length, such as the interview, sentences are also very uniform. This, to put it bluntly, is boring. It's a type of repetition, and unconscious repetition is very bad for your writing.

In the future, I'd advise mostly writing paragraphs composed of 2-4 sentences, and varying the length of those sentences in order to not bore your audience.

I hope this piece of advice can help you write better in your next article - good luck!
