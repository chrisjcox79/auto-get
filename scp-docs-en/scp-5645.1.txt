28 Nov 2020 14:41

There are a number of problems here, but the primary one is that this is merely a description.

SCPs are speculative fiction stories presented in the form of archival documents. Some are vignettes that paint an evocative scene over the course of a few paragraphs, some are sweeping multiple act novellas with twisting plots, some tell their stories through the ways that they toy with the framing itself but at its core, every SCP is a story. That is the point of an SCP.

Very interesting idea, but there are a few logical plot holes.

These noises include memetic kill-words such as “Cekliption”, “Diffechindic”, “Humplionicaned”, etcetera.
附加记录：    

Why would you specifically spell out memetic kill agents in a document? Even if its a LVL5 one like you stated, that could count as something very volatile and dangerous.

I feel like you could really expand upon this concept - why does this SCP display the Berryman-Langford agent specifically if the agent is so volatile and used for one of the Foundation's most buried secrets? Why is it spreading it around? Is it trying to inoculate people against SCP-001's safety protocols? Is it trying to use them to kill people? I really hope that this gets a rewrite because I love the concept and it has so much potential.
