The core idea here is definitely viable. Classic vanilla killer monsters are hard to pull off, but the anomaly is a good one to carry something like that. Ditch the targeting of people who know about it and the sound-trigger thing; this thing only needs the one party trick. It's a solid one.

I'm just going to do a rough breakdown here, if you take this to the forums for an overhaul and second try (and you should) PM me and I'll do a more in-depth one. I'm being a little blunt here because I think this is a strong idea but it will only work with a total overhaul.

The surface problems are tone breaks and bad formatting - scare italics in the Containment procedures, overdramatic statements of how dangerous it is, directly addressing the reader, hard-to-read paragraph organization lots of little things that draft critique can help you address.

More than that though, you're totally missing the mark on what makes this thing a good monster, and how to present it. There's a lot here telling us we should be afraid of this thing, and not nearly enough showing us any of it. Tied into that, you also show your cards way, way too early, especially with regard to the strongest parts of this.

For instance, Don't beat us over the head with everything that qualifies as a pipe immediately in the containment procedures. Keep it subtle, just say it has to be kept x meters from tube-like structures or objects, and describe how it moves between pipes and manipulates the flow of things through them. And then let it click into place when the containment team points a gun at it. You can do the same with containment breaches demonstrating the endless series of things which can be considered tubes. There's potential for this thing to be scary as hell, it just needs to be used right.
