                        

Bismuth Metal Crystals which were found at the location of SCP-5179's discovery location

Item #:  SCP-5179

Object Class:  Euclid

Special Containment Procedures:  SCP - 5179 is to be contained in a container which is made of bismuth metal sheeting on all surfaces. The container’s size should be large enough to give the entity space to move without coming into contact with the surfaces of the container.

Description:  SCP - 5179 is a winged serpent with no head or tail, rather the entity is a self enclosed loop and is often twisted so it can compress itself when in smaller areas. The entity has many sets of white feathered wings along its body at regular intervals. On the body segment of the entity, between the set of wings is a large eye with a diameter of 65 centimeters. The eyes are of different animals at different points in the entity’s body. The eyes of horses, cats, dogs, fish, humans, snakes and their close relatives have been noted on the entity's body but larger than their natural sizes. SCP - 5179 is covered in pale gold fur which has a lustrous quality to it.

SCP - 5179 does not require food or drink to sustain itself, rather it appears to require a proximity to bismuth metal and would, presumably, die or become inert without the metal in close proximity for prolonged periods of time. The entity does not appear to require sleep or periods of rest. If the entity were to come into contact with bismuth metal small third degree burns will appear on where the entity has made contact with the metal. These burns do not appear to heal.

Subjects who are in the presence of SCP - 5179 for an amount of time exceeding 10 minutes will experience a feeling of unnatural peace and calm which will persist indefinitely unless another subject acquires the same symptoms from SCP - 5179. If another subject acquires the symptoms of the entity, the previous subject’s symptoms will cease and the previous subject will enter a severe depression which results in the subject repeatedly attempting to take their own life. It is unknown if this latter effect on the subject is directly caused by the entity or caused via withdrawal from the effects of the entity.

SCP - 5179 never ceases movement while in containment. The entity will travel at extremely fast speeds for the size of its container yet shows considerable maneuverability while it travels in the air. The entity does not make any sounds except for when it has made contact with bismuth metal, in which case it releases a quiet scream. Personnel who hear this scream will feel a very mild burning sensation that lasts for no more than 2 days. This burning sensation is often compared to a bad sunburn by personnel. This effect of the entity is not considered severe enough to demand special containment procedures.

Discovery Log:  SCP - 5179 was first reported by an exploration team working in a bismuth mine on ██/██/19██. The mine was owned by ███████ ██████ Co. and the mine was located approximately ██ kilometers from the Mexico-Guatemala Border. Mining personnel reported finding a separate cave system 3 days before discovery of the entity. ███████ ██████ Co. was alerted of the discovery and they sent a team of 4 to explore the cave system 3 days later. 1 returned from the cave system approximately 11 hours later.

██████ ██████ returned with the symptoms in line with exposure to SCP - 5179. When asked what had happened to the other members of his party, ██████ ███████ is reported to have responded with, “They jumped off a ledge.” with jovial tone. ██████ ███████ was transported into the custody of local law enforcement. A second team of 6 was organized by ███████ ██████ Co. and sent into the cave system the following day.

Approximately 5 and a half hours after the teams entry into the cave system, ██████ ███████ is reported to have begun to cry hysterically in his cell despite being happy earlier, and was reported to be singing to himself prior to this. Less than 10 minutes later, ██████ ███████ began to ram his head into the walls of his cell with full force. Guards entered his cell and he was restrained. In response ██████ ███████ held his breath till he expired.

Only 1 member of the second team returned from the cave system with the same response and symptoms as the previous survivor of the first expedition. At this point the foundation was contacted and expeditions into the cave system were to be handled by the foundation. A D-class was sent in alone to identify the anomaly. The D-class was equipped with a video recorder, standard spelunking equipment, and a small firearm.

The recordings show a lengthy tunnel which led to a larger cave area after approximately 5 hours. The large cave had walls which were lined with luminescent bismuth metal crystals. The entity is then spotted and the D-class begins to experience the effects of the entity. Presumably, the survivor of the second expedition expired despite his corpse being found approximately 3 hours past this point.

With confirmation of the anomaly the D-class was instructed to return. Upon return, the D-class was put into confinement to identify the effects of the entity. Once the effects of the entity on the D-class were identified the foundation began to implement a plan to retrieve the anomaly. The foundation used drilling to access the entity’s location and was soon retrieved and was transported to Site ██ where SCP - 5179 is currently located.
