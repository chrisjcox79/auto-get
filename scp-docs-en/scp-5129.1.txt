Onhorse...

SCP-5129 is to be kept in a 15m x 15m Titanium room
附加记录：    

Aaand that's a downvote. Titanium shouldn't be capitalized. Horses generally need two acres of land- about 8km2 of land. 15mx15m is about 225m, far too small. Finally, titanium is only useful if you need something both light and strong. Otherwise, reinforced steel is almost always going to be better.

Steel shackles are to be locked to SCP-5129 hind legs to at least slow it down.
附加记录：

Reinforced sliding steel doors are locked in place when SCP-5129 goes on its rampage.
附加记录：    

 nature, you're likeoy better off not mentioning them.

Also,  or rage states aren't really seen as interesting. They're boting, and generally never used now.

Two foundation agents who have taken courses on exorcism and have been dispatched by their teacher are to be kept at SCP-5129's containment cell at all times.
附加记录：    

 what kind of exorcism? Shinto exorcisms are much different than catholic exorcisms. Furthermore, based on the SCP, it's unclear how an exorcist or someone with exorcist training would help here, as the idea of an exorcist is to banish evil spirits/demons from a host, object, or place.

As for the SCP itself, you've basically failed to understand ancient greek mythology. They have three seperate sections of Hades, the underworld. First, the greeks had Elisyum, a paradise where the truly blessed and honored would go upon death. Then you have Tartarus, their equivalent of hell where the Titans and those who commited truly reprehensible acts would go for eternal torment. Finally, you had Asphereus, where everyone else went; neither reward nor punishment, the souls of people sent here would be wandering aimlessly, forever caught up in the moment of their demise unless stirred out of it by mortals visiting the place.

None of these places are said to resemble what you described in the exploration.

Furthermore, a creature like this would most likely be associated with Ares, the God of War, not Hades, the God of Death, considering its habit to raid and pillage its local environs.

I strongly recommend you go to the site forums and work on the core concept of this, rather than go to the draft stage. The flaws of this SCP are fundamental in nature, beyond what I've mentioned, and attempting to draft it without focusing on the core concepts first will make it harder to fix.
