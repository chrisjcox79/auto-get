Unfortunately, I had to down-vote this. Reasoning:

+Grammar

-Close

~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

 Spag Bog |  This article has a lot of SPAG errors, notably missing commas, weird clinical tone errors, and some weird sentences:

SCP-5129 is to be kept in a 9x9x9 room its door is to be locked at all times and only to be opened when the room is being cleaned. When any personal member enters the room to clean it they must give it a gift or it will turn into its second form and escape
附加记录：    

A few things. Does the room have to be 9x9x9 or else it would escape? If not, I don't see why that's specified. Also 9 what? 9 kilometres or 9 metres - it isn't specified. Even if it was 9 metres, that cell is ridiculously large. Second, the staff must give it a gift - what kind of gift. Because you specify later that it has to be certain 'correct gifts'. Put these specifications in the conprocs - it could tell an interesting story in itself.

SCP-5129 is a female creature that has two forms the first being an albino doe and the second being a black wolf.
附加记录：    

This is just long-winded and a weird way of saying that. I'd change this to 'SCP-5129 is a creature with noted form-changing properties. The entity can take two forms, that of an unknown female cervid2 with thick black fur.

SCP-5129 is sapient and is capable of speech however, it speaks in Early Modern English.
附加记录：    

Two things) You're missing a comma in front, and Why is ** 'however'** here? However says 'it does x but it cant do y' but this says 'it can speak but it can speak'. Maybe change this to 'SCP-5129 is sapient and is capable of speech in Early Modern English3'

And so on for basically all the other sentences The main concern here is that sentences are long-winded and lack proper clinical tone, commas and other vital pieces.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

 In General |  A much more general issue with this, however, is the story. It isn't really there in any meaningful sense. We get a description of an anomaly which is meh in terms of its concept, and then we get a discovery log of an agent stating its anomalous effects again. This would be better if we learned more about the origins of the anomaly, how it acts and behaves, maybe interviews and an incident where someone doesn't give it the right gift. I'm of the belief that almost any idea with enough thought could be made into a good story, but right now, this isn't making it for me.

 Edit |  Well I'm bad at reading properly. Turns out it does specify what the gift needs to be. My point still stands, as needing fruit isn't really an interesting concept.

3. A form of English common in the Tudor period of Great Britain
