SCP Idea green light

23 Dec 2020 04:10

Seeking Greenlights:  Yes

Page Type:  SCP Article

Genre (Optional):  Drama

Elevator Pitch:  4 unseen ancient lineages of nautiloids are found around the Atlantic Ocean. Each one is drastically different from the other. Not only in physiology, but also in tactics, weapons, and speed.

Though they rarely interact, every five years, one individual from each of these nautiloid lineages will travel to the center of the Bermuda Triangle. After each battle, only one individual will leave the deathmatch.

Central Narrative:  Four undiscovered nautiloids species were discovered fighting in the middle of the Bermuda Triangle by an international oceanography survey. They were later discovered in different parts of the Atlantics with little overlap in population overlap.

SCP-XXXX-A are conical-shelled nautiloids resembling Orthoceras in physique. Shell length is 4 meters. The arm length is 1.5 meters each arm. Skin is red with a brown shell. During Autumn, the instances of SCP-XXXX-a will migrate to the southern Atlantic until mid-April.

+block

–block

SCP-XXXX-B are curved-shelled nautiloids living in the Caribbean. Shell length of the instance is 1.5 meters. A protrusion of iron from a meter up extends 20cm out from the shell. Arms of this instance are a meter long and hooked. Webbing is present between the arms 20cm from the base.

SCP-XXXX-C has a shell diameter of 1 meter. Resembling a form of a modern nautilus. Grooves are present where the walls of the air chambers are located. Skin is mostly navy blue.

SCP-XXXX-D are coiled-shelled nautiloids with a shell diameter of 2 meters. The shell is a gradient of orange and yellow with a black rock outer layer on it. Arms of SCP-XXXX-D instance are retracted and have microscopic hairs. Arms extend to 5 meters when in use.

Every 10 years, a ritual of fertility will be held by each instance of SCP-XXXX at the center of the Bermuda Triangle. The ritual ends until the last instance is alive. The sole survivor of the ritual, for example, an instance of SCP-XXXX-B, will have a population boom of three years for its instance.

Hook/Attention-Grabber:  Humans know more of what’s above them than what’s below the deep oceans. The oceans are scary indeed. About 90-97% of the ocean is still undiscovered. The ocean is the last frontier to travel on this planet. Many creatures are left undiscovered to this day.

Additional Notes:  Draft is almost complete. Sketches of the entities will come soon.
