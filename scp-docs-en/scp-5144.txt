Item #:  SCP-5144

AKA:  A Multiversal Joyride

Object Class:  Safe | Euclid | Keter

Special Containment Procedures:  SCP-5144 is 4 5 separate entities/objects. 5144-2 and 5144-3 have been contained but 5144-1 cannot be contained by any means in this reality (See SCP-5144-1 log for details). SCP-5144-4 is a titanic marble structure located in Iceland that is a gateway to another reality. SCP-5144-5 is an alternate reality of which connected to ours by SCP-5144-4 (See 5144-4 and 5144-5 logs for more details).

The containment cell of SCP-5144-1, 5144-2, and 5144-3, is a large aircraft hangar sized room located 30 meters underground at Site 38. The hangar is partially taken up by SCP-5144-3 due to its size. The entrance to the cell is a 10 by 6 foot sized metal door. Clustered near the entrance are some cushioned chairs and a coffee table. On the walls of the cell are paintings of other worlds and entities. Small statues are scattered around the room. A desk covered in pieces of metal is located near the sculptures, this is where SCP-5144-1 constructs them. The builds resemble miniature models of SCPs that have been used on 5144-1 in termination attempts.

Addendum| First Contact:  Upon March 17th, 20██ at 1:19 PM, SCP-179 was seen pointing toward the planet Neptune. On the far side of the planet, light distortions were visible. The light distortions continued for 2 hours before a massive explosion came from it. The explosion resembled a small supernova, which vaporized about 40% of Neptune's upper atmosphere. Out of the explosion a comet was seen on a direct collision course with Earth. Within 5 minutes, the comet had made it from Neptune to the Moon and slowed drastically. While passing by Mars, a radio transmission was picked up coming from the comet.

Radio Transmission detected:  

Radio Transmission Translation:  

Attempts to connect to the individual on the radio failed. The anomalous comet continued on its way to Earth, slowing gradually as it entered the atmosphere. At 3:28 the comet crashed in a field near Finley, Tennessee.

A large meteorite is found by researchers sitting in a 10 feet deep crater with the diameter being around 70 meters. The nearby oak forest had been scorched by the crash as well as an abandoned farmhouse. No one seemingly was injured during the crash. The Foundation quickly set up a perimeter around the crater and kept any news media from getting near the meteorite.

March 20th, 20██ at 6:57 PM, 3 days after the meteorite crashed. The meteorite began shaking for 30 seconds before it  says one of the researchers who tested the liquid to make sure. Inside the crater now lay a mysterious metal machine surrounded by a puddle of water. Scientists attempted to get samples of the machine but failed, no tools or weapons the Foundation had on site could damage the construct.

March 21st, 20██ at 12:34 PM, loud banging could be heard from inside the machine. D-class personnel was sent in to investigate the noises. The D-class claims to hear voices, yelling, inside the machine. Foundation agents told the D-class to try to make contact with the voice, of which the D-class did. The voice instructed them to locate a cargo bay door and remove and blockages.

The D-class followed the voice to the back of the machine and was instructed by the voice to remove the dirt blocking a large cargo bay door. An armed guard brought the D-class a shovel and they proceeded to remove the dirt. After it was cleared, a loud banging came from inside the cargo bay. 2 minutes of banging went on before the cargo bay door blasted open, heaving the entire machine a few feet in the air. Out of the machine came 2 figures; human male in a tuxedo and a large, dark blue creature (SCP-5144-1 and SCP-5144-2.)

SCP-5144-1 digital artwork by Researcher Arthur Johnson

Item #:  SCP-5144-1

AKA:  Mozart

Object Class:  Euclid Keter

Special Containment Procedures:  SCP-5144-1 cannot be contained by the Foundation but the lifeform does stay inside 5144's containment cell at Site 38 by choice. The 5144-1 can't be contained because of one of its anomalous abilities (Seen below).

Description:  SCP-5144-1 is a bipedal reptilian lifeform. Approximately 2.5 meters in height and weighs over 200kg. The flesh of the entity is a dark grey and the skin is covered by millions of small, dark blue scales. 5144-1 has a large head, in the shape of a snake head, with a pair of eyes on each side of the skull. Nostrils are located above the front lip. Entity has 4 fangs located inside its maw, 2 inches long each, that secrete the MF-1 substance in extremely concentrated amounts. The eyes glow light blue. The creature can communicate verbally despite a lack of vocal cords or tongue. The chest cavity of the creature is visible with a glowing blue orb floating inside it. The orb does not show up on any scanners however the hume level does. Inside the chest cavity of 5144-1, the hume level rapidly increases and decreases at an unsteady rate, however not affecting the hume level outside the chest cavity. If 5144-1 consumes any item, when the item enters the chest cavity, it is absorbed by the orb.

The lifeform's skeletal structure is constructed entirely of an unknown metal. Attempts to study the metal have failed due to 5144-1's anomalous ability. Whatever the bones are made of seems to have the same density as steel. The creature has only 3 digits on its hands/feet. Upon 5144-1's back are 4 hatches, each 4 inches in diameter, that open to extend long robotic limbs of the same metal the skeleton is constructed from. The limbs can extend up to 2 meters in length and each have a robotic hand on them. The robotic hands have 4 digits each. The robotic limbs can retract back into the body of 5144-1 however there is no visible changes to the chest cavity of the entity. SCP-5144-1 has a large serpentine tail stretching 2.5 meters in length with large plates made of the same skeletal alloy fused to the top.

Adorned on the tip of the tail is a small flower similar to a water lily, however glowing a pink hue. Inside the center of the flower is a cluster of powder known as MF-1 substance. The substance causes sedation if inhaled or consumed. When burned, MF-1 becomes an extremely powerful hallucinogen and sense dampener. SCP-5144-1 seems to be immune to the sedative ability but is still susceptible to the hallucinogen. SCP-5144-1 has its spine protected by smaller plates similar to the ones on its tail. Entity has no digestive organs. The respiratory system of the entity pumps a dark blue blood through the body of unknown means, as no lungs or heart are existent within 5144-1. SCP-5144-1 prefers to be identified as .

SCP-5144-1's origins are unknown. SCP-5144-1's knowledge of its existence is limited.

Behavior:  SCP-5144-1 is a rather benevolent lifeform. It is quite open to the Foundation of its existence however will not talk about a specific time in its past around 10 thousand years before contact. 5144-1 also seems to feel happy by making others feel happy, including trying to cheer up one guard who lost a friend in a containment breach at another site. The SCP seems to want to help the Foundation.

SCP-5144-1 prefers creating things. It will generally will construct canvases then paint detailed images of places it's been to and lifeforms it's seen. It will then try to show staff the paintings and explain the worlds they are of. But when questioned about a painting of a pink reptilian humanoid, 5144-1 refuses to talk. The creature also seems to enjoy constructing metal sculptures of other SCPs, weapons of alien construct, and otherworldly furniture. SCP-5144-1 donates its alien weapons to the researchers to study and possibly distribute to MTF units.

SCP-5144-1 has no sexual attraction to humans. However, it has shown interest in SCP-1472, claiming that Dr. Bright said something about Velociraptors in maid costumes. SCP-5144-1 has sent 4 requests to Dr. Paul Blart for permission to enter SCP-1472, permission has been denied.

Anomalous Properties:  SCP-5144-1 has proven not to be able to die. The central orb cannot be damaged by any known methods. The body of 5144-1 is durable but still destroyable. However when removed from the majority of the body or destroyed, the limbs or remaining body will disintegrate only to reconstruct around the central orb. If any portions are still missing after reconstruction, the nearest object will begin to disintegrate and reconstruct the missing body portions until the body is complete, then cease disintegration of nearest object.

SCP-5144-1 can launch concentrated heat from the palms of its robotic limbs. The beams of heat exceed over 2000 degrees Celsius but the heat stays confined to the 4 inch diameter of the robotic limbs. When activated, four beams of blue light protrude from the central core of 5144-1 and proceed to move through the flesh to connect where the robotic limbs enter the body. It is suggested that the energy is drawn from the central orb, but this theory is not fully proven.

SCP-5144-1 seems to have atom altering abilities. 5144-1 can manipulate the atomic makeup of any item and change it into something else. The manipulation seems to take a great amount of energy from 5144-1. The more complex something is, the harder it is for 5144-1 to change it. A simple glass cup is easier to alter than a motherboard of a computer. When exerting too much energy, 5144-1 will cease life functions, resulting in it falling to the ground, for approximately 25 to 30 seconds before restarting. 5144-1 afterwards will remain unconscious until it has regained its strength.

SCP-5144-1 can open portals to other universes/realities. It does this by firing 4 separate heat beams at a single point. When all four beams connect, it opens a portal that gets larger the longer 5144-1 continues its bombardment. The portals close shortly after being opened. The entity seems not to be able to control the reality that a portal is opened to, however this is possibly not true (See 5144-1 containment breach).

Photograph of SCP-5144-2

Item #:  SCP-5144-2

AKA:  Amidias

Object Class:  Euclid

Special Containment Procedures:  SCP-5144-2 resides in the containment cell of SCP-5144. It is requested to stay by SCP-5144-1 but will leave if its life or another's is in danger.

Description:  SCP-5144-2 is a humanoid synthetic construct controlled by an AI. 5144-2 is about 5 feet 11 inches in height and weighs about 85kg due to its robotic buildup. The AI controls the construct as its physical body. The AI and the construct were both made by SCP-5144-1. 5144-2 can wirelessly connect to any technology within 25 meters of it, however it is loyal to 5144-1 and will cease control of technology if 5144-1 wishes it. The AI seems to have human emotions, whether made by 5144-1 or acquired on its own is unknown.

The body resembles a white male of British descent. A mustache is visible on the face of 5144-2. 5144-2 frequently is seen wearing a fedora. It wears a tuxedo that is fused to the body of it. Attempting removal of the suit leads to the mechanical exoskeleton inside it being exposed.

Behavior:  SCP-5144-2 spends most time either conversing with 5144-1 or site personnel. When not doing that, 5144-2 is connected to the internet and learning information about this reality's earth. 5144-2 claims that it has never seen earth habited before, either a barren rock or completely gone. SCP-5144-2 can survive memetic kill agents and therefor can access level 05 files in the Foundation's database. SCP-5144-1 has requested 5144-2 to not do this however, of which it has obeyed.

Anomalous Properties:  SCP-5144-2 can wirelessly connect and control any electrical devices within 25 meters of it. This includes cars, phones, computers, neural implants, etc.

SCP-5144-2 is powered by unknown means and requires no consumption of materials or energy intake.

Addendum| Containment Breach 1:  SCP-5144-2 breached containment June 27th, 20██ at 10:36 AM. It connected itself to one of the computer terminals in Site 38 and was taking in data on the Foundation, completely encrypting 05 access in milliseconds. No vital information was accessed by 5144-2. The containment breach lasted only 7 minutes before 5144-1 pulled 5144-2 away from the monitor. 5144-2 was then sent to intense therapy for 3 weeks, claiming to see in the terminal. SCP-5144-2 had apparently accessed files on SCP-1471 and located files and artwork created by those affected by 1471-A.

Item #:  SCP-5144-3

AKA:  The Star Jumper

Object Class:  Safe

Special Containment Procedures:  SCP-5144-3 is contained at Site-38 in SCP-5144's cell. It is an inanimate object and is easily contained. However its size was the determining factor in 5144's containment cell size.

Description:  5144-3 is a large spaceship around ██ by ██ by ██ meters in size. This is why the containment cell is 100 by 50 by 40 meters in size. Whatever the ship is constructed of is too durable for any non-anomalous foundation weaponry to damage. However, 5144-3 has visible scorch marks and damage to its right thrusters and hull (See SCP-5144-1 interview for details).

Bottom level:  Located near the rear of the ship is the cargo bay door. Inside the cargo bay are metal crates of unknown  described 5144-1. The airlock opens directly into the bridge of the ship. In the center of the bridge is a 15 meter tall pedestal. Atop the pedestal is an open platform ,that 5144-1 has shown, activates a spherical hologram when 5144-1 is present. The hologram has the ability to detect 5144-1's thoughts and movements, moving exactly how he wishes it to. The hologram sphere glows a light blue and vaguely resembles an advanced computer setup. Ship diagnostics and status checks are visible on the hologram.

Top level: On the sides of the bridge are metal grate steps leading to the top of the ship. Up in the top level, there is a balcony overviewing the bridge and is about 3 feet higher then the command platform. A large nuclear reactor is visibly exposed in the center of the top area, also glowing a light blue but substantially brighter in comparison to the control hologram. On the walls around the reactor are many lockers each containing different items. (See list of items below) In the rear top level of the ship was a single metal door opening into a hallway that connected to a bedroom, a spare bedroom, a bathroom, and a charging station for 5144-2.

Anomalous Properties:  SCP-5144-3 is a vehicle from another universe. The technology used in its construction is greatly evolved.

Item #:  SCP-5144-4

AKA:  The Archway

Object Class:  Safe

Special Containment Procedures:  SCP-5144-4 is to be contained inside a perimeter fence approximately 2.3 kilometers from the structure and surrounding it completely. Guards are to patrol the perimeter fence at all times to stop any unauthorized entrance.

Description:  SCP-5144-4 is a large marble structure of circular shape and 2 kilometers in diameter with a height of 3 kilometers. 5144-4 was created by a containment breach of SCP-5144-1 when 5144-1 formed it using the surrounding landscape(See 5144-1 containment breach and abilities).

SCP-5144-4's exterior is a marble structure with no viewpoints inside. On the western side of the structure is the entrance, a 90 meter high by 40 meter wide archway. Above the archway etched in the marble reads  The interior of the structure is lit by floating lanterns visible around the structure at varying heights. The walls have engravings of unknown lifeforms and locations on them. Water is also seen seeping from holes in the walls to create pools. Roof of the structure has clouds that form randomly by unknown means.

Between the center of the structure and the entrance is a pedestal with SCP-1762 located atop. 1762 is openable but all attempts to remove it have failed. In the direct center of SCP-5144-4 is an identical archway to the entrance. When a living creature is within 36 meters of it, the archway will start to shimmer and generate light. If a living creature passes through the archway, they are teleported to an identical structure but without water flowing from the walls or lanterns lighting it. Where the entrance should be located is instead a solid wall of sandstone.

Anomalous Properties:  SCP-5144-4 is a gateway to another reality (SCP-5144-5).

SCP-5144-4 will not let SCP-1762 be removed from the pedestal inside it.

SCP-5144-4 does not allow radio contact between different sides of the portal.

Item #:  SCP-5144-5

AKA:  The Fallen Fantasy

Object Class:  Euclid

Special Containment Procedures:  No unauthorized personnel are allowed to enter SCP-5144-4.

Description:  SCP-5144-5 is an unknown reality or world connected to ours by SCP-5144-4, which was created by SCP-5144-1. 5144-5 is the landscape outside of the 5144-4 structure on that side. Access to 5144-5 is only accessible via a drilled tunnel from exit of 5144-4 to the surface. SCP-5144-5 seems to have one star it orbits, and 2 moons that orbit it. Time functions similar in 5144-5 as it does on earth. The day/night cycle seems consistent with Earth's.

The explored part of 5144-5 was once habited by seemingly intelligent lifeforms. The surrounding 5-7 kilometers from the shaft exit resembles a desert. Outside of that is a forest, not resembling any flora on earth, that looks to have recently survived a forest fire. In the distance, a large mountain range is visible with 30% of it being held aloft by anomalous means.

SCP-5144-5 is mostly void of life in the explored sector. However, large wormlike entities are visible burrowing under the sand. Entities flee when loud noises above 150 decibels are emitted. Entities have armored plating that requires explosives to break through. No other living entities are documented in the explored sector.

Anomalous Properties:  It is an alternate reality with unknown threats.

Mission Objective:  Set up temporary/ permanent base of operations on surface of SCP-5144-5.

Target Location:  Large white rock formation.

Mission Log: 

-Day 1-

The exploration team was deployed at the exit point of the mining shaft connecting SCP-5144-4 to the surface of 5144-5. The group began traversing the desert landscape in the direction of the large rock formation. 1 hour of traveling, the group encountered another wormlike entity like was encountered during the first and second exploration missions. Entity was terminated, no casualties reported.

The exploration team, about 3 hours into the journey, claim to have spotted a large flying object in the sky. Camera footage shows a large bird-like entity, estimated 9 meters tall, with red feathers, flying high above the group. The entity flew closer and landed on a sandstone boulder roughly 30 meters from the group. It began hitting the boulder it stood on with its large beak. The noise seems to have alerted one of the worm creatures, which began burrowing underground toward the boulder. The bird entity flew into the air and dove at the moving sand mound, spraying sand over the group. Camera footage was temporarily lost. After the team recovered, footage shows the bird entity back on the boulder consuming the worm. One of the MTF cadets made a joke about  which made some of the group laugh. Exploration team continued travel.

The team reached the large white rock form in 4 hours and 34 minutes. The rock formation is actually a massive skeletal structure about 200 meters long. The form looks vaguely similar to a lizard skeleton, however distinct horns are found on the back of the skull. The skeletal structure is damaged, with breaks in the ribcage and spine visible. 2 unknown appendages, possibly secondary arms, are located near the midsection of the entity. Scientists Anton and Mikkel Schmidt took samples of the bone. The team locate the skull of the deceased organism and make camp inside the open jaw of it. The maw of the creature is forced open by a large boulder that has broken half of the jaw on the right side. Evidence suggests the entity fell on the boulder and shattered its jaw on impact, however was probably dead before impact. One of the MTF noticed the sun setting in 5144-5 and started a small fire inside the skull. The group were instructed to rest for the next day. 2 MTF cadets held watch at the front of the skull.

-Day 2-

The group were awoken around 5:00 AM, in 5144-5, to the sound of thunder. Video footage showed a large storm coming toward the desert area from the , as one scientist described it, of lightning would strike the ground for approximately 32 seconds. The group are warned to stay inside the skull of the deceased organism until the storm passes. Radio contact was lost for 6 hours when the storm got between the camp at 5144-4 and the team in the field.

-Day 3-

Radio contact was restored. The storm had passed. The exploration group had begun rationing out food and water and were preparing to return to 5144-4. Level 3 Researcher, Linda Brooke, at 5144-4 gave them permission to return. The team returned to the exit shaft of 5144-4 in 3 hours and 59 minutes.

Post-Mission Data:  Chemical analysis does not show any connection from the deceased organism to that of Earthly life. The next few weeks after the mission, an airlock was constructed at the exit point of the mining shaft. 2 more missions to the skeleton structure camp were sent with supplies to reinforce the camp and make it more suitable for long-term survival. These missions did not have any significance and the logs were discarded.
