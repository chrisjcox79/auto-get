                        

Item #: SCP-5378

Object Class: Safe

Special Containment Procedures: SCP-5378 can not be contained in any structure. Stays in a public room in the facility or a personnel home willingly showing itself, if allowed to work with the SCP foundation.

No nutrition is necessary for SCP-5378 to survive. Can be given simple items it requests. Requires a new notebook every two months. (Enjoys drawing and writing/recording their experiences in them.)

At least one Level Three or above personnel that is assigned to SCP-5378 has to stay with SCP-5378 at all times. Any personnel who come into contact with SCP-5378 must treat them as a normal person. Communication and questions towards SCP-5378 is acceptable. Prefers to be called by their birth name, Happyness, but does not really care about it.

Description: SCP-5378 is a shape shifting entity that can only change colors into different hues and shades of brown. It can be recognized by its black scalaria with white pupils. Its original shape is similar to a venison with a stretched out spine which makes the neck and tail serpent like. Has a main of curly hair, Bovidae horns and feathered wings when calmed and in private.. Mostly appears as a well-dressed, 7'4 humanoid with white skin and deer like ears.

SCP-5378's shape changing skill happens at an atomic level to the point where they can change the elements they are composed of. Their unique ability makes them impossible to be contained. their shape shifting can even give off psychic energy. SCP-5378 can shift particles they come into contact with. SCP-5378 has the strange ability to cross breed with anything living.

Despite receiving thousands of years of bad experiences, SCP-5378 won't harm any living thing unless they feel the need to defend them self or others. SCP-5378 gave information about them self and other entities freely. Is willing to be experimented on and be a part of an experiment if they can also record the results in their notebooks.

Says that they 

SCP-5378 is one of the anomalous defenders against Keter class end of the world scenarios. if a worldwide catastrophic event takes place, SCP-5378 was ordered to take 1,000 humans into the 'emptiness' until danger passes.

-ALL INFORMATION ABOUT OR WITH SCP-5378 IS CLASSIFIED-
