                        

CAPTION-GOES-HERE

Item #:  SCP-5890

Object Class:  Euclid

Special Containment Procedures: 

SCP-5890 is to be kept in a standard humanoid containment cell. The floor of SCP-5890's cell is to be covered in a layer of sand at least 10 centimetres thick. Every 3 days SCP-5890's cell is to be cleared of any SCP-5890-A instances found growing. If SCP-5890 attempts to make physical contact with any member of the clearing team a piece of paper or other object created using wood is to be made visible to SCP-5890.

Description: 

SCP-5890 is similar in appearance to a human female though it's skin resembles smooth tree bark, most closely resembling that of Quercus Alba(white oak). It is approximately 1.65 meters tall and appears to be pregnant.

While it is capable of advanced reasoning and speech it does will spend most of it's time sitting in the middle of its containment cell.

Any contact made between bare skin and any part of SCP-5890 will result in the skin rapidly transforming into bark similar to SCP-5890's skin. If contact is broken in under 2 seconds only the top layer of the subjects epidermis will need to be removed to completely remove the bark like skin. If contact is held for more than 2 seconds up to flesh up to 1.5 centimetres from the surface will need to be removed. If the converted flesh is not removed within 4 hours rapid necrosis will set in, potentially spreading to the rest of the subject's body.

Every 2-3 days SCP-5890 will produce a single acorn[SCP-5890-A] of species Quercus Alba, approximately 3 times the average size of an acorn from that species, from its navel. SCP-5890 will then proceed to push the SCP-5890-A instance into the ground. SCP-5890 is able to push the SCP-5890-A instance in to the ground no matter what the ground is made of. SCP-5890-A instances will grow far faster than would be expected. The roots of SCP-5890-A instances are capable of growing through any material and do not appear to require water, nutrients or sunlight. If SCP-5890-A instances are allowed to grow in or near man made structures they will prefer to grow into the structures.

when questioned SCP-5890 seems to be unwilling or unable to answer any questions directly regarding itself.

Interview Log 5890-1 

Interviewed:  SCP-5890

Interviewer:  Dr Green

Dr Green:  Hello.

SCP-5890:  Hello.

Dr Green:  May I ask, what are you?

SCP-5890 does not reply.

Dr Green:  Okay, another question. Why do you plant acorns in the floor.

SCP-5890 does not reply.

Closing Statement:  After 5 minutes of waiting DR Green left SCP-5890's cell. SCP-5890 appears unwilling to cooperate.

Interview Log 5890-2 

Interviewed:  SCP-5890

Interviewer:  Dr Green

Foreword:  Dr Green walks in carrying several sheets of paper

SCP-5890:  (retreats into a corner) you would hold part of a butchered corpse.

Dr Green:  What?

SCP-5890:  You murdered a tree.

Dr Green:  Do you mean this paper?(he holds up the papers)

SCP-5890:  Yes.

DR Green leaves the containment cell and returns without the paper.

Dr Green:  Now can we talk?

SCP-5890:  (Moves closer to Dr Green) Yes.

Dr Green:  What was so terrifying about the paper?

SCP-5890:  You would be displeased by a human head.

Dr Green:  That sapling (points to a SCP-5890-A instance) why does it grow faster than normal.

SCP-5890:  It does not.

Dr Green:  Can you tell me anything about yourself?

SCP-5890 does not reply.

Dr Green:  Is this conversation going to accomplish anything?

SCP-5890:  (Quickly touches Dr Green's arm) Yes.

Dr Green:  (Looks at his arm which now resembles bark) What did you, what is this?

SCP-5890:  Your truth form.

Dr Green quickly left the room.
