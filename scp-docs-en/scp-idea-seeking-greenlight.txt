SCP Idea - seeking greenlight

21 Dec 2020 21:16

First time attempting to write an SCP and still pretty new to the site. I have a basic idea for this SCP and would like to get greenlighted for it.

Seeking Greenlights:  Yes

Page Type:  SCP Article

Elevator Pitch:  An SCP that seems completely human but has the ability to control other SCPs within hearing range by saying the word “Control”.

Central Narrative:  SCP-XXXX attempts to release all contained SCPs from the Foundation. However, SCP-XXXX is unsuccessful in doing so and is contained by the Foundation. After successfully containing the entity, the Foundation discovers that SCP-XXXX can understand and speak the English language. Doctor █ makes several attempts to question SCP-XXXX. In all attempts, SCP-XXXX willingly answers all questions except one : Why did SCP-XXXX attempt to release the other SCPs? When Doctor █ asks SCP-XXXX this question, it bows it’s head and goes silent for a moment. After this moment, it will lift it’s head to regain eye contact with Doctor █ and say “I refuse to answer anymore questions.” and becomes unresponsive to any further questions. To this day, the reason of why SCP-XXXX attempted to release the other SCPs is still a mystery.

Hook/Attention-Grabber:  The mystery of why SCP-XXXX wanted to release the other SCPs.

Additional Notes:  The only concern I have is that I am unsure if this idea is only interesting to me and no one else.

Re: SCP Idea - seeking greenlight

21 Dec 2020 21:49

Hi buddy, what you have here is still too raw and unpolished to be judged. You are still very far from the 400 words limit so my first suggestion is to expand your proposal here a bit more. Your idea is interesting but so far there is no knowing how it will develop. Some questions that might help you sort this out:

Who is this humanoid? Is it an anomalous human or a completely different entity who just so happens to look human?
Why does it want to release other anomalies? Does it have a grudge against humanity or the Foundation? Or is it sympathetic to the imprisoned SCPs?
Can he control anomalies that don't control their own powers? Or anomalies that are immovable objects?

These are some points that might help you flesh out your concept and bring it to the standards the community desires from new entries.
