SCP Idea SEEKING GREENLIGHT

31 Dec 2020 06:18

Page type : New SCP Article

Elevator Pitch : This SCP would be something simple, something that makes you wonder 

Central Narrative : ISo my SCP is a man in a hot air balloon that is designated safe as he only attacks in self defense. The Hot-Air balloon itself can reach up to extremely fast speeds and stop on a time and do things such as move in any direction or things like barrel rolls. A real physics breaking type entity. For example, the Balloon can easily reach Mach 3 and then stop instantly with no inertia, no drag, nothing and the man in said balloon doesn't fly out of the balloon or do any type of bracing for the oncoming stop. The SCP itself can initiate maneuvers such as barrel rolls.

Hook : The hook is that it has that funny/weird feel to it while not being that ridiculous that it takes people of out it.

From what I read I have to get the concept greenlight then the draft and then the final green light for it to become and  article.

Additional : I'm just thinking of how to  more of so It's not just a few paragraphs.

Re: SCP Idea SEEKING GREENLIGHT

01 Jan 2021 12:19

1) It's a bad sign when you're actually asking for suggestions on how to fill more spaceAlso, you don't need to mention how many green lights you need.

2) The hook is actually fine; it's fine to have a whimsical SCP in there every so often, but you may want to build that up a little more. Also, you can truncate most of the elevator pitch; just  is fine, although adding a little more as to its purpose would help.

3) The Central Narrative is your biggest problem. Not only do you have some grammatical issues that need to be worked on, but you're also lacking any kind of history with this guy. You need some sort of story, as well as who he would be on the defensive against. You have a hot-air balloon doing a lot of weirdness; there has to be some sort of origin story there. Figure out what it is.
