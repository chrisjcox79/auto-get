SCP Idea (Seeking Greenlight)

25 Nov 2020 04:16

Seeking Greenlights:  Yes

Page Type:  SCP Article

Elevator Pitch:  Essentially a wormhole disguised as a cloud as creation possibly from a GOI (yet to be decided.) The two clouds that obviously allow you to teleport to eachother are antipodal to eachother. Looking at the wormhole from the ground, it appears to look like a normal cloud. Edit lmao: However, viewed from above, it looks more akin to the image i have hopefully attached. UPDATE: Found It! This is actually the image that was inspiration for the idea They are static in the sky. Under each of the clouds are families that are linked (somewhat)

Central Narrative:  (Possible creation by a GOI is to be put on the sideboard of possibilities) This SCP could be an explanation for tons of aeronautical mishaps. - There are two families living in houses directly underneath both of the clouds, however they are both aware of the anomalous properties of the cloud. The families contain a father, mother, two children of indeterminent gender, and a dog, all of which look very similar. They are likely in contact with eachother, and despite the appearance similarities, are very different people. This is another aspect that I plan to flesh out a lot more (interview logs, experiments, addendums galore). another edit: Due to the fact that these 'wormholes' were likely created by someone or something sentient, they were probably not meant to be discovered by people! i imagine these came into existence before the first planes were flown, and so all they needed to do was disguise the lower part of the wormhole to appear as a cloud to the naked eye. However, viewed from above, the cloud DOES look like a wormhole, as to be easier to find by those who know what to look for.

Hook/Attention-Grabber:  It is based around a cloud, which is something that not many scps do. It also could be used to explain possible events in history, or something along those lines. The idea of a wormhole has been done a lot before, but i imagine that this object also has some amount of gravitational effect, leading to a possible earlier discovery than that of plane travel.

Additional Notes:  This is in somewhat early development! i have a lot of ideas and their base, although there is still some further fleshing out to do. I have a sandbox page, and it continues to grow

Answering other questions asked: The families may look similar to eachother from coincidence, or from a secondary memetic effect of the scip. Honestly, i dont really want to add another effect to it, since i dont want make this too convoluted, and so i might scrap the idea of them looking identical and maybe just have them be in contact. As for how they are in contact, i imagine through they ages the family advances with technology, meaning early on they may have contacted eachother through written letters, advancing towards today with cellphones  such? To answer what the families are doing, i believe that they are either related to the group that created the cloud (maybe the families worked together in the past to create it? which may have linked them? needs workshopped LOTS more). I think they just accept and appreciate the cloud, not quite worshipping it, but appreciating its existence. When the foundation inevitably finds out about the families, they may even be able to provide information as to the history of it? i am accepting of more criticism!
