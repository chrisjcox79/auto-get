SCP Idea: Elaine of Astolats Curse

08 Nov 2020 12:38

Seeking Greenlights:  Yes

Page Type:  SCP Article

Genre:  Drama/Emotional

Page Layout:  A standard SCP article.

Elevator Pitch:  SCP-XXXX must always be looking into a reflective surface. Be it a mirror, or glass, or water, she must never look at anything but a reflective surface. If her direct or paracentral vision ever looks at or is blocked by an opaque surface, a reflection desynchronisation will occur in any reflective surface in any given location in the world (designated as SCP-XXXX-1). The location of SCP-XXXX-1 appears random but the time frame and range in which SCP-XXXX-1 occur seem to increase with every breach in SCP-XXXX's protocol.

+block

–block

SCP-XXXX doesn't appear to age and has numerous scars across her body, with the deepest around her eyes. Her inner eyelids appear to be coated in a flexible, reflective material. The reflective surfaces SCP-XXXX look into also go through a process of , meaning they start to dull and crack over time, including the ones in her eyes. Researchers who look into the same surface as SCP-XXXX claim they feel a sense of shame, guilt or worthlessness when they cast a reflection. As if something is judging them.

Central Narrative:  SCP-XXXX's story unfolds through a number of interviews with SCP-XXXX-1's victims. During interviews, the victims describe the reflections screaming vitriol and abuse of a deeply personal nature. Throughout the interviews, many of the victim's comment that they find SCP-XXXX-1's judgements/insults both violating (insults SCP-XXXX-1 use suggest a definite breach of privacy) and confusing (as the believed no one could have witnessed the actions SCP-XXXX-1 speaks of). Following incidences with SCP-XXXX-1, the victim's become very uncomfortable around reflective surfaces, even after amnestics are administered.

Later, after further research, SCP-XXXX's head researcher decides to interview SCP-XXXX directly about her condition and why she causes SCP-XXXX-1. SCP-XXXX claims she doesn't know, only that before she was taken in by the Foundation, her captives mutilated her regularly. When asked why, SCP-XXXX commented, . This leads to researchers to believe that SCP-XXXX's condition isn't in reality to cause SCP-XXXX-1 but to prevent it.

Hook/Attention-Grabber:  After interviews with SCP-XXXX and observations in her character, Foundation researchers begin to believe that SCP-XXXX may be in fact Elaine of Astolat, also known as The Lady of Shallot from Alfred Tennyson famous poem. Her conditions aren't dissimilar to the women described in the poem. This supposed curse Tennyson describes may be a misinterpretation of SCP-XXXX's condition.

I'm moderately concerned by your reliance in Series I articles to carry some key details of the article. Crosslinking is alright, but crosslinking to S1 in this way is a tad strange.

Additionally, I've got some issues with the idea of the people in the mirrors — it comes off as comical more than anything? Like, my reflection insulting me and demanding to be free doesn't spark feelings of fear or dread, just mild bemusement.

The basic anomalous properties just seems rather, uh, ill-fitting for the atmosphere I'm picking up.

Thanks for replying.

I'm moderately concerned by your reliance in Series I articles to carry some key details of the article. Crosslinking is alright, but crosslinking to S1 in this way is a tad strange.
附加记录：    

They don't need to be there. I just used them to emphasis the effects SCP-XXXX has on the mirrors. It isn't necessary.

Additionally, I've got some issues with the idea of the people in the mirrors — it comes off as comical more than anything? Like, my reflection insulting me and demanding to be free doesn't spark feelings of fear or dread, just mild bemusement.
附加记录：    

I think I wasn't describing what I wanted correctly (not used to this). When I mean abusive, I wasn't imagining like . That's teenage, self-loathing movie stuff. I was picturing very deep and incredibly personal insults. Insults that come from very private things.

Think of it like this. How many reflections are around you right now? Not just mirrors, but windows, glass, metal, your phone, pictures frames, anything. Reflections are all around us, mirroring us all the time. That means they see everything. Think about what they're reflecting and what you're doing when they're reflecting you. They not only witness you washing your face and applying make up. They witness you degrade people online. They witness you killing people. They witness you doing vile, disgusting, shameful, perverted, pitiful things. And they have to mirror that.

Don't forget, what makes this worse is the reflections are always like this. Even after they've resynchronised. No matter what you do, someone is going to be watching. Judging.
