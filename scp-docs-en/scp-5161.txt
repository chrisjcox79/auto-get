Item #:  SCP-5161

Object Class:  Safe

Special Containment Procedures:  SCP-5161 is to be kept in a glass casing suspended on metal locks inside of a semi-dimmed wooden containment chamber resembling a child’s “playroom”, At no point should SCP-5161 be taken out of its containment chamber.

SCP-5161 should be guarded by three level 2 guards at all times, and only level 2 personnel are permitted into SCP-5161,s containment

Description:  SCP-5161 is a wooden children’s toy more commonly known as “Cup-And-Ball” with a red circle on the top end of the “cup” with green and black lines of the outer end of the “cup.”

SCP-5161 anomalous properties come into play when the user of SCP-5161 flips the toy upwards when it’s facing down, when the “ball” is falling it seems to land in the “cup” no matter which direction it is facing.

Testing results show that when the toy is damaged it will “steal” the users muscle mass

Subject:  D-4557

Procedure:  D-4557 was instructed to flip SCP-5161 upside down

Results:  The “ball” landed in SCP-5161’s “cup”

Analysis:  Even while upside down its anomalous properties are not affected
