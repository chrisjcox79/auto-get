                        

5306

Level#2

Containment Class:
euclid

Secondary Class:
none

Disruption Class:
dark

Risk Class:
warning

link to memo

Special Containment Procedures:  SCP-5306 is to be contained within a 10x10x10 meter locked room at all times at Site 19, sector 2. Instances of SCP-5306-2 to SCP-5306-5 that exit object should not be interacted with, but instances of SCP-5306-1-A and SCP-5306-1-B that exit object can be collected for testing with level 3 security clearance or higher and accompanied by guards. On a monthly basis, objects containment cell must be checked for ‘decorations’ on the walls of the containment cell, if there is, the containment cell must be cleaned with the accompany of guards, in case instances SCP-5306-1-A to SCP-5306-5 exit the object.

Description:  SCP-5306 is a Artificial Christmas Tree, previously manufactured and owned by the brand Macy’s, is fully decorated with Christmas ornaments, and is indistinguishable from a non-anomalous Artificial Christmas Tree. But, unlike a non-anomalous Christmas tree, object seems impervious to any type of hazard, such as heat. It is unknown if object is sentient, but all signs point to no.

When a human fully or even partially reaches inside to the interior of object, subject will vanish, and be teleported inside a small fungal forest, where there are two glimpses to other dimensions, one being SCP-5306-A, and the the other being SCP-5306-B. If subject attempts to approach one of these glimpses, they will be teleported to the dimension. Both SCP-5306- and SCP-5306-2 both have denizens, them being instances SCP-5306-1-A to SCP-5306-5 (with instances of SCP-5306-1-A being the most frequent)

Instances SCP-5306-1-A to SCP-5306-5  are a collection of small, sentient, Scandinavian Christmas Gnomes, such as ornaments and dolls, seeming to be constructed out of craft materials, such as yarn and cotton, that vary in size, shape, and intelligence, but all seem to have a tentacle like appendage on their “Heads”. These instances are seemingly free to exit object on there own accord, when they do exit, usually object will eject several non-anomalous objects, that appear to be Christmas decorations. Subjects will then proceed to ‘decorate’ objects containment cell, usually covering up any CCTV Footage within the cell. Instances are also universally hostile towards humans, usually attacking in swarms.

SCP-5306-A:  SCP-5306-A is a anomalous area located 500,000 meters below the earth, and appears to be a festively decorated subterranean village, inhabited by Instances SCP-5306-1-A to SCP-5306-5, and the villages infrastructure is assumed to have been created by Instances SCP-5306-1-A and SCP-5306-2. Additionally, SCP-5306-A is lightly guarded by 5-10 instances SCP-5306-1-A. During testing with object, subjects should take caution, and stealthy navigate SCP-5306-A.

SCP-5306-B:  SCP-5306-B is a anomalous area in a unidentifiable location, and appears to be a large metallic factory, with an estimated 200 million moving parts, that seems to be the creation site of instances of SCP-5306-1-A to SCP-5306-4, where these instances are massively produced. Test footage has shown instances of SCP-5306-1-A inputting materials such as fabric, cotton, yarn, and other craft materials, and fully functional instances of SCP-5306-1-A to SCP-5306-4 (They way they obtained these materials is currently unknown).

SCP-5306-1-A and SCP-5306-1-B:  SCP-5306-1-A is a collection of a uncountable number of small, Scandinavian Christmas Gnome Decorations, having a average height 17 centimeters, and has a tentacle like appendage on its “head”. Alone, an instance has been observed being capable of simple, repetitive tasks, but groups of these entities have shown to be capable of slightly more complex tasks, suggesting a hive-mind like connection. These entity’s seem to be taught to have universal aggression to humans, possibly by instances of SCP-5306-2 to SCP-5306-5, however, the affects of Class-C amnesties have shown to be effective on reducing aggression in SCP-5306-1-A, making testing possible.

SCP-5306-1-B is a collection of small, Scandinavian Christmas gnomes, that have a cylinder shaped body type, a short braids of pink yarn on each side, and a pink heart in the middle of the body, these instances have been seen extending their pink braids of yarn to accomplish tasks, which mostly consist of creating new structures within SCP-5306-A, and running the complex machinery of SCP-5306-B.

SCP-5306-2:  SCP-5306-2 is a collection of medium sized Scandinavian Christmas Gnomes, slightly taller than SCP-5306-1, in addition to be taller than SCP-5306-1-A, these entity’s seem to be more intelligent than SCP-5306-1. SCP-5306-2 instances have been seen guarding the interior of SCP-5306-A and SCP-5306-B, and also seem to be a part in the indoctrination process of SCP-5306-1.

** SCP-5306-3:** SCP-5306-3 is a large, Scandinavian Christmas Gnome, with these entity’s entire body almost fully covered with their ‘beard’, these entity’s are usually seen materializing when instances if SCP-5306-1-A and SCP-5306-2 ‘call’ for backup (using the bells at the end if their tentacle like appendage) these entity’s will consume their opponents, with only shreds of clothing recovered from the victim.

SCP-5306-4  SCP-5306-4 are a collection of 4 elongated Scandinavian Christmas gnome, with their bodies resembling that of a snake, with most of the body coated in the ‘beard’ these instances are the advisors of SCP-5306-5, but, can switch to combative duty if needed, if they do this, they usually attack their victims by strangulation, similar to that of a python.

SCP-5306-5:  SCP-5306-5 is a medium sized Scandinavian Christmas gnome, similar to appearance of SCP-5306-1, but on a smaller scale, this entity is confirmed to be the leader/mayor of SCP-5306-A and SCP-5306-B , as instances of SCP-5306-1-A to SCP-5306-4 seem to follow its order without question.

**SCP-5306-1-A testing protocol: ** When instances of SCP-5306-A exit object, there are to be collected and administered Class-C amnestic’s to remove the memory of their indoctrination, and placed within a testing chamber.

SCP-5306-1-A Testing Log: 

Test 5306-A

Standard Protocol was taken

A group of SCP-5306-1-A where placed in a standard test chamber with a mini obstacle course. With a caged instance if SCP-5306-1-A, after taking a moment to observe the course, they then began navigating the course, with some instances making them selfs bridges for the other entity’s to pass over, after 2 minutes, they successful completed the course, and were disposed of in a incinerator.

How does these things show more sense of teamwork then everyday people

-Senior Researcher [DATA EXPUNGED]
