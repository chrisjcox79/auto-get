Special Containment Procedures:  As of current, SCP-5958 does not need to be contained. All SCP-5958 resources are to be focused on successful reenactment of SCP-5958-B.

Prior Containment Procedures (Last Effective 12/31/2020):  SCP-5958 is to be kept in Site-411's Cognitohazardous Ward in a windowless cell. Food is to be delivered by Class-D personnel at a routine schedule.

Under no circumstances are Foundation personnel to interact with SCP-5958. Should any Foundation personnel attempt to interact with SCP-5958 in any capacity, they are to be terminated without warning.

Description:  SCP-5958 referred to a cognitohazardous entity by the name of Elias Cardinal that was in Foundation containment from April 7th, 2020 until January 1st, 2021. SCP-5958's cognitohazardous abilities were capable of changing one's memories.

SCP-5958's initial containment squad and Class-D personnel who interacted with SCP-5958 showed an express interest in arguing for SCP-5958, as well as behaviors not commonly associated with them prior to exposure to SCP-5958. As of current, only amnestic treatment seems to remove this effect.

Discovery:  On April 6th, 2020, SCP-5958 was found to be using its cognitohazardous abilities to amnesticize witnesses to assorted crimes1.

Foundation personnel acting as civilian authorities apprehended SCP-5958 later that night, before bringing SCP-5958 into Foundation containment at 1:29AM EST the following morning.

Explanation Report:  On January 1st, 2020, as part of Foundation-mandated containment checks2, SCP-5958 was interviewed by Senior Researcher Thatcher.

Senior Researcher Thatcher concluded the interview with the belief that SCP-5958's cognitohazardous abilities were not exclusive to SCP-5958 themselves, but rather to a kinetoglyph3 that SCP-5958 performed. Senior Researcher Thatcher claims that SCP-5958 demonstrated the kinetoglyph, but all attempts to recreate the effects have failed.

This kinetoglyph has been deemed SCP-5958-B. SCP-5958 itself has been deemed explained.

Footnotes
1. Including petty theft, breaking and entering, assault and battery, robbery, and various degrees of murder.
2. Annual process of examining all SCP objects in the Foundation's care to ensure containment procedures have not been obsoleted.
3. Kinetoglyphs, or kinetohazards, are mental and physical hazards that occur when an entity performs specific gestures and motions that interact with multiple spatial dimensions simultaneously, often with disastrous consequences.

To:  Dr. Rhett Thatcher

From:  Site Director Madeline Beckham

Date/Time:  January 6, 2021; 8:42PM EST

Subject:  Discussion About SCP-5958

~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Dr. Thatcher,

You have worked with the Foundation for nine years, Dr. Thatcher. Nine years of experience working with mechanical anomalies, working with everything from toasters to living constructs. And those nine years of top-quality work have earned you a few forgivable actions.

You used one of them up just now with SCP-5958. But you have very little experience with cognitohazards, apparently. As such, you are removed from all other SCP objects until this is fixed, and I want you in my office tomorrow morning at 9:00AM exactly to discuss how you're doing that.

Do not let this happen again. I can only defend you so many times.

Dr. Madeline Beckham

Site-411 Director
