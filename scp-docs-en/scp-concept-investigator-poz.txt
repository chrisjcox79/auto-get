SCP concept Investigator Poz

23 Nov 2020 03:05

Seeking Greenlight:  Yes

Page Type:  SCP Article

Hook:  A Private Investigator has been seen at multiple (31) instances near newly discovered SCPs. Seems to be unaffected by many SCPs and Amnestics.

Central Narrative:  Investigator Poz has been very elusive to the foundation and could be used as an asset. He, also, seems to know of SCPs and how to find them. Investigator Poz has not been located as of yet.

Page Layout:  Containment procedures and SCP information first. Then the instances of where he has been sighted.

Elevator Pitch:  The Private Investigator known as Investigator Howard Poz has been spotted at multiple sites of SCPs (31 known instances). He is drawn to SCPs for some anomalous reason not yet discovered. The reason the foundation knows this is an anomalous property is because he has found 3 in Russia, 2 in China, 4 in Canada, 12 across the USA, 3 in Brazil etc. Finding one or two is natural, but finding 31 before the foundation is unnatural. He seems to be unaffected by amnestics up to Class C if not more. All the SCPs he has been seen at are newly discovered. The foundation does not know if he is creating the SCPs or just discovering them. If the latter is true he could make a good asset for the foundation. However if the former is true, we need to take immediate action to secure the Investigator.

Level 3 Clearance required

The SCP known as Investigator Poz has been catalogued with 3 anomalous properties.

1. Immunity to Amnestics

2. Immunity to most SCP affects

3. The ability to create beings or objects with anomalous properties

Re: SCP concept Investigator Poz

23 Nov 2020 03:37

Responding to IRC Chat request.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

This tells me effectively nothing of what your article is going to detail. It's mysterious blank after mysterious blank, and too many blank spots inches closer and closer to nothing being said. Furthermore, an investigator that stumbles onto anomalies (obviously barring the amnestic thing) does not necessarily mean they are anomalous themselves, which is what the SCP format is about: documenting objects and entities that otherwise defy our understanding of modern science.

Altogether, there is no information here that indicates that this is ready to be made into an article. It's undercooked. I would suggest taking a moment to consider the following:

What kind of story do you want to tell?
What are the key points in that story?
How do you plan to showcase that in the SCP format?
What do you find entertaining about the article?

I am sorry, this seems like a cheap attempt to make an Author Avatar in your first article. This character is immune to basically all SCP anomalies AND can create anomalies which is the definition of OP.

I mean, he just seems like a supernatural SCP foundation.

I don't want to be harsh, but this doesn't interest me. I want to read stories about grounded characters with interesting motivations, that are centered around anomalies. I think you should go back to the drawing board, and think about a story you want to tell and then craft an anomaly that allows you to explore that story.
