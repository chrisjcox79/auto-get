Moved to Ideas Critique since there's no draft here.

Also noting that this:

An scp that knows everything there is know about the foundation, it's object class is: Thaumiel so it must be helpful? Well yes (sometimes) but mostly no, it hates almost everyone in foundation
附加记录：    

Doesn't make sense for a Thaumiel anomaly. If it's not a reliable form of containment, it shouldn't be Thaumiel. Doesn't matter how overpowered and intelligent it is; if the Foundation can't trust it, they won't use it. Moving forward, I suggest reading over this essay on common SCP pitfalls to get an idea of what elements to avoid putting in an SCP article.
