Re: SCP draft Crit

04 Jan 2021 00:54

Hello there,

due to clinical tone not being my forte I shall refrain from judging it. But I do like the concept and the visual it paints. The flow of the sentences is continuous and matches the current atmosphere you are aiming for. Although this kind of writing style might not be my preference. Still, this aspect is more of a subjective evaluation.

Storywise I think it makes for an interesting SCP and I like the idea of the changing classes. But to me, it feels like it is one image too much. I personally think that its effect could be stronger if the jump between them felt cruder and less caring.

All in all, I do enjoy the  you have painted here. The story itself has just enough ambiguousness left and captures a certain hunger for me, at least in me.

Good luck with your story.
