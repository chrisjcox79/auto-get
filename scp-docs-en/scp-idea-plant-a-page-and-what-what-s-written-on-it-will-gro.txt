SCP idea- Plant a page and what whats written on it will grow.

07 Dec 2020 02:37

Seeking Greenlights:  Yes

Page Type:  SCP Article

Page Layout:  Use of extensive test logs and interviews

Elevator Pitch:  Its an old notebook found with blank pages that when you write something on one of the pages and plant the page a tree grows and will fruit what's written on the page. The only limits is that what's written is that it must be a tangible item, the item is not fictitious, and the writer knows/has seen what they're writing about. In the event that item written does not fruit, the a paper with the written word will fruit instead. The fruited item will be identical to what was written but will taste, smell, and decay like an apple. Pages are replenished when closing the book.

Central Narrative:  Exploration of the uses and loopholes such as: creating a sapient being, creating existing SCPs, creating additional D class personnel, creating confinement cells, intention over what's written, and duplications of itself.

Hook/Attention-Grabber:  Creative potential of what can be made and some interviews with existential crises from the sapient creations an well as ideas of self image. The tone is slightly comedic with how discoveries of loopholes are made.

Additional Notes:  This is my first SCP and first work of creative fiction that I have done in a very long time. I am unsure to how in-depth I should go with the test logs or even whether this a creative enough concept.
