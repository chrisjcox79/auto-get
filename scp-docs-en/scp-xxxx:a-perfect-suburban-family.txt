SCP-XXXX: A Perfect Suburban Family

06 Jan 2021 01:40

Seeking Greenlight:  No

Page Type:  SCP Article

Genre (Optional): 

Elevator Pitch:  Completely normal family and Suburban house, or so it would seem. Somewhat self-containing, but regularly monitored. Strange sightings and initial anomalous properties such as extreme temperature.

+Narrative

-Narrative

Central Narrative:  The SCP Foundation caught wind of the supposed SCP due to a 911 call. As Foundation Agents further investigate the home and family they give contradictory reports. Initial reports show evidence that the home and entities residing in it exhibit anomalous properties. Further reports however state that there is nothing anomalous, and the Agents themselves suddenly become increasingly adamant that everything is perfectly normal and dismiss their own initial evidence which proved otherwise. A field test is done and Agents participating in it conduct separate investigations. All initial investigations during the field test turn up similar evidence of anomalous behavior. Further investigations done by the same agents show no evidence of anomalous behavior, as well as the Agents confidently stating that everything is normal. Foundation scientists theorize the home and family may be connected somehow, maybe even being one single entity that tries to keep up its own veil of normality around it for unknown reasons. Initial investigations turn up evidence of an anomaly, but later investigations do not as the SCP  The anomalous effects most likely apply individually. This theory is accepted and the home and family are classified as SCP-XXXX. Anyone investigating or documenting the SCP must work on it only once, and immediately report their findings. All initial evidence has been shown individually and only once to O5-██, O5-██, and the Ethics Committee for confirmation.

Hook/Attention-Grabber:  Is this a misunderstanding and the SCP Foundation is investigating a normal family, or is something else going on here?

Additional Notes: 

The SCP procedure of  gets implemented later for the documentation part, due to changes to the SCP document made by affected individuals (possible gimmick I'm thinking about) Furthermore, in the article itself it won't be as obvious that the home and family really are an SCP.

I'm mainly looking for constructive criticism. I'm basically just throwing the idea out there which is why it's plain and basic at this stage, and somewhat confusing.
