SCP-XXXX: Children of the Stars

29 Dec 2020 01:13

Incorporeal beings that possess children in order to walk among humans in order to perform a series of rituals to appease a deity of even higher power than them.

I moved and trimmed your thread summary, since it was taking up extra space in the main forum screen. Try to keep thread descriptions (the text in the little box under the thread title) and titles short so you don't crowd out other authors in the forum listing. ~Zyn

Seeking Greenlights:  Yes

Page Type:  SCP Article

Genre (Optional):  Mystery

Elevator Pitch:  Ten individual children have their consciousness taken over by incorporeal beings and become seemingly immortal for reasons that are currently unknown.

Central Narrative:  SCP-XXXX, which is not the children but rather what has taken control of the children's consciousness, are interviewed and studied by the researchers. During the interviews, they come to learn that they use the children in order to have a physical body in this reality and to accomplish particular rituals to appease a long overlooked god.

Hook/Attention-Grabber:  A possible, yet not direct relation to the Fifth Church and a connection to one of the monolithic figures depicted in one of the murals within SCP-4840.
