This needs expansion. Why is it a coca-cola brand ball? Why does it kill you? It just doesn't really work, in my opinion.

A few things:

testing shows it’s resistant to fire, gunfire, blunt force, high calibre explosions, and even being doused in lava
附加记录：    

Why would they test these things? When they discovered it, they would have realised it was indestructible, and it seems like it would be a waste of resources to just hit it with everything they have to destroy it. Just because something is indestructible doesnt mean that that fact needs to be tested, especially as foundation policy is to keep anomalies around.

it will start flashing the colors of the rainbow once something black, green, or shiny enters 2 feet within the radius of SCP-5279-2 or SCP-5279-1
附加记录：    

If it goes within 2 feet1 of itself, it activates? Because it is SCP-5279-2.

In general, this article has no narrative, and even an anomaly that's interesting on its own doesnt really work without that. So  -1.

Footnotes
1. By the way, that needs to be in metric, as the foundation uses UN standard units
