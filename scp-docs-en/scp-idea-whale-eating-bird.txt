SCP Idea Whale Eating Bird

20 Nov 2020 13:35

Seeking Greenlight: No, at least not yet.

Page Type: SCP Article

Genre:I haven't settled on one yet, though it will likely be action/suspense.

Layout: Information presentation

Elevator Pitch: A Keter class giant bird, living on an island in the Pacific, hunting whales like an osprey. Unnaturally large and fast, it occasionally attacks human ships.

Central Narrative: The Foundation deploys operatives to the North Pacific after a large bird attacks a group of whale watchers. After some investigation, they are unable to find anything, but stay posted. A few years later, a surfaced Soviet Submarine is sunk by the creature. The Foundation ramps up the search, eventually tracking it to a remote island between Chile and New Zealand. A submarine is sent to drop off a scout crew, but the sub is sunk because they underestimate how deep the creature can dive. A cruiser is deployed, and fires upon the creature when it attempts to dive at it. The bird survives, but the mission is called off to avoid potentially killing it. Another sub is deployed, this time equipped with an improved diving system to get down faster, and more ships surrounding the area to track it by RADAR. The crew treks up the small dormant volcanic island, and finds a nest made of logs with scattered bones. When the RADAR team radios them to say that the bird is returning home, they quickly find that it is coming faster than expected, and the submarine that they arrived on is unable to pick them up. They are forced to spend a night on the island with the bird, but it barely acknowledges them. It is determined that the creature is not a threat, and will be contained simply by baiting it away from coasts and sea lanes.

Hook: The Special Containment Procedure stands out compared to the SCPs I've read. The bird itself isn't dangerous enough to warrant destruction, and generally isolates itself. The Foundation would figure that it's more worthwhile to monitor it, and keep the thing away from humans.

This is my first Article, so I'm a bit nervous about it. Also, I'm aware that there is already a  Article, but the concept for this one is completely different.
