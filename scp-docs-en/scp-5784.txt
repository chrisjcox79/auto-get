                        

Item #:  SCP-5784

Object Class: Euclid

Special Containment Procedures: 

SCP-5784 is to be contained in Site-██ in a 4X4 meter cuboid room approximately 3 inches thick blast proof door. Any contact or any activity of SCP-5784 Should be immediately reported to Doctor ██████

In case of a containment breach, MTF-ETA-10 See No Evil should be dispatched to re-contain SCP-5784.

Description: 

SCP-5784 is a ordinary T.V which was used in the late 19s. SCP-5784 was brought to the foundations attention when mysterious disappearances in a orphanage in a nearby town located in Moscow, Russia.
