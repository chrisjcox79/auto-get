SCP-TBD: The Extradimensional Grand Station

25 Dec 2020 17:30

What if there was a subway system that went everywhere and everywhen? What if it went to pasts, presents, and futures where events unfolded differently? What wonderous possibilities could be waiting? And what untold horrors?

I moved and trimmed your thread summary, since it was taking up a lot of space in the main forum screen. Try to keep thread descriptions (the text in the little box under the thread title) short so you don't crowd out other authors in the forum listing. ~Zyn

Seeking Greenlights: Yes

Page Type: SCP Article

Genre: Document/Exploratory File

Elevator Pitch:

Entrance to anomaly first found in the form of an undocumented stairway in the New York City Subway shortly after 9/11.

This stairway, officially listed as SCP-TBD, also known as the Extradimensional Stairway by SCP personnel, leads to a massive series of passageways, officially listed as SCP-TBD-1

Visitors are guided by instances of artificial lifeforms consisting of programable matter/nanites. These instances take the form of the species they are interacting with.

This facility is controlled by a highly advanced AI system.

+block

–block

Due to the size of this facility, transportation to entrances are provided by a series of vacuum trains. According to readings provided by the onboard interfaces and the body-suit sensors worn by exploratory teams, these trains are capable of accelerating quickly to hypersonic speeds, but without producing the associated g-forces that would normally kill their occupants.

Central Narrative: Anomaly is a massive underground system that leads to an as yet undetermined number of places, including different planets and alternate realities. Various different species and alternate versions of humanity use this system, and the facility can be inhabited by a minimum of beings ranging in the thousands on any day.

Hook/Attention-Grabber: The finished article will list exploratory expeditions to different planets in the prime reality, as well as into different timelines. Many different references to different SCPs and settings will be included, such as instances of SCP-682 being escorted back to their stairway entrance, one by an android dragging him in a stun net (kicking, screaming, and shouting insults and threats), while the witnessing MTF just looks on, and an instance of a reality where the Foundation and anomalies has been disclosed to the public.

Additional Notes:

This is my first SCP entry. I currently have two versions of this working in my head: one is more positive and optimistic, and is my preferred direction. Then there is another that is abandoned, inhabited by an insane AI, malfunctioning killer androids, and horrors from alternate dimensions that have wandered into the system. Idea is extrapolated from some dreams I've had in the past, namely the vast vacuum train system and stairways that go deep underground and into a maze of passageways.
