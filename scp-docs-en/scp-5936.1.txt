PART TWO COMING SOON BARE WITH ME HERE
DISCLAIMERS:

YES, I KNOW THAT THE POEM SHOULDN'T RHYME AFTER TRANSLATING, IT IS A PLOTHOLE, I AGREE, BUT COME ON, A POEM THAT DOESN'T RHYME WOULDN'T BE AS COOL

DISCLAIMER 2 - YES, I KNOW THAT THE -1 ENTITIES ARE NOT DESCRIBED WITHIN THE DESCRIPTION BUT THE FILE IS IN-UNIVERSE OUTDATED PLUS, IN MY OPINION, DESCRIBING THEM BEFORE THEY APPEAR WOULD KINDA RUIN THE FUN THANK YOU

I started writing this approximately two months ago when my mother and I visited a part of our city that was almost forgotten about, with no one living there. Imagination did the rest of course.

This article is a series starter, with approximately ten future articles planned out to be set within this canon. Except some more soon.

Thanks for reading and I hope you enjoy :]

►Spoilers

▼Spoilers

The Children of Him used the entire district of a town as a ritual to open the fifth part of something (what that is is not said within the article, so you didn't miss anything, don't worry, please wait for future installments for the answer to what that is :p),
The district was frozen in time, essentially stopping when the ritual happened,
The lack of lifeforms is due to all bodies being taken for the ritual,
Only the bodies of the people were taken, whilst their souls remained in the place of the tragedy, screaming in anger, pain, and hatred for decades,
As the souls didn't want to be forgotten (hence the title of the article), they brought back the deleted region, only showing it to those that had the highest chance of helping them out (hence the requirements),
The souls want to get out, and therefore possess the bodies of those that come near, swapping their place with the newcomers in the 'noosphere of suffering',
The dead bodies were cultists that were present at the place of the tragedy who failed to protect themselves with the 'protection' glyph,
Provisional Outpost-120-1 was lost due to an attack and a giant 'body-swapping event', with 35936-1 instances getting out of the spatial anomaly,
Notice the multiple appearances of the number 'five', and keep that in mind for the future,
Why was the overvision of Site-120 given to five individuals, with one of them being a son of the previous director and the other being the previous project lead? Well, you'll have to wait for the answer to that one :p,
Asheworth is being suspected of somehow being involved in all of this (especially with the cult stuff) as the research into the Foundation database regarding the cult only lead to two files - one, redacted beyond belief, and Asheworth’s personnel file, which was heavily redacted as well.
