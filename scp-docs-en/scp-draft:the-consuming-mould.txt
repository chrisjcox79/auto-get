Re: SCP Draft: The Consuming Mould

24 Nov 2020 20:12

This idea has been done quite a few times before, virus, end of the world, don't let it escape. What makes yours different? I advise you to check out SCP-1462 as it has a similar effect as yours. My critiques and final thoughts will be included in several collapsibles below.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Critiques

Critiques

The container must be completely synthetic, and must be cleared of any organic matter, aside from SCP-XXX itself, any time that the container is opened.
附加记录：    

You should remove the comma between synthetic  and. You do not need a comma there because you have the word and  there. Commas only need to be put before the word and  if there is a list of three or more items or ideas. Instead format that sentence like this.

The container must be made of completely synthetic materials and must be cleared of any organic matter, aside from SCP-XXX itself, any time that the container is opened.

Lower clearances may enter, but only if accompanied by a level 3.
附加记录：    

Level three what? Ogre?

attempted termination of any subjects.
附加记录：    

I don't think that the foundation would use an unknown disease to kill people. They would kill by firing squad. If they really wanted to use an scp they would just use 173 or something. Why risk a containment breach on something that can be done with a lot less work.

To enter the wall, personnel must have level 2 clearance or higher.
附加记录：    

This should just be removed entirely. When a sentence is marked out there should be something after it that actually pertains to what the crossed out sentence was saying. Rather after yours you continue to explain stuff that has nothing to do with the prior sentence.

access for personnel of level 3 clearance or above only, as to prevent any SCP-XXXX-A or SCP-XXXX-B instances from breaking out and wreaking havoc.
附加记录：    

I would change the level clearance to level 4 if no experimentation or research is allowed as level three is just senior research personnel. Level four personnel are senior administrators tasked with keeping the foundation secure and preventing containment breaches.

Sorry for only critiquing your containment procedures but your article is rather long. Even though I couldn't give you tips on the rest I hope that this helps.

-

Finalthoughts

FinalThoughts

I see this theme of virus SCPs pop up quite often. Here is a list of a few and it can be very hard to take these common themes and make it interesting. I want you to read a few of these articles that have to do with viruses and take note of what you like and dislike about each. I also want to know what you will do for a plot line. In my opinion doing a virus/fungus SCP is so overdone it's going to be hard to make yours interesting and stand out from the rest. Good news is that there is an audience for everything. Definitely reach out to more experienced writers and critiquers for their thoughts as they will be a lot more helpful than I am and can help you set an interesting plot to go with the article. I hope you do well in your creative writing endeavors and have a good day!
