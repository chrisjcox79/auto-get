                        

SCP-5127 after the incident 5127-1

Item #:  SCP-5127

Object Class:  Safe Euclid

Special Containment Procedures:  SCP-5127 is to be placed on the personal break room, with no monitoring needed, however only security level 1 or higher personnel can touch or interact with it.with 1 security clearance level 2 guard and only clearance level 2 personnel may touch or interact with it.

Description:  SCP-5127 apparents to be a regular fridge, from both the inside and outside, it measures 60 inches in height and 28 in depth. Everytime someone opens its door any drink or food randomly appears, it looks like it can also randomly bring objects to it's inside. SCP-5127 can only grab any food or drink if it is within a 5 miles range, otherwise when you open it, it will absorb any organic material within 5 milles range, including water from a human body or even flesh. SCP-5127 apparents to have incurable effects on those who had their flesh ripped off.

Document SCP-5127a (regarding the recent accident 5127-1) : On December 2, 2009, Researcher Sophia █████ on lunch break time, attempted to use SCP-5127 to get something to eat, when Researcher Sophia █████ tried to interact with SCP-5127 a bit of flesh was removed from Researcher Sophia █████ finger skin. After Researcher Sophia █████ walked to the infarmary, doctors discovered that the place where the flesh was taken out was incurable.
