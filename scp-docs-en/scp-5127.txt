                        

Item #: SCP-5127 

Object class: Euclid 

Special Containment Procedures: 

SCP-5127 is to be kept inside a standard humanoid containment chamber. SCP-5127's scroll with an ink pen should be kept on his desk. SCP-5127 is to be worshipped weekly by Class-D personnel. In the event of a containment breach, SCP-5127's containment chamber is to be completely locked down, And chamber temperature should be dropped to -2.222 degrees celsius. Once the containment breach ends, The chambers temperature should be restored to normal. If SCP-5127 is not present in its containment chamber during a containment breach, All site doors are to be locked down to prevent escape of SCP-5127.

Description: 

SCP-5127 resembles an ancient Egyptian Anubis statue composed of clay. When temperature is above -2.222 degrees celsius and bellow 1,780°C (Clays freezing and melting point) SCP-5127 will become animate. Whenever 5127 is referred to as SCP-5127, SCP-5127 will demand to be referred to as Anubis.

It is found that SCP-5127 holds all knowledge of time before christ (B.C.) and writes it onto his scroll.

SCP-5127's scroll seems to be infinite, And therefore has been designated SCP-5127-1. At time of writing, SCP-5127 has written history of time up to 25 BC.

Interviewed: SCP-5127 

Interviewer: Doctor ███████ 

 

Interviewer:  Welcome SCP-5127, Have a seat.

SCP-5127:  Don't call me that. I am Anubis, And you must address me as Anubis.

Interviewer:  Uhm, Ok. Can you tell me about how you were created.

SCP-5127 (Muttering):  [INAUDIABLE] fool. Doesn't know common knowledge.

Interviewer:  Could you repeat that?

SCP-5127:  No. I am a god. You are a fool who doesn't address me by my name. You lock me up in some prison for gods, And you think that I will answer your question! Why can't I speak with one of my worshippers that visit me every week. They know how to talk to me. And how to worship me.

Interviewer:  I'm sorry, SCP-5127. But being a god doesn't do anything here. If you want any chance of getting out all you can do is atleast comply.

SCP-5127:  No.

Interviewer:  I see thats how it is, Then.

Doctor ███████ leaves and activates the room freezer. After SCP-5127 is frozen, SCP-5127 is sent back into containment.

 

Footnotes: 

A sample of the clay SCP-5127 is composed of shows that SCP-5127 was created 22 centuries ago.
