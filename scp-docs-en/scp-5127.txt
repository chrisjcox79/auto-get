                        

5127

Level2

Containment Class:
safe

Secondary Class:
keter

Disruption Class:
vlam

Risk Class:
caution

link to memo

SCP-5127 during process of forming

Special Containment Procedures:  SCP-5127 is to be kept in a standard humanoid containment chamber with walls made out of a thick layer of SCP-███ and a small layer of Titanium, a 20-meter area around SCP-5127 is to be flooded entirely and kept free from any form of human blood. In the case of an SCP-5127 Containment Breach the Site should be evacuated and locked down if SCP-5127-1 has not disappeared after 20 hours, MTF-Epsilon-11 and MTF-Eta-10 are to be called to the site.

Description: 

SCP-5127 appears to be a large sword composed of an unknown metal. It was discovered in Serbia, Nis in 2010 after a massive amount of reports of ghostly figure slaughtering people. The object is proven to be indestructible as it appears to regenerate lost material within a few seconds. All attempts to destroy the object have resulted in a massive containment breach of at least 10 SCP's at all times. If human blood is in an area of 10 meters around the object, it will create a large figure identified as SCP-5127-1. The period which takes the figure to form is still uknown. SCP-5127-1 appears to be a large humanoid figure composed of an unknown type of smoke and crimson-red eyes. The figure will first hunt down the human which has triggered it (now identified as SCP-5127-2). After successfully terminating the activator it will attempt to hunt down every living creature it has seen at least once while being active. SCP-5127-1 was proven to be extremely strong and durable to the point of tank shells and explosive ammunition barely affecting the figure. After the figure has hunted down its prey it will begin consuming it in a way similar to amoeba. It has been proven that the figure will grow stronger, taller, and faster with its killing spree increasing. The period of being active is determined by the number of prey it has hunted down. The function of the system of how much prey increases period is currently unknown.

Notes: 

Following The Event of ██December 20██, SCP-5127 will occasionally trigger and release SCP-5127-1 every ██th December ████ and kill between 100-189 human entities before disappearing into SCP-5127 again. Each of these events will be logged as 5127-██-Oct-██:██

Termination attempt A1 - 1st of October 20██ 

Procedure:  (D class is to approach SCP 5127-1 and to trigger. After SCP 5127-1 forms, MTF is to attempt to terminate the figure by opening fire on it using explosive ammunition. If the Class D refuses to trigger SCP 5127 they are to be terminated)

Dr █████:  D-934131 approach SCP 5127

D-934131:  (approaches the object)

Dr █████:  D-934131 proceed to cut yourself using it.

D-934131:  I don't know doc, is that necessary?

Dr █████:  D-934131 you will be terminated if you ignore any further instructions

D-934131:  (D-934131 proceeds to trigger SCP 5127-1).

SCP 5127-1:  (starts forming)

D-934131:  What the hell is that !?

SCP 5127-1:  (Begins walking towards D-934131)

D-934131:  Stay away from me!

Captian Augustus:  Open fire!

MTF:  (Opens fire but it doesn't seem to affect SCP 5127-1)

D-934131:  Go awa- (SCP 5127-1 slit D-934131's throat and afterward proceeds to breach

out of its containment).

Announcement:  SCP 5127-1 has breached the containment all personal are to evacuate

immediately!

Results:  SCP 5127-1 has breached out of its containment chamber and caused a

containment breach of ██ Keter class SCPs and claimed the lives of 9 personal.

All further termination attempts and tests are to be committed outside the facility.
