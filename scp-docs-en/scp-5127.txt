                        

SCP-5127, footage upon discovery

Item #:  SCP 5127

Object Class:  Safe

Special Containment Procedures:  SCP 5127 is to be kept in a 2m*2m*2m containment cell made out of titanium. Personal are to be at least 4 meters away from the artifact. In the case of the containment breach, MTF Sigma-9 and ETA-10 is to be called to the site. All deceased within range of SCP 5127 are to be incinerated.

Description: 

SCP 5127 is an ancient artifact of Mayan origin. The artifact is in the shape of a wolf and constructed of an unknown metal. The artifact appears to have the ability to resurrect people within 2 meters of it into mutants (labeled as SCP 5127-1) in a period of 2-8 minutes. After the deceased creature being resurrected the mutation will begin by their pigment losing its color and becoming grey, the number of muscles and limbs increasing, and their height decreasing. After the completion of the process, the mutant will become significantly more aggressive and predatorial. Mutants as well appear to listen to the commands of SCP 5127-A. SCP 5127-A is a creature carrying and protecting the artifact.

The creature has the ability to shapeshift but most of the time it will take a form similar to the Mayan god Itzamna. The creature possesses incredible strength, the ability to speak, and is massive hatred toward human beings and it will attempt to murder them in order to gain more servants. The creature displays incredible weakness to titanium. It has been determined that the creature is of Mayan origin.

Background: 

SCP 5127 was discovered in an ancient Mayan temple by a tourist. The tourist was recording the inside of the temple when he spotted an artifact under the rubbles. The tourist proceeded to pick up the artifact after which the creature formed and murdered the tourist. The foundation discovered the artifact, protector, and the footage after a high amount of reports of mutant creatures wandering around the Mayan temples. According to currently decoded Maya glyphs, the artifact was used as a weapon against nowadays Americans. After inevitable failure, the artifact protector and its powers were hidden for unknown reasons. Its creation is as well currently unknown

going to clean your filth from our land.

Dr. Vaughan:  What was this artifact originally used for?

(The creature didn't answer)

SCP 5127-A  I am not obligated to answer your questions filth!
