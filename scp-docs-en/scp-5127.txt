                        

Item #:  SCP-5127

Object Class:  Euclid

Special Containment Procedures:  scp-5127 is being contained at site-06-3 until the foreseeable future. No personnel should stand within 5 meters of scp-5127 this is due to its anomaly. No special amenities are to be given to scp-5127.

Description:  scp-5127 is an anomalous object that resembles a wax figure of an unknown origin. scp-5127 seems to be completely inanimate until it is approached by any living subject in 5 meters. If a living being enters the 5 meters seclusion zone scp-5127 will become semi-conscious and will not return to its inanimate state until it neutralises the subject. attempts to interview scp-5127 during its semi-conscious state but these attempts failed. All testing has been suspended.
