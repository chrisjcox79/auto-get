                        

Item  #: SCP-5127

Object Class : Euclid

Special Containment Procedures : SCP-5127-1 is to be contained in an average sized concrete room which holds a trapdoor with a chute which dispenses SCP-5127-1's meals. The room is to be soundproof. The room will have a bulletproof window looking in. Once a week a janitorial team is to clean the room of the matter produced by SCP-5127-1. The room is to be guarded by a single armed guard.

SCP-5127-2 is to be surrounded by concrete walls and guarded at all times. All entry must be via helicopter and authorized by a Site director. All living things spotted inside are to be terminated immediately.

SCP-5127-3 instances are to be confined to straitjackets and bound to a chair in an isolation room. No personal are to interact with SCP-5127-3 instances unless authorized.

Description:  SCP-5127-1 is a humanoid entity reaching 10 feet in height, and weighing 517 pounds. The entity's arms are disproportionate to its body, reaching down to its ankles, and ending in long, claw-like fingers. The entity's legs and torso seem to be proportionate to its height. It is skinny and boney, wears no clothing, and has skin the color of rustic metal. SCP-5127-1 is not to be viewed through photography or video. All who do are to be labeled as an SCP-5127-3 instance, and they are to be contained appropriately. Artwork of the entity does not have the same effect. SCP-5127-1 has the ability to open small holes across it's entire body. When this happens, the entity can play any known sound at any volume at any known location. Possible sounds can include conversations, music, and sound effects. And person who views the entity during this process will experience extreme symptoms of tripnaphobia, even if they have never had it before. SCP-5127-1 was brought into containment after reports came that an entire village had been killed, and that anybody who had walked into the village had not come out. This village has been marked as SCP-5127-2, and anomalous effects are as follows.

SCP-5127-2 is a village of average size, and it used to have a population of 257. After the village was attacked by SCP-5127-1, anomalous effects took place. Anybody who enters the village will be sent to SCP-5127-2, an endless village of houses and shops. Exit is only possible via helicopter. Reports have been made on the buildings. The further one goes into SCP-5127-2, the more anomalous it seems. Buildings have been reported to be made of glass, wires, and human flesh. Due to unknown circumstances, entities very similar to SCP-5127-3 can be seen wandering around. Interaction with these entities will cause them to become hostile and cause them to attack by swiping clawed hands. SCP-5127-3 instances are described as follows.

SCP-5127-3 are humans who have viewed SCP-5127-1 through picture or video. These people will begin to experience stress, anxiety, hallucinations, thoughts of suicide, and thoughts of genocide within 4 hours of viewing. Instances will attack all living things they see, and will hurt themselves intentionally in the process. Instances seem to be nearly immortal, as firing 3 full magazines of an automatic rifle into them has no effect, despite causing bullet wounds and bleeding.
