                        

Item #:  SCP 5127

Object Class:  Safe

Special Containment Procedures: 

SCP 5127 is to be kept in a glass box in a 4m*2m*3m room made out of titanium and monitored 24/7. If personal tries to resurrect the deceased body for 2nd time they are to be

stopped before the process of resurrection is completed. If SCP 5127-2 enters the body it is to be incinerated immediately.

Description: 

SCP 5127 was discovered in Chaco, Argentina in 2003 after civilians were consistently reporting deceased people rising from their graves.

SCP 5127 consists of SCP 5127-1 and SCP 5127-2. SCP 5127-1 is a strangely shaped book with covers made of an unknown harmful white substance and pages are made out of leather. The object appears to have the ability to resurrect deceased people.

The process of it resurrecting people consists of users bringing deceased humans within 2 meters to the object and writing the deceased human's name. People resurrected (now labeled as SCP 5127-3) do not appear to be hostile towards personal and seem to have trouble controlling their body.

Subjects have full memory of their past. Subjects also seem to be in better condition than they were in before their death. Subjects are more intelligent and stronger.

The subjects will be alive for a certain time. Time for how long the subject will be alive isn't determined since some instances of subjects died within

1 hour while some instances of subjects are still alive years after being resurrected. The subject can be resurrected only once.

If the user tries resurrecting

the same person twice the person trying to be resurrected will become possessed by SCP 5127-2. SCP 5127-2 is a shapeless black entity that comes out of SCP 5127-1 when it's triggered.

SCP 5127-2 gets triggered every time the user tries to resurrect the same deceased human twice. After possessing the body the human body will regenerate itself and grow 2 meters taller

than its original form, the skin will be peeled off and muscle tissues will get black pigment. The possessed body will afterwords roam around the accessible areas slaughtering

everything in its peripheral vision. SCP 5127-2 possesses incredible strength and resistance to bullets. Once the period between 6-7 hours passes or the possessed body is taken

down it will leave the deceased person's body and go back in SCP 5127-1.

Notes:  Following the event of ████████ SCP 5127-2 will now attempt to possess bodies that are being resurrected for 1st time.
