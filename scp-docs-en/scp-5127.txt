                        

Item Number: 5127

Object Class: Euclid

Special Containment Procedures 

SCP-5127 Is to be kept in light containment storage at Site-██ for testing with D-Class Personnel.

Any instances of SCP-5127 found outside of it's regular storage location must be reported to the SCP Foundation at once, as a large concentration may easily take control of an entire city if spread throughout the water system, as has previously occurred in the state of ███████.

Description: 

SCP-5127 is a parasite that attaches itself to it's victims through ingestion of SCP-5127.

It manifests as a purple mold usually found on items such as fresh vegetables and fruits.

It can multiply and spread throughout entire farms in mere minutes, as it has been enhanced by unknown means to find the quickest way to infect a human body.

Known Side-Effects of ingesting SCP-5127: 

Approximately 12-48 hours after having consumed 5127 it will begin to use your body as a blood bag.

It will start by draining small amounts of the body's blood, until at least two weeks have passed.

If SCP-5127 is not destroyed before this time, the following side effects will be irreversible:

Eyes Sores, Weak Knees, Extreme Weight Loss, Pale Skin, Heart Burn, and Cramps.

After experiencing any of these symptoms, the victim of SCP-5127 is deemed already dead. At this point in time the parasite will start taking influencing the thoughts of the victim's mind, attempting to separate them from any other persons who try to interact with them. Soon the victim will lose all free will and the parasite will take full control.

D-Class Test Results 

███ D-Class personnel have ingested SCP-5127, each with results varying in-between the above side-effects.

After their mind has been completely overtaken by the parasite, the body will go into anaphylactic shock for approximately 3hrs as the parasite starts moving and working the limbs.

In █ cases, the bodies of the D-Class have turned completely purple before melting into a bright liquid, which was then quickly burned and destroyed.

If you accidentally come in contact with SCP-5127 

Have yourself taken to the nearest SCP Site to be examined and have the parasite removed before any physical or psychiatric damage is made to the body or mind.
