                        

SCP-5127

Item #:  SCP-5127

Object Class:  Keter

Special Containment Procedures:  Personnel with appropriate clearance are able to be informed of SCP-5127. Should there be any signs of SCP-5127 approaching planet Earth, O5 Council are to be informed about it immediately.

Description:  SCP-5127 is a planet roughly six (6) times bigger than planet Jupiter. SCP-5127's shape is complementary to an oblate spheroid. It appears to look identical to planet Earth. It is believed that one (1) Earth year on SCP-5127 is equal to ten (10) Earth years on Earth. Due to this, SCP-5127's spin speed is roughly 4471m/s.

SCP-5127 does appear to have O23.

Addendum 5127.1:  SCP-5127 has two (2) moons orbiting it: SCP-5127-1 and SCP-5127-2. These moons do not have any abnormalities compared to the moon known as “Luna”. SCP-5127 orbits around a sun that is called SCP-5127-3. SCP-5127-3 has twenty (20) x-class solar flares in one (1) Earth year. Should one occur, it will hit SCP-5127. However, SCP-5127-3's solar flare that hits SCP-5127 makes it larger depending on the size of the flare.

Addendum 5127.2:  SCP-5127 was discovered by NASA4 at ██-██-20██. It was discovered that SCP-5127 was moving towards planet Earth.

Footnotes
1. Oxygen.
2. An object that did not originate from SCP-5127, SCP-5127-1, SCP-5127-2 and/or SCP-5127-3.
3. However, Phaeton is a hypothetical planet and Dr. ███████ suggested that Phaeton was destroyed due to centrifugal forces.
4. National Aeronautics and Space Administration
