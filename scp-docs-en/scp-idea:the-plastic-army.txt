SCP Idea: The Plastic Army

22 Dec 2020 18:23

Seeking Greenlights:  Yes

Page Type:  SCP Article

Genre:  Drama/Emotional, History

Page Layout:  The page will have the basic SCP article layout, but with addendums sharing the development of the army’s story.

Elevator Pitch:  A collection of living Gundam models. Have organs underneath the plastic armor plates, but they do not match any living species. The models speak in the memories of American soldiers in the Vietnam War that seem to be from another timeline.

Central Narrative:  Tells the story of American soldiers in the Vietnam War. The models were found in a former Vietnamese battlefield with significant damage. On the surface, there is nothing particularly strange about them. However, there appear to be functioning organs beneath the plastic armor plates that do not match up to any known species. Although they appear to be human-like, they contain DNA that varies greatly from those of humans. The living parts of all of the models are of the same species. The models cannot move, but they are known to speak. It speaks from the perspective of several soldiers such as General Richard Hall from the dates of November 8, 1955, to May 9, 1961. It begins with Richard Hall getting drafted, and later the other soldiers are seen getting drafted. The story from here on out consists mostly dialogue between the models, shouting during battles, and various promotions. At any points in time where a bodily fluid would be excreted by one of the soldiers, the model excretes the same fluid. Recorded excretions include blood, sweat, tears, and urine. Although the story encompasses almost seven years, the full seven years are not played out. Only select days are played out. These days include battles, significant interactions, and promotions. The full day is always played out. On the final day, there is a battle that all of the soldiers take part in. In this battle, they each comment on a creature that looks somewhat like a dog before dying. Upon death, they bleed profusely for 5 minutes before the organs cease function. After 214 hours, the story begins again the exact same way, in a cyclical nature. The real versions of these soldiers all had different positions. They all died on July 24, 2006.

Hook/Attention-Grabber:  Emotional story told by Gundams.

Additional Notes:  I just really want this to be its best, since I'm not a super experienced writer.
