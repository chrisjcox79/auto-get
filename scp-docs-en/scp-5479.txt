The former entrance to an instance of SCP-5479-1.

Item #:  SCP-5479

Object Class:  Keter

Special Containment Procedures:  SCP-5479-1 should be stored in the nearest Foundation site if they are small enough to be clandestinely transported. If not, they are to be covered in sound-muffling foam and permanently condemned.

Description:  SCP-5479 is a phenomenon where enclosed spaces containing precisely one living human will inexplicably become impossible to enter or leave. Any physical boundaries will become rigid and indestructible while an invisible membrane will prevent egress through what would otherwise be gaps in the space. Following this, the SCP-5479 affected space (SCP-5479-1) will be modified in some way, generally incorporating some mechanism or physical property which is harmful - but not lethal - to the person inside (SCP-5479-2). Instances of SCP-5479-2 are immortal and have never been observed to perish or lose consciousness even when it would be logical for them to do so.
Examples of SCP-5479:

Date:  4th of November, 1977.

Target:  Jonathan Myers, 47 years old at the time of becoming an instance of SCP-5479-2, male, previously accused and acquitted for the drowning of his infant daughter.

Effect:  Myers became trapped inside his car while parked outside his workplace. The interior of the vehicle rapidly filled to the brim with wet cement which, as of January 1st 2018, has yet to set. The car produces muffled knocking sounds on occasion, as if something is striking it from the inside.

Date:  3rd of March, 2007.

Target:  Clara Janice, 17 years old, female, cited by the late Edgar Santiago as one of the reasons behind his suicide.

Effect:  Janice’s bedroom became an instance of SCP-5479-1 while she was asleep. Her curtains were drawn at the time so the exact nature of the phenomenon cannot be established, however researchers have reported hearing the creaking of rope and wet squelching while standing outside her room.

Date:  Unknown.

Target:  Unknown.

Effect:  The SCP-5479-1 instance is a coffin dated to 2,300 BC, unearthed in the Shaodong region of China at the bottom of a foul smelling lake. SCP-5479-2, which has been pulverized beyond recognition, constantly grows new eyes from random points on its body. The eyes constantly pop from being squeezed against the side of the casket, causing aqueous humours to leak out of its seams. Of note is that there is a local legend in the area which describes a young woman who miscarried kidnapping the children of other families, all of which gathered together and wept .

Effect:  Ashwood broke into a chemistry classroom after which it became an instance of SCP-5479-1. A , et cetera. Ashwood is currently in eighty seven jars as part of a scientific investigation into the rate at cockroaches will eat different parts of the body.

Date:  19th of April, 1989.

Target:  Albert White, 68 years old, male, discovered to be part of a child sex trafficking ring after being affected by SCP-5479.

Effect:  A spherical zone with a radius of two metres centered on White transformed into an instance of SCP-5479-1 while he was standing alone in his chapel. Numerous incandescent iron pins have been forced into his body in areas such as under his fingernails, into his eyes and through his throat. His nerves have been partially extracted from his body, presenting themselves as wing-like objects behind his back. A length of razor wire has been looped under the skin of his nether regions and is constantly moving in a uniform manner. A torus of molten lead with a diameter of two inches has been wrapped around his throat.

Date:  20th of July, 1958.

Target:  Patrick Cain, 27 years old, male, owner of a slaughterhouse whose products were found to contain human tissue. He was never definitively connected to the scandal.

Effect:  Confined within his house. Every day at 6am the sounds of various tools such as angle grinders, electric saws and screwdrivers emit from his bedroom. On occasion, they are drowned out by loud screaming or moist gurgling. At noon, crunching and the shuffling of cutlery can be heard from the kitchen alongside various compliments to . At 6pm, the bathroom produces a loud and continuous crunching until midnight whereupon the house falls silent. The cycle repeats every 24 hours. 6 sausages are occasionally ejected from the front door, typically incorporating Cain's teeth, nail clippings and hair. Since the discovery of the anomaly the total mass of these sausages has exceeded 1,089kg.
