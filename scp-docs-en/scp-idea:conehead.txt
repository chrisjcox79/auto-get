SCP Idea: Conehead

18 Dec 2020 15:54

Seeking Greenlights:  Yes

Page Type: SCP Article 

Genre (Optional): N/A 

Page Layout (Optional):  Basic SCP article, maybe with a few test logs.

Elevator Pitch:  A entity that has the appearance of a dog, except all of its fur is a clean dark grey. It also has a Elizabethan collar (cone) on its head, hence the name. If you attempt to look at the face in the cone, there is a strange force, almost like a invisible wall, or a reverse magnet stopping you from looking. No matter how much force, you simply cannot look.

Central Narrative:  SCP-XXXX is a entity that closely resembles a domesticated dog with the height of approximately 0.3 meters. It has dark solid grey fur all around it’s body, and has no tail. Weirdly enough, it has no body parts except for limbs and the torso, and head. The bottom of its feet where you would usually find paws, is just more fur. It’s crouch where you would find the anus and genitals, has nothing. It’s just more fur. It also has a Elizabethan collar on its head, that covers all of its face. If somebody attempts to look at it, a strange force stops them from doing so. No matter how hard they try, and no matter how much force pushing to see its face, the invisible wall simply will not go away. If it turns it’s head, so does this invisible wall. If a camera or any kind of device that can capture imagery is placed to where it could take a picture of its face, the device will not take the picture. If a video is already being recorded and is attempted to be placed in the angle of recording SCP-XXXX, the camera will break. The entity does not make any sound, at all. Not even footsteps or body movements. The entity is believed to be very stupid, likely even brain dead. This is plausible because the entity has been seen doing mindless things like occasionally bumping into the walls of it’s containment chamber, wandering around completely unaware of its surroundings, and little to no awareness of it being in danger. The entity seems harmless to most living beings. It is not known if it is not intelligent enough to harm others, or simply dosent have intentions of doing so. The only harm it does, which might not even be on purpose is when it interacts with domestic dogs. Oddly enough, the canine family are the only living creatures that can view its face. However, once the canine sees its face, it will start to act scared, nervous, and various other worrisome feelings and thoughts. This activity will continue for days. Eventually after a week or so, the canine will enter a state of sudden extreme panic and fear, but eventually stops after a few hours. This can lead up to the canine even dying. The behaviour stops after 5-8 weeks, and the canine goes back to normal.

Sorry for making a bit of a lengthy post, but I thought this was an interesting idea. Please let me know what you think, and if you have criticism, I will gladly take it. Thanks for reading!
