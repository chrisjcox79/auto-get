                        

Item #: SCP-5188

Object Class: Safe

Special Containment Procedures: this object must be kept in specific conditions to appease itself; must be placed in a room similar to the one that it was found in, that being a teenager boy’s room with some peculiar specialities. The room must be blacked out and away from most light, it must contain a gaming pc with RGB lights being the only illuminated object; there must also be an exceptionally small cot which is presumed to be the bed of the teenager boy. Must also have a formula series F08-NR DX racer that is set to the shortest setting for optimum recollection of the room in which this object was ceased. This object should be placed in the direct centre of the room to avoid any meaningful disasters. If deciding to enter the room for whatever reason subjects must be walking on their knees to replicate the height of the teenager as well as excess grease placed into the hair on the subject choosing to enter; it is recommended that they have spots for a more convincing appeal. SCP-5188-2 must be let in every midnight to feed SCP-5188 -1 and can only be let in via cat flap as SCP-5188-2 cannot interact with doors.

Description: SCP-5188-1 is said to be a clean pan, this pan is exceptionally clean and under close observation has shown not a single microbe or pathogen or any other grime to be found upon this object. If anything is placed onto SCP-5188 it will be almost instantly cleaned which sometimes leads to chaotic affects (see more in addendum 1).

Despite possessing some supernatural powers this entity is a perfect carbon copy of the latest █████ expertise non-stick aluminium frying pan of 21cm with the only difference being that it has danger mouse imprinted where the █████ logo should be with the initials of A.L; █████ have no recollection of this pan ever being created and the day this would found was the same day that █████ stopped production of the █████ expertise non-stick aluminium frying pan of 21cm.

supposed teenager boy that A.L would have looked like

Dimensions are 21cm diameter with a depth of 6cm. The handle is 13cm long and 5cm in diameter. Weighs 853g. Is oven safe up to 175 degrees Celsius but is not microwave safe, proven to be dishwasher safe but dishwasher has no cleaning effect. Black in colour with a non-conductive handle

SCP-5188-2: white blob-like lifeform that dwells in the same living space alongside SCP-5188-1; this will not leave the sight of the pan and will provide £200 or $270.58 when SCP-5188-1 calls out for it late at night. The blob consists of an off white viscous matter. Seems to act like a fatherly figure towards SCP-5188-1.

Addendum 1: SCP-5188-1 has cleaned numerous objects and so testing has exceeded point or trailing on a human subject. Dr ██████ obliged to testing and after following the procedures went to try and be cleaned by SCP-5188-1. Upon stepping on the pan the effects were clear to see. All and any dirt was soon removed by the pan and Dr ██████ said that he felt little microorganisms crawling around his entire body at once. The test had to soon be stopped as he was aggressively being cleaned and anything impure was stripped from his body so all hair and grime on his skin was removed alongside any bugs or microscopic creatures living around his body but without these to protect us in our everyday life he soon dropped from feeling so weak and the experiment had to be cancelled. Upon examination his skin was perfectly clean but all slight wounds on any part of him let the pan work it’s way inside and most of his organs and blood flow had stopped.

Addendum 2: the teenager boy that this pan was taken from has never been heard or seen before; after taking SCP-5188-1 people seem to have forgot about the teenager boy and the boy himself has not been seen since the removal of the pan but SCP-5188-1 has not seemed to notice the disappearance of the boy due to our safety precautions

Addendum 3: if the safety procedures are not followed then SCP-5188-1 seems to grow a level of sentience by spraying out every disease and illness within a 10 metre radius until every surface is completely covered; upon touching any of these surfaces it will kill people instantly from every disease (this includes diseases that have long been extinct like plague, cholera, scurvy, smallpox, etc). It is not known how SCP-5188-1 has these and it is said that this pan could have been on earth for a very long time slowly gathering all these diseases but then SCP-5188-1 must also possess time travel or future prediction abilities.

Addendum 4: further testing has shown that the mysterious disappearance the teenager boy links with the finding of a clean pan and the initials carved into the pan coincide with the name of the boy that went missing; the pan has also been known to groan late at night and will only stop when precisely £200 or $270.58 worth of food has been placed into the room showing that SCP-5188-1 is alive to some extent. Studies of the pan show that this pan makes people ‘feel’ lifeless when around the object and so SCP-5188-1 must have some correlation with the taking and adapting of souls.

Incident SCP-5188- 1: MTF Zeta-9 (“Mole Rats”) were sent to investigate strange noises coming from a homestead in ██████. Upon first entry the home was covered in the viscous white substance and was oozing out of every surface. The echoing of groaning was throughout the homestead and was most powerful in A.L’s room. When entering the room the groaning reached 15 kilohertz. Upon inspection there rested SCP-5188-1 in the mathematical centre of the room with SCP-5188-2 just about to provide £200 worth of food; MTF Zeta-9 are not sure of where this £200 worth of food came from as house was completely barren at the time. MTF Zeta-97 and the rest of the squadron was forced to flee.

Addendum 5: SCP-5188-2 has been proven to be made of male gametes: sperm after vigorous sensory testing and has been dubbed by junior staff as “coomer dad” due to its protective nature over SCP-5188-1
