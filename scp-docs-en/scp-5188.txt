Item #:  SCP-5188

Object Class:  KETER OH GOSH RUN

Special Containment Procedures:  SCP-5188's cell door must be chained by 10 heavy padlocks, have a 5 digit access code, a level 4 card reader, and voice and oral recognition. NOBODY is allowed into SCP-5188's cage UNDER ANY CIRCUMSTANCES  (See incident 5188-A-1).

All level 2 and below must stay 100 feet away from SCP-5188. There must be 5 heavily armed guards, with direct access to the MTF and RRT.

Description:  SCP-5188 is a standard amazon box, with no anomalous effects. SCP-5188's anomalous affects only trigger when touched. When touched, SCP-5188 will suddenly sprout human arms and legs, through the bottom. It looks like a mother████ing cardboard spider. SCP-5188 will then undergo a rage state, where it will kill everything in its path by [DATA EXPUNGED] them.

Exactly 69 seconds (1 minute and 9 seconds) after a human is killed, more  drag them away. They then appear again, with their face taped to the cardboard box. These spiders manifest only when SCP-5188 kills someone, therefore they are dubbed SCP-5188-1, and are to be treated as such.
