Scp 5133

23 Dec 2020 19:55

Seeking Greenlight:  Yes

Page Type:  Scp Article

Elevator Pitch:  Scp 5133 gets contained for an anomaly that has is blocked by personnel, as any description of appearance or behavior will cause the people involved to worship Scp 5133.

Central Narrative:  Site 138 staff had all fallen into Scp 5133's memetic ability, no replies had been filed to site fund managers, two mtf operatives go to try to contain any escaped anomaly's after believing that site 138 had suffered a containment breach. They arrive and all scp's are contained except Scp 5133 where they report that it is memetic. Perfect containment cant be made as the original anomoly can be discovered and cared for if no information can be learnt.

Hook/Attention-Grabber:  The archived containment procedures are not what anyone would expect, as it states any personnel showing disliking to Scp 5133 are to be terminated via incinerator, and the archived description has been blocked by upper site command.

Additional Notes:  Site 138 is just a random number I picked so finding a site that stores anomalies that could have been corrupted by a memetic hazard. And a site that can house things you dont know.

Re: Scp 5133

24 Dec 2020 14:53

+Let'sit!

-Let'sit!

I won't mince words here; this entire concept page is quite messy and doesn't fully convey everything I need to know about your idea.

5133 gets contained for an anomaly that has is blocked by personnel
附加记录：    

I have no idea what you're trying to say here.

as any description of appearance or behavior will cause the people involved to worship Scp 5133.
附加记录：    

Just like any number of mind-altering, compulsion anomalies. Uninteresting.

Well, even bland anomalies can be passable if the story execution is good enough, so let's take a look at that.

two mtf operatives go to try to contain any escaped anomaly's after believing that site 138 had suffered a containment breach.
附加记录：    

A false containment breach could be a good Hook if this idea wasn't so messy as well as just plain incomplete. What is the anomaly? All we get is that it's probably Memetic, and that it gives you a compulsion to worship it.

Perfect containment cant be made as the original anomoly can be discovered and cared for if no information can be learnt.
附加记录：    

I'm going to assume 'can be discovered and cared for if no information can be learnt' was intentional. So, the best way to contain it is tonot know about it?

By that logic, just destroying all data files on it and administering amnestics would be the way to go.

I'm sorry, but I'm immensely not feeling this.

'Non-descript thing you can't know about' has the potential to be interesting, especially with a false containment breach, but this is severely underdeveloped and was so messily presented, picking through it was more difficult than it should have been.

My immediate recommendation would be to find a way to make the Anomaly less bland. Develop that.

Second, make this page kess messy.

Apologies if this was harsh.

Anywho, I hope I helped and I bid you a good day. 👍

P.S.  I recommend not choosing an SCP number while you're still trying to obtain Greenlights. SCPs in development are labelled as SCP-XXXX. The SCP-5133 slot could be taken well before you get to it. It's a possibility.
