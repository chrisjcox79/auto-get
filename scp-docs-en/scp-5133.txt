                        

Containment class:  safe

Item number:  5133

an image captured of SCP 5133 during test 5133-1

Containment procedures:  SCP 5133 is to be kept in a locked container in darkness. The containers are to be guarded by MTF President-5, the crab catchers. No children are allowed near SCP 5133. Any instances of SCP 5133-1 are to be squished underfoot.

Description:  SCP 5133 are a collection of chocolate eggs of unidentified brand. They have no packaging and small lines and swirl designs on the exterior. When put under an X-ray they appear hollow. Their anomalous properties appear when broken, a fully formed crab of unknown species will emerge from the egg, henceforth known as SCP 5133-1. This SCP 5133-1 is biologically identical to other crabs, however after mating a female will give birth to chocolate eggs. The process behind this is unknown as the eggs seem to be larger than the SCP 5133-1 instaces themselves.

+5133-1

-5133-1

[Date: ███]

Subject: 

D-███

Procedure: 

D-███ was ordered to enter a well lit featureless room with 3 instances of SCP 5133. He was told to interact with the eggs how he wished.

Results: 

D-███ cracked open the first egg with his fingers, the resulting SCP 5133-1 instance snapped at his finger, drawing small amounts of blood, then retreating. The second egg was stepped on, when D-███'s foot was lifted a crushed crab lay mixed with the egg shell. The D class then inserted the 3rd egg into his mouth and bit down. He said that the egg tasted just like any other chocolate egg, and that the crab tasted like any other live crab. He failed to respond to why he knew what a live crab tasted like.
