                        

Item #:  SCP-5133

Object Class:  Euclid

Special Containment Procedures:  There are no current reliable means of containing SCP-5133, currently it is contained in a 2 meter x 2 meter(7 ft x 7 ft) square room made of steel about 8cm (3 inch) thick inside of Site-██. Inside this room it is often stored on a pedestal in the center of the room, however it is often found in other places in the chamber. The chamber is to be kept cold, about 4 degrees Celsius (60°F) while SCP-5133 is inside. This is a privilege, and not a necessity. SCP-5133 can freely roam the Foundation with supervision of personnel with level 2 clearance or higher. If the object is seen outside of it’s chamber unsupervised, it is to be placed under supervision, or escorted to its containment chamber. Any resistance or misbehavior from SCP-5133 is to be noted, and the air conditioning in the objects cell is to be turned off until the next day as a form of punishment. If the object becomes hostile it is to be placed in a separate containment chamber that is under -6 degrees Celsius (30°F) for a minimum of 3 hours. SCP-5133 has the ability to leave it’s chamber at any point in time, making it difficult to contain, however it chooses to stay within the Foundation for the most part, giving it the classification of Euclid

Description:  SCP-5133 appears to be a life size bust of King David from the Hebrew Bible. The object is about 63cm wide (16 in). Close research on the object has determined that it is made of a mixture of 43% porcelain 37% terracotta, and 19% concrete, and 1% uranium. SCP-5133 is sentient, and is capable of speech in languages such as English, French, Hebrew, and Greek. Despite having any muscles or muscle like structure in the bust, it’s face remains very animated when speaking, or reacting to situations. It believes itself to be more intelligent than everybody else, often referring to personnel as “Pleb” or “Infidel” with the exception of Dr. ███████ Ashe, who it refers to as “Sir”. The object has shown the ability to telepathy, as well as affect living organisms mentally. SCP-5133 is capable of mind reading, forcing thoughts upon others, and killing via anomalous, telepathic means. The process of said killing, as described by victims of SCP-5133, includes a faint ringing sound that gets louder over the course of 5 or so days, eventually causing the subject's eardrums to burst, followed by death seconds after. Personnel have been able to survive this, however with major hearing damage. Most instances of survival happen due to SCP-5133 being told to stop, or threatened. Any surviving victims are to be given an immediate psychiatric evaluation and treated accordingly. The object has been known to move via rolling on the floor, sliding, or even levitating, although this method of travel is the least common. SCP-5133 is often cooperative with staff and often has “enjoyable” conversations with them, as described by Officer ██████.

Addendum 5133:1 Discovery:  SCP-5133 was originally on display at the ██████ Museum in █████ ███, Jerusalem. The first anomalous sighting of the object was on ██/██/████ at 4:30pm. It was reported as stolen to the authorities despite having no evidence of a break in, or of the cabinet it was in being disturbed in any way. Stationed operatives from Mobile Task Force Iota-10 had investigated and found SCP-5133 inside an abandoned building nearby, sitting on a cinder block. SCP-5133 was taken into custody with no resistance from the object. It wasn’t until it was put into containment that researchers had discovered it’s ability to think and speak.

Addendum 5133:2 Test Logs: 

Test A - 07/16/2020 

Conducted by 

Dr. J. Ashe

Subject: 

SCP-5133

Procedure: 

SCP-5133 will be presented with a 54 page math assignment, all having to do with overly elaborate equations. It will be timed and and it's score will be documented.

Results: 

After about 140 seconds had passed, SCP-5133 had finished the entire assignment with a perfect score. All 547 questions had a perfect answer with no sign of cheating, or manipulation to the assignment.

Additional Notes: 

SCP-5133 appeared to have no struggle during the assignment and even seemed insulted that we had believed it would have any struggle at all. The object appears to excel in mathematic problem solving.
附加记录：    

Test B - 07/24/2020 

Conducted by 

Dr. R. Farlow

Subject: 

SCP-5133

Procedure: 

SCP-5133 will be asked to end world hunger, it's reactions and response will be documented and covered in an interview.

Results: 

After the question was asked, SCP-5133 had said “I would not be able to do that on my own, however, using your primitive, yet still effective technology, I’m sure killing off enough people to make that happen is quite possible.”

Additional Notes: 

According to SCP-5133, decreasing the human population drastically would be the most effective way of ending world hunger. It appears that it did not take morals into account when making this statement.
附加记录：    

Test C - 08/07/2020 

Conducted by 

Dr. J. Ashe

Subject: 

SCP-5133

Procedure: 

SCP-5133 will be given a copy of the English Dictionary and asked to recite the entire thing A-Z based solemnly off of memory.

Results: 

SCP-5133 was given the dictionary and after about 3 and a half minutes, the object began listing off words A through Z. It had listed each word flawlessly and even read out the longest word in the English language without even stuttering.

Additional Notes: 

SCP-5133 appears to have an extremely long term memory and an incredible reading ability.
附加记录：    

Test D - 08/23/2020 

Conducted by 

Dr. J. Ashe

Subjects: 

SCP-5133, D-0907

Procedure: 

D-0907, recently diagnosed with lung cancer, will be brought to SCP-5133. The object will be asked how to cure her cancer. Procedures done and/or stated by SCP-5133 will be documented and brought up in an interview conducted by Dr. Farlow

Results: 

After SCP-5133 was told to cure D-0907 of her cancer. SCP-5133 remained still and silent for a few moments. Soon after, D-0907 reported experiencing some slight tinnitus. Dr. Farlow had told SCP-5133 to stop, that it was going to kill her, however I had told it to continue. Simply to see what would happen. 5 days later D-0907 was reported dead, with the cause of death being a burst eardrum, and major brain damage.

Additional Notes: 

SCP-5133 had a very distinct look on it's face the entire time D-0907 was in the room, as if it took a lot of focus, or energy to terminate personnel in such a matter.
附加记录：    

An Interview Conducted by Doctor R. Farlow 

Interviewed:  SCP-5133

Foreword:  This interview will cover the results of the test logs and to get further information about the object.

 

Dr. Farlow:  Hi, SCP-5133.

SCP-5133:  Good afternoon, ape, what is it you wish to speak to me about?

Dr. Farlow:  It's about our recent tests, the tests conducted by Dr. Ashe and I. I just want to get a little more information about your intellect and, more importantly, your moral choices.

SCP-5133:  I suppose I have some time to spare for you to ask your questions

Dr. Farlow:  That's great. I would first like to ask, how are you able to memorize things so quickly, and so well? Like with the dictionary, you only took a few minutes to read the entire thing, and memorize every word. I don't understand how that could be possible.

SCP-5133:  I just acquire the basic memory and literary skills to do so, it's not complicated at all.

Dr. Farlow:  I see, very interesting. Well, could you explain how you got these skills?

SCP-5133:  Why do you ask?

Dr. Farlow:  I'm just curious is all. I might want to acquire these skills for my self.

SCP-5133:  I just have these skills, it's as simple as that. I guess I was born with them.

Dr. Farlow:  ? Are you saying that you have parents? That there are more living busts like yourself?

SCP-5133:  I would assume that there are, there are more hairless apes in this world other than you, so I would think there are more organisms similar to me.

Dr. Farlow:  So your not sure?

SCP-5133:  That is correct.

Dr. Farlow:  Alright, well, back on topic. I want to ask you about D-0907.

SCP-5133:  What about her?

Dr. Farlow:  Why did you think the best means of curing her was to kill her? Why did you not take ethics, or morals in general into consideration. Killing her isn't curing her.

SCP-5133:  She no longer has cancer. Does she?

Dr. Farlow:  What the hell? [Dr. Farlow began to raise his voice] You think killing a person is making them better? She could have been cured, she could have lived, but you killed her! Why?

SCP-5133:  [SCP-5133 began to grow frustrated] There is no reason to shout. You asked me to get rid of her cancer, and did I not deliver?

Dr. Farlow:  You think you did something good? Tell me something. If you're so smart, why didn't you take ethical treatment into consideration?

SCP-5133:  [SCP-5133 began raising it's voice] Do NOT insult my intelligence, you idiotic fool. I'm the one who got rid of her cancer, if I had the moral high ground in that situation, she would have died of her cancer sooner than it would have been cured

Dr. Farlow:  Are you sure? Many people have survived cancer, why wouldn't she. I think you just wanted an excuse to kill. You're a mindless killing machine.

Dr. Ashe:  [From outside the room] Dr. Farlow don't provoke the SCP

[SCP-5133 raised a pencil in the room and brutally murdered Dr. Farlow]

 
附加记录：    

Closing Statement:  Dr. Ashe had rushed into the room afterwards and took SCP-5133 to it's freezer room and Dr. Farlow's body was taken to the lab for a biopsy. This incident will further be recognized as Incident:5133-F

An Interview Conducted by Doctor J. Ashe 

Interviewed:  SCP-5133

Foreword:  This interview was a confrontation after the aftermath of Incident:5133-F

 

Dr. Ashe:  What the hell happened in there with Dr. Farlow?

SCP-5133:  Sir, you told him yourself, not to provoke me, and yet, he still did. This was his fault.

Dr. Ashe:  No, you did this, you killed Dr. Farlow. Why the hell do you feel the need to kill people when they have a problem, or when they piss you off?

SCP-5133:  When someone does not deserve life, they get it taken away, and if someone is better off dead than alive, due to a sickness, or disorder, they should be killed. It's as simple as that.

Dr. Ashe:  [Dr. Ashe sighs] I get that you are smart, and that you believe that morals only get in the way of intellect, but I know that you tend to be understanding. Killing people like this isn't the best option, sure, it doesn't affect you, but you have to take account of other people.

SCP-5133:  That is the most idiotic thing I've ever heard.

Dr. Ashe:  That is all I wanted to ask, we've turned the AC off in your chamber for the time being, if we have another incident like this, I will personally put you in the incinerator, no matter what the Ethics Committee has to say about it.

 
附加记录：    

Closing Statement:  SCP-5133 was taken back to it's chamber and kept under camera surveillance.
