                        

Item #:  SCP-5133

Object Class:  Euclid

Special Containment Procedures:  SCP-5133 and nearby forest have been purchased by the Foundation for the purpose of on-site containment. All unauthorized individuals attempting to enter the premises are to be turned away and administered amnestic treatment if required. On-site security must wear non-foundation clothing and be armed with concealed weapons.

Experimentation with SCP-5133 may only be performed with Class D personal with no prior mental health condition.

Description:  SCP-5133 is a single bedroom hunting lodge located in the Forests of Dean. SCP-5133 is constructed of pine wood and has tinted glass windows. The entire interior of SCP-5133 is painted white and decorated in a minimalist style. SCP-5133 contains all amenities expected of a fully furnished holiday home. Investigation has determined that SCP-5133 was built in 200█ by the company [DATA EXPUNGED]  to rent out to customers. SCP-5133 was discovered by MTF Eta-10 following the arrest of ██ previous occupants of SCP-5133.

SCP-5133 is a Class B cognitive hazard but only affects the thalamus. Subjects exposed to SCP-5133 report:

Sensory phenomena
Insomnia
Loss of consciousness
Coma
Violent Outbursts

Addendum: Experiment 5133-1 

The Purpose of experiment is to understand how SCP-5133 affects its victims. D-0327 is a 32-year-old Caucasian male of average build and appearance. After examination by Dr ████, it had been determined that D-0327 has no pre-existing mental health condition. D-0327 was placed in SCP-0327 and spent 2 weeks there. D-0327 would receive an interview twice a day and an MRI scan every 3 days. Dr ████ noticed that D-0327's behaviour did not have drastic change until interview 5133-6 where D-0327 appeared tired and malnourished. When asked about this, D-0327 reported that he had not had much of an appetite. D-0327 sleeping and eating habits become more unhealthy as time passed. After the 2 weeks were over, D-0327 was moved to class d containment in Site-72. 4 days after returning to Site-72, D-0327 was involved in a riot in which he killed and [DATE EXPUNGED] ██ D class and █ Security. The following interview was conducted by Dr ████.

Interviewed:  D-0327

Interviewer:  Dr ████

 

Dr ████:  I didn't expect to see you again. I am sure you know what I am here.

D-0327:  You want to know what happened?

Dr ████:  I know what happened, I want to know why.

D-0327:  There wasn't a reason why, it was a spur of the moment thing. I just wanted to do it.

Dr ████:  But why? Let me use D-6273 as an example, he was the first you killed. Why did you kill him?

D-0327:  I didn't plan to kill anybody, I had a desire and an impulse, and I wasn't able to stop myself acting on the impulse

Dr ████:  OK, when did you start feeling these impulses

D-0327:  Ever since I was [REDACTED]. But back then, I could control them. Now, I just do whatever I feel like doing, I can't control it.

Dr ████:  Do you feel any remorse over your actions?

D-0327:  [D-0327 remains silent]

Dr ████:  I have heard enough. D-0327, you have now been designated as SCP-5133-1 and marked as E-Class personal. You will be contained and used for further study. Do you have anything to say?

SCP-5133-1:  Fuck you!

 

Closing Statement:  After this interview, I requested that SCP-5133-1 was subject to an MRI scan. The results came back as normal. I quantified the different areas of the brain to find out how strong each area was. All areas were normal except from the orbital frontal cortex which was massively reduced. It appears that even after subjects leave SCP-5133's presence, they are still affected by SCP-5133. SCP-5133-1 has been terminated.
