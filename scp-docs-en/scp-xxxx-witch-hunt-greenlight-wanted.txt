SCP XXXX Witch Hunting (Greenlight Wanted)

02 Jan 2021 00:58

Seeking Greenlights:  Yes

Page Type:  SCP Article

Elevator Pitch:  Scp XXXX is a phenomena where people affected begin to believe that there are witches among them. Scp XXXX spreads to anybody that hears a victims talking about Scp XXXX itself. Scp XXXX is still being researched as trying to find out how it appears at random intervals to random people.

Central Narrative:  Document goes into how the first recorded case was the Salem witch trials and how the foundation has been able to cover it up to be religious decisions.

Hook/Attention-Grabber:  I feel history into the Salem witch trials and how it spread before the foundation was even created and how the foundation worked to cover it up.

Additional Notes:  Ive thought of this idea for a while and I think it is simple enough to be a good first scp yet it could still provide an interesting story for the reader, hope you enjoy the idea. If you were to enjoy the idea and thinking of critique if you could stay in touch I would be very grateful.
