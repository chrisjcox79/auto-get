                        

Object class:  Safe

containment measures 

SCP-5563 should be stored on a shelf with a thick glass cover over it, lifted off only for tests. No cell is needed.

description 

SCP-5563 is a small iron bucket. The catch is when you look into the bucket, there seems to be no bottom. All tests have never found the bottom of the bucket. SCP-5563 does not seem to be sentient. However, since it is small, it poses no threat to humans. It does pose a threat to other smaller SCPs like SCP-999, though, as small SCPs can fall in there and never return. So far, scientists have divided the space into three sections: the sunlit zone, the midnight zone and the power off zone. Sunlight ends at the midnight zone, and all cameras seem to power off in the power off zone. Nobody can remember when and where it was found, as it seems to destroy memories of its origins.

Test items
test results

Camera
Powered off at around 400 ft. down.

Brick
Never heard a thud, perhaps still falling.

Impact sensor
Powered off at around 420 ft. down.

Rat and heart-beat monitor
Died of pressure changes at about 200 ft. down.
