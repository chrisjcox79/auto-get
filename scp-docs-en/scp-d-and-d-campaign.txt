Re: SCP D and D campaign

03 May 2019 17:09

Sure, it sounds really fun. Maybe your party is a task force, grabbing and bagging anomalous items/individuals. Different roles and abilities could be different classes. The only difficulty would be fully fleshing out rules for things unique to the SCP universe. Something that may be more useful for your purposes could be the Monster of The Week roleplaying game by Evil Hat Productions.

https://www.evilhat.com/home/monster-of-the-week-resources/

Though w/ a bit of homebrew work, it'd make for an interesting DD campaign.

This is just off of the top of my head, but here are some substitution/conceptual ideas for classes to get you started. (The magical/anomalous classes could be previously contained anomalies that were deemed useful enough to be put on a task force)

Original Class
SCP Foundation Equivalent

Barbarian
Anomalous Being/Mutation?

Bard
Scientist/Professor/Doctor

Cleric/Druid
Cultist

Fighter
Regular Ol' Foor Soldier

Monk
Martial Artist/Hand-to-Hand Combat Specialist

Paladin
Possibly an enhanced individual?

Ranger
Sniper

Rouge
Specialist/Stealth Operative

Sorcerer
Psionic/Psychic

Wizard
Classic Magic-User

It's not perfect, just filled this out as a rough example.

Hope that helped! Feel free to ask anything. :)

Re: SCP D and D campaign

10 Dec 2020 03:40

I've been thinking about this too, and I think the GOC might actually be a better group for the party to be a member of than the Foundation. It seems like the GOC employs magic-users, arcane and divine, more than the Foundation does, and they're also more focused on combating and destroying anomalies than capturing them, as are most DD parties. There's not much fun about Nine-Tailed Fox sweeping up after a breach or the Mole Rats getting picked off in another endless underground tunnel, but a GOC party can just roll up and start killing shit.

For extra fun, set it in an SCP-579, assuming that Pietro Wilson failed in this timeline.
