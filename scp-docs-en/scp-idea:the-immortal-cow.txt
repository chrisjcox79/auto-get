SCP idea: The immortal Cow

09 Dec 2020 10:41

Seeking Greenlights: yes

Page Type: SCP Article

Genre : Horror, sci-fi

Idea: The idea is simply a creature with god like powers who acts like a simple cow if not disturbed.

Central Narrative: A simple cow apparently of normal size, however the animal cannot die having out of the ordinary regenerative abilities. If every single particle of the Cow were disintegrated, the beast according to some mysterious laws, would be able to move in the abdomen of a living being of its choice in the form of a fetus, and then exit the victim by ripping his intestine. If the cow were to be eaten (even just a part of its body, not all of it) it would not only do the things explained above, it would also transform consumers into half-cow men full of blood and milk. When the cow reaches two years of age, it will no longer grow and will develop the ability to teleport. If one were to drink its milk or its derivative, the subject would begin to go crazy, seeing strange demons coming out of the sky and hearing aching animal screams. The only way to capture the animal is to capture it in its fetus form and put it in a cage who resembles a hill full of grass with a light blue sky and a bright light, similiar to the sun.

Additional Notes: I think that no creature similiar to this one exist and I think it's quite a cool idea, if you are interested I can link the sandbox
