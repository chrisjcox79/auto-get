                        

*Item Number: SCP-5968

*Object Class: Euclid

Special containment Procedures:  If SCP-5968 should be at any point discovered to have the capability of mind control, it should immediately be destroyed to prevent a K-class world ending scenario. If SCP-5968 shows any sign of changes to its physical state, any on site personnel is to destroy SCP-5968.

SCP-5968 will be kept in a 1 by 1 air tight reinforced glass display box, when personnel must conduct testing, there must be at a minimum of five Beta-7(Maz Hatters) field agents and a minimum of four level four researchers.

Description:  SCP-5968 is a gaming headphone that is capable of terminating any of its users. SCP-5968 is covered in a layer of rust and is made of a highly toxic metal that resembles chlorine. Although it does not react violently towards air, it will decay any metal and cells that it touches. SCP-5968 is capable of moving after the subject puts on the set. It is not yet able to sustain complete control of the subject since it decays the subject in such a violent way that the subject will die in approximately 2 minutes.

Whether SCP-5968 has the capability to control the subject from far away is yet to be known, though it can produce spore-like cells (SCP-5968-1) that can cause infection through inhalation and digestion, and will cause its’ hosts cells to decay and rot. If SCP-5968 comes across any means of flesh and is able to contact it, it can cause the subject excruciating pain, that is if the subject is yet still alive.

After decaying its target, SCP-5968 will be in a 5 minute hibernation, the reason is yet to be known. After gaining enough energy to attack again, SCP-5968 will release SCP-5968-1 into the air around it. The infectee (SCP-5968-1A) of SCP-5968-1 will, after a 3 minute incubation period, begin a change in their personality, and they will start to have visions of death and destruction. SCP-5968-1A will then be expected to have headaches and digestion problems. SCP-5968 will then lure them toward its display box, willing them to open the box and put on the headset.

Containment breach Procedures:  If SCP-5968 breaches containment, a full site lockdown should be initiated to avoid any of the infection spreading into the community. Then four Beta-7 field agents, including one D-class, should be sent to retrieve the anomaly. The D-class should first enter contact with SCP-5968, while the anomaly has yet to decay the D-class, the four Beta-7 officers should escort SCP-5868-1A into SCP-5968's containment chamber. The door is then closed to leave SCP-5968 and SCP-5968-1A to themselves.
