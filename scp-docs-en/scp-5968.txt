Special Containment Procedures:  In all the places where SCP-5986 specimens have been found, a wall is to be built around them, restricting access from the public. Places where SCP-XXXX can grow are to be monitored daily by MTF Theta-4 (“Gardeners”) for specimens.

Description:  SCP-5986 is a species of plant, known to the public as Artica flora. Massive data about SCP-5986 is known to the public due to it having a similar appearance to Bellis perris1 and it being found by Canadians. SCP-5986 can only grow in areas with a temperature of less than, or equal to, 4°C.

SCP-5986 possesses multiple anomalous abilities:

Able to produce seeds, seemingly forever, leading to mass population
Able to drop the temperature around it down to up to -53°C
Able to launch it’s seeds with distances up to 500 meters at rates of 102 mph
Able to make the surrounding area pile up with up to 60cm of snow
Able to survive on any solid object, as long as it’s surface temperature less than, or equal to, -90°C
Sentience and sapience

Using genetic testing, specimens of SCP-5986 seems to have been around for 500,000 years, if not longer.

Incident Log-5986:  The following is text found in a journal in ███████, Canada. The journal was found in an excavation, unrelated to the foundation.

9th Feb, 1840

Today I found something strange out while hunting. I’m in Canada right? Cold, freezing. Daisies, they’re supposed to grow in warmer places, from what I’ve heard. I wouldn’t know too much would I? But, today I found a daisy! Right here in ███████, a daisy! Well, other than that, not seen nothing strange.
附加记录：    

10th Feb, 1840

Found more of them daisies. At least 10. I tried to pick one of them, give it to my daughter but the others shot seeds at me like arrows. You believe it? What next, finding sunflowers on the Himilayas?
附加记录：    

11th Feb, 1840

Today I came home with cuts and bruises. Any guesses? Those darn daisies! Kept hitting me ‘til I was forced to retreated. Still stings like mad though. I think I’ll stay well away from hunting now. Maybe kill those things.
