Reasons are yet to be discovered. Upon 10 seconds of entering, the Class-D Personnel has been killed and eaten afterwards.

Another thing, SCP-5156 looks as if it was growing every day. It was 5'4 before, it is 5'7 now. Considering before was just yesterday, evolution for this entity is fast compared to normal humans.

Also, it's capabilities. Regeneration . We have managed to grab SCP-5156's DNA and inserted it to a Class-D Personnel. The patient re-grew limbs within seconds of being torn apart. Luckily, it can be put down by shooting it in the head.

But, what if it mutates inside the patient making it strong? This could mean easier operations and recontainment. If you think about it, this kind of power could really be implemented to MTF and SD personnel alike. But there is yet a lot to discover. This is Dr. ██████ signing out.
