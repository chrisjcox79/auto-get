                        

Object #:  SCP-5587

Object class:  Euclid Keter

Special containment procedures: 

SCP-5587 is allowed to roam any location in any SCP site and leave at any time of his choosing. No attempts to stop SCP-5587 from entering or exiting any location should ever, under any circumstances, be attempted. SCP-5587 has his own personal room in site 16, measuring 20x20x20 meters. Any requests by SCP-5587, including human or animal sacrifice, should be followed out to staff’s best abilities. SCP-5587 has security clearance equal to that of the o5 council.

Description: 

SCP-5587 appears as a male human child, around ten (10) years of age. Their is no physical oddities about SCP-5587, but he measures in the top 99% in intelligence of all humans. SCP-5587 appears to possess and completely control some form of omnipotence and omnipresence. SCP-5587 is one of the most powerful SCPs, due to his ability to create a XK-class end-of-the-worldscenario. The foundation will do almost anything for them, to prevent mass destruction, earning dislike from most staff.

SCP-5587 was first brought into foundation custody on 2-2-20██. He was captured due to reports of a school teacher running around ████████, Oregon in the United States of America screaming about a “Demon Child.” Upon investigating this, the SCP Foundation found SCP-5587 playing hopscotch with two (2) frightened children, the numbers and boxes drawn in bodily fluids and instead of a stone, they used a flaming █████. Underestimating it’s power, the foundation took him to what was formerly know as site-██.

For the first nine (9) months, SCP-5587 refused to speak, leading the foundation to believe he was mute. Below is a list of experiments that where conducted before he was given full access.

D-4361, a convicted rapist
D-4361 was given the same instructions as D-8256
As soon as D-4361 entered SCP-5587’s cell, D-4361 disappeared and sixteen (16) spectral humanoid figures appeared in the room, along with two desks, each with two chairs each, a platform with a desk and chair on it, and twelve (12) chairs to the side. Each one of the creatures took a seat at one of the chairs, filling all but one, a chair behind one of the two desks facing the platform. D-4361 then appeared in the empty seat, looking bewildered. The various beings talked to each other, and, after reviewing audio logs, this was determined to be some form of trial, with the beings serving as the judge, jury, defense attorney, prosecution and prosecution attorney, while D-4361 was the defense. SCP-5587 did not talk the entire time, serving as a typist. At the end of the experiment, he slipped a transcript under the cell door. After 15 hours, the jury decided that D-4361 was not guilty. All of the unknown entities disappeared and a stack of evidence appeared in the observation room, proving that D-4361 was innocent. D-4361 was administered class-A amnestics and cleared of all charges the next day.
