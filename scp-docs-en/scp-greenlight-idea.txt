SCP Greenlight Idea

22 Dec 2020 19:00

Seeking Greenlights:  Yes

Page Type:  SCP Article

Genre (Optional):  Horror-ish

Elevator Pitch:  A creature, which in dormant form heavily resembles a metal dog crate. It takes that shape in order to lure animals (and potentially people) inside, where it will commence torture and feed on their pain.

Central Narrative:  After a gruesome, high-profile pet dismemberment, the SCP Foundation investigates the scene. They confiscate the crate in which the remains were found, and subsequent testing reveals it is in fact anomalous. At first, it is believed that the crate only moves when no one is looking at it, but testing reveals it's sentient and moves when it thinks no one is watching. Its hunting morph is a four-legged creature bearing a passing resemblance to a bear. When a biological creature enters the crate in dormant mode, the door will close and lock, and the wires of the cage will pierce its prey in deliberately non-lethal areas, in order to keep it alive as long as possible. After feeding on its victim's pains, it will typically enter its hunting morph, tear up the body into tiny shreds in an attempt to hide the evidence, and look for a new area to lie in wait.

Hook/Attention-Grabber:  A non-biological predator that preys on pain, hunts and kills sweet family pets, and has developed a merciless camouflage system.
