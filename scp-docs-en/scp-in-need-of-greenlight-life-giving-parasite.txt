SCP IN NEED OF GREENLIGHT!!! LIFE GIVING PARASITE

27 Nov 2020 00:26

Seeking Greenlights:  Yes

Page Type:  SCP Article

Elevator Pitch:  The scp is a life giving parasite that infect inanimate objects and gives them vital organs and personalities. These instances usually become suicidal and begin to ponder on why they were given life. All instances that the foundation has encountered have been friendly and interactive. as If they were human. The foundation contains them by putting them in separate decompression tanks. They have monitors on the side that keep track of their vital signs. The foundation also keeps track of the lifespan of the infected instances. On one occasion, a parasite left the object. That object: (a toothbrush) was able to continue functioning as a regular tooth brush.

Central Narrative:  It is a parasite that gives every day objects the ability to live.

Hook/Attention-Grabber:  The origin of the parasites are unknown. There are currently 110 encounters on the foundation record.
