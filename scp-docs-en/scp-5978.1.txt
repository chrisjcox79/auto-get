Author, did you happen to follow the feedback-seeking process noted in the How to Write an SCP Guide? There are a lot of immediately noticeable errors, most of which could have been quickly fixed if noted before a mainsite post. For example:

SCP-5978 is a hourglass, standing 9.5 inches tall and has a 6-inch base.
附加记录：    

Since .
The Foundation is a scientific organization, and uses metric measurements. Inches are not metric measurements.
The parallel structure of  is inconsistent.
It's not certain what measurement for the base is. Presuming it's got a circular base, the measurement would be specified to the diameter rather than the length of a side.

The rest of the article is similarly in need of edits to maintain the clinical tone that a professional researcher would use. If you haven't looked at the feedback process yet, I highly recommend doing so if you want to submit a successful page to the mainsite.
