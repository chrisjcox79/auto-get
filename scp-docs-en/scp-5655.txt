                        

Item #:  SCP 5655

Object Class:  Keter

Special Containment Procedures:  Personal who are exposed to the manifestation of SCP 5655 is to be terminated, regardless of rank, in case the exposed personnel are trespassers or spies. Personnel who are suspected to be the victims of the SCP 5655 manifestation within December are to be put into quarantine until proven innocent of exposure.

SCP 5655 has no containment cell because capture of SCP 5655 is impossible.

Description:  SCP 5655 is an elderly male measuring 2.12 metres in height, SCP 5655 wears a red drizabone which has multiple toys and bells dating from the 1950’s attached to the exterior. All toys show signs of corrosion and rot. SCP 5655 has high levels of pigmentation and obesity. The hands of SCP 5655 are normally worn by mildly damaged Christmas mittens, SCP 5655 will only take these mittens off when aggressive. SCP 5655 hands suffer from severe frostbite and overgrown nails ranging 2-4 centimetres in length, it is unclear how the fingers of SCP-5655 remain attatched.

SCP 5655 is very fond of being called Saint Nick, Santa Claus, or The Jolly Fat Man and will only respond directly when either of these names are called. SCP 5655 can be easily lured with chocolate-chip cookies and milk, these must be close to an indoor tree decorated in baubles, tinsel, twinkle lights, and a Christmas star or angel. If any of these are not within sight of each other, SCP 5655 will not be interested. Upon the consumption of the cookies and milk, incineration of dishes or containers that held the cookies and milk is to be made, as well as the tree to reduce contamination risk.

SCP 5655 will manifest nearby an individual who has committed an unlawful act in the duration of the month December. These exposed subjects are to be classed as SCP 5655-1-A, the numerical digit is determined on the order of exposed subjects. SCP 5655 will stalk SCP 5655-1-A until they promise to never commit any crimes and fix their unlawful act. If the subject(s) do not do any of the following by the 25th of December will result in the aggression of SCP 5655 who will disembowel all instances of SCP 5655-1-A. Survivors of disembowelment will suffer from extreme frostbite and growth of coal within the intestines, it is currently unclear how the coal grows at this time.

Other reported manifestation of SCP 5655 is very rare. SCP 5655 will manifest nearby individuals who have not committed any unlawful act or have not intentions to will result in receiving a gift wrapped in Christmas wrapping paper and a cotton ribbon, a recorded pattern in the colour of ribbon has been noted that the colour determines the personality of the subject, however it is unclear what the colours mean. The gift given by SCP 5655 to these subjects are inanimate objects the subject has desired most recently. All instances of gifts fit within a normal household.
