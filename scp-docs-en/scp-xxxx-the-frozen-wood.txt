SCP-XXXX 

29 Dec 2020 00:38

Seeking Greenlights:  Yes

Page Type:  SCP Article, Tale (after SCP is established)

Genre (Optional):  Horror, Drama/Emotional

Class:  Euclid

Elevator Pitch: 

+block

–block

SCP-XXXX is a snowy, Birch forest varying in size, but often appears in a 10 KM x 12 KM radius. The subject seems to teleport to areas around the world at random (along with anything inside it), leaving a large, empty area in its wake, but is limited to cold climates and/or places that are experiencing severe winter storms. Strangely, the area always seems to be entirely sterile, no life can survive, be it plant, animal, bacteria, or even tardigrade!

Central Narrative: 

+block

–block

The SCP tale I was planning for this follows a lone huntsman as he has unknowingly stumbled into this forest looking for game. He starts feeling weak, his vision blurry, and the snow taking on a slight red-ish hue. He starts coughing up blood, eventually too weak to go any further and contemplates his own mortality as he slowly dies in the snow. A lot of this story will have to do with what goes on in the character's mind, and how he deals with his impending death. (just a summary of the story)

Hook/Attention-Grabber: 

+block

–block

As stated before, the forest cannot support any life whatsoever, except for the trees that grow in it; these trees, designated SCP-XXXX-A, release a toxic, spore-like pollen that sterilizes and kills all known terrestrial lifeforms (many SCP's are not affected), but thankfully, only within it's area of effect. These spores are inhaled and break down the cells of the organism, causing; internal bleeding from torn organ tissue, destruction of muscle fibers/tissue leading to paralysis, vomiting blood, asphyxiation, convulsions and/or seizures, always ending with total organ failure and death.

After tracking it for years and sending hazmat-suit equipped recovery teams into the area, a total of 247 human bodies have been discovered, as well as the corpses of wildlife native to many different regions. Strangely, these bodies were perfectly intact, no decay had occurred as there were no bacteria left to break the bodies down.

Additional Notes: 

- The spores from SCP-XXXX-A cannot survive outside of SCP-XXXX's area of effect, thus, the only way to study the spores is to be within the subject itself.

- Due to its often secluded location and it's deadly nature, as long as the Foundation is careful about the spores, a well protected base could be built inside the area as well as utilizing its random teleportation effect to avoid detection by the public!
