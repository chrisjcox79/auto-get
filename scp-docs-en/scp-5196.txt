                        

Item #:  SCP-5196

Object Class:  Euclid

Special Containment Procedures:  SCP-5196 is to be kept in an standard Anomalous Item Locker at Site-16, and is to be checked daily. Checking SCP-5196's internal files is to be done by Level 3 staff and above. Contents are strictly not to be edited, renamed, copied or moved to other devices, or deleted. All can be viewed apart from the .mp4 file, which is not to be viewed outside of the bi-weekly, routine viewing, to be viewed by Class D subjects, as well as those willing to participate. Apart from said routine viewing, it is not allowed outside the Item Locker area.

Description:  SCP-5196 is a black, 4GB SanDisk Cruzer USB drive, internally named Regal Cinema Theatre 4. The body is decorated with two yellow stripes on it. The USB drive was recovered from Regal Cinemas Stadium in █████████. The USB drive's body itself is not harmful in any way. Contents on the USB drive change routinely, anywhere from 1 week to a month, depending on the contents located on the .mp4 file. Nothing in the USB drive allows it to change, so it's questioned whether SCP-5196 is actually sentient, or the work of a highly skilled programmer.

When viewed regularly, contents on the drive always contain one .mp4 file, varying in time. The video quality will either be 720p, 1080p, 1440p, or 2160p. (Which is 4k.) It will also always contain a .txt file, always titled .

Upon a viewing of the .mp4 file, a reenactment will happen in real life. If the .mp4 file is not viewed, all select victims of said reenactment will suffer through a lethal ailment. Strong individuals usually cough or vomit blood, receive intense headaches, receive abnormal amounts of itchiness, or contract abnormal, searing pains. Weak individuals can die instantly. This can only be relieved by watching the .mp4 file.
