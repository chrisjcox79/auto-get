                        

Item #: SCP-5196
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

An image of SCP-5196 in containment.

Object Class: Safe
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Special Containment Procedures: SCP-5196 is to be contained in a 5x9 standard humanoid containment cell with a remote-controlled steel cage in the center of the cell. This item is restricted to personnel under Level 2 clearance. Physical contact should be restricted outside of testing purposes.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Description: SCP-5196 is an inanimate humanoid object that takes the form of a golden statue with minor scratching to the front. Upon physical contact with SCP-5196, the subject (Now stated as SCP-5196-2) will be melted into a molten gold liquid that will then fuse into SCP-5196, furthermore, if an instance of SCP-5196-2 should be caught SCP-5196-2 should be brought to a high power freezing device that is able to reach -20 degrees Celcius for no less than 72 hours.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Test log-0130

Dr. Koliski [Class-D 5124, please enter the containment chamber.]

D-5124 entered the chamber without resistance.

Dr. Koliski opened the cage containing SCP-5196.

Dr. Koliski [Class-D 5124, please approach and make contact with SCP-5196]

D-5124 pauses for 5 seconds then approaches SCP-5196 and places a hand onto SCP-5196

SCP-5196-2 then begins melting into a gold liquid while screaming.

SCP-5196-2 then begins crawling to SCP-6701 then fusing with SCP-6701.
