The God Killer

26 Nov 2020 07:41

Seeking Greenlights:  Yes

Page Type:  SCP Article

Genre (Optional):  Small bit of Horror, Drama/Emotional, bit of Action, some History

Page Layout (Optional):  Standard Description and Containment Procedures, interview, discovery log, and requests.

Elevator Pitch:  it is basically a man that can change his size to anything he wants, is completely invincable and unstoppable. (except for the gases that the foundation make) he was originally made to kill gods and devour them. shredding them down to their atoms. but he gets betrayed by the humans and sent to the SCP universe.

Central Narrative:  the basic idea of this, is that the SCP is from another universe. in that universe the main mythology gods and deities exist (like Odin and Zeus) each one basically owning a portion of the world (like the olympians getting Greece and the Egyptians getting Egypt) and making it their own. but after millions of years of the gods and other smaller deities ruling the world. the humans become fed up. they combine magic and science to create SCP-XXXX (no number) this entity is made to destroy these “gods” one by one and free man kind. but little do they know that with every god, deity and demi-god it kills it gains a little bit of humanity. eventually after killing and devouring every last god, it has become essentially human in emotions. but when it returns and is told the kill code, it lives and realises that they tried to kill him after all it did for them. It begins to attack the humans, but they open a wormhole and it is sent to the scp universe, where he gets contained and evetually stored.

Hook/Attention-Grabber:  whats special about this idea is that we get to see the building of a god from another world. and after he learns about being human he instantly doesn't want to be one because of what his people did to him. so it's like a betrayal story that makes people feel empathy for him, then sadness as he gets betrayed again in the SCP universe.

Additional Notes:  i would love this idea to reach draft status, as i have had this idea for a novel for a long time and i thought it would be great to get feedback from here. and if it became and scp. it would make a great story character to have in many different stories.

anyway im blabbering too much and i really thank you for reading my idea! :)

Re: SCP - The God Killer

26 Nov 2020 13:14

No worries; keep in mind I'm not an official critter, so take what I said with a grain of salt. Regarding the similarity to God of War, it's not necessarily a bad thing if you can put your own spin on it (and it's well written). Regarding the Mary-Sue comment, I was referring to the fact that the entity is near-indestructible; there's already a lot of articles like that on the site, so it comes across a little cliché. Just an idea, but maybe he was much stronger in his original dimension, but in the ours, he's less formidable? But again, this is all just my opinion; if you can pull off a near-indestructible demigod, go for it!

I'm not sure if you know, but posters are typically expected to contact seasoned critters from IRC Chat. If you don't contact anyone, you might be waiting a while before someone authorised to give greenlights will come along.

Good luck with your article!
