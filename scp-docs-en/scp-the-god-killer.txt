The God Killer

26 Nov 2020 07:41

Seeking Greenlights:  Yes

Page Type:  SCP Article

Genre (Optional):  Small bit of Horror, Drama/Emotional, bit of Action, some History

Page Layout (Optional): 

Standard Description and Containment Procedures, interview, discovery log, and requests.

Elevator Pitch: 

It's a man that can change his size, can shred any solid to nothing by eating it and is moderately unstoppable, this is subject to change as I would like him to have weaknesses the foundation can exploit. He was originally made to kill the ancient Gods (this includes all mythological gods and biblical) and devour them. But he eventually achieves this goal and gets betrayed by the humans, getting sent to the SCP universe.

Central Narrative  Hook/Attention-Grabber 

the SCP is from another universe. In that universe Gods and deities exist, each one owning a portion of the world. But after years of the gods and other smaller deities ruling the world, the humans become fed up. They combine magic and science to create an invincible SCP-XXXX this entity is made to destroy these “Gods” one by one and free man kind. But little do they know that with every god and deity it kills it gains a little bit of humanity. after killing and devouring all the gods, it has become human emotionally. But when it returns and is told the kill code, it lives and realizes that they tried to kill it after all it did for them. it begins to attack the humans, they open a wormhole and it is sent to the scp universe, where it loses his invincibility, learning to cope without it. But one scientist doesn't like it even though he is harmless to the SCP universes humans. So with the approval of the O5 council, gases are tested on the entity. learning how to sedate it. the scientist that made the request isn't happy and creates a gas that can knock the entity forever. the ethics committee and the O5 are not happy decommissioning the gas. the doctor takes a vial and antagonizes the entity, making it aggressive and begin to hate humans again. finally it kills the doctor and releasing the gas, knocking him out. the foundation realizes the entity is too dangerous to wake up due to its mental state so they decide to, against the ethics committees judgment, store it indefinitely in a humanoid containment locker at site 132.

No worries; keep in mind I'm not an official critter, so take what I said with a grain of salt. Regarding the similarity to God of War, it's not necessarily a bad thing if you can put your own spin on it (and it's well written). Regarding the Mary-Sue comment, I was referring to the fact that the entity is near-indestructible; there's already a lot of articles like that on the site, so it comes across a little cliché. Just an idea, but maybe he was much stronger in his original dimension, but in the ours, he's less formidable? But again, this is all just my opinion; if you can pull off a near-indestructible demigod, go for it!

I'm not sure if you know, but posters are typically expected to contact seasoned critters from IRC Chat. If you don't contact anyone, you might be waiting a while before someone authorised to give greenlights will come along.

Re: SCP - The God Killer

27 Nov 2020 04:44

1) As noted, there is a word limit and you are apparently well above that. See if you can shrink it down.

2) As noted there are already a number of invincible god-killers on the site, yours is just invincible in a different way. Also, the way he got affected by the gas is a little convoluted.

3) Interestingly, you may be better off focusing on his humanity more than his invincibility. If the portal into the SCP Universe removed his invincibility and now he has to deal with life without a large measure of his invincibility and/or power, it may be a more interesting character to explore, and it differentiates him better from his fellows.
