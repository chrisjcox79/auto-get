                        

Item #:  SCP-5138

Object Class:  Euclid

Special Containment Procedures: 

SCP-5138 is contained at Site-81.

Must be kept in a 2x2x2 foot steel box with each wall 20 centimeters thick.

and and 2 airlocks to prevent SCP-5138's poisonous gas.

If SCP-5138 breaches, all personnel on site must put on a gas mask because of the intense poisonous liquid and gas that SCP-5138 emits.

No more than 2 personnel may enter at any given time to prevent casualties and breaches if the chicken escapes the gas and liquid emitted by it will spread fast through the foundation

The box must be decontaminated checked for weak points and cleaned once a day.

if a weak point is found the box it is to be switched.

Upon removal of SCP-5138-1 they must be put inside a steel box under the temperature of 0° Celsius for 24 hours.

Instances of SCP-5138-1 must be separated from SCP-5083 to prevent replication of SCP-5138.

If SCP-5138-1 hatches, it is to be neutralized for the safety of the foundation.

Description: 

SCP-5138 was found in a barn in 1979 the barn and farmer looked melted and in 1 pen was a creature and a chicken.

the creature with SCP-5138 is deemed SCP-5138 and is easy to scare and appearance is unknown.

SCP-5138 appears to be a chicken and turkey combined that leaves behind radioactive waste that is similar to the acidity of hydrochloric acid.

This radioactive waste is known as SCP-5138-2.

SCP-5138-1 instances are eggs laid by SCP-5138.

They will taste like anything the consumer is craving and then will fill the consumer with joy.

10 minutes after eating SCP-5138-1 will start to melt you from the inside out

Any living thing breathing SCP-5138-2 will go unconscious and when woken up will instantly go mad once 2 minutes pass the victim will see hallucinations after 10 minutes will start wandering around acting like different barn animals and then finally after 12 minutes will scream in pain then fall to the ground.

SCP-5138-1 when hatched immediately grows and becomes a adult studies on how this happens are still going on.
