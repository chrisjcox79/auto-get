                        

Item #:  SCP-5138

Object Class:  Euclid

Special Containment Procedures: 

SCP-5138 is to be guarded by armed guards at all times. No personnel with less than Level 3 Clearance may enter SCP-5138 under any circumstances unless under testing Purposes.

Description: 

SCP-5138 appears as a small grove of Japanese White Birch (Betula Platyphylla) in [REDACTED], Scotland. The anomaly always appears in autumnal colours and sheds a seemingly infinite amount of otherwise non-anomalous leaves. Walking down the grove will lead to a bamboo archway, which, when entered transports individuals to a traditional Japanese garden, hereby referred to as SCP-5138-1. The garden has been reported to evoke feelings of peace in all cases. upon exiting through the archway subjects have reported being transported to a similar grove tho the one aforementioned, the only difference being that this one stretches on indefinitely. This grove is known as SCP-5138-2. Returning to SCP-5138-1 is now impossible due to the archways disappearance. All subjects confined in SCP-5138-2 for more more than 20 minutes have reported seeing a figure in the trees, the figure has been designated SCP-5138-A. SCP-5138-A is known to be sentient and extremely hostile. Upon sighting any humans within SCP-5138-2, SCP-5138-A will attempt to remove the eyes of the subject(s) in question and impale their corpse(s) on a branch of a tree in SCP-5138-2. SCP-5138-A is approximately 2.8 meters tall and weighs 250 kilograms. The anomaly's skin is identical to the bark of the trees in the grove. SCP-5138-A also has hair resembling that of the common tree lichen. SCP-5138-A can be goaded to attack earlier than the time specified above by attempting to cause damage to the tree. SCP-5138-A has also proven almost immune to nearly all forms of attack, examples include; blunt force trauma, laceration, and even standard assault rifle fire. SCP-5138-A has never been sighted outside SCP-5138-2, this may suggest that he is a guard to the grove rather than a malevolent entity.
