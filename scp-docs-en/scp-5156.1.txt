Revisions.

14 Dec 2020 17:07

Even if I can't, I want to say a few things.

First Problem: There is no formatting. If you want your SCP class object to be taken seriously, than at least add some formatting.

Second Problem: I see a lot of SCP-096 in here. Some more editing to more distinguish this from 096 is necessary.

Third Problem: The Exploration files need a lot of work, primarily focused on the usage of formatting and content.

Fourth Problem: not structured that well. then again, Formatting.

Look at How To Write An SCP, it should help you get some of this together more.
