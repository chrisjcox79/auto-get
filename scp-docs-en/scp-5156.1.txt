So let's start

SCP-5156 is to be kept in a 1767.84 centimeters wide cell with special NORA security cameras and iron doors due to SCP-5156's exceptional strength.
附加记录：    

What. 1767.84 centimeters? Why so specific? If the cell is 1767.85 will it die? This is a common problem with SCPs (heck I've done it a few times before), you don't need to specify the lengths of the cell. You can just say 

SCP-5156 is a six foot tall humanoid that looks roughly like a king with white eyes.
附加记录：    

Huh? Looks roughly? Weird. Also looking like a king could mean anything. It could be an Egyptian king or a British king etc. You can just say 

+block

–block

SCP-5156 is able to merge with anything granting it the body and/or powers due to it's virus.
附加记录：    

Ok first off, the footnote could have easily been in the paragraph. Think of footnotes like parentheses. Next, what do you mean by merge with anything? Does it do a body snatcher and replicate them? Or is it like the Thing where it kill and replaces the person. Also what's ?

SCP-5156 can speak english, spanish, and french. SCP-5156 is very intelligent and is able to learn very quickly.
附加记录：    

I believe the words English, Spanish, and French should be uppercase. Also this is kind of bland to be honest. Like cool it's very smart, it's never brought up again. It just feels like a X-man architype.

SCP-5156 can't attack the person they morph into due to being in the body and if the body SCP-5156 is in gets destroyed so does SCP-5156.
附加记录：    

How do they know that? Did SCP-5156 tell them? I guess this is the only  it has. Think of humanoid SCPs as something great (seeing into the future) with unsuspecting consequences (you can't look at food because it will look like feces). That's what made SCP-187 a good SCP since it's a monkey's paw effect. I suggest you also look into more modern humanoid SCPs since SCP-187 is kind of old (but still not terrible).

SCP-5156 was first discovered on 11/██/███ in a factory at ██████ and was found by J████ B████ during a security search at the █████ factory. SCP-5156 was trying to attack J████ B████ due to unknows reasons. SCP-5156 was in a 6 foot box when found for unknown reasons.
附加记录：    

Uh, I feel like it was redacted a little too much. I want to at least know the mysterious JB's first name. Also, the factory could've been named to give the SCP more life and interest. Since you would want to know more about the factory. Also did the month have to be blocked out in the date? It seems really silly.

██████: Hello SCP-5156 today you are here so we can find out why you were in a box got it?
附加记录：    

Commas can sometimes be annoying but in conversations it can act as a break in a sentence. If I read this sentence out loud then it would be a mouthful. The way I'd put it is 

well I fell out of her body and into the box everybody said I was too dangerous and just kept me in there with the lid closed.
附加记录：    

First, well should be capitalized since it was the first word in a sentence. Second, how strong is that box? Earlier you said they had to get iron doors (I think the SCP sites do have iron doors on all SCP cells though) and this smart guy gets trapped in a box? This is also after everyone said he was too dangerous.

After the interview SCP-5156 was taken back to it's cell and the research team started to see if they could get more information about █████████ ██████████ no progress has been achieved.
附加记录：    

It's means it is. Its is for referring to objects that posses something. For example, its got a minifridge.

Subject: D-34734

Time: June 27th 4:43 PM EST
附加记录：    

The Subject and Time have to be bolded.

Result:  SCP-5156 went into D-34734's body as planned and started causing chaos. While the chaos happened a guard removed SCP-5156 from D-34734's body
附加记录：    

What kind of chaos? Just saying causing chaos makes the reader less interested in the story.

Analysis:  SCP-5156 can cause chaos inside a body but it it very easy to stop.
附加记录：    

First you said it was dangerous, and now it's just easy to stop? This is a bit confusing. I suggest going to the forums for help on making your SCP.
