SCP Greenlight - The Marvelous Transformation Alchemy Machine

05 Dec 2020 00:33

Looking to receive opinions on the creation of an SCP which functions with several functions taken from historical alchemy.

I moved and trimmed your thread summary, since it was taking up extra space in the main forum screen. Try to keep thread descriptions (the text in the little box under the thread title) and titles short so you don't crowd out other authors in the forum listing. ~Zyn

Seeking Greenlights:  Yes

Page Type:  SCP Article

Genre (Optional):  Horror, Comedy

Page Layout:  Standard, with several reports included from uses of the SCP.

Elevator Pitch:  A coin-slot 'entertainment' machine that allows the user to create transformation-inducing fluids using alchemy.

Central Narrative:  The device is initially used to create relatively 'tame' creations (improved mental acuity, stamina, etc.), similar to SCP-294, before more biologically traumatic and transformational contents are created, with a particularly clever researcher versed in alchemy theory creating a fluid that results in [REDACTED].

Hook/Attention-Grabber:  A device that uses historic alchemy in order to create transformative concoctions, requiring only a US 25 cent quarter, and a nontrivial amount of mercury.

Additional Notes:  Some aspects I'm concerned are the potential  status of such a device to create fluids that provide benefits to the user without much difficulty. I also am unsure how to make device 'dangerous' outside accidental or deliberate misuse.

Re: SCP Greenlight - The Marvelous Transformation Alchemy Machine

07 Dec 2020 19:13

Let's see what we have here.

[+]crit

[-]crit

So my concern here is that this is just 294 but with alchemy. I think this machine would be too similar to 294 to be successful on its own. You have the spark of a story with the researcher creating a potentially dangerous substance, and I would suggest to expand upon that. What did they do? What were their motivations? What are they planning to do next?

As you mention, this machine would not be dangerous on its own, so you may as well create a story around it about how people misuse it. I still think it would be too similar to 294, but if you expand the alchemy thing, this will have a little more potential. Maybe instead of a 'modern' device, it would be better to do it as an 'ancient' one?

Overall, I think you need to add more story to this.

Feel free to contact a member of the Butterfly Squad to get more crit, or PM me to take another look.
