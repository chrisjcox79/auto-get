SCP idea: punk rock jesus

01 Jan 2021 05:15

His appearence is exactly that of biblical Character of Jesus Christ, although his hair has been replaced with a large, black-and-purple dyed mohawk, and his beard has also been dyed the same color. He also carries a large electric guitar shaped like a cross.

Once materializing, he'll sing a song to the child about their sins and the punishments satan has waiting for them.
