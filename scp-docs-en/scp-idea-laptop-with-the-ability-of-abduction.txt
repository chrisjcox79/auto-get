SCP Idea - Laptop with the ability of abduction

20 Dec 2020 07:50

Seeking Greenlights:  Yes

Page Type:  SCP Article

Genre (Optional):  Mystery

Elevator Pitch:  A brand less laptop is discovered in a suburban home that has the ability to abduct people after certain requirements are met. The victims who complete these requirements are then abducted by the laptop and is transported into a video where they are essentially trapped for life.

Central Narrative:  The brand less laptop was discovered in a suburban home by a responding officer after a report of blood-curdling screaming. The officer never reported back to the precinct and the precinct contacted the foundation. 2 Foundation agents were dispatched and discovered the laptop with its screen wide open and the agents also found what was presumed to be the responding officer's firearm.

Upon inspection by SCP researchers, it was found that the laptop contained many irregular properties. The exterior of the laptop cannot be destroyed. All damages that the laptop sustained were quickly healed by the laptop itself. The laptop also displayed a website that could not be closed or minimized. The website hosted a list of videos that each had an identical runtime of 8 minutes. The most bewildering aspect of the videos is the thumbnails. The thumbnails have an unnatural ability to change depending on who is viewing the website. It has been discovered that if the viewer appears in front of the camera of the laptop then the thumbnails will begin to change. What the appearance of the thumbnails change into depends on what the viewer finds intriguing. It has been theorized that the camera of the laptop scans the mind of the viewer in order to determine what the viewer finds appealing or intriguing. When the viewer finds a video with a specialized thumbnail, the viewer feels compelled to click on the video, even if they were warned about the dangers of the laptop. In all cases, the videos do not display what the thumbnail suggests and instead displays a disturbing video. The video shows a black room with a trapped individual under a singular light that illuminates their crying eyes. The last few frames of the video is crimson red, with the trapped victim screaming in pain. If amnestics are not administered to the viewer within an hour of when the viewer watched a video, then they are transported into a video themselves.

The Foundation have made attempts at trying to locate people who were trapped in the video. Many of the researchers theorized that the laptop was sending them to a specific location. There were attempts to track the IP address of the website itself. However, on all occasions, the IP address of the website was hidden and despite the best efforts of the Foundation, it was never discovered. Another way, the Foundation tried to locate the victims of the laptop was by using a D-Class personnel. By strapping a GPS tracking device on the body of a D-Class personnel, researchers might have been able to track where the laptop was sending its unfortunate viewers. However, the laptop bested the researchers again as the GPS tracking device was left behind at the Foundation. Despite the failure of this trial, it did provide a very crucial answer that the laptop was protecting itself by leaving behind objects that could have made the laptop vulnerable such as the officer's firearm and the GPS tracking device.

Agents have tried to discover more about the origin of the laptop. A receipt was found when the agents inspected the suburban home that displayed the transaction of the laptop at an electronic store. When the agents ventured to the store and inquired about the laptop, none of the staff remembered selling the item. Puzzled by this outcome, the agents returned to the foundation. About a week later, a video was discovered that showed a person wearing the same uniform as the employees of the electronic store. It was then hypothesized that the employee who sold the laptop had succumbed to watching a video on the website and was subsequently trapped.

Hook/Attention-Grabber:  The main hook is that the people who are abducted are neither confirmed to be dead or alive. There is no clear indication in the videos that the victims die and there is only a single scene in the last 3 seconds of the video that show the victims in extreme pain.

Additional Notes:  I'm still writing the article. Any feedback would be greatly appreciated on this idea.

Re: SCP Idea - Laptop with the ability of abduction

29 Dec 2020 15:31

Summoned via PM.

I'm afraid this seems rather cliche on the face of it - trapping people in a video game isn't the most ubiquitous idea in the world, but it's not particularly original and it's rather overdone. It's not something I find particularly interesting nor particularly unique - it's a thing that does a thing, and worse still, that thing is essentially just violence. Whilst not directly killing people, it functionally does as much and that's the kind of thing we've seen a lot of onsite.

However, it's generally more important that you do something interesting with an anomaly than anything else. Currently, you haven't really got a story, and that's your biggest issue. You've got more detailed description of the mechanics of the anomaly, but not an actual narrative or story. You need to tell a story in your article - that's was SCPs really are, vehicles for storytelling, so it's really the most important bit. The crucial question is, what's the story here?

Summoned via PM: 

Hi there Costansin . Let’s take a look at your concept.

So my main issue is that there are details in the concept which are very vague. Without you providing these details, it’s hard to understand the overall concept and the direction you’re intending it to go in. For example:

The brand less laptop has a shrouded origin
附加记录：    

Not a good start here. You need to at least have some idea how it came into being, why it’s doing the things it’s doing.

The requirements include appearing in front of the in-built camera of the laptop and watching at least 1 video on a website that the laptop displays at all times.
附加记录：    

Firstly, why these requirements? They seem entirely random. Also, what video is this, and what website? Third, how are these requirements connected to each other that result in the anomaly occurring?

The victims cannot escape and the Foundation is unable to rescue them.
附加记录：    

Okay, but how does the Foundation attempt to?

My last piece of feedback is the same as OCuin's: your concept is missing a central narrative. What you’ve indicated in that section is mere description of how the anomaly functions, not the story surrounding how it was first discovered, what happens to its users, or what the Foundation does to investigate it.

If you decide to revise the concept / respond below, do drop me another PM and I’ll take another look. Best of luck!

Re: SCP Idea - Laptop with the ability of abduction

02 Jan 2021 06:21

Hi Costansin,

I've read your revised concept on the forums. It's definitely more detailed in terms of the discovery and test logs on the laptop. You've described how the scp works in alot of detail, but we still don't understand why it's doing this and why is it choosing its victims at random. Okay, so they watch a video that's  to them? So what? It's goal is simply to get as many victims as possible?

Consider revising the way the laptop picks / chooses its victims, especially since you're going for a mind-affecting anomaly. For example, could it be that the laptop allows a user to chat with a dead loved one from his/her past? This is a more natural way of engaging a user without having to affect his/her mind. An alternative would be for the previous user (now a prisoner in the laptop) to communicate with the new victim in order to trick him/her to take their place. You see where I'm going with this? Your anomalous effects should be connected in some way. Instead, they still seem entirely random.
