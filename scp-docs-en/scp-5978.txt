                        

Curse of St. Mary

Item #:  SCP-5978

Object Class:  Keter

+required

-granted

[Special Containment Procedures:  SCP-5978 is to be contained in a no way to enter the circular enclosure housing SCP-5978. Each trapezium is heavily monitored with cameras and guarded by mobile task force.

The circular enclosure is to be 10th trapezium.

Escape prevention method has been proposed by the 05-council and many outside consultants. For complete prevention of escape by SCP-5978, Foundation researchers are to follow procedure lockdown and are not be opened. Heavy artillery and Bombers are to surround site-15A.

Procedure parallel base A:  All research staff, split into groups of 5 are to be assigned in each trapezium before lock down. simultaneously the mobile task force should take there position outside the facility as well with high artillery and bombers. Researchers are to splash holy water across the Trapeziums, task force are not to help the researchers.

Procedure parallel base B:  Each researcher must surround the the trapeziums with crosses and portraits on Jesus parallel to one another. simultaneously reciting the words- .

Procedure parallel base C:  A female impregnated D-class is to offer her unborn child to

SCP-XXXX-Mary. The procedure can be performed without pain using epidural injection. The blood from the delivery is to be collected in a bucket and each male researcher must dip there hair in the blood. If the D-class refuses, Task force is to shoot her with a tranquilizer. D class is to apprehended after the Procedure.

procedure parallel base D:  Each researcher and Mobile task force are to kneel down and pray for SCP-5978 in Latin.

During the procedure, outside assigned Task force are to be in high alert in case SCP-5978 escapes. They must open fire or try to re-contain it. If it does manage to escape however, an investigation must be held for the finding of SCP-5978.

Each procedure must take place with 1 hour interval in between.

After procedure parallel base D is completed. The trapeziums are to reopen and the staff free to leave.

Procedure shining moon:  the next morning at 3 a.m., All the researchers are to observe the the sky, with their hands in a prayer like posture, preaching- ,until the clock hits 3:30. Researchers are allowed to blink while staring. If any researcher are to disobey during this procedure they are to be apprehended and suspended for an entire month.

Each researcher and task force members have to go to monthly physiological therapy/counseling. researches and D-class are given rewards If protocols are followed properly. But D-class women are expected to be impregnated by the next time procedure parallel is performed.

Any errors during the procedures may lead to the following outcomes: 

Escape of SCP-5978
Curse being put upon the the disobedient staff or D-class. The curse may vary from person to person.
Complete annihilation of the facility
Death caused to the disobedient staff or D-class
An end of the world type scenario.

If SCP-5978 does escape however, Procedure Saint Hunt  is to be initiated. A full search should be conducted with hunting dogs, tanks, heavy artillery vehicles and armored vehicles. And SCP-5978 is to re-contained. Although if the containment procedure fails 3 times at a row, SCP-5978 is to be hunted again for the 4th time and this time it is to be terminated if possible. But, During escape of SCP-5978, Site 15A is to detonated.]

+

-granted

[Description: SCP-5978 is an zero mass. SCP-5978 has facial features such has a nose, But has hollow eyes and a Wide open mouth (which also appears to be hollow). SCP-5978 appears to have a female body. She wears a tunic covered by a scapular and cowl and a veil. SCP-5978 face appears to have a slight pigment of red on it as well.

Description: SCP-5978 is an zero mass. SCP-5978 has facial features such has a nose, But has hollow eyes and a Wide open mouth (which also appears to be hollow). SCP-5978 appears to have a female body. She wears a tunic covered by a scapular and cowl and a veil. SCP-5978 face appears to have a slight pigment of red on it as well.

The cause of the miss coloring on of the face is currently unknown. As studying of the entity is difficult due to containment procedure. Direct eye observation of SCP-5978 will lead to one the following- 

Curse being put upon the viewer
Death
Loss of eye site
Color blindness
Various forms of cancer and other diseases
Loss of loved ones of the viewer
The viewer being turned into a Idolomantis diabolica (Instances referred to as SCP-5978-X)

Consequence may vary depending on the viewer.

In instances of a D-class (SCP-5978-1) being turned into an Idolomantis diabolica, intellectual is shown to be very similar to that species, anatomy shows regular organs belonging to the species. There are 8 known instances as of date. 6 have passed away.

Due to this, SCP-5978 should only be observed through the cameras and Communication should be done through the speakers. The observation room is in the 10th trapezium. the 9 trapeziums are windowless and heavily guarded at all times. the inner 7 trapeziums can only be accessed by a higher authority approval.

SCP-5978 was reported in Bangalore, India. SCP-5978 was found motionless in St.Mary Basilica.

The following is the log recorded during the capture of SCP-5978. 
附加记录：    

SCP-5978 Capture attempt (Audio to text) 
附加记录：    

SCP-5978:  *Deep breathing*

MTF-OMEGA-captain:  Halt! Put your arms up in the air and hand over yourself to us. No brute force shall be used if you do as we say.

General:  Sir! The anomaly has killed every Human in hundred mile radius.

Professor Arthi Nair:  MTF! Describe the appearance of the entity.

General:  The entity appears to be human. Female body in an outfit that resembles a nun. Its standing motionless. It appears to have killed everyone in a hundred mile radius of the basilica.

MTF-OMEGA-captain:  Its walking towards us. We repeat, stay where you are and put your hands in the air! Open Fire!

*screeching *

Dr.Arthi Nair:  Captain! Do you copy me!

SCP-5978:  *breaths heavily*
附加记录：    

End of log.
附加记录：    

The foundation tried to capture SCP-5978 for 4 years. And in the year 2014,

SCP-5978 was finally bought under the custody of the foundation. After the capture of

SCP-5978 was put in its enclosure, holy water was sprinkled in circle around it and the chamber was sealed immediately.

SCP-5978 rarely moves. Standing in one place for months. SCP-5978 is cooperative with the foundation and will reply over the speakers in the room, But speaker conversation is to be carried out only once thrice a day. SCP-5978 has also been seen moving its head a full 360 degrees, with the sound of rattling bones.

As of date SCP-5978 is estimated to be originated somewhere around 30-35A.D as a poem in a letter suggests written by Jesus Christ. She is also mentioned in in a small in one of Jesus's books.

Written below is an extract from the Letter roughly translated from Latin.

[Addendum: 

Professor Albert:  I am sorry mam, but i do not quit understand the reason for the long and complicated containment of SCP-5978. Can we bring down the Containment a bit?

Professor Arthi Nair:  I was born and raised in a very religious Family in Karnataka. My father always told me tails about the Saint of Hell. At first it seemed like a horror story just to scare me. But, 20 years later, seeing a glimpse of her full potential through my own eye. I am afraid nothing can be done to bring down the procedure.

Professor Albert:  But mam, whats the true meaning behind these rituals?

Professor Arthi Nair:  We do not question the ways of god Albert. He knows whats best for all of us, He shows us light in darkness. And hence we pray for him. And I repeat, The procedure or the containment shall not be changed, not even a bit.

Professor Albert: 

Professor Arthi Nair:  Shall her wits and will be contained withing trapezium walls of such. Her isolation be her punishment for her betrayal on life. May so the kind soul reading this, I pray upon you and your loved ones. And so he said Albert. You see my fellow scientist, a god can never be killed by us mortals, only gods can kill gods. We can only fool them with our collective minds.

Professor Albert:  Can SCP-2845 defeat SCP-5978?

Professor Arthi Nair:  Making 2 gods fight will probably lead to the end of the world! But, the final decision is left upon the 05 council. We do as we are told.

Professor Albert:  Yes mam!

End of log ]
