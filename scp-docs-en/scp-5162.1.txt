08 Dec 2020 14:26

The image is also unattributed.

+crit

-crit

SCP-5162 is an American soldier who fought in World war two
附加记录：    

It's written as World War II. You could also say 'who participated in World War II' to add more clinical tone.

SCP-5162 has the ability to produce fire and to manipulate it
附加记录：    

Huh??? How can you create fire? For one, you can't create matter or destroy it, and if this is a reality bender, it's not clear.

he also has hysterical strength and can jump approximately 1.5 meters (4.92 ft.) and he doesn't age.
附加记录：    

Hysterical strength? What does that even mean? This should be reworded as 'SCP-5162 possess a large amount of physical strength and endurance the Foundation only uses metric. The doesn't age part sounds wrong in my head. It should be reworded.

Private █████████ █████████ was SCP-5126's closest friend
附加记录：    

Closest friend kind of ruins clinical tone here. Make it 'SCP-5126's companion' or something.

SCP-5162 reportedly raided a bunker alone and eliminated 17 Nazi soldiers during the Normandy invasion
附加记录：    

This doesn't add anything to the article. It's not a description; it's his achievements. Add this to an addendum.

8 bodies were burnt by SCP-5162's fire, 6 were snapped neck, 3 were their bodies were torn apart.
附加记录：    

I can assume you're not native English, but '6 were snapped neck' makes zero sense. It should be 'six of his opponents received a cervical fracture,' and '3 were their bodies torn apart' also makes zero sense, and even if it was grammatically correct, it still makes no sense; how do you rip a body?

For the interview, it needs some grammatical corrections.

Overall, this article is not the best, and I don't think it has the quality that it needs to survive. This suffers from X-Man syndrome; a humanoid SCP which only has a special power or two and nothing else interesting about it. -1.
