OK, the big problem here is that it's justboring. There isn't anything here but some cabin that makes you angry. The problem is made worse by the lack of a narrative. The one you do have is short, generic, and the dialogue is pretty clunky.

Think about a story. Who were those people the MTF unit arrested? Who built this cabin? Who is that D-Class? Who is that doctor? These are all possible narratives. This could possibly be brought back as something related to a cult or general mystery.

I would recommend changing up the effect a little. Make it unique. I'll leave that up to you.

The positives? Well, you have good clinical tone in most places. There's no real spelling and grammar mistakes. Those are nice.

So keep trying my guy!

Adding onto this, it feels more like a Series 1 SCP, which doesn’t mean it can’t be interesting, it just doesn’t really hold up to the standards we have today. Also there are some issues with the clinical tone. I would look into this and double check any future SCPs you make.

For example:

SCP-5133 is a Class B cognitive hazard but only affects the thalamus.
附加记录：    

This should be changed to be:

“SCP-5133 is a Class B cognitive hazard, however it only affects the thalamus.”
