Conceptually speaking, this article doesn't really make itself unique. The anomaly just seems to be a bigger, sentient Earth that absorbs planets and materials in order to grow in mass. Theres no further explanation. Nothing for the reader to latch onto or really get involved with. The length doesn't really do it justice in this regard.

Logically, I'm unsure of how researchers would be able to identify that this planet has sentience. Did they go to it and interview it? How do they know that its absorbing mass? There are just a lot of open-ended statements with no real investigation or investment logically to back them up. The suspension of disbelief ultimately broke for me as I read it.

If you decide to rewrite this, I would heavily advocate for expansion. Remove the redactions and the expungments, give us a story, or something more than an empty conceptual trope. Anomalys like this need something to back them up, especially if you're intending to make it short-form. An emotional impact, a conceptually unique or wacky punchline, just anything more than 

EDIT:  Should further mention that including the  You should really try to put a spin on this and include something to attach the readers with, or try a new anomaly (or even expand your wordcount to include addenda and exploration).

SCP-5127's shape is complementary to an oblate spheroid.
附加记录：    

In mathematics, a complement is the set of elements that complete a universal set that are not part of the set in question, eg, an oblate spheroid.

I know this is a mistake, but this line implies that the shape of 5127 is all R3 embedded shapes that are not an oblate spheroid, which I think is a weirdly fascinating idea.
