Some Problems

22 Nov 2020 07:32

I recommend you try posting to the sandbox website, it is specifically designed to receive criticism and help you improve the SCP before posting. Even experienced writers use the sandbox a lot and make tons of edits before they post their SCP here. Also if you haven't read through this page yet I recommend to do so. http://www.scpwiki.com/how-to-write-an-scp Now on to my personal thoughts of this SCP. There is a lot wrong with this, but there is also a lot of potential. For starters the photo is in the wrong place, and the interview log isn't properly formatted. To fix these you should reread the formatting section of the  page. Secondly there are some more subtle errors, like how the containment box is 5x5 when it should be 5x5x5. But all of that can be fixed with some minor editing. A bigger problem is why the interview log is included. It doesn't tell us anything new except for explain how the effects were discovered, and that's just a little boring. Either change what the interview is about or cut it entirely. By far the biggest problem with this SCP is that there isn't anything particularly weird or scary about it. It would be more interesting if, for example, it did more than just erase memories. Like maybe it could give you fake memories, or if it slowly made you lose all of your memories. Just something to make the reader think about it after they've finished reading.
