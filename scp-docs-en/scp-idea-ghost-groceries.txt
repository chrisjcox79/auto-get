SCP Idea - Ghost Groceries

15 Oct 2020 22:29

Seeking Greenlights:  Yes

Page Type:  SCP Article

Page Layout: Standard (Item #, Object Class, Special Containment Procedures, Description, Several Addenda about capacity problem and an interview with the former supermarket manager about the events that occurred before the Foundation's involvement)

Elevator Pitch:  SCP is a former supermarket building where discontinued brand name products spontaneously materialize without explanation (i.e., ).

Central Narrative:  In 2005, the Foundation took over possession of a supermarket building shortly after anomalous discontinued brand name products began spontaneously materializing in the public shopping area. These  were all, at one time, sold in the region of the US where the supermarket was located before their companies discontinued their production, distribution, and sale. Later, they began materializing without explanation within the supermarket. The Foundation became involved after receiving contact reports from within the DEA and FDA about the unexplained manifestation of brand name over-the-counter medications that had been banned and classified as Schedule I controlled substances.

The SCP's shopping area mostly remains the same as it was before the store closed. Behind the shopping area is the backroom which is now used by Foundation personnel for tasks related to the monitoring and examination of the ghost groceries each time they appear. The Foundation has been conducting experiments but, so far, has made no discoveries about where the anomalous brand name products originate. In addition, the Foundation is concerned about the building's decreasing ghost grocery capacity and has requested personnel to submit suggestions on how to solve the problem. (These suggestions, incidentally, seemingly indicate Foundation personnel have low regard for this SCP.)

Hook/Attention-Grabber:  Where do brand name products go when they die? They go to SCP-XXXX (i.e., consumerism's afterlife). Here, within the confines of an ex-supermarket, the Foundation monitors, logs, and examines every defunct brand name product that manifests upon its shelves hoping to find out where it comes from and why it's here.

Additional Notes:  I originally submitted an article with this concept to the Drafts Critique forum three years ago. It obviously wasn't that good so I set it aside in the SCP Sandbox and later revised it. However, before I do any further work, I'm submitting the concept to Ideas Critique to see how it's received.

Re: SCP Idea - Ghost Groceries

20 Oct 2020 19:42

Here are some more details about the 

The discontinued products are new when they materialize on the shelves and retain this state as long as they remain in old supermarket building and surrounding parking lot. Once removed from this area, they immediately decay, spoil, ferment, go stale, or become inert. Non-perishable products and those with longer shelf lives age but remain edible or usable. Also, the discontinued products have all been processed or manufactured. There are no materializations of basic foods such as fruits, nuts, vegetables, meat, poultry, fish, flour, rice, cornmeal, salt, sugar, eggs, milk, sour cream, plain yogurt, or cheese.

Discontinued over-the-counter drugs are discussed in the interview section when the former store manager mentions the problematic manifestation of old Bayer Heroin bottles in the pain reliever aisle.
