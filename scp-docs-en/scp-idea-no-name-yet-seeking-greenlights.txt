SCP idea, Seeking Greenlights

29 Dec 2020 05:47

Seeking Greenlights:  Yes

Page Type:  SCP Article

Page Layout:  I will use the regular format. Information will be presented in the form of interviews, procedures, detailed descriptions and firsthand accounts.

Elevator Pitch : It is a mechanical humanoid woman who has a large hatch in her stomach. Occasionally the hatch will open and a roughly head-sized block of wood drops out. The robot woman then sits down at a table to work and bites chunks into the wood block. Over time over time she will sculpt a figurine from the block, all the leftover material having been ingested by her. Most of the figurines she makes are very creepy and each is it's own sub-scp with their own anomalous properties themed around different mental illnesses. She continues doing this until the entire block has been used up at which point she consumes all her creations and starts the process again.

Central Narrative:  As hinted at above, this scp is themed around mental illnesses and what would it be like if they were to take a physical form. They are intended to be the embodiment of those health conditions with the figurines linked to the most common mental illnesses appearing most often. It is not going to be blatantly obvious that any of this is the case like 'oh this one makes you feel depressed, this one gives you schizophrenia', each figurine is intended to appear as it's own interesting anomaly to a surface level reader while to someone with a keener eye the symbolism behind each doll will be apparent for them to see.

Hook/Attention-Grabber:  First off I am not aware of any other robotic humanoids that eject a block of material from their body to sculpt small figurines out of so as far as I know it is very unique and different. Secondly I plan to create a wide variety of figurines that this scp has made which will allow for people to come up with theories and ideas for them and due to their large number there is likely to be at least one or two someone will find interesting.

Additional Notes:  I could use some help coming up with a name. So far I've considered all the generic ones like Dollmaker, Sculptor, Sculptor's Handmaid, etc. but none of them are that good. If anyone has some more unorthodox ideas then don't be afraid to mention them.
