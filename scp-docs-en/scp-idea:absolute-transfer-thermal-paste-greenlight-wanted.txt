SCP Idea: Absolute Transfer Thermal Paste (Greenlight Wanted)

18 Nov 2020 02:07

Seeking Greenlights:  Yes

Page Type:  SCP Article

Elevator Pitch:  This SCP is a tube of Absolute TransferTM Thermal Paste. When applied to a single object, no effect is observed, however, when a second object is applied to the paste on the first object, all thermal energy from the second object is transferred into the first object, causing the second object to reach a temperature of 0 Kelvin, however only momentarily, as the object is heated up by air molecules hitting it. The first object gains the exact amount of thermal energy that was lost from the second object.

Central Narrative:  This SCP was found during a raid on a member of Anderson Robotics (If that makes sense), and was confiscated and tested, as no trademark for  Thermal Paste existed at the time.

Hook/Attention Grabber:  This SCP has the ability to completely transfer all energy from one object to another.

Additional Notes:  Does it make sense for the Foundation to raid the home of a member of Anderson Robotics, if not, are there any other GoIs that would make sense in this context?
