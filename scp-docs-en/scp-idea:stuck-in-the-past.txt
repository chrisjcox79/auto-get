SCP Idea: Stuck in the Past

06 Dec 2020 07:00

Seeking Greenlights:  Yes

Page Type:  SCP Article

Elevator Pitch:  Basically, it's a set of three anomalous access dial-up Internet access numbers, each with the same anomalous property: when you connect to it, your web browser is pretty much transported back to the late 2000s. Everything is as it was back then: web sites, web services, social media are all unchanged from their original forms back then, and it can all be interacted with by users of this SCP. For all intents and purposes, this SCP is a technological time machine. Oh, and it's way faster than regular dial-up.

Central Narrative:  The Foundation discovers the existence of this SCP in 2017, and a few years later they use it to interview some people from the past. Through these interviews, they discover the possible origin of the SCP, and eventually they find one of its' creators. He sorta explains how it was made and why it was made, going in depth about the state of the modern internet and how much he hates it.

Hook/Attention-Grabber:  There are many internet-based SCPs, but I have yet to see one which is directly affiliated with the internet itself rather than just using it as a medium through which to do its )

Additional Notes:  I've already typed up a draft of the entire article + the chat/interview logs, if you want me to post it here I can.

Thanks for reading my idea, everyone! This is my second real SCP idea that I've posted here, and it's the first one that I'm really, truly super proud of ^u^ It's been such a long time, I honestly forgot how much I love writing
