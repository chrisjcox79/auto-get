Fold

15 Jun 2020 10:36

This might have some potential as a concept, but the writing is poor throughout the whole thing. The clinical part can use some polish, sentences like

The subject will be instantly transported to a child's birthday party in an alternate world, however, details vary between reports.
附加记录：    

Don't read very well. The way the sentence is structured isn't short and to-the-point enough to seem clinical. Like, how did they know it's an , what exactly makes them think it's alternate (and in which way it's alternate, for that matter? Writing in general terms like that usually isn't a good idea, because they don't give enough information). Also, there are smaller errors like

heard of this and extracted subject
附加记录：    

First of all,  or something along those lines would be more fitting. The tone is off in many parts of the piece, to see more information on that I'd suggest this guide.

The dialogue is very manufactured and dry. The characters just say what needs to be said for the story to progress, the -1 himself mainly gives a common exposition of what happened during the party

I was so scared
附加记录：    

Characters announcing how they feel is a very cheap way to portray emotions and it feels very unrealistic. It's even more unrealistic when it's done twice in one log. Besides that, the point of the interview seems to be partially reiterating things that we already know from the description, unless there are some details I'm missing.

Overall, I don't think it's ready for the mainlist at all, and therefore I'm downvoting.
