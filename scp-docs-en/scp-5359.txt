                        

5359

Level3

Containment Class:
esoteric

Secondary Class:
thaumiel

Disruption Class:
ekhi

Risk Class:
caution

link to memo

Special containment procedures: 

Electrified fences have been positioned around all entrances to SCP-5359, and an additional steel bulkhead door covers the entrance to SCP-5359-03. Seven (7) armed guards are to rotate between every building in SCP-5359, with additional guards patrolling the interior of SCP-5359-03. All guards are to leave the area after 7:55pm and return at 6:05 AM

Description: 

SCP-5359 designates a group of buildings in ████, USA. SCP-5359 consists of seven (7) buildings designated SCP-5359-01 through -07. A circular stone clearing with a 30 meter circumference is in the center of the building. Each of these buildings has a different anomalous property as listed below.

SCP-5359-01 is a hardware store, labeled Terrio Tools. Any human who enters SCP-5359-01 and does not leave within fifty-six (56) seconds becomes an instance of SCP-5359-01-A. Instances of SCP-5359-01-A develop breasts if they do not have them prior to transformation (though testing reveals that testicles still remain). SCP-5359-01-A instances refer to themselves as human females named Rebekah Marrison, and develop a urge to have curly, brunette, hair through any means (including shaving their heads and replacing it with other’s hair.) Instances of SCP-5359-01-A act friendlier than previously, but retain most of their former memories and personality. This has resulted in several staff members becoming instances, but they have been declared safe and are still permitted to remain on staff. No item inside of SCP-5359 have been found to be anomalous.

SCP-5359-02 is a night club labeled as “N-N-Night Life House!” Any human entering SCP-5359-02 experiences a sudden increase in hormones, making them extremely emotional. They often become exited, happy and have an urge to engage in sexual activity. Twenty plastic, red cups are on a table, each is always full with a brown liquid, which is always cold and full under any circumstance. Testing has revealed that this substance is ordinary beer, but upon consumption, it instantly causes an extreme lack of rational decision making, beyond the effects of any alcoholic beverage. Anyone who engages in sexual activity or masturbation while inside SCP-5359-02 will find themselves unable to leave SCP-5359, being blocked by some invisible force, making them susceptible to SCP-5359-08.

SCP-5359-03 is an eight story plaza hotel, with fifty separate rooms (in addition to the first floor lobby and the hallways) on each floor. When a living creature enters one of these rooms and closes the door behind them, the door will lock and can only be opened from the hallway. All walls of SCP-5359-03 have proven to be virtual indestructible. Several Keter class SCP’s are housed in these rooms. Instances of SCP-5359-08 will not enter locked rooms in SCP-5359-03.

SCP-5359-04 is a pawn shop. It contains a variety of common objects, but attempts to remove them has revealed that they are part of the wall and/or shelves, which are in turn attached to the wall. When currency is placed on the counter, approximately 5-10 minutes later, the money will vanish and a small cardboard box will appear, containing an object, often more valuable than the monetary value of the amount given. Objects given include gold bars, silver statues and precious gemstones. It is know SCP-5359-04 will accept USD, Canadian dollars, yen, pesos, euros and pounds. When an item is taken from SCP-5359-04, the holder will have a powerful sense of generosity and will often become philanthropists, donating large amounts of money to charities.

SCP-5359-05 is a large furniture store, reminiscent of a standard IKEA. Any furniture inside of SCP-5359-05 appears to be sentient, with the ability to bend parts of itself similar to appendages. These creatures (labeled SCP-5359-05-A) are sometimes hostile or friendly, but often pay little to no attention to humans. It appears most instances of SCP-5359-05-A are fearful of instances of SCP-5359-08, though no signs of aggression between the two have been reported. Testing has revealed that instances of SCP-5359-05-A are composed of standard material, with no explanation as to how they are able to move or think.

SCP-5359-06 is a store selling various board games and video games. There is nothing anomalous about SCP-5359-06, only earning it’s SCP number due to it’s relationship to SCP-5359. All games inside SCP-5359-06 appear to be unofficial copies, containing gruesome topics not part of the original game. One such instance in the board game “Settlers of Catan,” in which the basic resources are replaced with human sacrifices and and body parts. In some cases, playing these games results in mental trauma, especially with realistic video games. As of █/█/2017, no video games already depicting violence are to be played in SCP-5359-06 without clearance from someone with level 4 clearance (see incident report 5359-01)

SCP-5359-07 is a grocery store containing solely fruits and liquids on shelves and tables. All fruits in SCP-5359-07 are composed of a thin outer layer surrounding a large amount of human blood. All liquids are also replaced with human blood. Testing has revealed that the blood is standard O-, but when any creature consumes more than five (5) ML of it, they develop an obsessive craving for human blood, trying to consume everything in SCP-5359-07 and the blood of others. If a living creature comes into contact with something affected by SCP-5359-07, they will then become affected as well. Any outbreak of SCP-5359-07’s influence is extremely hard to contain. At some time around midnight, all items in SCP-5359-07 restock themselves.

SCP-5359-08 is an unknown number of transparent, incorporeal, humanoid entities that appear at 8:00 PM and disappear at 6:00 AM. They roam SCP-5359 and will run at any living creatures, stoping their heart instantly upon contact. These creatures are completely silent and appear as three dimensional shadows approximately the same size and shape of the average human.

incident report 5359-01 

Agent ███ was testing various games within SCP-5359-06. He came across a copy of the video game “Silent Hill” It is unknown what he saw, but a recording shows bright colorful flashes on the screen. Agent ███ entered a catatonic state and has not yet recovered.
