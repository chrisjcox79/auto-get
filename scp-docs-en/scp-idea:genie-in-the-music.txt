SCP Idea:Genie in the Music?

23 Nov 2020 21:35

Seeking Greenlights:  Yes

Page Type:  SCP Article

Genre (Optional):  Horror

Page Layout (Optional):  Description, how it was found, attempts to communicate, and also some D-class sacrificing. Not entirely sure how I would go about it quite yet.

Elevator Pitch:  A Djinn like creature that eats the consciousness of people lures its victims in with music and pacify them with hallucinogens. As it consumes it becomes stronger and more physical.

Central Narrative:  The central narrative I'm going to follow is a survivor of the stereo's effects, a young man who he and his young adult friends are trying to convert this run down warehouse into some kind of club. Initially, it goes well until his buddy brings in a new blue tooth stereo they found in his attic they found locked in a strongbox. It has a surprising range of sound and usually plays very good music, however they can't seem to get it to play what they want. But the cool steam it releases is a great aesthetic for the club. A few things happen that are weird one of their friends almost ODs, the stereo turns on at random times while he's sleeping there, weird dreams and it moves about but nothing extreme. Night of their grand opening they get a respectable crowd showing up. As the party starts the thing begins producing more steam than ever, and people begin dropping like flies but none of them seem to notice things are going wrong. The whole time he knows somethings wrong he can feel it but even as he collapses and as a shadowy cloud descends over him he can't bring himself to leave. Because it feels so good. And this goes on for a while, and since there are so many people it can just feed of their surface consciousness not killing anyone right away. It becomes more and more physical even becoming an ambiguous member of their crew but no one seems to notice. It's on the last night with their biggest crowd yet that things get bad and it begins gorging itself, and people start dying. Luckily the foundation catches wind and swoops in saving him and four other people out of almost sixty.

Hook/Attention-Grabber:  I want to convey this feeling of the thrill of a party basically, but alongside the feeling that something is not right, and suddenly it's too late you realize that your helpless and that there is no escape from a now looming predator. I think lots of us will understand the hypnotic power of music, I want to give it a literal twist I think.

Additional Notes:  I fear I cannot convey what I'm trying to do with this article properly, but I hope you enjoy what I'm laying now.

I've got to say this seems like a Series I article, in that it has little narrative beyond describing the scary murder monster.

I think you need to try and develop some narrative:

Characters 
Plot  - beyond the scary thing eating D-Class
Themes  - you mention trying to capture the feeling of abandon in the thrill of a party, why not focus a bit more instead of it just being an excuse to get people dead?
An Arc  - what is the journey the reader goes through? Where do we start and where do we end up?

Re: SCP Idea:Genie in the Music?

26 Nov 2020 16:18

Summoned via PM: 

Hi again jpdelta6. Let’s take a look at your new concept.

Okay, I agree with everything Grigori Karpin is saying – basically, we need to work on building a narrative within your existing concept. Basically, you can’t just have a cool idea and inspiration for a scp these days – you have to show us how it works through the eyes of other characters within your article.

For example, what you’ve listed under “Central Narrative” is really you just describing the scp in more detail and how it functions. That’s not a story. A story involves a character encountering problems and trying to solve then. So, to give you an example:

“A shy college dormitory student discovers an unbranded cylinder Bluetooth stereo one day. He plays it in his room, and suddenly, he becomes the most popular student on campus, with people flocking to his room to party day and night… Little does he realise his strength fading day by day, and he starts to hear mysterious voices calling payment for his soul.”

Do you see how I’ve merely focused on a small aspect of your scip and turned it into a compelling discovery log? You can expand on this on choose another area to focus on. But we need to see a narrative in your articles before most of us are willing to greenlight concepts.

If you decide to revise the concept / respond below, do drop me another PM and I’ll do my best to take another look at it. For now, all the best!
