Genre (Optional):  Horror

Page Layout (Optional):  Standard SCP Article

ElevatorPitch

Closeme

Elevator Pitch:  SCP-XXXX called (Midnight sun) is a:

⦁ Large 45m dark quadrupedal aquatic life form ,similar in appearance to the prehistoric, mosasaurs.

⦁ A Tentacle mane around the back of cranium is capable of ripping flesh and infecting through a hypodermic needle located at the tip of each tentacle.

⦁ It travels at speeds = or + 400km/h and has a Strength to body to ratio of 20:1.

⦁ It can only viewed at night, during viewing avoid direct eye contact with any of the 7 pupils and avoid viewing for more than 1 minute continuously. No attempts at photographing or videoing should be made.

⦁ Only one image exists of SCP-XXXX, this image is a crude drawing made by a victim of SCP-XXXX.

⦁ It was on the move until it was contained at the THE RING 1 as a result of its ever increasing area of influence.

CentralNarrative

Closeme

Central Narrative: 

SCP was first alerted to the foundation through an agent located in NOAA(National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration), NOAA noticed a drastic change in salinity in an area 1km2 somewhere in the Atlantic. Initial expedition by NOAA to discover source of disturbance led to complete failure and resulted in the loss of all life over a period of days. Data emitted and intercepted during time of expedition discovered: salinity in the water had changed drastically and that the underwater local flora/fauna had mutated to become highly aggressive to human life2. Upon the destruction of this first NOAA expedition the foundation decided to investigate. Initial SCP expedition to the site resulted in loss of all SCP Personnel over a period of a few days, transcripts relayed back to the foundation by the last surviving agent revealed the following:

1. SCP causing this anomalous event was found to be extremely aggressive and intelligent.

2. All agents fell victim to the same events upon seeing the SCP: Severe/Intense Migraines, Hallucinations of past events, Inability to distinguish reality from fantasy, Anorexia/Changing of Skin tone, Pulling of hair, Gouging of eyes, Ripping of fingernails/toenails, Laceration of skin around legs, feet, forearm, face and jaw and Tooth loss.

3. Transcript mentioned seeing colleagues obsessing over a crude drawing of the SCP and one agent in particular saying .

Hook/Attention-Grabber:  SCP most dangerous ability is that of of salinity conversion3, which if left uncheck can cause cataclysmic change in weather, flora/fauna, and cause an unknown chain of butterfly effects.

Footnotes
1. A site designed specially to contain SCP-XXXX somewhere in the Atlantic
2. These two abilities occur innately and mutation is that of only being aggressive
3. changing the salinity of water to make it saltier or more like fresh water

So looking at this SCP here, it seems almost overly complex. You describe what it looks like, but not how it does anything listed. I am also rather bugged about the site you refer to as  I have no idea what this is, or why it holds significance. If you mean just another site, try Site-xxx. Re: SCP idea- Leviathan of The midnight Sun (Mitternachtsonne)

02 Dec 2020 07:23

Hey there Anonymousauthor, I’ll be critting your idea today!

Note that I am not an official reviewer, so take what I say with a grain of salt. With that being said, let’s begin the crit!

+Section-by-Section

Wooooosh

Elevator Pitch

So your SCP is some kind of large aquatic creature (nice physical description btw). However, I have much the same concerns as Captain Cold in regards to how the Foundation knows it’s appearance so well. Not only this, but why are they such problems associated with looking at it? Is it cognitohazardous? Also:

[]located somewhere in the Atlantic at a Site known as The Ring.
附加记录：    

I would have assumed you meant in or around the Ring of Fire, but that’s in the Pacific. So where is this Ring? And why was it constructed?

Central Narrative

You explain the abilities of this Leviathan in more detail here, where it mutates the surrounding sea life and makes them hostile. Unfortunately, this feels very similar to SCP-3069, in that the anomaly mutates all surrounding sea life. Not only this, but like Angryman22 and Captain Cold I am concerned about this narrative, which seems to consist only of research missions ending in death. I’d recommend changing it.

Hook/Attention Grabber

Ok, but why can it alter the salinity around itself? This just seems like an extra ability tacked onto the anomaly in order to increase the pressure it puts on the Foundation.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

+Thoughts

Ithis

The idea is similar in ability to SCP-3069, the narrative is cliche and uninteresting, and the hook adds an extra ability randomly, making the whole thing feel a bit disjointed.

As an extra explanation for the narrative, explorations that end in death are completely fine (I actually have a soft spot for them myself). However, people usually expect some kind of twist, deeper narrative, or reveal in these exploration logs, so work on that.

I’d recommend three things:

1. Find something to seperate this concept from SCP-3069.

2. Work on your narrative! Brainstorm ideas, think of an emotion you want your readers to feel, and let that guide the direction your story takes.

3. Check out the Guide Hub for some great essays on ideation and story-ing!

And finally, to sum up your concept in one sentence1:

A large leviathan-like creature (presumably cognitohazardous) that mutates all the surrounding sea life and increases the salinity of the water surrounding it, with devastating effects on its environment.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Anyway, that’s it for my crit today. Thanks for reading, and I hope this helps!

Footnotes
1. This is to help you get an idea of the bare bones of what a critter sees when they read your summary. You may find something you don’t like here, or that we’re getting the wrong impression.
