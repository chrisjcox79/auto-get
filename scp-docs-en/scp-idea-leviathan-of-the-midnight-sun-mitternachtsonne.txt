SCP idea- Mitternachtsonne [Seeking Greenlight]

20 Nov 2020 01:35

Seeking Greenlights:  Yes

Page Type:  SCP Article

Genre (Optional):  Horror

Page Layout (Optional):  Standard SCP Article

ElevatorPitch

Closeme

Elevator Pitch:  SCP-XXXX called (Midnight sun) according to eyewitnesses is a:

⦁ Large 45m dark quadrupedal aquatic life form ,similar in appearance to the prehistoric, mosasaurs which presents a significant cognitohazard. Physically viewing it without experiencing its effects are almost impossible with some exceptions.

CentralNarrative

Closeme

Central Narrative: 

SCP was first alerted to the foundation through an agent located in NOAA(National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration), NOAA noticed a drastic change in salinity in an area 1km2 somewhere in the Atlantic. Initial expedition by NOAA to discover source of disturbance led to complete failure and resulted in the loss of all life over a period of days. Data emitted and intercepted during time of expedition discovered: salinity in the water had changed drastically and underwater local flora/fauna had mutated to become highly aggressive to human life1. Upon the destruction of this first NOAA expedition the foundation decided to investigate. Initial SCP expedition to the site resulted in loss of all SCP Personnel over a period of a few days, transcripts relayed back to the foundation by the last surviving agent revealed the following:

1. Initial encounters with the SCP by personnel appeared to suggest SCP was benign even going so far to allow personnel to feed it at night, but it did not allow personnel to photograph or video it being careful to avoid being captured by any photographic device.

2. All agents fell victim to some of the following upon seeing the SCP over a period of days: Hallucinations of past events, Inability to distinguish reality from fantasy, Pulling of hair, Gouging of eyes, Ripping of fingernails/toenails.

3. The lead agent saw some of his fellow colleagues commit suicide to avoid the effects of SCP fully manifesting citing that they could not bear the pain. Lead agent went on to mention, even though they all gave similar descriptions of SCP they were unsure what the SCP truly looked like, upon hallucinating a number of events. On the last entry of the transcript lead agent mentions laughter and a smiling face in his head whilst noting the SCP was circling the ship.

Hook/Attention-Grabber:  SCP most dangerous ability is that of of salinity conversion2, which if left uncheck can cause cataclysmic change in weather, flora/fauna, and cause an unknown chain of butterfly effects. But no one can be sure what it truly is.

Footnotes
1. These two abilities occur innately and mutation is that of only being aggressive
2. changing the salinity of water to make it saltier or more like fresh water

Re: SCP idea- Leviathan of The midnight Sun (Mitternachtsonne)

02 Dec 2020 07:23

Hey there Anonymousauthor, I’ll be critting your idea today!

Note that I am not an official reviewer, so take what I say with a grain of salt. With that being said, let’s begin the crit!

+Section-by-Section

Wooooosh

Elevator Pitch

So your SCP is some kind of large aquatic creature (nice physical description btw). However, I have much the same concerns as Captain Cold in regards to how the Foundation knows it’s appearance so well. Not only this, but why are they such problems associated with looking at it? Is it cognitohazardous? Also:

[]located somewhere in the Atlantic at a Site known as The Ring.
附加记录：    

I would have assumed you meant in or around the Ring of Fire, but that’s in the Pacific. So where is this Ring? And why was it constructed?

Central Narrative

You explain the abilities of this Leviathan in more detail here, where it mutates the surrounding sea life and makes them hostile. Unfortunately, this feels very similar to SCP-3069, in that the anomaly mutates all surrounding sea life. Not only this, but like Angryman22 and Captain Cold I am concerned about this narrative, which seems to consist only of research missions ending in death. I’d recommend changing it.

Hook/Attention Grabber

Ok, but why can it alter the salinity around itself? This just seems like an extra ability tacked onto the anomaly in order to increase the pressure it puts on the Foundation.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

+Thoughts

Ithis

The idea is similar in ability to SCP-3069, the narrative is cliche and uninteresting, and the hook adds an extra ability randomly, making the whole thing feel a bit disjointed.

As an extra explanation for the narrative, explorations that end in death are completely fine (I actually have a soft spot for them myself). However, people usually expect some kind of twist, deeper narrative, or reveal in these exploration logs, so work on that.

I’d recommend three things:

1. Find something to seperate this concept from SCP-3069.

2. Work on your narrative! Brainstorm ideas, think of an emotion you want your readers to feel, and let that guide the direction your story takes.

3. Check out the Guide Hub for some great essays on ideation and story-ing!

And finally, to sum up your concept in one sentence1:

A large leviathan-like creature (presumably cognitohazardous) that mutates all the surrounding sea life and increases the salinity of the water surrounding it, with devastating effects on its environment.
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Anyway, that’s it for my crit today. Thanks for reading, and I hope this helps!

Footnotes
1. This is to help you get an idea of the bare bones of what a critter sees when they read your summary. You may find something you don’t like here, or that we’re getting the wrong impression.

Hey again, thanks for clarifying things for me!

Anyway, on with the crit:

So at the moment this concept seems like a giant, many-tentacled middle finger to the whole oceanic ecosystem. This isn't inherently bad, but my main problem is that the narrative doesn't really go anywhere. People die, people go insane by looking at it, and that's the end. There isn't any twist, some subversion, a big reveal, anything. I'd suggest working on your narrative a bit more (sorry to be vague) and adding a twist or something.

I also wouldn't recommend tying it into SCP-3069. While they both have detrimental effects on the ocean, that is where their similarities end.

++

--

What if these leviathans were actually responsible for maintaining life-harbouring levels of salt, and now they're waking up (presumably, they're mega pissed that we're polluting the ocean)?

What if a sample of their DNA reveals it is the exact same as a common species of eel, leaving researchers frantic to find what caused the eel to change into this?

This is all I could think of for now, hopefully it provides some inspiration!

Anyway, that's it for my recrit, hope this helps!
