SCP idea- Leviathan of The midnight Sun (Mitternachtsonne)

20 Nov 2020 01:35

Seeking Greenlights:  Yes

Page Type:  SCP Article

Elevator Pitch:  SCP-XXXX is large, dark, quadrupedal aquatic lifeform located somewhere in the Atlantic at a site known as The Ring. SCP can only viewed at night at designated platforms 350m below sea level. Videoing or photographing SCP-XXXX must be avoided as well as viewing it for more than 1 minute continuously, or making direct eye contact.

Central Narrative:  SCP-XXXX was first alerted to the SCP foundation through a field agent located inside of NOAA. NOAA identified a drastic change in salinity in an area roughly 1km2 somewhere in the Atlantic. Field Agents Smith, Jones, Roger, Greeves and Alex investigated the site of this anomalous activity using a SCP ship Horizon. Upon arrival the agents confirmed a change in salinity within the area as well as mutated and aggressive versions of the local fauna and flora. All agents sent to investigate this anomalous area during this initial Expedition, never returned. The last contact with the foundation in the transcript logs ended with the lead agent Smith saying:

After a considerable loss of life and 3 more expeditions were the foundation able to secure SCP.

Hook/Attention-Grabber:  SCP-XXXX is capable Phycological Physical Mutation creating a sub entity of various forms like the Gespenst( wraith). SCP is also capable of salinity conversion, which if left uncheck can cause cataclysmic change in weather, flora/fauna, disrupt oxygen levels.

Additional Notes: Constructive criticism is greatly appreciated.👍
