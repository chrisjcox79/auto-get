                        

Item #:  SCP-5334

Object Class:  Euclid

Special Containment Procedures:  SCP-5334 is to be contained in a locked container at all times at Site-64. A transparent window is to be in place within the container. Two guards must remain at the entrance to SCP-5334. When entering SCP-5334, two guards is to be with personnel at all times. Guards must also be armed with flame throwers.

Description:  SCP-5334 is a 8 feet long, 1 foot wide, and 2 feet, 6 inches tall Chilopoda. SCP-5334's body segments are covered in a thick shell, with dark brown stripes covering the black shell. Protruding from the shells are 2 feet tall horns, which are used as a defense mechanism. A pair of legs appear on every body segment. SCP-5334 has around 173 body segments. SCP-5334's head is an inch longer than its body segments, and contains a horn at the tip of its head. The horn is a inch larger than the previous horns. Two 1 foot antennae protrude from the face of SCP-5334 as well in back end. Two pincers, 1 foot and 8 inches each, are attached to SCP-5334's head, which are used to grab and crush prey.

SCP-5334 has been seen to crawl on walls and on the ceiling, but hardly ever does so. SCP-5334 has shown intelligence of a 12 year old human, such as recognizing faces, and threat. When approaching SCP-5334, SCP-5334 would try to crush its target with its large pincers. After the target is killed, SCP-5334 would tear away its skin and suck its blood.

Addendum:  [SCP-5334a]  If a human or an animal has been brushed by the antennae of SCP-5334, the human or animal is to be terminated immediately. Corpses, of any species, is to be burned immediately. If a target has been brushed by the antennae of SCP-5334, the target would die in 3 to 5 minutes. Their corpse would split open at the chest area, revealing four thin legs, reminiscent of SCP-5334. The legs would lift up the corpse. The face splits open and two large horns protrude from its face. The entity is known as SCP-5334-1. SCP-5334-1 is hostile against all life, and will attack anything it sees, except SCP-5334. SCP-5334-1 can't be destroyed by gunfire, even if multiple organs have been obliterated. SCP-5334-1 can only be destroyed by fire.

Addendum:  [SCP-5334b]  SCP-5334 was found in an old home in Kokomo, Indiana, two months after a meteorite had struck 16 meters away. SCP-5334 attacked the couple living at the home, and turned her into SCP-5334-1. The husband managed to burn SCP-5334-1 with a flame thrower he had, but the same didn't work with SCP-5334. An SCP team, who were about to take the meteorite to the facility, was alerted by the noise and went to investigate it. SCP-5334 was successfully secured.
