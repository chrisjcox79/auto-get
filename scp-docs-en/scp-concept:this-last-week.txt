SCP Concept: 

23 Dec 2020 13:56

Seeking Greenlights:  Yes

Page Type:  SCP Article

Genre:  Drama/Emotional, possibly a little Horror (I think - hard to pin down)

Page Layout:  Nothing incredibly special at this stage. If this ends up being my first published article, I don't want to be too ambitious and not be able to follow through without experience first.

Elevator Pitch:  Time loop, town repeating for decades. No-one in town's aware it's happening.

Central Narrative:  Loop - when first documented - lasted 1 week, resetting at midnight (internal date noted, but when undecided).

Log of events in cycle, taken not long into repeats. Includes local activities. Occasional person enters - unfortunates from before containment who didn't get out in time (e.g. Sole car enters. Driver confused because brother was in car in front, implying brother left town pre-midnight, while he didn't).

Affected bubble's temporal hazard, as anyone who enters becomes trapped in repeats if they don't leave pre-reset, arrival happening every time (shown with unfortunate agent). Living things from town cannot be removed, disappearing when extracted. Non-living objects can be, however, duplicates taking their place next repeat. Objects/people introduced from outside arrive at same time in later loops.

Speculated to be unstable spacetime bubble, causing repeats and leaking details from other realities, overwriting native. No cause determined.

Hook/Attention-Grabber:  Subtle changes between loops - only couple tiny things in given repeat (e.g. teapot's colour or someone's breakfast). Small things eventually snowball into drastic shifts over countless cycles - e.g. street names, businesses and even identities (extreme example's bartender originally documented as male replaced by woman). Log of noted changes over time provided, starting out with small things before they're moved to unseen comprehensive file, log's remainder focusing on significant alterations.

Article end: Researcher contacts superior. Too subtle to tell before, but they're now sure loop's shrinking. Not physical space, but timeframe, and events aren't exiting the loop - they're compressing, events getting closer together and causing problems. Example: Car driving through crossroads, another driving from other road seconds after. Compressed, crossings get closer until cross at same time, and either stop or crash. Regardless, it's a major change, causing butterflies. They speculate that as bubble grows increasingly unstable, timeframe will keep shrinking, events compacting until . It's suggested personnel prepare in case something emerges sooner.

'Emerges' is deliberate wording. Speculation that distortion/timeline leakage caused by something pushing into our reality.

Compression's resulted in short calm immediately pre-midnight, where there's no more events. Town disappears temporarily, leaving semi-circular crater. Was emergence of this that revealed compression.

Additional Notes:  Still trying to get SCP approved, so any feedback's good.
