I suppose that, to me, it still works because I suspect that the mental side effects are less due to something inherent in the device and more due to something inherent in human nature. Sexual excitement is about as close as humans get to a pleasure center that has a wire going into it (despite all of the sci-fi written in the aftermath of Skinner that posited , the human brain is complex enough that we don't have a central pleasure center to zap with the effects that we get on, say, mice). There have been a few studies showing that internet porn is highly addictive for large segments of the population. It's free, it's easy, it gives a massive jolt to the pleasure centers, and there are generally no negative side effects from the consumption itself. The only real limiting impulse is that people get bored after hours of watching tab A go into slot B and start wanting something more novel.
