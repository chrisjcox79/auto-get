I'll throw a few comments in before this goes away

 A couple very basic pet peeves:

No SCP should be allowed to wander around the containment site.

When something  to do or be something, the implication is it is/does something completely different and the actual thing should be interesting enough to elaborate on somewhere in the article.

 aspect doesn't really suit this object and feels tacked on.

 This guy is pretty much a living Mood Ring, which is somewhat amusing but you need to take that somewhere interesting. Since it's a pretty straightforward anomalous effect, maybe the way to go is to give him a good backstory. I was hoping to see that in the interview, but it didn't really add anything. There wasn't a hook on this one to make it at all memorable.
