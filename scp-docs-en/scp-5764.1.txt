+Reveal

-Close

Object Class:  Safe

Special Containment Procedures:  SCP 5764 is impossible to contain physically, instead, it was convinced by the foundation to stay at Site-43, in a standard cell.
附加记录：

His presence is to be monitored constantly, as he roams the site's halls, passing through walls and occasionally slipping into any room imaginable, including another SCP's cell,
附加记录：    

What does  Although even something like this provides a multitude of logical and in-universe errors. Why is this being allowed? Why hasn't the Foundation moved or convinced the anomaly to be moved towards another secure area? The list goes on)
