SCP Idea - A house and its occupant

28 Nov 2020 05:48

Seeking Greenlights:  Yes

Page Type:  SCP Article

Genre (Optional):  Currently unknown. Not sure what to categorize this SCP's genre.

Page Layout (Optional):  Image, containment procedures, description, diary/interviews/addenda

Elevator Pitch:  2 anomalous entities designated SCP-XXXX-A and B. XXXX-A is a middle-aged woman living inside the house, who can summon any object upon request, usually in a similar manner to . XXXX-B is a house, in which objects not belonging to direct family members (children and significant other) placed in it vanish after a couple hours.

Central Narrative:  (will be told from a first person perspective of XXXX-A, from a diary, as well as interviews with Foundation personnel) The woman first discovers her powers on accident, and over the course of a couple years, discovers both the extent and limitations of her anomalous abilities, as well as trying to explore the limit of the alternate dimension from which she can summon objects. The diary follows the woman's (Claudia) life as she begins to adapt to being an anomaly, as well as being introduced to the Foundation, with whom Claudia cooperates with willingly, but under certain rules: she must keep her children and husband, and her day-to-day life must not be interrupted constantly; in return, she performs tests with the Foundation, and consents to being amnesticized after tests, in order to not spread Foundation information.

Hook/Attention-Grabber:  Description of SCP told from perspective of a burglar. XXXX-B summons human leg bones covered in old, decayed flesh, as well as an unknown fluid.

Additional Notes:  I want to use the addendum section to insert this diary, but also slip in some references to the woman's background in the description. This SCP is a horror styled version of the cliche  and your mom is the only one who can find the object in the house. I have made some changes here in this post as requested by Savanac, however, some of the changes requested are planned to be written and explained in the article.

I write things. Usually they're pretty bad, but I have fun with it.

Here's some crit:

can summon any object upon request
附加记录：    

I'd like some further explanation on how this works. Does she open up the alternate dimension, reach in, and pull out an item? Or is she like Mr. Deeds from SCP-662 and goes out of view, only to return with the item?

diary
附加记录：    

Diary storytelling is a bit overdone, it's perfectly fine, it's just been done many, many times. Perhaps everything is told through a mix of journal entries and interviews with SCP-XXXX-A?

discovers her powers on accident
附加记录：    

What was this accident? You don't need to have every single detail in here, but fill in these blanks.

the extent and limitations of her anomalous abilities
附加记录：    

Which are?

trying to explore the limit of the alternate dimension
附加记录：    

What happens when she does? I just need an overview of what you plan, in addition to what the plan is based off of.

Genre (Optional): Joke
附加记录：    

This doesn't seem like it is a -J? I think you should remove this from the genre to avoid confusion.

Genre (Optional): Horror
附加记录：    

I'm not really getting any horror vibes from this, a woman can summon objects in her house.

objects not belonging to direct family members [] vanish after a couple hours
附加记录：    

I'd like to know more about how her and the house's powers work, can she summon anything? Or can she only summon objects belonging to direct family members (those objects then disappearing from wherever they were before)? And what defines ownership of an item that won't disappear?

XXXX-B summons human leg bones covered in old, decayed flesh, as well as an unknown fluid.
附加记录：    

Before this point there was no mention of SCP-XXXX-B having the ability to summon anything, and what does this line mean?

Description of SCP told from perspective of a burglar
附加记录：    

The description is written from the perspective of a Foundation researcher trying to convey exactly what the SCP is and does in an effective manner. This does not change unless your SCP has some anomalous property that makes it so (see: ●●|●●●●●|●●|●)

being introduced to the Foundation.
附加记录：    

I like the idea of this, but what exactly happens? What are her thoughts on interacting with the Foundation? If that is indeed what you intend to convey. I need an overview on the execution of the story as well as the story.

Re: SCP Idea - A house and its occupant

28 Nov 2020 23:59

I'll address these in order.

I swapped the order of the entities - XXXX-A is the woman, and XXXX-B is the house. Sorry for confusion.

+block

–block

1) Currently, diary entries describe how she summons things. She first figures out she has some anomalous properties after an interaction with a strange man who enters her house. When asked to find an object, she has an urge to reach forward and grab for it, upon which her hand enters a location not of this dimension. She can then grab the object and retrieve it. Isn't so much like a portal or some large wormhole that opens, but more similar to if you were to reach your hand into a pool of water, and the surface of the water was actually just in the air. (It isn't water, but its the same idea.)

2) I agree with you on the overused diary entries. I'll work on mixing them up with diary and interviews, but I don't want to bog down the article with multiple (possibly 4-5) interview collapsibles. I'm not sure if people want to read that.

3) See number 1.

4) Extent: she can summon any object upon request. Limitations: she has to be able to recognize what they are (not something vague, like the color purple), and she has to be inside whichever house is designated as XXXX-B.

5) I'm really not sure what to do here in terms of just XXXX-A exploring the dimension on her own. I want to start to introduce the Foundation more heavily around this point.

6) Will remove both joke and horror. I don't know what to categorize this SCP as, though.

7) See 4. Additionally, what I wish to define as an item that won't disappear is any item that is owned directly by either XXXX-A, her husband, or her children. Things such as furniture, a television, beds, microwave, etc, do not disappear. But if, say, XXXX-A's mother-in-law's cousin visits, and leaves her glasses in the house (XXXX-B), the glasses will disappear after some time.

8) I didn't word this properly. The description is written in a clinical, SCP-Researcher tone. There is a police report inserted in the Description section which contains an interview between a police officer and a burglar who broke into XXXX-A's house (XXXX-B).

9) I haven't gotten to the actual meat and potatoes of her introduction to the foundation yet. The Foundation will eventually obtain a copy of the police report and send a field agent to investigate, upon which the Foundation will respond with a bit more presence (possibly a supposed gas leak, evacuating nearby neighbors, leaving the Foundation free to inspect both the house and SCP-XXXX-A and her family for 'illness' or whatever happens when you're exposed to lots of natural gas.)

Thank you for your criticism. I'll make the appropriate changes.

I write things. Usually they're pretty bad, but I have fun with it.
