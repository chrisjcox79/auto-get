                        

Item #:  4273

Class: Keter Neutralized

Containment procedures 

SCP-4273 is to be contained in a locked box with little to no access to humans.

SCP-4273 is to be held in a small box with a code lock on it. It is currently being decided whether it should be handed out to a guard.

Description 

SCP-4273 is was a pistol. When held by someone, it urges the holder telepathically to shoot the nearest person. It will also morph into the weapon best for shooting the nearest person. When that person is killed, it repeats the process until 5 people have been killed.[WARNING: Gets a bit dark after this part]

It then urges the person telepathically to shoot themselves with the gun. After all that is finished, the gun resets to a pistol.

Sometimes the gun will try to escape with the holder. The gun was neutralized in event 4273-C. Now, it resembles a pistol and cannot be changed. It also cannot manipulate people anymore.

discovery 

SCP-4273 was found after police recovered a strange gun from a shooting. It seemed to have changed from when it was bought. Records show it was bought as a pistol but when it was recovered, it was a shotgun. Even stranger, the shotgun changed onto a pistol overnight. The SCP foundation got word of this and was recovered. The weapon was originally ranked safe, until event 4273-A happened. It was then ranked Eucild. Then event 4273-B happened. 6 people were killed, and it was ranked keter. Finally, event 4273-C neutralized it.

Event info
(if test) Test results

4273-A
The weapon was given to a guard for protection against an on going Chaos insurgency raid. 10 minutes later, the guards outside shot that guard, who was trying to escape with the gun. After the raid, its class was changed to Eucild. Things discovered in event 4273-B were not discovered here because uit was impossible to tell whether a body was shot by the guard or by the CI
N/A

4273-B
The weapon was tested on a D-class. 2 of the observers, 3 guards and the D-class were killed. Its class was then changed to Keter.
N/A

4273-C
A small piece of metal was inserted into the barrel of the pistol. It was then given to a D-class. The gun shifted into a minigun (the only thing suitable to smash the bulletproof glass). After 1 bullet was shot, the gun started to overheat and, after 1 minute exactly, blew up. The D-class was killed and everyone observing was injured, but the injuries were minor burns and cuts. The gun somehow managed to survive the blast, but the chunk of metal was now dust.
4273 was neutralized, as all further test showed that the gun was now just a normal gun. It seems as if the gun's sentience was killed.
