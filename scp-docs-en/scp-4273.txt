                        

Item #:  SCP-4273

Object Class:  Safe Euclid

Special Containment Procedures:  SCP-4273 is to be kept at Site-95, in a standard size anomalous vehicle containment bay with all surfaces covered in blast-resistant plating.

Every 6-7 months SCP-4273 is to be checked for maintenance issues. Should there be issues with any of its components, they are to be fixed as soon as possible to avoid any risk of SCP-4273 becoming unstable. Under no circumstances is SCP-4273 to be allowed to 1

Should SCP-4273 endure massive amounts of physical damage during testing or field use, it is to be put in between Tesla coils approved by Level-3 engineers until its self-repair protocol is complete. In accordance with Event-4273-4-CB, every 25th of December SCP-4273 is to be immediatly incapacitated via the C4 package in the middle of the chamber. SCP-4273 will be allowed to activate repair protocols after the 27th of December, and the C4 package replaced.

Should SCP-4273 enter an unstable state as indicated by the sudden rapid vibration of SCP-4273, the blast doors leading to the entrance of its containment bay are to be closed immediately. The blast doors are to be kept closed for approximately thirty to forty minutes until all signs of the anomalous heat cease, at which point cleanup and maintenance is to commence. SCP-4273 is to be handled with caution for the next 24 hours after said event.

SCP-4273-1 is currently kept in a standard anomaly locker. SCP-4273-1 is only to be used in testing and field usage by personnel with the requirements for a bonding procedure. Personnel are to rotate shifts to avoid a potential containment breach.

In the event that any SCP-4273 instance is encountered outside of containment, MTF-Epsilon-212 “Dragon Riders” is to be immediately dispatched to contain or terminate the instance, depending on the current situation. 2

Description:  SCP-4273 is a black Mack Titan semi-truck, weighing approximately 125 tonnes. SCP-4273 has been observed accelerating towards 240km/h within 2 seconds, despite not facilitating the engine power necessary to achieve said speeds[3 This has also allowed SCP-4273X to be extremely resistant to physical damage, as several tests and incidents have shown SCP-4273 being left as rusted and crumpled, yet completely immune to further harm of the SCP. See Addendum:Event-4273-4-CB  for more details.

SCP-4273 is shown to have a self-repair protocol, which is only observed when SCP-4273 is exposed to any source of electricity with high voltage. The exact method of this protocol's activation is unknown, but the hood of SCP-4273 will open, and a pair of conjoined jumper cables attached to any source of electricity. If the source is out of reach, the cables will act as a conductor, causing the source to emit an unknown form of energy that has been described as “Pure electricity”, although the exact nature of this energy has yet to be studied extensively due to the longevity of the protocol.4

SCP-4273’s former most notable property, however, is the vast amount of weaponry and components stored in different areas of SCP-4273’s body, as most should not be able to fit inside of SCP-4273 due to the size of several of the weapons.

A list of these weapons and components with locations and activation methods has been documented.

NOTE:  Personnel assigned to SCP-4273 that attempt or request any further attempts into studying and understanding SCP-4273 are required to view the list to become familiar with the components. Personnel not assigned to SCP-4273 are not required to view the list.

The vehicle’s ignition key, designated as SCP-4273-1 is constructed in a highly unusual fashion. It is currently the only object that activates both its engine, and its weapons, and is considered the most valuable component of SCP-4273.

SCP-4273-1 can only be used by personnel who meet specific requirements.7

SCP-4273-2 refers to any trailer attached to SCP-4273. Trailers will gain anomalous properties once attached to SCP-4273, all having varying properties depending on the attachment, yet some have been found to have constant properties, namely the growth of barbed and razor wire along any edges on the outside of the vehicle.

Addendum:  Recovery log and After-Incident-Report-4273-RC

SCP-4273 was recovered from a small town in El Paso, Texas, where a local demolition derby was being held. Doctor Sparks, who was on vacation at the time, witnessed SCP-4273’s effects when an attack by local drug cartels attempted to storm the area, presumably to kill several workers seeing as how later investigation revealed drug usage, though SCP-4273 was used to successfully terminate all attackers. However, the driver and previous possessor of SCP-4273-1 was fatally shot during the event. Dr. Sparks attempted to aid the driver, who instructed them to take SCP-4273 and let it run free before expiring. SCP-4273 was secured and contained, and the remaining survivors were given Class C amnestics. See after-incident-report for more information.

Interviewed: Subject-█ a survivor of the incident, the manager of the event.

Interviewer:  Doctor Sparks

Foreword: Interrogation of Subject-█ after recovery of SCP-4273.

 

Doctor Sparks:  Are you aware of why you are here?

Subject-█:  Not exactly, but if I had to take a guess, I’d say it's because of what happened earlier?

Doctor Sparks:  Where Was the truck found exactly?

Subject-█:  You mean the Titan?

Doctor Sparks:  Yes, do you know how it got there?

Subject-█:  I don’t know man, it just showed up, key and all.

Doctor Sparks:  You mean to say that SCP-4273 just showed up out of thin air?

Subject-█:  I mean, kind of. It just showed up one day, we tried it out and next thing we knew, we had a new attraction for the show, I mean we weren't about to let this pass up. You don’t just get a gift and question where it came from. Plus, a few months ago, we were running low on cash.

Doctor Sparks:  You mean you all could operate SCP-4273 without any trouble?

Subject-█:  Technically no. Seeing how big a hippy ███ was, it just wouldn’t start, even when he tried to hotwire it. Heck, when ███████ decided to become a medical doctor, the truck wouldn't start until he got drunk and even then it seemed to stop sometimes like he was trying to fight back against his inner rage.

Doctor Sparks:  You mean to tell me that SCP-4273 will not respond to anyone with good intentions?

Subject-█:  Two things. One, terrible nickname for it, just wanted to point that out. Two, actually no, it just worked for any one of us who just liked to live a little, have an adrenaline rush, enjoy high octane action, that kind of stuff. Look, basically as long as we didn't give a crap for our safety or others, we could drive it no problem9 Or at least that's how I was told why the thing wouldn't budge when I tried to drive it. I've driven some of the most complex things with no sweat, but this just caused my head to spin. That or it didn’t like me. Either way, I guess that's how ██████ was able to drive that thing with no problem.

Doctor Sparks:  I see. Was the attack by that gang-related to SCP-4273 in any way?

Subject-█:  Not really, I mean one of them did notice it and tried to get in before ██████ knocked ‘em out and got in and just annihilated them! Funny thing is, she was supposed to go up next for her performance with10, as you call it,spc-4372?

Doctor Sparks: Close. While we also found that you had several workers who were in touch with the gang, are you sure no other motives were the cause for the attack? Like a rivalry or territory violation?

Subject-█: No, I told them to get sober, obviously they didn’t, had to pay a huge debt, decided to give our tour a bad name, trashed everything, and now we're here. Honestly, this is the second biggest mishap we have had in the derby's history. Now can I please go? You know, I still have this thing to run. We’re putting on a christmas special soon, and it’s gonna be the big reveal!

Addendum-4273-T2:  The following is a partial log detailing tests conducted on SCP-4273 to find any additional anomalous properties should a trailer be attached. The tests shown are the most noticeable in changes in trailer properties and appearances, as most are repetitive and do not seem to change other than the growth of barbed and razor wire on the sides of the trailers.13

+log

-log

Test C - Date██/██/██

Subject:  SCP-4273, one tank trailer, primarily used for transporting gasoline.

Procedure:  The Trailer car was successfully attached to SCP-4273 without incident. The tank was loaded with gasoline beforehand.

Results:  No noticeable differences other than barbed wire “growths” the entire holding tank was composed of several layers of Lead, Titanium, and Cobalt, all of which are not used in such vehicles. Its weight remained the same. The gasoline appeared to be mildly corrosive.

Notes:  It appears that SCP-4273 changed the trailer to either hold a more aggressive substance or to withstand potential damage dealt in case of an emergency. This may be useful if SCP-4273 is approved for field use, as there are quite a few hostile substances we've encountered over the years.-Sparks
附加记录：    

Test J - Date██/██/██

Subject:  SCP-4273, one field artillery cannon.

Procedure:  The field artillery cannon was attached to the truck after security personnel were able to goad D-2001 into operating SCP-4273. The artillery cannon was not armed.

Results:  The artillery cannon displayed signs of sentiance, and attempted to move and drag SCP-4273 as it headed towards the exit area of testing zone-23. The artillery cannon was capable of producing a variety of sounds, metallic and equine in nature. The cannon fired three rounds before being detached from SCP-4273, where it ceased all movement.

Notes:  Because of this event, all trailer attachments involving weapons or objects that could be used as weapons are prohibited from testing until more information about SCP-4273 can be found.
附加记录：    

Test K - Date██/██/██

Subject:  SCP-4273, one horse trailer

Procedure:  The Trailer car was successfully attached to SCP-4273 without incident. A domestic horse was loaded into the trailer beforehand.

Results:  After about 5 seconds after being attached, the trailer started shaking violently. Upon opening the doors, an unusually large wild horse14, resembling the domestic horse from before, ran out and bit several agents while bucking frantically. It was eventually sedated via injection from 3 horse tranquilizers. The subject was eventually released into the wild after confirmation that the horse did not show any other signs of anomalous properties and was deemed safe for release.

Notes:  Along with the recent information gathered by several Task forces, this proves that SCP-4273 is indeed meant for use in combat scenarios or other such uses, as the horse was not for racing. I currently advise that usage of SCP-4273 be handled with proper care if it is granted usage.-Sparks

Addendum 4273-A-R/R:  Attempted raid and recovery.

On ██/██/████, several Foundation investigative personnel had reported a warehouse that was discovered to have been holding more than 66 instances of SCP-4273, almost all of which were in severe condition by a group of urban explorers who had reported it to a local newspaper in [REDACTED]. Foundation agents embedded in the area were able to suppress the report before it could be properly published, with witnesses given Class-A amnestics. Agents were able to locate the warehouse as described by witnesses.

Upon attempting to interact with and contain the SCP-4273 instances, all of the instances proceed to enter an unstable state and proceed to explode simultaneously. No remains were found afterward. During the event, one agent reported seeing an unusually large horse with red skin in the warehouse during the climax of the event. While an examination of the area did not report a body, a collection of documents was found within a blast resistant safe.

Addendum: Event-4273-4-CB

On the 25th of December, ████, SCP-4273 proceeded to manually activate, emitting sounds resembling grinding metal. SCP-4273 proceeded to breach containment, killing 3 personnel and causing massive damage to the site at large17 before exiting the main building, eventually leaving the entirety of site-95. Recovery teams eventually located SCP-4273 in a remote town, causing massive property damage as well as causing, at current estimates, thirty civilian casualties. Recontainment of SCP-4273 took 5 hours after it had breached containment.
