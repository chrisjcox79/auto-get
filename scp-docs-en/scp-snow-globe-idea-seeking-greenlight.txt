SCP Snow Globe Idea Seeking Greenlight

26 Nov 2020 02:57

Seeking Greenlights:  Yes

Page Type:  SCP Article

Elevator Pitch:  An anomalous snow globe that contains a fully functional society of individuals who have been transported into it. The victims of the globe are almost instantaneously erased from all historical records and human memory.

Central Narrative:  In December, 2019, a low level foundation member purchased a snow globe from a Christmas shop to gift his significant other. It caught his eye because of the realistic landscape of the snow globe, but when the gift was opened a half a month later, there were certain features that had appeared on the landscape that the foundation member were not there when he purchased the globe, such as a crop field and a few crude houses. The foundation personnel brought the globe to the foundation and has since been on 24-hour observation by a camera which has documented the progress of the internal civilization.

+block

–block

While on a routine sweep of the Alaskan Bush, a foundation team reported the immediate disappearance of a team member, as if he had been swallowed up by the air in front of him. As foundation command began to question the team on the specifics of the disappearance immediately following the incident, they could no longer recall the existence of the team member at all, and foundation records of the team suggested likewise. The identification of a disappearance was nonetheless recorded. The anomalous properties of the snow globe were only discovered when the camera observing the snow globe recorded a flare gun fire from within the forest of the globe approximately 3 minutes after the disappearance of the team member. Currently no attempts have been made from the inhabitants of the interior to communicate with the foundation, and it is unknown whether the victims are aware of their situation. When foundation personnel revisited the site of the disappearance, nothing occurred, and the source of victimization remains unknown.

Hook/Attention-Grabber:  While the idea of a miniature self-contained civilization isn’t completely unique, the events that resulted in the discovery of the anomaly is testament to the aptitude of the foundation. Someone had to be curious enough, or the foundation is thorough enough, that the cross reference between the report of the disappearance and the observation of the flare within the globe was able to occur. Regardless, it was a fun and engaging idea to develop.

Additional Notes:  I’m open to ideas on making the civilization itself more engaging, but I think the disconnect between the inhabitants of the globe and the foundation is interesting on its own. I’m also considering including a label that says SCP Foundation, which would allude that the globe might be a failed experiment and/or one from another reality.

This:

The victims of the globe are almost instantaneously erased from all historical records and human memory.
附加记录：    

Is inconsistent with this:

a foundation team reported the immediate disappearance of a team member, as if he had been swallowed up by the air in front of him. As foundation command began to question the team on the specifics of the disappearance immediately following the incident, they could not recall the existence of the team member at all, and foundation records of the team suggested likewise. The identification of a disappearance was nonetheless recorded.
附加记录：    

+block

–block

if the victim instantaneously disappears from human memory and records, then the agent wouldn’t have noticed. Depending on how strong the editing is, maybe even if there was footage of the victim’s disappearance, that too would be erased. You see what I mean?

Someone had to be curious enough, or the foundation is thorough enough, that the cross reference between the report of the disappearance and the observation of the flare within the globe was able to occur.
附加记录：    

as noted above, there wouldn’t necessarily be a way to tell this from footage you have to discern the strength of the reality warping this item would create.

there’s not much in the way of a narrative here. This is “strange thing’s details” and “vaguely how it is discovered”

What will the overall arc of the narrative be? Novelty of the anomaly is not enough to engage usually because it’s hard to really make a novel anomaly. Instead the focus should be on how you’ll craft your story.

So what’s the article about, besides the discovery? If it’s solely about the anomalous nature of the item, that isn’t a narrative, that’s an entry in a DD rule book. Why do you find this interesting? What will engage the reader in your story?

Re: SCP Snow Globe Idea Seeking Greenlight

27 Nov 2020 05:55

Thank you for your feedback! I've put together some thoughts in response to your critiques that clear some things up if you'd like to take a look.

+block

–block

In reference to the inconsistencies you mentioned, I made the forum post before writing the sandbox, so I've flushed things out there that I didn't realize were vague in the post, with this aspect being one of them. Essentially there's an extremely quick deterioration of memory and records, so  aren't the correct words for it in hindsight, and I specify in the sandbox that it takes about 30 seconds from the incident to the time of total erasure, as if the snow globe is rapidly sucking their existence away.

A second distinction that I now realize is unclear in the post is that the snow globe itself has already been obtained by the Foundation when the MTF member disappears and the report is made. Essentially, the snow globe is constantly being recorded so that the foundation can study an isolated instance of human society, and as a result of this non-stop recording, it's able to capture the flare. The MTF team would not have been recorded themselves, but there would have been time to report the disappearance with the hinderance of specifics quickly evaporating from the minds of the witnesses. I'll take the time now to revise the forum post to clear up these discrepancies.

I do hear you on your DD rulebook entry analogy though, and I've had a few ideas floating in the back of my head on ways to enlarge the importance of the item itself. Notably, I've been considering including multiple instances of the snow globe that come into the possession of the Foundation, and the consequent discovery that the environments are linked to one another. I'm also open to constructing a narrative around someone who manages to find a way out, although I would have to take time to determine what life on the inside would truly be like.
