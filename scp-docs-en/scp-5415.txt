                        

Item #:  SCP-5415

Object Class:  Euclid

Special Containment Procedures:  If SCP-5415 is to vanish from its containment cell, it is to be tracked down and brought back to a higher security containment cell. An interview must be done to get an understanding of why it was to vanish.

SCP-5415 is to be locked up in a very tight area to stop its movement. SCP-5415 will have high security to watch it so it does not vanish. As this object moves over time, it will slowly start to mold and rot away. The SCP foundation will try there best to prevent SCP-5415 from molding away.

Description:  SCP-5415 is a kids toybox that is capable of slowly moving. SCP-5415 is 51 centimeters tall and is 102 centimeters in length.

This object weighs approximately 15.4 kilograms. SCP-5415 is capable of moving approximately 1 meter over a span of 24 hours.

The object will move only when the target is sleeping. When the target has gone to sleep the object will gradually start moving closer to the bed.

If the object is disturbed by the target it will return to the beginning location in a span of 24 hours. SCP-5415 will move an extra meter every night that it was touched.

SCP-5415 has no humanoid features. If SCP-5415 were to get to their target after many nights, the top lid of the chest would unbolt its self and unroll wide open. Inside SCP-5415 is nothing but a shadow. When the chest is fully unbolted and unrolled wide open a mysterious energetic force will pull the target into itself in a heartbeat and the object will vanish back into a toy store the next morning.

Recent Incidents

Details:  SCP-5415 has starting to show up all over the world. Little kids of all ages are starting to vanish from there homes because of the mysterious object.

Solution:  The SCP foundation will make sure every single duplicated SCP-5415 will be contained in separate cells.

Details:  SCP-5415 has been reported to leave its cell around midnight and found in a toy store later that day.

Solution:  SCP-5415 will be brought back to the foundation and make sure no one knows about SCP-5415.
